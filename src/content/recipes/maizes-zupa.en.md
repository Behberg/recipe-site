---
title: Rye bread soup with whipped cream
description: A sweet dark rye bread soup with dried fruit, cinnamon and whipped cream. Grandma’s dessert.
steps:
  - Cut the rye bread into pieces and dry them in the oven at 150 °C for about 15 minutes.
  - Pour boiling water over the bread and leave to swell for 1 hour or overnight.
  - Push the mixture through a sieve or blend until smooth.
  - Add the dried fruit, sugar and cinnamon. Simmer over a low heat for 15 minutes, stirring often.
  - Add the lemon juice and let it cool.
  - Serve cold with whipped cream.
---

A dessert made from yesterday’s bread: nothing goes to waste, and it tastes like childhood.
