---
title: Pork in gravy with potatoes
description: Tender braised pieces of pork in a sour cream sauce with onions. Served with boiled potatoes.
steps:
  - Cut the meat into cubes, season with salt and pepper and brown it in oil.
  - Add the chopped onions and grated carrot and fry for 5 minutes.
  - Sprinkle over the flour, stir and pour in the water. Add the bay leaves.
  - Braise, covered, over a low heat for 1 hour, until the meat is tender.
  - Stir in the sour cream, heat through and serve with boiled potatoes and cucumbers.
---

A classic of Latvian canteens and home kitchens, loved by several generations.
