---
title: "Buffalo cauliflower \"wings\""
description: "Crisp pieces of cauliflower in a hot sauce: a party snack that meat eaters finish too."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 220 °C and line a baking tray with baking paper. Break the cauliflower into bite sized florets."
  - "Whisk the flour with the plant milk, garlic powder, paprika and salt into a thick batter."
  - "Dip the florets in the batter, roll in the panko and place on the tray."
  - "Bake for 20 minutes, turn and bake for 5 more minutes."
  - "Mix the hot sauce with the oil and maple syrup, pour over the florets and toss. Bake for another 8 to 10 minutes. Serve with celery sticks."
---

Add the sauce only at the end, so the coating stays crisp.
