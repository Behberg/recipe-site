---
title: "Onion and bacon tart"
description: "Crisp shortcrust tart with slowly softened onions, bacon and a creamy egg filling. Excellent both warm and cold."
steps:
  - "Rub the flour, salt and butter into crumbs, add the water and knead quickly. Wrap in cling film and chill for 30 minutes."
  - "Slice the onions into thin half rings and cook them gently in a pan for 20 minutes until soft and golden. Cut the bacon into strips and fry."
  - "Heat the oven to 190 °C. Roll out the pastry, line a 24 cm tin and prick with a fork. Bake for 12 minutes."
  - "Beat the eggs with the cream, grated cheese, pepper and nutmeg."
  - "Spread the onions and bacon over the base, pour over the egg mixture and bake for 25 to 30 minutes until set."
tips:
  - "Do not rush the onions: the slower they cook, the sweeter the tart."
---

This tart turns plain onions into a festive dish. In Germany it is baked in autumn to go with the new wine.
