---
title: "Chicken and vegetable tortilla wraps"
description: "Quick tortilla wraps with chicken, fresh vegetables, cheese and a garlic yoghurt sauce. A packed lunch in 15 minutes."
steps:
  - "Mix the yoghurt with crushed garlic, dill, salt and pepper."
  - "Shred the chicken and chop the tomatoes and cucumber."
  - "Warm the tortillas in a dry pan for 20 seconds so they are pliable."
  - "Spread each with sauce and top with lettuce, chicken, vegetables and cheese."
  - "Fold in the sides, roll up tightly and cut in half on the diagonal."
tips:
  - "Toasted seam side down in a pan, the wraps turn crisp and stay closed."
---

A wrap is a sandwich that does not fall apart in your bag. That is exactly why it is great to take along.
