---
title: Grey peas with bacon
description: "Latvia’s national dish: soft grey peas with crispy bacon, onions and a glass of kefir."
steps:
  - Wash the peas and soak them in cold water overnight.
  - Drain, cover the peas with fresh water and simmer gently for about 1 hour, until soft. Salt only at the end.
  - While the peas are cooking, dice the bacon and fry it in a pan until golden and crispy.
  - Add the finely chopped onions and fry for another 5 to 7 minutes, until soft and sweet.
  - Drain the cooked peas, mix with the bacon and onions and salt to taste.
  - Serve hot with a glass of cold kefir or soured milk.
tips:
  - If the peas have not been soaked, cooking can take up to 2 hours.
  - The kefir is optional, but it is what makes the dish truly Latvian.
---

A Latvian Christmas table without grey peas is unthinkable. They say every pea you eat brings luck in the coming year, so help yourself to a proper portion.
