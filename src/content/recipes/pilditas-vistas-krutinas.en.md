---
title: "Spinach and cheese stuffed chicken breasts"
description: "Chicken breasts with a melting mozzarella, cream cheese and spinach filling, baked in the oven. Looks special, weekday effort."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C."
  - "Chop the spinach and mix with the cream cheese, diced mozzarella and crushed garlic."
  - "With a sharp knife cut a deep pocket into each breast without cutting all the way through."
  - "Fill the pockets with the cheese mixture and close with cocktail sticks."
  - "Rub the chicken with oil, salt, pepper and paprika. Bake for 22 to 25 minutes."
  - "Rest for 5 minutes before slicing. Serve with salad."
tips:
  - "For juicy chicken take it out when the centre reaches 72 °C."
---

When the knife cuts through and green, melting cheese flows out, it looks like something far more complicated.
