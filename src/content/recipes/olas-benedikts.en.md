---
title: "Eggs Benedict with ham and hollandaise"
description: "The brunch classic: toast, ham, a poached egg and buttery hollandaise sauce. Looks like a café, made at home."
steps:
  - "For the sauce, melt the butter. Whisk the yolks with the lemon juice and a spoonful of water in a bowl set over a pan of simmering water until foamy."
  - "Slowly pour in the hot butter in a thin stream while whisking, until the sauce thickens. Season with salt and keep warm."
  - "Bring a pan of water with the vinegar to the boil, then lower the heat to a gentle simmer. Crack an egg into a cup."
  - "Swirl the water and slide the egg into the centre. Poach for 3 minutes and lift out with a slotted spoon."
  - "Toast the bread and top with the ham and the egg."
  - "Spoon over the hollandaise and sprinkle with pepper and chives."
tips:
  - "If the sauce starts to split, take the bowl off the heat and whisk in a spoonful of cold water."
---

Eggs Benedict turns an ordinary Saturday into an occasion. The secret is fresh eggs and no rushing.
