---
title: "Rowan and apple jelly"
description: "Clear, ruby red rowan and apple jelly with a pleasant bitter edge. Wonderful with game, duck and cheese."
yield: "about 4 jars of 0.3 l"
steps:
  - "Pick the rowan berries off the bunches after the frosts and wash them. Quarter the apples, skins and cores included."
  - "Cover the berries and apples with the water and simmer for 30 minutes until everything is soft."
  - "Tip into a muslin bag or sieve and let the juice drip through for several hours. Do not squeeze, so the jelly stays clear."
  - "Measure the juice: use 800 g sugar per litre. Boil for 10 to 15 minutes, skimming off the foam."
  - "Test on a cold plate: if a drop sets, the jelly is ready. Fill sterilised jars."
tips:
  - "Apples provide the pectin, so the jelly sets without gelatine."
---

Rowan berries were long thought of as bird food. In a jelly their bitterness becomes elegance.
