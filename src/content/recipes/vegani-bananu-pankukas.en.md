---
title: "Vegan banana pancakes"
description: "Fluffy pancakes with no eggs or milk: the banana binds the batter and adds sweetness."
steps:
  - "Mash the banana in a bowl with a fork until smooth."
  - "Add the plant milk, sugar, cinnamon and salt and stir."
  - "Sift in the flour and baking powder and fold briefly. The batter should stay thick, with a few small lumps."
  - "Heat a little oil in a pan over medium heat. Fry small pancakes for 2 minutes on each side."
  - "Serve with maple syrup and berries."
---

The riper the banana, the sweeter the pancakes.
