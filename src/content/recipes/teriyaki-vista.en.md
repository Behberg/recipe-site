---
title: Teriyaki chicken with rice
description: Glazed chicken thighs in a glossy, sweet and savoury teriyaki sauce with rice and sesame.
steps:
  - Rinse the rice and cook it according to the packet.
  - Mix all the sauce ingredients in a small bowl.
  - Cut the chicken into large pieces and fry in oil over a high heat until golden on all sides.
  - Pour over the sauce and cook for 3 to 4 minutes, until the sauce thickens and coats the meat in a glossy glaze.
  - Serve with rice and sprinkle with sesame and finely sliced spring onions.
---

Quicker than ordering a takeaway, and much tastier.
