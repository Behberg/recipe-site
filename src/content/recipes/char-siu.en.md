---
title: "Char siu (Chinese honey glazed roast pork)"
description: "Cantonese char siu: pork in a sweet soy, honey and five spice marinade, roasted to a sticky red glaze. Served with rice."
steps:
  - "Cut the pork into long strips about 4 cm thick."
  - "Mix the soy sauce, oyster sauce, half the honey, the five spice, crushed garlic, ginger and sesame oil. Marinate the pork for at least 4 hours or overnight."
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C. Place the pork on a rack over a tin with a little water in it."
  - "Roast for 35 minutes, turning and basting with the marinade."
  - "Mix the remaining honey with a spoonful of marinade, brush over the pork and roast for 10 to 15 minutes more, until caramelised at the edges. Slice."
tips:
  - "The water in the tin stops the honey burning and smoking."
---

In Hong Kong char siu hangs in every restaurant window. At home it tastes just as good and smells even better.
