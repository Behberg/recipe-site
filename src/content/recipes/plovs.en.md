---
title: Plov
description: Central Asian rice slowly cooked with lamb or beef, carrots, garlic and cumin.
steps:
  - Wash the rice in several changes of water until clear, and soak for 30 minutes.
  - Heat oil in a heavy based pan and fry the onion rings, then the meat until browned.
  - Add the carrots cut into strips, the cumin and coriander. Cook for 10 minutes.
  - Pour in half the water, add salt and simmer, covered, for 40 minutes.
  - Spread the drained rice evenly on top, push in the garlic bulbs and add water to 2 cm above the rice. Do not stir!
  - Cook over a medium heat until the water has evaporated, then cover and cook over a low heat for another 25 minutes.
---

In Uzbekistan plov is cooked in huge cauldrons, and only by men.
