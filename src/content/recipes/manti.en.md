---
title: "Manti (steamed meat and onion dumplings)"
description: "Large steamed Central Asian dumplings with a juicy meat, onion and cumin filling. Served with sour cream or yoghurt."
steps:
  - "Mix the dough ingredients and knead for 10 minutes until firm and smooth. Wrap and rest for 30 minutes."
  - "For the filling, finely chop the onions and mix with the meat, cumin, salt, pepper and a little water."
  - "Roll the dough out thinly and cut into 10 cm squares."
  - "Put a spoonful of filling and a small piece of butter in the middle of each. Bring the corners together and pinch the edges to form an envelope."
  - "Oil a steamer basket, arrange the manti and steam for 40 to 45 minutes."
  - "Serve hot with sour cream, yoghurt or butter."
tips:
  - "No steamer? Use a metal sieve over a pan of boiling water with a lid."
---

Manti are a festive dish in Central Asia. The whole family makes them together, which matters as much as eating them.
