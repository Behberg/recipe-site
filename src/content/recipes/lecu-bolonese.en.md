---
title: "Lentil bolognese with spaghetti (vegetarian)"
description: "Thick, hearty vegetarian bolognese with red lentils, vegetables and tomatoes. Vegan, cheap and just as good as with meat."
steps:
  - "Finely chop the onion, carrot and celery. Soften in oil in a pan for 8 minutes."
  - "Add the garlic and tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes."
  - "Add the rinsed lentils, passata, stock, oregano, salt and pepper."
  - "Simmer gently for 25 minutes, stirring, until the lentils are soft and the sauce is thick."
  - "Meanwhile cook the spaghetti. Serve with the sauce and cheese if you like."
tips:
  - "The sauce freezes well in portions: perfect for quick dinners later."
---

In this sauce the lentils break down and resemble minced meat. Many people do not notice the difference.
