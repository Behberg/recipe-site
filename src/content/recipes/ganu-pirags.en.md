---
title: Shepherd’s pie
description: A stew of minced meat and vegetables under a blanket of mashed potato, baked in the oven.
steps:
  - Boil the potatoes and mash them with the butter, milk and salt.
  - In a pan, fry the onion and grated carrots, add the meat and fry until browned.
  - Stir in the tomato paste and flour, pour in the stock and add the thyme. Simmer for 15 minutes, adding the peas at the end.
  - Put the meat into a dish, spread the mash on top and make ridges with a fork. Sprinkle with cheese.
  - Bake in the oven at 200 °C for 25 minutes, until the top is golden.
---

Made with lamb it is shepherd’s pie; made with beef, the British call it cottage pie.
