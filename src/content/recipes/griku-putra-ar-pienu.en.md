---
title: "Buckwheat porridge with milk and butter"
description: "Fluffy buckwheat with hot milk, butter and honey. A filling, gluten free breakfast that many remember from childhood."
steps:
  - "Rinse the buckwheat and add it to boiling salted water."
  - "Simmer covered over a low heat for 15 minutes, until the water is absorbed."
  - "Take off the heat and leave covered for 5 minutes."
  - "Spoon into bowls, add butter and honey and pour over hot milk."
tips:
  - "Toast the buckwheat in a dry pan for 3 minutes first for an even nuttier flavour."
---

Buckwheat with milk is the breakfast parents made when they wanted the children full until lunch.
