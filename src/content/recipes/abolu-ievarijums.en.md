---
title: Apple jam with cinnamon
description: Amber apple jam with cinnamon and lemon. It smells like apple pie.
yield: about 4 jars of 0.5 l
steps:
  - Peel and core the apples and cut them into thin slices.
  - Wash the lemon, grate the zest and squeeze out the juice.
  - In a pan, mix the apples, sugar, water, lemon and cinnamon stick. Leave for 2 hours.
  - Simmer over a low heat for 35 to 40 minutes, stirring, until the apples are translucent and the syrup is thick.
  - Remove the cinnamon stick. Pour the hot jam into jars and seal.
---

A great way to use up the autumn apple harvest.
