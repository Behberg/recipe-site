---
title: "Latvian barley sausages (Christmas sausages)"
description: "Traditional Latvian barley sausages with smoked bacon, onions and marjoram, baked until crisp. A Christmas table classic."
steps:
  - "Soak the casings in warm water and rinse them several times."
  - "Rinse the barley and simmer in the stock for 30 minutes, until nearly soft and the liquid is absorbed."
  - "Dice the bacon and render it in a frying pan, add the finely chopped onions and fry until golden."
  - "Mix the barley with the bacon and onions, marjoram, salt and pepper. It should be juicy and well seasoned."
  - "Using a funnel or sausage nozzle, fill the casings loosely and tie every 30 cm. Do not overfill or they will burst."
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C. Put the sausages in an oiled tin, prick them with a needle and bake for 50 to 60 minutes, turning, until browned. Serve with lingonberries."
tips:
  - "Barley sausages freeze well raw; bake them straight from frozen, adding 20 minutes."
---

Making sausages at Christmas is a family job. Someone fills, someone ties, and everyone argues about the marjoram.
