---
title: "Aubergine caviar (vegetable spread)"
description: "Slow cooked aubergine caviar with peppers, tomatoes and carrots. Great on rye bread, with meat or preserved for winter."
yield: "about 5 jars of 0.5 l"
steps:
  - "Dice the aubergines, sprinkle with salt and leave for 20 minutes. Rinse and squeeze dry."
  - "In a large pan fry the onions and grated carrots in oil for 10 minutes."
  - "Add the diced peppers and fry for 5 more minutes. Add the aubergines and fry for 10 minutes."
  - "Add the grated tomatoes, sugar and salt. Cover and simmer for 40 minutes, stirring."
  - "Add the garlic and vinegar and simmer for 5 minutes. Blend part of it if you like."
  - "Fill sterilised jars while hot and seal."
tips:
  - "Salting the aubergines draws out bitterness and stops them soaking up so much oil."
---

Aubergine caviar is a classic that used to be in every fridge. The homemade version is much fresher than shop bought.
