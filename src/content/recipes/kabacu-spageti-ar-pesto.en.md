---
title: "Courgette spaghetti with basil pesto and tomatoes"
description: "Light courgette noodles with fresh basil pesto, pine nuts and cherry tomatoes. A gluten free summer dinner in 20 minutes."
steps:
  - "Cut the courgettes into long, thin strands with a spiraliser or vegetable peeler."
  - "For the pesto, blend the basil, toasted pine nuts, parmesan, garlic, oil and salt."
  - "Warm the courgette noodles in a pan over a medium heat for 2 minutes, just to soften slightly."
  - "Take off the heat and stir in the pesto and halved tomatoes."
  - "Serve straight away with a little more parmesan."
tips:
  - "Do not overheat the courgette noodles or they release water and turn soft."
---

When there are more courgettes than anyone can eat, you can turn them into pasta. Light but satisfying.
