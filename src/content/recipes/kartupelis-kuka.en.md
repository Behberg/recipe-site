---
title: “Potato” cake
description: Legendary chocolate biscuit truffles with condensed milk and cocoa. No baking.
yield: about 12 cakes
steps:
  - Blitz the biscuits into fine crumbs. Chop the nuts.
  - Beat the butter with the condensed milk and cocoa.
  - Mix with the biscuit crumbs and nuts until the mixture is moist and holds together.
  - Shape into oval, potato shaped cakes and roll them in cocoa.
  - Put in the fridge for at least 1 hour.
---

Anyone who grew up in Latvia recognises this dessert from the very first bite.
