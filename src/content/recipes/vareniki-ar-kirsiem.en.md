---
title: "Cherry vareniki (sweet dumplings)"
description: "Tender boiled vareniki filled with juicy cherries and sugar, served with sour cream. A summer sweet dish that freezes well."
steps:
  - "Mix the dough ingredients and knead for 5 minutes to a soft, smooth dough. Wrap and rest for 30 minutes."
  - "Pit the cherries and let the juice drain. Save the juice for the sauce."
  - "Roll the dough out thinly and cut out circles with a glass."
  - "Put 3 or 4 cherries in each, sprinkle with sugar and starch. Fold over and seal the edges firmly."
  - "Cook in boiling salted water for 4 to 5 minutes after they float."
  - "Bring the cherry juice to the boil with a spoonful of sugar. Serve the vareniki with sour cream and the juice poured over."
tips:
  - "The starch thickens the juice inside so the dumplings do not burst while cooking."
---

When the sour cream swirls into the dark red cherry juice, the plate looks like a painting.
