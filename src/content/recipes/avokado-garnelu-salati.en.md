---
title: "Avocado and prawn salad with lime"
description: "Fresh avocado and prawn salad with cherry tomatoes, coriander and a lime dressing. Light and festive in 15 minutes."
steps:
  - "Dice the avocado and sprinkle with half the lime juice."
  - "Halve the tomatoes."
  - "Whisk the rest of the lime juice with the oil, salt and pepper."
  - "Toss the leaves, avocado, tomatoes and prawns, drizzle with the dressing and scatter with coriander."
tips:
  - "Freshly cooked prawns taste best: boil them for 2 minutes in salted water with lemon."
---

This salad looks like it came from a restaurant, yet it needs only 15 minutes and almost no cooking.
