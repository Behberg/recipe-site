---
title: "Green pea and mint soup"
description: "Bright green pea soup with mint and sour cream, made with frozen peas. A fresh flavour, ready in 20 minutes."
steps:
  - "Soften the onion in butter in a pan for 5 minutes."
  - "Add the stock, bring to the boil and add the peas. Simmer for 5 minutes."
  - "Add the mint leaves and blend until completely smooth."
  - "Stir in the lemon juice, salt and pepper."
  - "Serve with a spoonful of sour cream and a mint leaf."
tips:
  - "Frozen peas are sweeter than fresh shop peas because they are frozen straight after picking."
---

A spring soup you can make at any time of year. A bag of peas in the freezer and 20 minutes.
