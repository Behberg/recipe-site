---
title: Albóndigas in tomato sauce
description: Spanish meatballs in a rich tomato sauce with pepper and garlic.
steps:
  - Soak the bread in the milk and squeeze it out. Mix with the meat, egg, crushed garlic, parsley, salt and pepper.
  - Shape into walnut sized balls and brown them in olive oil on all sides. Take them out.
  - In the same pan, fry the onion, add the pepper and tomatoes. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  - Put the meatballs into the sauce and simmer, covered, for 15 minutes.
  - Serve with bread or rice.
---

In Spain they are served as tapas in small clay dishes, but with rice they make a proper dinner.
