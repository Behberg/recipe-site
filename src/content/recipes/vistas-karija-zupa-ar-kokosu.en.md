---
title: "Chicken curry noodle soup with coconut milk"
description: "Fragrant, gently spicy chicken soup with coconut milk, curry, rice noodles and lime. Warming and ready in half an hour."
steps:
  - "Fry the onion in oil for 5 minutes, add the garlic, ginger and curry powder and cook for 1 minute."
  - "Pour in the stock and coconut milk and bring to the boil."
  - "Add the thinly sliced chicken and pepper and simmer for 10 minutes."
  - "Cook the noodles according to the packet and divide between bowls."
  - "Season the soup with lime juice and salt, ladle over the noodles and sprinkle with coriander."
tips:
  - "Keep the noodles separate or they swell in the soup and go soft."
---

This soup is like a warm blanket on a dark evening. Coconut milk makes it mellow and the curry warms you to the bone.
