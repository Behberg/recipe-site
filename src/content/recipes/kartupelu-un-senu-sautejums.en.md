---
title: "Potatoes and mushrooms in sour cream"
description: "A hearty potato and mushroom stew in sour cream with onions and dill. Autumn home cooking that tastes of the countryside and forest."
steps:
  - "Slice the mushrooms and fry in butter in a large pan for 8 minutes until the juices evaporate. Add the onions and fry for 5 more minutes."
  - "Add the potatoes in large cubes, the bay leaf, salt and pepper."
  - "Pour in the hot water, cover and simmer for 20 minutes, until the potatoes are soft."
  - "Stir in the sour cream and heat for 3 more minutes without boiling."
  - "Scatter with plenty of dill and serve with pickled cucumbers."
tips:
  - "Wild mushrooms make it much more fragrant, but button mushrooms work too."
---

This is what gets cooked after a mushroom hunt, when tiredness is great and appetite even greater.
