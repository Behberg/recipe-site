---
title: Upside down apple tart (tarte Tatin)
description: Caramelised apples under crisp puff pastry. After baking, the tart is turned upside down.
steps:
  - Peel and core the apples and cut them into quarters.
  - In an ovenproof pan (about 24 cm), melt the sugar until it turns into a golden caramel. Stir in the butter and vanilla.
  - Pack the apples tightly into the caramel, rounded side down. Cook for 10 minutes, until the apples soften slightly.
  - Cover with a round of puff pastry and tuck the edges into the pan.
  - Bake in the oven at 200 °C for 25 to 30 minutes, until the pastry is golden.
  - Wait 5 minutes, put a plate on top and quickly turn it over. Serve warm with cream or ice cream.
---

Legend has it that the Tatin sisters turned their tart over by accident, and the result was better than the original.
