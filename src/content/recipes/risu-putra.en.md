---
title: Rice pudding with milk
description: A creamy, slowly cooked rice porridge with milk, cinnamon and butter.
steps:
  - Rinse the rice and cook it in the water for 10 minutes, until the water is absorbed.
  - Add the milk, sugar and salt. Cook over a very low heat for 20 to 25 minutes, stirring often.
  - When the porridge is thick and creamy, stir in the butter.
  - Serve with cinnamon sugar or jam.
tips:
  - The porridge thickens as it cools. Add a little milk when reheating if needed.
---

A Sunday morning classic that also works for dinner.
