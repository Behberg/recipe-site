---
title: Alfajores
description: Tender cornflour cookies sandwiched with caramelised condensed milk and rolled in coconut.
yield: about 15 alfajores
steps:
  - Beat the butter with the icing sugar, then mix in the yolks and vanilla.
  - Add the cornflour, flour and baking powder and knead into a soft dough. Chill for 30 minutes.
  - Roll out to 5 mm thick and cut out small rounds.
  - Bake in the oven at 175 °C for 10 to 12 minutes. The cookies should stay pale.
  - Once cool, sandwich them in pairs with the caramel and roll the edges in desiccated coconut.
---

In Argentina alfajores are sold at every kiosk, but homemade ones are much more tender.
