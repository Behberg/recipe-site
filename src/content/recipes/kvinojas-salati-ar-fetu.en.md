---
title: "Quinoa salad with feta, cucumber and tomatoes"
description: "Filling quinoa salad with feta, cucumber, tomatoes, olives and a lemon dressing. A healthy, gluten free packed lunch."
steps:
  - "Rinse the quinoa in a sieve, add to boiling salted water and simmer for 12 to 15 minutes until the water is absorbed. Let cool."
  - "Chop the cucumber and tomatoes and finely chop the onion."
  - "Whisk the oil with the lemon juice, salt and pepper."
  - "Mix the quinoa with the vegetables, olives and parsley and pour over the dressing."
  - "Crumble the feta on top."
tips:
  - "Always rinse quinoa: it removes the bitter coating."
---

This salad keeps well in the fridge for 3 days, so it is great for making lunches for the whole week.
