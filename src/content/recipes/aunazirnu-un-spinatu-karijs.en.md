---
title: "Chickpea and spinach curry with coconut milk"
description: "Creamy chickpea curry with spinach, tomatoes and coconut milk. Vegan, filling and made from storecupboard ingredients in 35 minutes."
steps:
  - "Fry the onion in oil for 5 minutes, then add the garlic, ginger and curry powder and cook for 1 minute."
  - "Add the tomatoes and coconut milk and bring to the boil."
  - "Add the rinsed chickpeas and simmer for 15 minutes."
  - "Stir in the spinach and cook until wilted."
  - "Season with salt and lime juice. Serve with rice."
tips:
  - "Mash some of the chickpeas with a spoon to thicken the sauce."
---

When the fridge is empty but there are chickpeas and coconut milk in the cupboard, dinner is guaranteed.
