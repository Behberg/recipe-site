---
title: "Homemade ham and mushroom pizza"
description: "Homemade pizza with a thin, crisp yeasted base, tomato sauce, mozzarella, ham and mushrooms. Better than takeaway and a family night treat."
steps:
  - "Mix the dough ingredients and knead for 8 minutes. Cover and leave to rise for 1 hour."
  - "Heat the oven to its maximum (250 °C) with a baking tray inside."
  - "Divide the dough in 2 and roll out thinly on baking paper."
  - "Spread with tomato sauce, sprinkle with oregano and top with the mozzarella, ham and sliced mushrooms."
  - "Slide onto the hot tray on the paper and bake for 10 to 12 minutes, until the edges are brown and the cheese bubbles."
tips:
  - "The hot tray is the secret to a crisp base: it works like a pizza stone."
---

Friday night homemade pizza, with everyone adding their own toppings, is a family tradition in many homes.
