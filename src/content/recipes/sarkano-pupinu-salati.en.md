---
title: "Red bean salad with sausage and croutons"
description: "Quick red bean salad with smoked sausage, cheese, sweetcorn, garlic and crunchy croutons. Ready for a party in 15 minutes."
steps:
  - "Drain and rinse the beans."
  - "Cut the sausage and cheese into strips."
  - "Mix the beans, sausage, cheese, sweetcorn and parsley."
  - "Stir the crushed garlic into the mayonnaise and mix with the salad."
  - "Stir in the croutons just before serving so they stay crunchy."
tips:
  - "For a vegetarian version, use fried mushrooms instead of sausage."
---

This salad is a party classic because it is quick, cheap and always disappears.
