---
title: Chicken Kiev
description: Chicken breast filled with herb butter in a crisp coating. Butter pours out when you cut it.
steps:
  - Mix the butter with the chopped herbs, garlic and salt. Shape into 4 small sticks and freeze for 20 minutes.
  - Beat the breasts thin between sheets of cling film. Season with salt and pepper.
  - Wrap a stick of butter in each breast, sealing the edges tightly.
  - Coat in flour, egg and breadcrumbs, then once more in egg and breadcrumbs.
  - Fry in oil until golden, then bake in the oven at 180 °C for 10 minutes.
tips:
  - The double coating stops the butter from escaping while cooking.
---

Careful when cutting: the hot butter tends to squirt out.
