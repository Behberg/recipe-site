---
title: "Date and walnut loaf"
description: "Dark, moist and gently sweet date loaf with walnuts. Wonderful with butter alongside a cup of tea, and it keeps fresh for days."
steps:
  - "Chop the pitted dates, pour over the boiling water and add the bicarbonate of soda and butter. Leave for 15 minutes."
  - "Heat the oven to 170 °C and line a loaf tin with parchment."
  - "Stir the sugar and egg into the date mixture."
  - "Add the flour with the baking powder, salt and chopped walnuts and mix."
  - "Pour into the tin and bake for 55 to 60 minutes. Cool completely before slicing."
tips:
  - "The loaf is even better the next day: wrap it in cling film and let it rest."
---

The soda helps the dates melt into the batter, so the loaf is dark and moist without much butter.
