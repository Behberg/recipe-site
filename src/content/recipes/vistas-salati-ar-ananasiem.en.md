---
title: "Chicken salad with pineapple and cheese"
description: "A party chicken salad with pineapple, eggs, cheese and walnuts. Sweet, savoury, creamy and always the first to disappear."
steps:
  - "Dice the chicken, eggs and pineapple. Let the pineapple drain well."
  - "Grate the cheese. Toast the walnuts lightly in a dry pan and chop."
  - "Mix the mayonnaise with crushed garlic."
  - "Combine everything and season with salt and pepper. Garnish with nuts."
tips:
  - "You can layer the salad in individual glasses for a more festive look."
---

Tinned pineapple was once hard to come by, which made this a real party dish. It has stayed that way.
