---
title: Garlic prawns (gambas al ajillo)
description: Prawns sizzling in hot olive oil with garlic and chilli. Serve with bread to mop up the oil.
steps:
  - Pat the prawns dry and salt them. Slice the garlic thinly.
  - In a small pan or clay dish, heat the olive oil with the garlic and chilli over a medium heat until the garlic starts to turn golden.
  - Add the prawns and paprika and cook for 2 to 3 minutes, turning, until the prawns turn pink.
  - Sprinkle with parsley, squeeze over some lemon and serve at once, still sizzling.
---

In the tapas bars of Madrid they arrive straight from the heat, with the oil still bubbling.
