---
title: "Savoury loaf with ham, olives and cheese"
description: "French style savoury loaf cake with ham, olives and cheese. Perfect for picnics, party nibbles and packed lunches."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C and line a loaf tin with parchment."
  - "Beat the eggs with the milk, oil, salt and pepper."
  - "Sift in the flour and baking powder and mix."
  - "Stir in the diced ham, olives and grated cheese."
  - "Pour into the tin and bake for 40 to 45 minutes until golden. Serve warm or cold in slices."
tips:
  - "Instead of ham you can use smoked chicken or sun dried tomatoes."
---

In France this loaf is called cake salé, and it is a guest of honour in every picnic basket.
