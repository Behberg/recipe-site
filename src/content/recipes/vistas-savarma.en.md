---
title: Chicken shawarma
description: Spice marinated chicken in lavash with vegetables and garlic sauce. Better than from a kiosk.
steps:
  - Marinate the chicken in the yogurt with lemon, garlic, spices and salt for at least 1 hour.
  - Cook in a very hot pan or under the grill for 12 to 15 minutes, until the edges are browned.
  - Let the meat rest, then slice it into thin strips.
  - For the sauce, mix the mayonnaise or yogurt with the crushed garlic and salt.
  - Spread the lavash with the sauce, add the meat and vegetables and roll up tightly.
  - Toast the rolled shawarma in a dry pan for 1 minute on each side.
---

Homemade shawarma: you know what is inside, and there is plenty of meat.
