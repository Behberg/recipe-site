---
title: "Pickled wild mushrooms"
description: "Classic pickled wild mushrooms with bay leaves, allspice and garlic. The autumn harvest saved for the winter table."
yield: "about 3 jars of 0.5 l"
steps:
  - "Clean the mushrooms and cut up large ones. Boil them in salted water for 15 minutes, skimming off the foam, and drain."
  - "For the brine, put the salt, sugar and spices in the water and bring to the boil."
  - "Add the mushrooms to the brine and simmer for 10 minutes. Add the vinegar at the end."
  - "Pack the hot mushrooms and brine into sterilised jars with a garlic clove in each. Seal."
  - "Turn upside down and cool. Ready to eat after a week."
tips:
  - "Firm mushrooms pickle best: porcini, orange and brown birch boletes and slippery jacks."
---

A jar of mushrooms you picked yourself is a point of pride on the winter holiday table.
