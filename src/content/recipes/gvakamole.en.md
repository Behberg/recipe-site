---
title: Guacamole
description: A fresh avocado dip with lime, coriander, onion and chilli. Ready in 10 minutes.
steps:
  - Halve the avocados, remove the stones and scoop the flesh into a bowl.
  - Mash with a fork, leaving some chunks.
  - Add the lime juice straight away so the avocado does not turn brown.
  - Stir in the finely chopped onion, deseeded tomato, chilli and coriander.
  - Salt to taste and serve with tortilla chips or tacos.
---

Good guacamole starts with a good avocado: it should give when you press it gently.
