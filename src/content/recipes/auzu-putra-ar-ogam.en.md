---
title: Porridge with berries
description: Creamy oat porridge with milk, a knob of butter and fresh or frozen berries.
steps:
  - Bring the milk to the boil in a small pan with a pinch of salt.
  - Add the oats and simmer over a low heat for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring often.
  - Take off the heat, stir in the honey and leave to stand for 2 minutes.
  - Divide between bowls and top with a knob of butter and the berries.
---

A warm breakfast that keeps you full until lunch.
