---
title: Steak with garlic butter
description: A beef steak with a dark crust and melting garlic and herb butter.
steps:
  - Take the steaks out of the fridge 30 minutes before cooking.
  - Mix the butter with the crushed garlic and chopped parsley. Chill.
  - Pat the steaks dry, rub with oil and season generously with salt and pepper.
  - Cook in a very hot pan or on a grill for 3 to 4 minutes on each side (medium).
  - Let them rest for 5 minutes and top with the garlic butter.
tips:
  - "A thermometer helps: 54 °C in the middle means a medium steak."
---

A simple but impressive dish to treat dad with.
