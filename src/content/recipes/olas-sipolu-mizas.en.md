---
title: Easter eggs dyed with onion skins
description: Eggs dyed the traditional way with onion skins and leaf patterns. Natural, beautiful and chemical free.
steps:
  - Pour 1.5 l of water over the onion skins and boil for 20 minutes to get a dark dye. Let it cool slightly.
  - Wash the eggs. Press a small leaf or flower petal onto each egg.
  - Wrap each egg tightly in a piece of nylon stocking or muslin and tie it.
  - Put the eggs into the dye and add the salt and vinegar. Boil for 10 to 12 minutes.
  - Cool in cold water and remove the wrapping and leaves.
  - Rub the dried eggs with a drop of oil to make them shine.
tips:
  - The more skins, the darker and redder the colour.
---

On Easter morning the eggs battle it out: whoever’s shell survives will have a lucky year.
