---
title: Roast duck with apples
description: A festive duck with crispy skin, stuffed with tart apples and marjoram. For St Martin’s Day and Christmas.
steps:
  - Wash and dry the duck, then score the skin in a diamond pattern with a sharp knife without cutting into the meat.
  - Rub inside and out with salt, pepper and marjoram. If you have time, leave it in the fridge overnight.
  - Cut the apples into wedges and stuff them into the duck. Close the opening with toothpicks.
  - Put it breast side up in a roasting tin and add some water. Roast in the oven at 160 °C for 2 hours, basting with the fat every 30 minutes.
  - Brush with honey, raise the temperature to 200 °C and roast for another 20 to 30 minutes, until the skin is crispy.
  - Let it rest for 15 minutes. Serve with the baked apples, braised cabbage and potatoes.
tips:
  - "Save the duck fat: potatoes roasted in it are unforgettable."
---

On St Martin’s Day there had to be poultry on the table, so the household would prosper in the coming year.
