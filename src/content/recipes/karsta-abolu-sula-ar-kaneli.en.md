---
title: "Hot spiced apple juice with cinnamon"
description: "Warm, fragrant apple juice with cinnamon, cloves and orange. An autumn drink that warms you up and smells of apple pie."
steps:
  - "Pour the apple juice into a pan and add the cinnamon, cloves and orange slices."
  - "Heat gently for 15 minutes without boiling."
  - "Stir in honey to taste."
  - "Pour into mugs with thin slices of apple."
tips:
  - "It tastes best with cloudy, natural juice from local apples."
---

When autumn apples are pressed at home, this is the best way to drink the juice on a chilly evening.
