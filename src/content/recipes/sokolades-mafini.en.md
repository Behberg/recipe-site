---
title: "Double chocolate chip muffins"
description: "Moist, chocolatey muffins with melting chocolate chips. One bowl, 15 minutes of work and a children’s party hit."
yield: "12 muffins"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 190 °C and line a muffin tin with paper cases."
  - "In a bowl mix the flour, cocoa, sugar, baking powder and salt."
  - "In another bowl whisk the eggs with the milk and oil."
  - "Stir the wet ingredients into the dry just until no flour is visible. Fold in half the chocolate chips."
  - "Divide between the cases and scatter the rest of the chocolate on top. Bake for 20 to 22 minutes."
tips:
  - "Overmixing makes muffins tough: the batter can be lumpy."
---

Chocolate muffins are what children want to take to school on their birthday. And there is always at least one too few.
