---
title: "Yoghurt parfait with granola and berries"
description: "A layered breakfast in a glass: thick yoghurt with honey, crunchy granola and fresh berries. Ready in 5 minutes and looks festive."
steps:
  - "Mix the yoghurt with the honey."
  - "In glasses layer a spoonful of yoghurt, a spoonful of granola and some berries."
  - "Repeat the layers to the top."
  - "Finish with berries and a little granola. Serve straight away so the granola stays crunchy."
tips:
  - "Homemade granola makes this parfait even better."
---

A parfait is an ordinary breakfast that looks like dessert. Children eat it without coaxing.
