---
title: "Non alcoholic mulled wine with spices"
description: "Fragrant alcohol free mulled wine made with grape or blackcurrant juice, orange, cinnamon and cloves. A festive drink for the whole family."
steps:
  - "Pour the juice and water into a pan and add the spices and ginger."
  - "Slice the orange, squeeze half the slices into the pan and keep the rest for garnish."
  - "Heat gently for 15 minutes without boiling so the spices release their aroma."
  - "Stir in honey to taste. Strain and pour into mugs with a slice of orange."
tips:
  - "Never boil it: boiling dulls the juice and makes the spices bitter."
---

When the grown ups have mulled wine, the children want their own. This version smells exactly the same and everyone can drink it.
