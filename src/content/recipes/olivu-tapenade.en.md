---
title: "Olive tapenade on toasted baguette"
description: "French olive tapenade with capers, anchovies, garlic and lemon. A salty, intense spread for toasts, ready in 10 minutes."
steps:
  - "Pit the olives."
  - "Put the olives, capers, anchovies, garlic, lemon juice and thyme leaves in a blender."
  - "Pulse, gradually adding the oil, to a coarse paste."
  - "Slice and toast the baguette. Spread with the tapenade."
tips:
  - "For a vegan version leave out the anchovies: the flavour will be milder."
---

Tapenade comes from Provence, where olives are everyday food. A thin layer is enough, as the flavour is strong.
