---
title: "Virgin mojito with lime and mint"
description: "A refreshing alcohol free mojito with lime, mint and sparkling water. A summer party drink everyone can enjoy."
steps:
  - "Cut the lime into wedges and put them in a glass with the sugar and mint leaves."
  - "Muddle with a pestle or the handle of a spoon to release the juice and mint aroma."
  - "Fill the glass with crushed ice."
  - "Top up with sparkling water, stir and garnish with a sprig of mint."
tips:
  - "Do not crush the mint too hard: torn leaves turn bitter."
---

A mojito without alcohol is just as fresh as the real thing. And you can drink it all afternoon.
