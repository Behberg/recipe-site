---
title: "No bake biscuit cake with sour cream"
description: "The simple no bake biscuit cake: biscuits dipped in milk and a creamy sour cream filling. Overnight it softens like a real layer cake."
steps:
  - "Whisk the sour cream with the sugar and vanilla sugar. Whip the cream separately and gently fold it in."
  - "Dip the biscuits one by one in the milk for 2 seconds and lay them in a dish in a single layer."
  - "Spread with cream. Repeat the layers until the biscuits are used up, finishing with cream."
  - "Grate the chocolate over the top."
  - "Chill for at least 6 hours or overnight so the biscuits soften."
tips:
  - "Do not soak the biscuits for long: they will soak up the cream and soften on their own."
---

This cake was at countless childhood birthdays when there was no oven or no time. It still goes first.
