---
title: "Buddha bowl with quinoa and chickpeas"
description: "A colourful bowl of quinoa, roasted sweet potato, crisp chickpeas, avocado and tahini dressing."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C. Dice the sweet potato and drain the chickpeas. Toss with the oil, paprika and salt and roast for 20 to 25 minutes."
  - "Rinse the quinoa and cook it in twice its volume of water for 15 minutes."
  - "Shred the cabbage finely, grate the carrot and slice the avocado."
  - "Stir the tahini with the lemon juice, water and a pinch of salt."
  - "Fill the bowls with spinach, quinoa, the roasted and raw vegetables, drizzle with the dressing and sprinkle with sesame."
---

One bowl holds grains, pulses, vegetables and good fats, and keeps you going until evening.
