---
title: "Pumpkin rice porridge with milk"
description: "Soft, golden pumpkin porridge with rice, milk and butter. A sweet autumn breakfast that children love."
steps:
  - "Cut the pumpkin into small cubes, add the water and simmer covered for 10 minutes."
  - "Pour in the milk and add the rinsed rice, sugar and salt."
  - "Simmer over a low heat for 25 minutes, stirring often, until the rice is soft and the pumpkin has broken down."
  - "Stir in the butter. Serve sprinkled with cinnamon."
tips:
  - "Butternut and Hokkaido pumpkins are the sweetest."
---

When pumpkins pile up in autumn, this porridge is the best way to start eating them at breakfast.
