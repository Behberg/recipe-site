---
title: "Korean style spicy carrot salad"
description: "Spicy, crunchy Korean style carrots with garlic, coriander and hot oil. A favourite side for meat that keeps for a week in the fridge."
steps:
  - "Grate the carrots into long, thin strips, add the salt and sugar, squeeze with your hands and leave for 15 minutes."
  - "Add the vinegar, crush in the garlic and mix."
  - "Pile the coriander and chilli flakes on top."
  - "Heat the oil in a pan until it starts to smoke and pour it straight over the spices so they sizzle."
  - "Mix, cover and chill for at least 3 hours."
tips:
  - "The hot oil opens up the aroma of the coriander and chilli: it is the key step."
---

This salad was created by Soviet Korean communities and became popular across the former USSR. It is always at Latvian markets.
