---
title: "Pickled garlic cloves"
description: "Crunchy, mellow pickled garlic cloves with dill. A favourite snack with meat and stronger drinks, preserved for winter."
yield: "about 3 jars of 0.5 l"
steps:
  - "Separate the garlic into cloves and peel them. It is easier if you pour boiling water over them for 5 minutes first."
  - "Blanch the cloves in boiling water for 2 minutes and cool immediately in cold water."
  - "Put the dill, bay leaf and peppercorns into sterilised jars and pack in the garlic tightly."
  - "For the brine, boil the water with the sugar and salt, take off the heat and add the vinegar."
  - "Pour the hot brine into the jars, seal and cool. Ready to eat after 2 weeks."
tips:
  - "If pickled garlic turns bluish, it is harmless: it is just the garlic reacting with the vinegar."
---

Pickled garlic is the jar that gets opened for a barbecue or party table, and it always disappears.
