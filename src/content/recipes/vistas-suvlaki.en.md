---
title: Chicken souvlaki with tzatziki
description: Marinated chicken skewers with lemon and oregano, served with pitta and a cucumber yogurt sauce.
steps:
  - Dice the chicken. Marinate with the lemon juice, olive oil, garlic, oregano, salt and pepper for at least 30 minutes.
  - For the tzatziki, grate the cucumber, squeeze out the liquid and mix with the yogurt, garlic, dill and salt.
  - Thread the chicken onto skewers.
  - Cook on a grill or in a griddle pan for 10 to 12 minutes, turning, until cooked through.
  - Serve with warmed pitta, tzatziki, tomatoes and onions.
---

Greek street food that also works brilliantly on a country barbecue.
