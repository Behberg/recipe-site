---
title: "Mushroom stroganoff"
description: "A creamy mushroom sauce with paprika and mustard, served with pasta. No meat, no dairy."
steps:
  - "Cook the pasta according to the packet."
  - "In a large pan fry the sliced mushrooms in the oil over high heat for 6 to 8 minutes, until browned."
  - "Add the chopped onion and garlic and fry for 3 minutes. Stir in the paprika and flour."
  - "Pour in the stock, coconut milk, soy sauce and mustard. Simmer for 5 minutes, until the sauce thickens."
  - "Season with salt and pepper, scatter with parsley and serve with the pasta."
---

Fry the mushrooms over high heat and take your time: the brown crust carries all the flavour.
