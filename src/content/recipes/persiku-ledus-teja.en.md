---
title: "Homemade peach iced tea"
description: "Refreshing homemade iced tea with fresh peaches and lemon. Far less sugar than shop bought and a real fruit flavour."
steps:
  - "Pour the boiling water over the tea bags and leave to brew for 5 minutes. Remove them."
  - "Chop two peaches, blend with the sugar and stir into the hot tea."
  - "Add the cold water and lemon juice. Chill in the fridge for at least 2 hours."
  - "Strain and pour into glasses with ice and slices of the remaining peach."
tips:
  - "Do not brew the tea for more than 5 minutes or it turns bitter."
---

Once you taste homemade iced tea, sweet shop drinks start to taste like syrup.
