---
title: "Curd cheese crescent cookies with jam"
description: "Tender, flaky crescents made with curd cheese dough and filled with thick jam. Grandma’s cookies that work the first time."
yield: "24 crescents"
steps:
  - "Mix the flour with the baking powder and sugar, add the cold butter in pieces and rub it in with your fingertips to crumbs."
  - "Add the curd cheese and quickly bring together into a dough. Divide into 3 and chill for 30 minutes."
  - "Heat the oven to 190 °C. Roll each piece into a circle and cut into 8 triangles like a pizza."
  - "Put a teaspoon of jam on the wide end of each triangle and roll up towards the point."
  - "Place on baking paper, brush with egg and bake for 18 to 20 minutes until golden."
  - "Dust with icing sugar once cool."
tips:
  - "The jam must be thick or it will run out. A curd or poppy seed filling works too."
---

These crescents were always in a tin in grandma’s cupboard. They smell of Sunday afternoons.
