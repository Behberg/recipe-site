---
title: Moussaka
description: A Greek aubergine bake with a lamb or beef sauce and béchamel.
steps:
  - Cut the aubergines into 1 cm slices, salt them and leave for 20 minutes. Pat dry.
  - Brush with olive oil and bake in the oven at 200 °C for 20 minutes, until soft.
  - In a pan, fry the onion, garlic and meat. Add the tomatoes, cinnamon, salt and pepper and simmer for 20 minutes.
  - For the béchamel, melt the butter, stir in the flour, pour in the milk and cook until thick. Cool slightly and stir in the egg and half the cheese.
  - Layer aubergines, meat sauce and more aubergines in a dish. Top with the béchamel and the rest of the cheese.
  - Bake in the oven at 180 °C for 45 minutes. Let it rest for 20 minutes before cutting.
---

Cinnamon in the meat sauce is the Greek secret that makes moussaka so special.
