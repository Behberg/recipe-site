---
title: "Fish baked in foil with vegetables and lemon"
description: "White fish baked in foil parcels with courgette, tomatoes, lemon and dill. Juicy, healthy and with no washing up in 30 minutes."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C and prepare 2 large sheets of foil."
  - "Slice the courgette thinly, halve the tomatoes and slice the onion into half rings."
  - "Put the vegetables in the middle of each sheet, lay the fish on top and season."
  - "Add lemon slices and dill and drizzle with oil. Seal the foil tightly."
  - "Bake for 18 to 20 minutes. Open the parcels carefully and serve."
tips:
  - "You can prepare the parcels in the morning and just put them in the oven in the evening."
---

Fish in foil steams in its own juices with vegetables. It is the healthiest way to cook it and the simplest.
