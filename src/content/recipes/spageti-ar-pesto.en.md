---
title: Spaghetti with basil pesto
description: Homemade green pesto with basil, pine nuts, parmesan and garlic. Lunch in 15 minutes.
steps:
  - Lightly toast the pine nuts in a dry pan until fragrant.
  - In a blender or mortar, grind the basil, nuts, garlic and a pinch of salt.
  - Gradually pour in the olive oil, then stir in the finely grated parmesan.
  - Cook the spaghetti al dente in salted water. Keep half a cup of the pasta water.
  - Toss the spaghetti with the pesto and a little pasta water until the sauce is creamy. Serve straight away.
tips:
  - "Do not heat pesto in the pan: the basil flavour stays fresher."
---

In Genoa pesto is traditionally made in a marble mortar. A blender works just as well.
