---
title: Adjarian khachapuri
description: Boat shaped Georgian cheese bread with melted cheese, egg yolk and butter.
yield: 4 khachapuri
steps:
  - Mix the dough ingredients and knead until the dough is soft. Leave to rise for 1 hour.
  - Grate and crumble the cheeses and mix them together.
  - Divide the dough into 4 pieces and roll each one into an oval. Roll the long edges inwards and pinch the ends together to form a boat.
  - Fill the middle with cheese and bake in the oven at 220 °C for 12 to 15 minutes.
  - Take out, press a hollow into the middle of the cheese, drop in a yolk and bake for 2 more minutes.
  - Serve hot with a knob of butter. Stir the cheese, yolk and butter together before eating.
---

Eat it with your hands: tear off the crust and dip it into the melting cheese.
