---
title: "Homemade adjika (spicy tomato and pepper relish)"
description: "Hot, fragrant homemade adjika made from tomatoes, peppers, garlic and chilli, preserved for winter. Great with meat, pasta and on bread."
yield: "about 6 jars of 0.5 l"
steps:
  - "Wash the tomatoes, peppers and chillies and remove the seeds and stalks. Peel the garlic."
  - "Mince or blend everything together."
  - "Put the mixture in a large pan and add the sugar, salt and oil. Simmer gently for 40 minutes, stirring often."
  - "Add the vinegar and simmer for 5 more minutes."
  - "Fill sterilised jars while hot and seal immediately. Turn upside down and cool under a blanket."
tips:
  - "Adjust the chilli to taste: for children, make it without chilli and with garlic only."
---

One jar of adjika in winter turns plain pasta or potatoes into food with character.
