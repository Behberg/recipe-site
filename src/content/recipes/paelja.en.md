---
title: Seafood paella
description: Spanish saffron rice with prawns, mussels, chicken and peppers. A dish for a big crowd.
steps:
  - Soak the saffron in 3 tablespoons of warm stock.
  - In a large pan, brown the chicken pieces in olive oil until golden.
  - Add the onion, pepper and garlic and fry for 5 minutes. Add the grated tomatoes and smoked paprika.
  - Tip in the rice, stir and pour in the hot stock with the saffron. Salt. From now on, do not stir.
  - Cook over a medium heat for 15 minutes. Press the prawns and mussels into the top and cook for another 8 minutes.
  - Take off the heat, cover with a tea towel and let it rest for 5 minutes. Serve with lemon.
tips:
  - The crispy layer on the bottom of the pan is called socarrat, and it is the tastiest part.
---

Paella does not need a special pan. The biggest one you have at home will do.
