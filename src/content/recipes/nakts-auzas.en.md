---
title: "Overnight oats in a jar with yoghurt and berries"
description: "Oats soaked overnight in yoghurt and milk, with banana and berries. A breakfast that waits for you ready in the fridge."
steps:
  - "In a jar mix the oats, yoghurt, milk, chia seeds and honey."
  - "Stir in half a mashed banana."
  - "Put the lid on and refrigerate overnight."
  - "In the morning stir, adding a splash of milk if needed. Top with berries."
tips:
  - "Make jars for 3 days at once: they keep well in the fridge."
---

Overnight oats are the best present you can make for tomorrow’s self. No stove, no time needed.
