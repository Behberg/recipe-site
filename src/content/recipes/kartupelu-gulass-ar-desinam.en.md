---
title: "Potato goulash with sausages and paprika"
description: "Thick potato goulash with sausages, pepper, marjoram and caraway. A cheap, filling one pot dish that is even better the next day."
steps:
  - "Fry the onions in oil in a pan for 8 minutes, then add the diced pepper."
  - "Stir in the paprika, tomato paste, caraway and marjoram and cook for 1 minute."
  - "Add the diced potatoes, pour in the hot water and season with salt and pepper."
  - "Cover and simmer for 25 minutes, until the potatoes are tender and start to break down, thickening the sauce."
  - "Add the sliced sausages and simmer for 5 minutes more."
tips:
  - "Floury potatoes break down and thicken the goulash: exactly as it should be."
---

Potato goulash is weekday food in Austria and Germany. It feeds many for little money.
