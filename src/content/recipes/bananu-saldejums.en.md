---
title: "Banana nice cream from frozen bananas"
description: "Creamy ice cream made from frozen bananas with no sugar and no ice cream machine. A vegan dessert in 5 minutes that children can make with you."
steps:
  - "Peel ripe bananas, slice them and freeze for at least 4 hours."
  - "Put the frozen slices in a blender or food processor."
  - "Blend for 2 to 3 minutes, scraping down the sides now and then, until smooth and creamy like soft serve."
  - "Set aside half. Add the peanut butter and cocoa to the other half and blend again."
  - "Serve straight away, or freeze for 30 minutes to firm up."
tips:
  - "The riper the bananas, the sweeter the ice cream. Brown spots on the skin are good."
---

When the bananas in the fruit bowl start to brown, this is the best way to rescue them. Children do not believe it is only banana.
