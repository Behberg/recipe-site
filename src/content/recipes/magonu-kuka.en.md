---
title: "Poppy seed cake with lemon glaze"
description: "A moist poppy seed cake with lemon zest and a tangy glaze. The taste of old fashioned cafés and still a favourite."
steps:
  - "Pour the hot milk over the poppy seeds and leave to swell for 30 minutes."
  - "Heat the oven to 175 °C and line a tin with paper."
  - "Beat the butter with the sugar, beat in the eggs one at a time and add the lemon zest."
  - "Stir in the poppy seeds with the milk, then the flour with the baking powder. Pour into the tin and bake for 40 to 45 minutes."
  - "When cool, pour over the icing sugar and lemon juice glaze."
tips:
  - "Soaked poppy seeds make the cake moist and deepen the flavour."
---

Poppy seed cake used to be in every old patisserie window. Baked at home it is even better.
