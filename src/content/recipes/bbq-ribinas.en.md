---
title: BBQ pork ribs
description: Slow roasted spiced pork ribs with a sticky barbecue sauce, falling off the bone.
steps:
  - Remove the thin membrane from the underside of the ribs.
  - Mix the sugar and spices and rub them all over the ribs.
  - Wrap tightly in foil and bake in the oven at 150 °C for 2.5 hours.
  - For the sauce, boil all the ingredients for 5 minutes, until thickened.
  - Unwrap the ribs, brush with the sauce and cook in the oven at 220 °C or on a grill for 15 to 20 minutes, brushing once more.
---

Slow cooking in foil is the secret: the meat becomes so tender it falls off the bone by itself.
