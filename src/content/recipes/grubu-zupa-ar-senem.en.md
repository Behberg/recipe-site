---
title: "Pearl barley and mushroom soup"
description: "Hearty pearl barley soup with mushrooms, potatoes and carrots. Cheap, vegetarian and very comforting."
steps:
  - "Rinse the barley in several changes of water, add to the stock with the bay leaf and simmer for 30 minutes."
  - "Meanwhile fry the onion and carrot in butter, add the sliced mushrooms and fry for 8 minutes."
  - "Add diced potatoes to the soup and simmer for 10 minutes."
  - "Add the mushrooms and simmer for another 10 minutes, until the barley and potatoes are soft."
  - "Season with salt, pepper and parsley."
tips:
  - "Soak the barley in cold water overnight and it cooks twice as fast."
---

Pearl barley is unfairly forgotten these days. This soup is a reminder of how well it goes with mushrooms.
