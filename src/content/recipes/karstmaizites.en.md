---
title: Hot sandwiches with ham and cheese
description: Oven baked open sandwiches with ham, cheese and tomato. A quick dinner or breakfast.
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 200 °C.
  - Spread the slices of bread thinly with mayonnaise or butter and put them on a baking tray.
  - Top each with ham and a slice of tomato and sprinkle generously with cheese.
  - Bake for 6 to 8 minutes, until the cheese has melted and the edges are golden. Sprinkle with herbs.
---

In Latvia hot sandwiches get made when there is a little of everything left in the fridge.
