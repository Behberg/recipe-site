---
title: "Pork gyros in pita with tzatziki"
description: "Homemade Greek gyros: pork marinated with oregano and lemon, seared and wrapped in pita with tzatziki, tomatoes and onion."
steps:
  - "Cut the pork into thin strips and mix with the oil, lemon juice, 2 crushed garlic cloves, oregano, paprika, salt and pepper. Marinate for at least 1 hour."
  - "For the tzatziki, grate and squeeze the cucumber and mix with the yoghurt, the remaining garlic and salt."
  - "Sear the meat in batches in a very hot pan or on the grill for 5 to 6 minutes, until browned with crisp edges."
  - "Warm the pitas in a pan."
  - "Top the pitas with tzatziki, meat, tomatoes and onion, wrap and serve."
tips:
  - "In Greece a few chips often go into the pita too: it is traditional."
---

Gyros is Greece’s number one street food. At home it is just as good if the pan is hot enough.
