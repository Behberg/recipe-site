---
title: "Lenten borscht with beans"
description: "Ukrainian borscht without meat: beetroot, cabbage and beans give such a rich flavour that nobody misses it."
steps:
  - "Bring the water to the boil with the bay leaves and salt. Add the diced potatoes and cook for 10 minutes."
  - "In a pan fry the onion and grated carrot in the oil for 5 minutes. Add the grated beetroot, tomato paste, vinegar and sugar and cook for 10 minutes."
  - "Add the shredded cabbage to the pot and cook for 10 minutes."
  - "Add the beetroot mixture and the rinsed beans and cook for 10 more minutes."
  - "Stir in the crushed garlic and pepper. Take off the heat and leave to stand for 15 minutes. Serve with dill."
---

Vinegar added to the beetroot keeps the borscht bright red.
