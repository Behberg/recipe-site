---
title: "Cabbage boil with sausage"
description: "The viral Southern US dish: cabbage wedges, potatoes, corn and smoked sausage braised in spicy garlic butter."
steps:
  - "Cut the cabbage into 8 wedges, keeping a bit of core in each so the leaves hold together."
  - "Halve the potatoes, cut the corn into 3 pieces and slice the sausages thickly."
  - "Melt the butter in a small pan and stir in the chopped garlic, paprika, Cajun seasoning and lemon juice."
  - "Arrange the cabbage, potatoes, corn and sausage in a large pot or roasting tin. Pour in the stock and drizzle over the garlic butter."
  - "Cover with a lid or foil and braise over low heat or in a 200 °C oven for 40 minutes until the potatoes and cabbage are tender."
  - "Before serving, spoon the buttery juices from the bottom over everything again and scatter with parsley."
tips:
  - "No Cajun seasoning? Mix 1 teaspoon of sweet paprika with half a teaspoon each of chilli, garlic powder, oregano and thyme."
  - "Add prawns for the last 5 minutes and it becomes a proper seafood boil."
  - "Cabbage is cheap all year, which makes this one of the most budget-friendly family dinners."
---
