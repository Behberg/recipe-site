---
title: "Marry Me chicken in creamy tomato sauce"
description: "The world's favourite chicken dinner: golden chicken breast in a cream, parmesan and sun-dried tomato sauce."
steps:
  - "Slice thick fillets in half horizontally so they cook evenly. Season with salt and pepper and dust with flour."
  - "Heat the oil in a pan and fry the chicken for 5 minutes on each side until golden. Lift onto a plate."
  - "Melt the butter in the same pan, add the chopped garlic and fry for 1 minute."
  - "Add the chopped sun-dried tomatoes, oregano and chilli flakes, then pour in the stock and cream. Simmer for 3 minutes."
  - "Stir in the parmesan, return the chicken to the pan and simmer gently for 8 minutes until cooked through and the sauce has thickened."
  - "Scatter with basil and serve with pasta, mashed potato or bread for mopping up the sauce."
tips:
  - "The name comes from a joke: the dish is so good someone is bound to propose after eating it."
  - "Boneless chicken thighs work too and stay even juicier."
  - "Double the chilli flakes for a spicier sauce."
---
