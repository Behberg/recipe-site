---
title: "Clear fish soup with cod"
description: "Light, clear fish soup with cod, potatoes and dill, the way it is made on the Latvian coast. Ready in half an hour."
steps:
  - "Put a whole onion, the bay leaves and peppercorns in the water and bring to the boil."
  - "Add diced potatoes and sliced carrot and simmer for 12 minutes."
  - "Cut the cod into large pieces and add to the soup. Simmer gently for 7 to 8 minutes without stirring, so the fish stays whole."
  - "Remove the onion and bay leaves. Add salt, the butter and plenty of chopped dill."
  - "Serve with a lemon wedge and dark rye bread."
tips:
  - "Salmon, perch or zander work just as well as cod."
---

In fishing villages the soup was made from whatever was in the net that morning. The fresher the fish, the simpler the cooking.
