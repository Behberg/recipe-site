---
title: Oat cookies
description: Oat cookies that are crisp at the edges and soft in the middle, with raisins and cinnamon.
yield: about 20 cookies
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  - Beat the butter with the sugar, then mix in the egg.
  - Add the oats, flour, baking soda, cinnamon and raisins. Stir together.
  - Spoon balls of dough onto a baking tray and flatten them slightly.
  - Bake for 10 to 12 minutes, until the edges are golden.
---

Cookies you can bake together with the kids.
