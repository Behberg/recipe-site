---
title: Strawberry cream cake
description: An airy sponge with fresh strawberries and vanilla whipped cream. A summer celebration cake.
steps:
  - Whisk the eggs with the sugar until very pale and thick, about 8 minutes.
  - Gently fold in the flour with the baking powder. Pour into a lined tin.
  - Bake in the oven at 175 °C for 25 to 30 minutes. Cool and cut into two layers.
  - For the cream, whip the cream with the mascarpone, icing sugar and vanilla to stiff peaks.
  - Spread cream and sliced strawberries on the first layer and put the second layer on top.
  - Cover with cream and decorate with whole strawberries. Chill for at least 2 hours.
---

A cake to say thank you to mum.
