---
title: Homemade fish fingers
description: Crisp cod fingers in breadcrumbs, baked in the oven. With potatoes and a quick sauce.
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 220 °C. Line a baking tray with baking paper and brush it with oil.
  - Cut the fish into fingers, pat dry and season with salt and pepper.
  - Coat in flour, beaten eggs and breadcrumbs. Arrange on the tray and drizzle with oil.
  - Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, turning halfway, until golden.
  - For the sauce, mix the mayonnaise, chopped pickle and lemon juice.
---

The same fish fingers children love, only made from real fish.
