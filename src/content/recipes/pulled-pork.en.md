---
title: "Oven pulled pork"
description: "Slow roasted, tender pork with smoked paprika, shredded with forks and tossed in barbecue sauce. For burgers and parties."
steps:
  - "Mix the paprika, sugar, garlic powder, cumin and salt. Rub generously into the pork and leave overnight if you can."
  - "Heat the oven to 150 °C. Put the pork in a deep dish, add the vinegar and a glass of water and cover tightly with foil."
  - "Roast for 4.5 to 5 hours, until the meat falls apart easily."
  - "Shred the meat with two forks and mix with the cooking juices and barbecue sauce."
  - "Serve in burger buns with coleslaw."
tips:
  - "The slower and longer it cooks, the more tender the meat: do not rush."
---

Pulled pork is a Southern US barbecue tradition you can recreate in the oven. It feeds a big crowd with little work.
