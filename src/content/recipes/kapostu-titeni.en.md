---
title: Cabbage rolls
description: A meat and rice filling wrapped in cabbage leaves and slowly braised in a tomato and sour cream sauce.
steps:
  - Cut the core out of the cabbage. Put the head into boiling water and remove the softened leaves one at a time. Shave the thick veins thinner.
  - Cook the rice until half done. Fry the onion and carrot.
  - Mix the mince with the rice, half the vegetables, salt and pepper.
  - Put a tablespoon of filling on each leaf and wrap it into a parcel.
  - Arrange the rolls in a pan or roasting tin and put the rest of the vegetables on top.
  - Mix the tomato paste, sour cream and water, pour over the rolls and braise over a low heat or in the oven at 180 °C for 50 to 60 minutes.
---

Time consuming but worth it: cabbage rolls can be frozen for next time.
