---
title: Baba ganoush
description: A smoky aubergine dip with tahini, garlic and lemon.
steps:
  - Prick the aubergines with a fork and cook them on a grill, over a gas flame or in the oven at 220 °C for 30 to 40 minutes, until the skin is charred and the inside is completely soft.
  - Cool slightly, scoop out the flesh and let it drain in a sieve for 10 minutes.
  - Mash with a fork together with the tahini, garlic, lemon juice and salt.
  - Spread on a plate, drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with paprika and parsley.
---

The more the skin chars, the smokier the flavour.
