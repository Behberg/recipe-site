---
title: "Tofu scramble"
description: "A breakfast scramble made from tofu with turmeric, tomatoes and spinach. Ready in ten minutes."
steps:
  - "Pat the tofu dry with kitchen paper and crumble it into small pieces with a fork."
  - "Fry the chopped onion in the olive oil for 2 minutes."
  - "Add the tofu, turmeric, smoked paprika, salt and pepper. Fry for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring."
  - "Add the halved tomatoes and the spinach and warm for 1 more minute, until the spinach wilts."
  - "Serve on toast."
---

Turmeric gives the golden colour, smoked paprika a deeper flavour.
