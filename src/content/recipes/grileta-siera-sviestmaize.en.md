---
title: Grilled cheese sandwich
description: A golden sandwich fried in butter with stretchy, melting cheese. Perfect with tomato soup.
steps:
  - Butter the slices of bread on one side.
  - Put the cheese on the unbuttered side of one slice and cover with the other slice, buttered side out.
  - Fry in a pan over a medium low heat for 3 to 4 minutes on each side, pressing lightly.
  - When the bread is golden and the cheese has melted, cut diagonally.
tips:
  - "A low heat is the key: the cheese has time to melt before the bread burns."
---

An American home classic that tastes best on a rainy day.
