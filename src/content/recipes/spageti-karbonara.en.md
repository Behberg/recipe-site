---
title: Spaghetti carbonara
description: A real Roman carbonara with egg yolks, pecorino and crisp guanciale. No cream.
steps:
  - Bring a large pan of salted water to the boil and cook the spaghetti al dente.
  - Cut the guanciale into strips and fry slowly in a dry pan until the fat has melted and the meat is crisp.
  - In a bowl, whisk the yolks, egg, finely grated cheese and plenty of pepper.
  - Transfer the cooked spaghetti to the pan with the meat and take the pan off the heat.
  - Add the egg mixture and 3 to 4 tablespoons of pasta water. Toss quickly until a creamy sauce forms.
  - Serve straight away with extra cheese and pepper.
tips:
  - The pan must not be hot when you add the eggs, or they will scramble.
---

In Rome no cream goes into carbonara. The creaminess comes from the eggs, cheese and pasta water.
