---
title: "Cranberry, white chocolate and orange loaf cake"
description: "Soft butter loaf cake with tart cranberries, white chocolate chunks and orange zest. A winter coffee table showpiece."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 170 °C and line a loaf tin with paper."
  - "Beat the butter with the sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs one at a time."
  - "Stir in the orange zest, the flour with the baking powder and the milk."
  - "Gently fold in the cranberries and chopped white chocolate."
  - "Pour into the tin and bake for 50 to 55 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean."
tips:
  - "Toss frozen cranberries in a spoonful of flour before folding them in so they do not sink."
---

Tart cranberries and sweet white chocolate are a pairing made for winter evenings and the Christmas table.
