---
title: Sangria
description: A refreshing red wine drink with oranges, apples and cinnamon. For summer parties.
steps:
  - Slice the oranges and lemon into rounds and dice the apple small.
  - In a large jug, mix the fruit with the sugar and cinnamon.
  - Pour in the wine, stir and put in the fridge for at least 2 hours.
  - Just before serving, add the sparkling water and ice.
tips:
  - For an alcohol free version, use grape juice.
---

Sangria does not need expensive wine. What matters is the time it takes for the fruit to give up its flavour.
