---
title: "Creamy mushroom pasta"
description: "Creamy pasta with browned mushrooms, garlic and parmesan in a cream sauce. A simple, filling dinner in 30 minutes."
steps:
  - "Cook the pasta in salted water according to the packet. Save 1 cup of the cooking water before draining."
  - "Fry the chopped onion in butter in a frying pan for 3 minutes."
  - "Add the sliced mushrooms and fry over a high heat for 8 minutes until browned. Add the garlic."
  - "Pour in the cream and add the parmesan, salt and pepper. Simmer for 3 minutes."
  - "Stir in the pasta, adding a little cooking water to make the sauce silky. Sprinkle with parsley."
tips:
  - "Fry the mushrooms in a big pan and do not stir too often so they brown rather than stew."
---

Pasta with mushrooms is one of the most popular dinners in Latvian homes. With wild mushrooms in autumn it is even better.
