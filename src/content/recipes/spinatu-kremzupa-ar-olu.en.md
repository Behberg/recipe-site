---
title: "Cream of spinach soup with boiled egg"
description: "Bright green cream of spinach soup with cream and nutmeg, served with halved boiled eggs. Springlike and ready in 30 minutes."
steps:
  - "Soften the onion in butter in a pan for 5 minutes."
  - "Add the diced potatoes and stock and simmer for 12 minutes."
  - "Add the spinach and simmer for 3 minutes until wilted."
  - "Blend until smooth and stir in the cream, nutmeg, salt and pepper."
  - "Serve with halved boiled eggs."
tips:
  - "Do not cook the spinach for long: it loses its bright colour."
---

Green soup with egg is an Easter time classic in many European homes. It tastes of spring.
