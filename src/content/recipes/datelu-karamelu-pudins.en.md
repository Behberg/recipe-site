---
title: Sticky toffee pudding
description: A moist, warm date sponge drowned in hot butterscotch sauce.
steps:
  - Chop the dates, pour over the boiling water, add the baking soda and leave for 10 minutes.
  - Beat the butter with the sugar, add the eggs one at a time, then the flour with the baking powder and the dates with their liquid.
  - Pour into a greased tin and bake in the oven at 180 °C for 30 to 35 minutes.
  - For the sauce, melt the butter with the sugar in a small pan, pour in the cream and boil for 3 minutes.
  - Cut the hot pudding into squares and pour plenty of sauce over it.
---

A British pub dessert that has no equal in winter.
