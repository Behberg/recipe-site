---
title: "Baked pork with potatoes and cheese (“French style meat”)"
description: "A party classic in Latvia: pork, potatoes, onions and tomatoes baked under a golden cheese crust. All in one dish."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 190 °C and grease a baking dish with oil."
  - "Cut the pork into 1 cm slices, pound lightly and season with salt and pepper."
  - "Slice the potatoes thinly, the onions into half rings and the tomatoes into slices."
  - "Layer the potatoes in the dish and season, then add the onions, the pork and the tomatoes."
  - "Spread with mayonnaise or sour cream and scatter generously with grated cheese."
  - "Bake for 45 minutes, until the potatoes are soft and the cheese is browned. Rest for 10 minutes before cutting."
tips:
  - "Chicken breast works instead of pork, with the same baking time."
---

Nobody in France knows this dish, but in Latvia no birthday or New Year table is complete without it.
