---
title: "Lazy cabbage rolls in tomato and sour cream sauce"
description: "All the flavour of cabbage rolls without the rolling: meat, rice and cabbage patties braised in a tomato and sour cream sauce."
steps:
  - "Parboil the rice. Finely shred the cabbage, pour boiling water over it for 2 minutes, then squeeze it dry."
  - "Mix the mince, rice, cabbage, half the finely chopped onion, the egg, salt and pepper."
  - "With wet hands shape oval patties and brown them on both sides in oil."
  - "In a pan fry the rest of the onion and the grated carrot, add the passata and water and stir in the sour cream."
  - "Lay the patties in the sauce, cover and simmer gently for 30 minutes."
tips:
  - "You can also bake them at 180 °C for 40 minutes: the patties stay whole and the sauce thickens."
---

Everyone loves cabbage rolls, but nobody wants to roll them. This version solves both problems.
