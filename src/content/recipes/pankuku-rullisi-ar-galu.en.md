---
title: "Pancake rolls with meat filling"
description: "Thin pancakes with a juicy minced meat and onion filling, folded into parcels and fried in butter. They freeze and reheat beautifully."
steps:
  - "For the batter, whisk the eggs with the milk, sugar and salt, then stir in the flour and oil. Rest for 15 minutes."
  - "Fry thin pancakes on one side only."
  - "For the filling, fry the onion in oil, add the mince and cook for 10 minutes, breaking it up. Season."
  - "Put a spoonful of filling on the cooked side, fold in the sides and roll into a parcel."
  - "Fry the rolls in butter on both sides until golden. Serve with sour cream."
tips:
  - "You can freeze the rolls before frying and cook them straight from frozen over a low heat."
---

Meat pancake rolls are what mum froze in big batches. When time was short, dinner was a 10 minute job.
