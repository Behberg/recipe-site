---
title: "Homemade hot mustard from mustard powder"
description: "Hot homemade mustard from mustard powder that hits the nose just right. For aspic, sausages, meat and the Christmas table."
yield: "about 1 jar of 0.3 l"
steps:
  - "Put the mustard powder in a bowl."
  - "Gradually add the boiling water, stirring constantly, to a thick, smooth paste without lumps."
  - "Stir in the sugar, salt, oil and vinegar."
  - "Spoon into a jar, close and leave at room temperature for 1 day. Then keep in the fridge."
tips:
  - "Freshly made mustard is bitter: the heat and flavour settle after a day."
---

Homemade mustard is hotter than any shop mustard. With aspic and cold meats it is essential.
