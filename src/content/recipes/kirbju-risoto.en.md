---
title: "Pumpkin risotto"
description: "A creamy orange risotto with no butter or cheese: the creaminess comes from mashed roasted pumpkin."
steps:
  - "Dice the pumpkin and roast at 200 °C with 1 tablespoon of the oil for 20 minutes. Mash half of it."
  - "In a pot fry the onion and garlic in 2 tablespoons of the oil for 4 minutes. Add the rice and fry for 2 minutes, until translucent."
  - "Pour in the wine and let it evaporate. Then add the hot stock a ladle at a time, stirring and waiting until it is absorbed. 18 to 20 minutes in total."
  - "Stir in the pumpkin mash and cubes, nutmeg, salt and pepper. Leave covered for 2 minutes."
  - "Fry the sage leaves in the remaining oil until crisp. Serve the risotto with the sage and pumpkin seeds."
---

A risotto should be loose enough to spread slowly on the plate like a wave.
