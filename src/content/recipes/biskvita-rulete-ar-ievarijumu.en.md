---
title: "Swiss roll with jam"
description: "Light and airy sponge roll filled with raspberry jam. A classic teatime cake that bakes in only 12 minutes."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C and line a baking tray with parchment."
  - "Whisk the eggs and sugar for 8 minutes until pale and tripled in volume."
  - "Sift in the flour and baking powder and fold in gently with a spatula from the bottom up."
  - "Spread the batter evenly on the tray and bake for 10 to 12 minutes until the top springs back."
  - "Turn the hot sponge out onto a tea towel, peel off the paper and roll up with the towel. Let cool."
  - "Unroll, spread with jam, roll up tightly again and dust with icing sugar."
tips:
  - "Roll the sponge while hot so that it does not crack."
---

The jam roll was the queen of the Soviet era coffee table. It was baked whenever guests turned up unexpectedly.
