---
title: "Iced coffee (iced latte) at home"
description: "Refreshing iced coffee with milk and ice, ready in 5 minutes. Just like the café, for a fraction of the price."
steps:
  - "Make the coffee strong and, if you have time, let it cool."
  - "While the coffee is still warm, stir in the honey or syrup."
  - "Fill a tall glass with ice and pour in the milk."
  - "Slowly pour the coffee on top so it forms pretty layers. Stir before drinking."
tips:
  - "Freeze coffee in an ice cube tray: then melting ice does not water the drink down."
---

On a hot summer morning hot coffee does not appeal, but going without is not an option. An iced latte is the compromise.
