---
title: "Turkey breast in creamy mustard sauce"
description: "Tender, lean turkey breast in a cream, wholegrain mustard and thyme sauce with peas. A quick, light dinner in one pan."
steps:
  - "Cut the turkey into strips and season with salt and pepper."
  - "Fry in oil over a high heat for 5 minutes. Set aside."
  - "In the same pan fry the onion and garlic for 3 minutes."
  - "Pour in the cream, add the mustard, thyme and peas. Simmer for 3 minutes."
  - "Return the turkey and warm through for 2 minutes. Serve with rice or potatoes."
tips:
  - "Turkey is very lean: cook it briefly over a high heat to keep it juicy."
---

Turkey is a good change from chicken when you fancy something different. In this sauce it is tender and tasty.
