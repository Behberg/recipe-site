---
title: Tom yum soup with prawns
description: A sour and spicy Thai soup with prawns, lemongrass, lime and mushrooms.
steps:
  - Bruise the lemongrass with the back of a knife and cut into pieces. Slice the ginger.
  - Bring the stock to the boil with the lemongrass, ginger and chilli and simmer for 5 minutes.
  - Add the sliced mushrooms and simmer for 3 minutes.
  - Add the prawns and cook until they turn pink.
  - Take off the heat and add the fish sauce, sugar and lime juice. Serve with coriander.
---

A good tom yum is sour, salty, hot and slightly sweet all at once.
