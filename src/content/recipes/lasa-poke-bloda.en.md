---
title: "Salmon poke bowl with avocado and rice"
description: "A colourful salmon poke bowl with sushi rice, avocado, cucumber, edamame and a soy sesame sauce. A fresh, healthy lunch in 40 minutes."
steps:
  - "Rinse and cook the rice and while warm mix with the rice vinegar. Let it cool to lukewarm."
  - "Cube the salmon and mix with the soy sauce and sesame oil."
  - "Slice the avocado, cucumber and radishes. Boil the edamame for 3 minutes."
  - "Put the rice in bowls and arrange rows of salmon, avocado, cucumber, edamame and radishes on top."
  - "Sprinkle with sesame and spring onions and drizzle with the remaining sauce."
tips:
  - "Raw salmon must be very fresh or previously frozen. Smoked or cooked salmon works too."
---

Poke comes from Hawaii but has become a lunch favourite worldwide. It is like deconstructed sushi.
