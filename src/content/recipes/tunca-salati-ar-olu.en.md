---
title: "Tuna salad with egg and vegetables"
description: "Filling tuna salad with egg, cucumber, tomatoes and sweetcorn. A quick, protein rich lunch in 15 minutes."
steps:
  - "Boil the eggs for 9 minutes, cool and quarter them."
  - "Drain the tuna and flake with a fork."
  - "Cut the cucumber into half moons, the tomatoes in half and the onion into thin slices."
  - "Mix the yoghurt or mayonnaise with the lemon juice, salt and pepper."
  - "Put the salad leaves, vegetables, tuna and eggs in a bowl and pour over the dressing."
tips:
  - "Great for a packed lunch: keep the dressing in a separate little pot."
---

A tin of tuna in the cupboard is the surest dinner insurance. This salad turns it into a proper meal.
