---
title: "Golden milk with turmeric"
description: "A warm, spiced drink with turmeric, ginger and cinnamon. An evening ritual instead of coffee."
steps:
  - "Pour the plant milk into a small pan and add the turmeric, ginger, cinnamon and pepper."
  - "Heat over medium heat for 4 to 5 minutes, whisking. Do not let it boil."
  - "Take off the heat and stir in the maple syrup."
  - "Pour into mugs through a sieve and dust with a pinch of cinnamon."
---

A pinch of black pepper helps the body absorb the good things in turmeric.
