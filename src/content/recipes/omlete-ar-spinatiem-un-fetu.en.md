---
title: "Spinach and feta omelette"
description: "A soft, fluffy omelette with fresh spinach and salty feta. A high protein breakfast ready in 10 minutes."
steps:
  - "Whisk the eggs with the milk, salt and pepper."
  - "Melt the butter in a frying pan over a medium heat, add the spinach and cook for 1 minute until wilted."
  - "Pour in the eggs, let them set slightly, then draw the edges into the centre with a spatula."
  - "When the top is almost set, crumble the feta over one half."
  - "Fold the omelette in half and serve straight away."
tips:
  - "Do not let the heat get too high: the omelette should stay soft and yellow, not brown."
---

A good omelette is one of the first things every cook learns. This is its green, healthy version.
