---
title: "Hot sea buckthorn tea with honey and ginger"
description: "Bright orange sea buckthorn tea with honey, ginger and orange, just like in Latvian cafés in winter. Warming, uplifting and full of vitamin C."
steps:
  - "Crush the sea buckthorn berries (fresh or frozen) with a spoon or pestle to release the juice."
  - "Put the berries, ginger slices, orange slices and cinnamon in a teapot."
  - "Pour over the boiling water and leave to steep for 5 minutes."
  - "Once the tea has cooled slightly, stir in the honey."
  - "Strain into mugs and serve hot."
tips:
  - "Add the honey only when the tea is off the boil so it keeps its qualities."
---

Sea buckthorn tea has become the symbol of Latvian winter cafés. At home it is even better and costs much less.
