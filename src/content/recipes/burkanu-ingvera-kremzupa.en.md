---
title: "Carrot and ginger soup with coconut milk"
description: "Bright orange carrot soup with ginger and coconut milk. Warming, vegan and full of vitamins."
steps:
  - "In a pan soften the onion in oil for 5 minutes, then add the grated ginger and garlic."
  - "Add the sliced carrots and stock. Simmer for 20 minutes, until the carrots are very soft."
  - "Blend until smooth and stir in the coconut milk and lemon juice. Season with salt and pepper."
  - "Toast the pumpkin seeds in a dry pan."
  - "Serve drizzled with a spoonful of coconut milk and scattered with the seeds."
tips:
  - "Adjust the ginger to taste: it brings warmth and a little heat."
---

When it is a grey November outside, the colour of this soup alone lifts your mood.
