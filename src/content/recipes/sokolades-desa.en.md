---
title: "Chocolate salami with biscuits and nuts"
description: "A childhood classic: chocolate salami made from broken biscuits, cocoa, butter and nuts. No baking, it sets in the fridge."
steps:
  - "Break the biscuits into small pieces and crush some to crumbs. Chop the nuts."
  - "In a pan over a low heat melt the butter with the sugar, cocoa and milk, stirring until the sugar dissolves. Do not boil."
  - "Pour over the biscuits and nuts and mix."
  - "Tip onto cling film, shape into a sausage and wrap tightly."
  - "Chill for 4 hours. Roll in icing sugar before slicing."
tips:
  - "The icing sugar coating looks like the white bloom on a real salami."
---

Chocolate salami is sliced into rounds with tea. It was one of the first sweets children were allowed to make themselves.
