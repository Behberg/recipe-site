---
title: Spaghetti with garlic and chilli (aglio e olio)
description: "The simplest Italian pasta: spaghetti with garlic, olive oil, chilli and parsley. Ready in 15 minutes."
steps:
  - Cook the spaghetti al dente in well salted water. Keep a cup of the pasta water.
  - While the pasta is cooking, slice the garlic thinly.
  - Heat the olive oil with the garlic and chilli in a pan over a low heat until the garlic is barely golden. It must not burn.
  - Add the spaghetti and half a cup of pasta water and toss vigorously until a light sauce forms.
  - Stir in the chopped parsley and serve straight away.
---

In Rome this pasta is made late at night, when the fridge is almost empty.
