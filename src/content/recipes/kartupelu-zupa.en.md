---
title: Potato soup with sausages
description: A thick potato soup with carrots, leek and fried sausages. A filling lunch in one pot.
steps:
  - In a pan, cook the onion and leek in butter for 5 minutes.
  - Add the diced potatoes and carrots and pour in the stock. Simmer for 20 minutes.
  - Mash some of the potatoes right in the pan to thicken the soup.
  - Slice the sausages into rounds, fry them in a pan and add them to the soup with the marjoram.
  - Season with salt and pepper and serve with parsley.
---

In Germany this soup is eaten with rye bread. We agree.
