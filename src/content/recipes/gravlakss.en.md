---
title: Gravlax
description: Salmon cured in salt, sugar and dill, with a mustard sauce. Ready after two days in the fridge.
steps:
  - Check the salmon for bones and cut it into two pieces.
  - Mix the salt, sugar and pepper. Rub the mixture over both sides of the fillets.
  - Spread plenty of dill over one piece and lay the other piece on top, skin side up.
  - Wrap tightly in cling film, put in a dish, weigh it down and keep in the fridge for 48 hours. Turn every 12 hours.
  - Wipe off the salt and slice very thinly.
  - For the sauce, whisk the mustard, honey, vinegar, oil and chopped dill.
tips:
  - Before curing, you can keep the salmon in the freezer for 48 hours to make it completely safe.
---

The name means “buried salmon”: fishermen once cured it by burying it in the sand.
