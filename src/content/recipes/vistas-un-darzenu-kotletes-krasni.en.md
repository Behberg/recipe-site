---
title: "Oven baked chicken and vegetable patties"
description: "Juicy chicken patties with hidden courgette and carrot, baked in the oven with no frying. Children eat their vegetables without knowing."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C. Line a baking tray with paper and oil it lightly."
  - "Finely grate the courgette and carrot and squeeze out the juice."
  - "Mix the chicken with the vegetables, egg, oats, cheese, dill, salt and pepper."
  - "With wet hands shape 12 patties and place on the paper."
  - "Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, turning after 12 minutes, until golden."
tips:
  - "The patties freeze well and are good for school lunchboxes."
---

Courgette and carrot simply vanish in these patties. Children eat them and ask for more.
