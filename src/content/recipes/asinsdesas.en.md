---
title: "Latvian blood sausages with barley"
description: "Traditional Latvian blood sausages with pearl barley, pork fat, onions and marjoram. A Christmas dish with ancient roots, served with lingonberries."
steps:
  - "Soak and rinse the casings. Simmer the barley in the milk over a low heat for 30 minutes to a thick porridge. Cool to lukewarm."
  - "Dice the pork fat finely, render half of it and fry the finely chopped onions in it."
  - "Strain the blood. Stir the blood, onions with their fat, the remaining diced fat, marjoram, allspice, salt and pepper into the porridge."
  - "Using a funnel, fill the casings loosely and tie every 25 cm."
  - "Place the sausages in hot, not boiling, water (80 °C) for 20 minutes. When pricked, clear liquid means they are done."
  - "Before serving, bake at 180 °C for 25 to 30 minutes until crisp."
tips:
  - "Order the blood in advance from butchers at the market and use it the same day."
---

When a pig was slaughtered in the old days, nothing went to waste. Blood sausages are the tastiest result of that wisdom.
