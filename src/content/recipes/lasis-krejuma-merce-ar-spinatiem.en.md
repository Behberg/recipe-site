---
title: "Creamy Tuscan salmon with spinach and tomatoes"
description: "Juicy salmon in a creamy garlic, parmesan and spinach sauce with cherry tomatoes. An elegant one pan dinner in 25 minutes."
steps:
  - "Cut the salmon into 4 pieces and season. Fry in oil for 3 minutes on each side. Set aside."
  - "In the same pan fry the garlic for 30 seconds, add the halved tomatoes and cook for 2 minutes."
  - "Pour in the cream, add the parmesan and simmer for 2 minutes."
  - "Add the spinach until wilted and stir in the lemon juice."
  - "Return the salmon to the sauce and warm for 2 minutes. Serve with rice or pasta."
tips:
  - "Return the salmon only at the end so it does not overcook."
---

This is one of the most popular salmon recipes because it looks like a restaurant dish but is so simple.
