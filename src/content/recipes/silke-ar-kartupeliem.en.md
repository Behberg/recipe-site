---
title: Herring with potatoes and sour cream sauce
description: Salted herring, boiled potatoes and a sour cream sauce with onions. An everyday Latvian classic.
steps:
  - Boil the potatoes in salted water.
  - Cut the herring into pieces.
  - Slice the onion into thin half rings and mix with the sour cream, dill and pepper.
  - Serve the warm potatoes with the herring and sour cream sauce.
tips:
  - If the herring is very salty, soak it in milk for 30 minutes.
---

A dish that tastes exactly like it does at grandma’s in the countryside.
