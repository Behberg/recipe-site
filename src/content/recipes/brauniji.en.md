---
title: Chocolate brownies
description: Moist, dense chocolate brownies with a crackly top. A real treat for chocolate lovers.
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 180 °C and line a square tin with paper.
  - Melt the chocolate with the butter over a water bath.
  - Whisk the eggs with the sugar for 3 minutes, then stir in the chocolate mixture.
  - Sift in the flour, cocoa and salt and mix. Stir in the nuts.
  - Bake for 20 to 25 minutes. The middle should be slightly moist.
  - Let it cool completely before cutting into squares.
---

Real brownies are not a cake. They should be dense and moist.
