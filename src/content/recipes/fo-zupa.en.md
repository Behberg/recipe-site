---
title: Beef pho
description: A Vietnamese noodle soup in a fragrant broth with star anise, thinly sliced beef and fresh herbs.
steps:
  - Halve the onion and ginger and char them in a dry pan until black.
  - Toast the spices and add them to the stock with the onion and ginger. Simmer gently for 45 minutes.
  - Strain and add the fish sauce and sugar.
  - Cook the noodles according to the packet. Slice the meat very thinly.
  - Put the noodles and raw meat into bowls and pour over the boiling broth, which cooks the meat instantly.
  - Serve with sprouts, herbs, lime and chilli.
---

In Vietnam pho is eaten for breakfast. Everyone adds herbs and lime to their own taste.
