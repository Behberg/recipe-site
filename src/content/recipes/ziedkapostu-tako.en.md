---
title: "Roasted cauliflower tacos"
description: "Spicy oven roasted cauliflower in corn tortillas with avocado, red cabbage and lime."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 220 °C. Break the cauliflower into small florets."
  - "Toss with the oil, paprika, cumin, chilli and salt. Spread on a baking tray and roast for 20 to 25 minutes, until the edges are dark."
  - "Shred the cabbage finely and massage it with half the lime juice and a pinch of salt."
  - "Mash the avocado with the remaining lime juice and salt."
  - "Warm the tortillas in a dry pan. Fill with avocado, cauliflower, cabbage, coriander and chilli sauce."
---

Roast the cauliflower until the edges are nearly black: that is where the flavour is.
