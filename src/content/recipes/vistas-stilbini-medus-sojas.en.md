---
title: "Honey soy baked chicken drumsticks"
description: "Sticky, glossy chicken drumsticks in a honey, soy and garlic glaze, baked in the oven. A children’s favourite with 5 minutes of work."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C."
  - "Mix the soy sauce, honey, oil, crushed garlic and ginger."
  - "Toss the drumsticks in the sauce and lay them in a roasting tin in one layer. Keep the leftover sauce."
  - "Bake for 40 to 45 minutes, basting with the sauce every 15 minutes, until the skin is dark and sticky."
  - "Sprinkle with sesame seeds and spring onions. Serve with rice and cucumber."
tips:
  - "If the glaze starts to burn, add a few spoonfuls of water to the tin."
---

Drumsticks are the cheapest part of the chicken and the tastiest. This is the recipe the family asks for more than any other.
