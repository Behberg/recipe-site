---
title: Buberts with berry sauce
description: An airy vanilla semolina dessert with whisked egg whites and a tart cranberry or raspberry sauce.
steps:
  - Bring the milk to the boil with the sugar and vanilla. Sprinkle in the semolina and boil for 10 minutes, stirring constantly.
  - Take off the heat and stir in the egg yolks.
  - Whisk the whites to stiff peaks and gently fold them into the warm mixture.
  - For the sauce, boil the berries with the water and sugar for 5 minutes. Dissolve the starch in a little cold water, stir it in and bring back to the boil.
  - Serve the buberts warm or cold, with the berry sauce poured over.
---

As soft as a cloud. Children and grandparents alike love this dessert.
