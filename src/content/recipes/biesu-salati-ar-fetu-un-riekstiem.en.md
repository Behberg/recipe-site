---
title: "Beetroot salad with feta, walnuts and honey dressing"
description: "Colourful beetroot salad with creamy feta, toasted walnuts and a honey balsamic dressing. Simple but restaurant pretty."
steps:
  - "Peel the beetroot and cut into cubes or wedges."
  - "Toast the walnuts in a dry pan for 3 minutes."
  - "Whisk the oil with the balsamic, honey, salt and pepper."
  - "Arrange the salad leaves, beetroot, crumbled feta and nuts on a plate."
  - "Drizzle with the dressing just before serving."
tips:
  - "Beetroot roasted in foil for 1 hour is sweeter and more aromatic than boiled."
---

Beetroot is one of the cheapest vegetables, but with feta and nuts it becomes a party starter.
