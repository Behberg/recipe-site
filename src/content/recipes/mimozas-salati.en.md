---
title: "Mimosa layered salad with tuna"
description: "Layered mimosa salad with tuna, potatoes, carrots, cheese and a topping of grated egg yolk that looks like mimosa flowers."
steps:
  - "Boil the potatoes, carrots and eggs and let them cool. Separate the egg whites from the yolks."
  - "Chop the onion finely and soak in cold water for 5 minutes to take away the sharpness. Drain."
  - "Layer in a glass dish: grated potatoes, flaked fish with the onion, grated carrots, grated cheese and grated egg whites. Spread each layer thinly with mayonnaise."
  - "Cover the top generously with finely grated egg yolks."
  - "Garnish with dill and chill for at least 2 hours so the flavours meld."
tips:
  - "Grate everything finely and do not press the layers down, so the salad stays light."
---

The name comes from the yellow top, which looks like spring mimosa. That is why it is a classic for International Women’s Day and parties.
