---
title: "Lazy vareniki (curd cheese dumplings)"
description: "Soft curd cheese dumplings known as lazy vareniki. 25 minutes, 5 ingredients, and children eat them with delight."
steps:
  - "Mash the curd cheese with a fork and mix with the egg, sugar and salt."
  - "Stir in the flour to a soft, slightly sticky dough."
  - "On a floured surface roll the dough into a sausage, flatten it slightly and cut into 2 cm pieces."
  - "Cook in a large pan of boiling salted water for 3 to 4 minutes after they float."
  - "Lift out with a slotted spoon, toss with butter and serve with sour cream, jam or honey."
tips:
  - "Do not add too much flour: the less there is, the softer the dumplings."
---

They are called lazy because you do not have to seal each one. The taste is exactly like the real thing.
