---
title: "Tuna and potato fish cakes"
description: "Crisp tuna fish cakes with potato, spring onion and lemon zest from storecupboard ingredients. A quick, cheap dinner in 25 minutes."
steps:
  - "Drain the tuna and mash the potatoes."
  - "Mix the tuna, potatoes, egg, spring onions, lemon zest, half the breadcrumbs, salt and pepper."
  - "Shape into 8 cakes and coat in the remaining breadcrumbs."
  - "Fry in oil for 3 to 4 minutes on each side until golden."
  - "Serve with salad and a yoghurt or tartare sauce."
tips:
  - "The potato holds everything together: it is the best binder."
---

Two tins of tuna and a couple of potatoes become a dinner even fish sceptics eat.
