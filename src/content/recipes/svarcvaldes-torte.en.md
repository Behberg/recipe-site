---
title: Black Forest cherry cake
description: A chocolate sponge with cherries and whipped cream. The most famous German gâteau.
steps:
  - Whisk the eggs with the sugar until very pale, 10 minutes. Fold in the flour, cocoa and baking powder.
  - Bake in the oven at 175 °C for 30 to 35 minutes. Cool and cut into three layers.
  - Bring the cherries and sugar to the boil, add the starch dissolved in water and cook until thickened. Let it cool.
  - Whip the cream with the icing sugar until stiff.
  - "Sprinkle the layers with liqueur or juice. Stack: sponge, cherries, cream, sponge, cherries, cream, sponge."
  - Cover with cream, press chocolate shavings onto the sides and decorate with cherries. Chill for at least 4 hours.
---

The cake is named after the Black Forest mountains, where the famous cherry schnapps is made.
