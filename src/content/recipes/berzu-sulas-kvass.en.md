---
title: Birch sap kvass
description: A lightly sparkling spring drink made from birch sap, raisins and lemon.
yield: 3 litres
steps:
  - Strain the birch sap through muslin.
  - Add the sugar and stir until it dissolves.
  - Add the unwashed raisins and the sliced lemon.
  - Cover with muslin and leave at room temperature for 2 to 3 days.
  - When bubbles start to form, bottle it and keep it in a cool place. After a week the kvass will be sparkling.
---

Birch sap flows for only a few weeks in spring. This way you can enjoy it all summer long.
