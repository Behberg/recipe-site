---
title: "Courgette fritters with cheese and dill"
description: "Golden courgette fritters with cheese, garlic and dill, served with sour cream. A great way to use up a courgette glut."
steps:
  - "Coarsely grate the courgettes, sprinkle with the salt and leave for 10 minutes."
  - "Squeeze the grated courgette hard in a tea towel to get out as much liquid as possible."
  - "Mix with the eggs, flour, cheese, crushed garlic and chopped dill."
  - "Fry spoonfuls in oil for 3 minutes on each side until golden."
  - "Serve with sour cream."
tips:
  - "Squeezing out the liquid is the key step: otherwise the fritters are soggy and fall apart."
---

In August courgettes grow faster than you can eat them. These fritters disappear faster than you can fry them.
