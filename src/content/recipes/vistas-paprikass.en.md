---
title: Chicken paprikash
description: Chicken in a mild paprika and sour cream sauce. Serve with noodles or potatoes.
steps:
  - In a pan, fry the chopped onions in oil until soft.
  - Take off the heat, stir in the paprika, then add the chicken, sliced pepper and tomato.
  - Pour in the stock, salt and simmer, covered, for 35 minutes.
  - Mix the sour cream with the flour, add a little of the hot sauce, then stir it into the pan.
  - Heat until the sauce thickens. Serve with noodles or boiled potatoes.
---

A red sauce and sour cream: the favourite pairing of Hungarian cooking.
