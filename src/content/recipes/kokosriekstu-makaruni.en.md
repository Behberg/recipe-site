---
title: "Coconut macaroons dipped in chocolate"
description: "Chewy coconut macaroons made from egg whites, sugar and coconut, dipped in chocolate. Gluten free and just 5 ingredients."
yield: "about 20 macaroons"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 165 °C and line a baking tray with paper."
  - "Whisk the egg whites with the salt until frothy. Stir in the sugar and vanilla sugar."
  - "Add the coconut and mix to a moist mixture."
  - "Spoon into mounds and press them together with your fingers."
  - "Bake for 18 to 20 minutes, until the edges are golden. Once cool, dip the bases in melted chocolate."
tips:
  - "The mixture can be made ahead: it keeps well in the fridge for 2 days."
---

Coconut macaroons are another way to use up leftover whites. They are so good you start saving whites on purpose.
