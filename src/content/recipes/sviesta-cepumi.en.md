---
title: "Simple butter cookies"
description: "Crumbly, melt in the mouth butter cookies with just 5 ingredients. Pipe them, cut them with cutters and decorate them with children."
yield: "about 40 cookies"
steps:
  - "Beat the soft butter with the icing sugar until pale and creamy."
  - "Mix in the egg yolk, vanilla sugar and salt."
  - "Add the flour and quickly bring together into a dough. Wrap in cling film and chill for 30 minutes."
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C. Roll the dough out 5 mm thick and cut out cookies with cutters."
  - "Bake on baking paper for 10 to 12 minutes, until the edges are just golden."
  - "Let them cool on the tray, as warm cookies are fragile."
tips:
  - "Do not knead for long: the less you work the dough, the more crumbly the cookies."
---

These are the cookies you bake with children. Even if the shapes come out wonky, the taste is always exactly right.
