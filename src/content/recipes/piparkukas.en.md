---
title: Gingerbread cookies
description: Fragrant Christmas gingerbread with ginger, cinnamon, cloves and orange zest.
yield: about 60 cookies
steps:
  - In a small pan, warm the honey with the sugar and spices until the sugar melts. Take off the heat and stir in the butter.
  - When the mixture is lukewarm, stir in the eggs and orange zest.
  - Mix the flour with the baking soda and gradually knead it into the dough. The dough will be soft.
  - Wrap in cling film and put in the fridge for at least one night. This lets the spices come through fully.
  - Roll the dough out thinly (2 to 3 mm) and cut out shapes with cutters.
  - Bake in the oven at 200 °C for 6 to 8 minutes. Cool and decorate with icing.
tips:
  - The dough keeps in the fridge for up to 2 weeks, so you can make it well ahead.
---

The smell of gingerbread in the house means Christmas has arrived.
