---
title: "Grilled vegetables with garlic and balsamic"
description: "Barbecued courgette, aubergine, peppers, onions and mushrooms in a garlic and balsamic marinade. A colourful side or vegetarian dinner."
steps:
  - "Slice the courgette and aubergine lengthways 1 cm thick, the peppers into wide strips and the onions into thick rounds."
  - "Mix the oil with crushed garlic, balsamic, chopped rosemary, salt and pepper."
  - "Brush the vegetables with the marinade and leave for 15 minutes."
  - "Grill over a medium heat for 3 to 5 minutes on each side, until marked and tender."
  - "Serve warm or at room temperature, drizzled with the rest of the marinade."
tips:
  - "Grill mushrooms and small vegetables in a grill pan or on skewers so they do not fall through."
---

While the meat cooks, there is always room for vegetables at the edge of the grill. They often go first.
