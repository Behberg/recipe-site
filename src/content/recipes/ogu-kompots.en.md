---
title: Bottled berry compote
description: A clear, sweet berry compote for winter made from blackcurrants, redcurrants, cherries or other garden berries.
yield: 3 jars of 1 l
steps:
  - Wash the berries and divide them between sterilised jars, filling them a third full.
  - Boil the water with the sugar until the sugar dissolves.
  - Pour the hot syrup into the jars right to the top.
  - Seal straight away, turn upside down and wrap in a blanket until cool.
---

In winter it is diluted with water or drunk just as it is, like in childhood.
