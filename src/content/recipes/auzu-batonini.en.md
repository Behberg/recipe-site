---
title: "Oat bars with honey and raisins (flapjacks)"
description: "Chewy, filling oat bars with honey, butter, raisins and seeds. A healthier snack for school bags and hikes."
yield: "12 bars"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C and line a 20 × 20 cm square tin with paper."
  - "In a pan over a low heat melt the butter with the sugar and honey until the sugar dissolves."
  - "Take off the heat and stir in the oats, raisins, seeds and salt."
  - "Press the mixture firmly into the tin and smooth with a spoon."
  - "Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until the edges are golden brown."
  - "Cut into bars while still warm, but only lift them out of the tin once completely cool."
tips:
  - "Swap the raisins for dried cranberries, apricots or chocolate chips."
---

These bars are what to pack for a long journey. They do not crumble in the bag and give energy for hours.
