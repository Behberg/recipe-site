---
title: "Ginger shots with lemon and turmeric"
description: "Fiery, energising ginger shots with lemon, orange, turmeric and honey. A small sip of strength in the cold season."
yield: "about 8 shots"
steps:
  - "Wash the ginger and cut into pieces; you can leave the skin on."
  - "Squeeze the lemons and orange."
  - "Put the ginger, juice, water, turmeric, honey and pepper in a blender. Blend for 1 minute."
  - "Strain through a fine sieve or muslin, pressing well."
  - "Pour into a small bottle and keep in the fridge for up to 5 days. Drink 30 ml in the morning."
tips:
  - "Black pepper helps the body absorb the turmeric."
---

One shot in the morning wakes you up better than coffee. The first time it will bring tears to your eyes.
