---
title: "Latvian sprat sandwiches with egg and pickle"
description: "A Latvian party table classic: toasted bread with garlic mayonnaise, smoked sprats, egg and pickled cucumber. Ready in 25 minutes."
steps:
  - "Cut the baguette into diagonal slices and toast them on both sides in a dry pan or the oven."
  - "Mix the mayonnaise with crushed garlic and spread thinly on the toasts."
  - "Top each with a slice of egg and a slice of pickle."
  - "Lay 1 or 2 sprats on top."
  - "Garnish with a sprig of dill and serve."
tips:
  - "Lift the sprats out carefully with a fork so they do not break. The oil is great for toasting the bread."
---

Riga sprats are one of Latvia’s best known products. Sprat sandwiches appear on every party table, from New Year to birthdays.
