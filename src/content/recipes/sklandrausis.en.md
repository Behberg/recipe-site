---
title: Sklandrauši
description: Livonian rye tarts from Kurzeme with a potato and carrot filling and cinnamon.
yield: 12 tarts
steps:
  - Boil the potatoes, drain and mash with the butter. When cool, stir in the egg and a little salt.
  - Grate the carrots finely and stew them with a little water and butter for 15 minutes, until soft. Cool, then add the sugar, sour cream, egg and cinnamon.
  - For the dough, mix the flour with the salt, work in the cold butter and gradually add water until the dough is pliable.
  - Roll the dough out thinly and cut out rounds (about 10 cm across). Fold the edges up to form a rim.
  - Put a layer of potato in each tart first and a layer of carrot on top.
  - Bake in the oven at 200 °C for 20 to 25 minutes, until the edges are golden. Serve with cinnamon and sour cream.
---

Sklandrausis is listed in the EU register of traditional specialities. In it, potatoes and carrots turn into a real dessert.
