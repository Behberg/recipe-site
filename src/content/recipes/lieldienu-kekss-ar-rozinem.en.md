---
title: "Easter bread with raisins and candied peel"
description: "A tall, soft yeasted Easter bread with raisins, candied peel and lemon icing. The festive loaf for the Easter table, baked the day before."
steps:
  - "Dissolve the yeast in the warm milk with a spoonful of sugar and 100 g of the flour. Leave to froth for 20 minutes."
  - "Whisk the eggs with the rest of the sugar and the vanilla sugar. Add to the starter with the lemon zest, salt and remaining flour."
  - "Knead for 10 minutes, working in the soft butter bit by bit, until smooth and elastic. Knead in the raisins and peel."
  - "Leave to rise somewhere warm for 1.5 hours, until doubled."
  - "Divide between tall tins, filling them a third full. Leave to rise until the dough almost reaches the rim."
  - "Bake at 180 °C for 35 to 45 minutes. Once cool, pour over the icing sugar and lemon icing."
tips:
  - "If the top browns too fast, cover with foil. Test with a wooden skewer."
---

Easter bread is baked on Holy Saturday and sits in the centre of the table on Sunday morning with the dyed eggs.
