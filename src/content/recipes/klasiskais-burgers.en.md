---
title: Classic burger
description: A juicy beef patty with cheddar, lettuce, tomato and house sauce in a soft bun.
steps:
  - Divide the meat into 4 and shape flat patties slightly wider than the bun. Press a small dip in the middle.
  - For the sauce, mix the mayonnaise, ketchup, mustard and chopped pickles.
  - Season the patties with salt and pepper only just before cooking.
  - Cook in a very hot pan or on a grill for 3 minutes, flip, add the cheese and cook for another 2 to 3 minutes.
  - Toast the buns.
  - "Assemble: bun, sauce, lettuce, patty with cheese, tomato, onion, pickles, bun."
---

A good burger needs good meat, a hot pan and nothing more.
