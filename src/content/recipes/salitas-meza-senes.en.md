---
title: "Salted wild mushrooms"
description: "Traditionally salted wild mushrooms with garlic, dill and blackcurrant leaves. A crunchy winter snack with potatoes and sour cream."
yield: "about 3 jars of 0.5 l"
steps:
  - "Clean the mushrooms, cut up the large ones and boil in salted water for 5 minutes. Rinse in cold water and drain."
  - "Put the dill, leaves and peppercorns in the bottom of jars or a crock."
  - "Layer the mushrooms caps down, sprinkling each layer with salt and slices of garlic."
  - "Cover with leaves, put a plate on top and weigh it down."
  - "Keep somewhere cool. The mushrooms are ready after 3 to 4 weeks."
tips:
  - "Before eating, rinse the mushrooms and dress them with sour cream and onions."
---

Salting mushrooms is one of the oldest Latvian preserving traditions. They kept in the cellar all winter.
