---
title: Falafel
description: Crisp chickpea balls with herbs and spices. Serve in pitta with tahini sauce.
yield: about 20 falafel
steps:
  - Drain the soaked chickpeas (do not cook them!) and dry them well.
  - In a food processor, grind the chickpeas with the onion, garlic, herbs and spices to a coarse mixture.
  - Stir in the baking soda and chill for 30 minutes.
  - Shape into balls and fry in oil at 175 °C for 3 to 4 minutes, until deep golden.
  - For the sauce, mix the tahini with the lemon juice, salt and water until creamy.
  - Serve in pitta with vegetables and the sauce.
tips:
  - "Tinned chickpeas will not work: the falafel will fall apart."
---

Crisp outside, green and soft inside: that is a real falafel.
