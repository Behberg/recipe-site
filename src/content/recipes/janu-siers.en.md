---
title: Jāņi cheese
description: The yellow caraway cheese without which Midsummer is unthinkable. Made at home with milk, cottage cheese and eggs.
steps:
  - Bring the milk to the boil in a large pan. Add the cottage cheese and heat, stirring, until the whey separates and turns yellowish green.
  - Pour the mixture through muslin and let the whey drain.
  - Return the cheese to the pan and add the butter, salt and caraway. Heat over a low heat, stirring constantly.
  - Beat the eggs and stir them into the mixture. Stir until it becomes smooth and glossy and starts to come away from the sides of the pan.
  - Put the hot mixture into muslin, gather it into a round and place it under a weight (for example a board with a jar of water).
  - Let it cool in the fridge for at least 6 hours or overnight.
---

The round Jāņi cheese symbolises the sun. Serve it in thin slices with beer and bacon pīrāgi.
