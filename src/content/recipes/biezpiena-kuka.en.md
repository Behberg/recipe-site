---
title: Cottage cheese cake
description: A creamy cottage cheese cake with a crisp shortcrust base and a hint of lemon.
steps:
  - For the base, rub the flour, sugar and cold butter into crumbs, add the egg and knead quickly. Chill for 20 minutes.
  - Press the dough over the base and up the sides of the tin.
  - For the filling, blend the cottage cheese until smooth, then add the eggs, sugar, sour cream, starch and lemon zest.
  - Pour the filling into the tin and bake in the oven at 170 °C for 55 to 60 minutes, until the middle no longer wobbles.
  - Let the cake cool in the oven with the door ajar, then in the fridge for at least 4 hours.
tips:
  - Cooling it slowly helps stop the top from cracking.
---

Latvian cottage cheese makes this cake lighter than an American cheesecake.
