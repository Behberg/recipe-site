---
title: "Okroshka (cold summer soup with kvass or kefir)"
description: "Cold summer soup okroshka with potatoes, eggs, cucumbers, radishes and sausage, topped up with kvass or kefir. Refreshing on the hottest day."
steps:
  - "Cut the potatoes, eggs, cucumbers, radishes and sausage into small, even cubes."
  - "Finely chop the spring onions and dill and rub them with the salt to release their juice and aroma."
  - "Mix everything in a large bowl with the mustard."
  - "Just before serving pour over ice cold kvass or kefir and stir in the sour cream."
  - "Serve straight away, with an ice cube if you like."
tips:
  - "Keep the chopped ingredients separate and add the liquid in the bowl: that way the okroshka stays fresh."
---

Every family argues over whether okroshka needs kvass or kefir. Both are right.
