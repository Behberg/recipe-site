---
title: Fried Baltic herring in marinade
description: Fried Baltic herring soaked in a sweet and sour onion marinade. A great cold starter.
steps:
  - Cut the heads off the herring, gut and wash them. Pat dry and salt.
  - Coat in flour and fry in oil for 2 to 3 minutes on each side, until golden.
  - For the marinade, boil the water with the sugar, salt, bay leaves and pepper. Add the onion rings and boil for 2 minutes. Take off the heat and add the vinegar.
  - Arrange the herring in a dish and pour over the hot marinade.
  - Once cool, put it in the fridge for at least 12 hours.
---

The recipe comes from seaside fishing villages, where herring was cooked in large amounts.
