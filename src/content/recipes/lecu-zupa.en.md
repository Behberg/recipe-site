---
title: Turkish red lentil soup
description: A velvety red lentil soup with cumin, lemon and spicy paprika butter.
steps:
  - Rinse the lentils until the water runs clear.
  - In a pan, fry the onion and grated carrot, then add the tomato paste and cumin.
  - Add the lentils and stock. Simmer for 20 minutes, until the lentils fall apart.
  - Blend the soup until smooth and season with salt and pepper.
  - Melt the butter with the paprika and drizzle over each portion. Serve with lemon.
---

In Istanbul it is eaten from morning to night, and rightly so.
