---
title: "Peppers stuffed with rice and beans"
description: "Colourful peppers with a Mexican filling: rice, black beans, sweetcorn and tomatoes."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C. Halve the peppers lengthways, remove the seeds and place in a baking dish."
  - "Fry the onion and garlic with the cumin and smoked paprika in the oil for 4 minutes."
  - "Add the rice, rinsed beans, sweetcorn and half the tomatoes. Season with salt and pepper and stir."
  - "Fill the peppers. Pour the remaining tomatoes into the bottom of the dish."
  - "Cover with foil and bake for 20 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 10 to 15 minutes. Serve with coriander and lime."
---

A good way to use up yesterday's rice.
