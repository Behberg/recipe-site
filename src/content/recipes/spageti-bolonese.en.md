---
title: Spaghetti bolognese
description: A rich, slowly simmered meat and tomato sauce with spaghetti and parmesan.
steps:
  - Chop the onion, carrot and celery very finely and cook them in olive oil for 10 minutes.
  - Add the garlic and mince and fry until the meat is browned.
  - Stir in the tomato paste, then the tomatoes. Season with salt and pepper.
  - "Simmer over a very low heat for 45 minutes. At the end, stir in the milk: it makes the sauce softer."
  - Cook the spaghetti al dente and toss with the sauce.
  - Serve with grated parmesan.
---

Time is the best seasoning. The longer it simmers, the tastier it gets.
