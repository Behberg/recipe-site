---
title: "New potatoes with dill and butter"
description: "Summer’s simplest luxury: boiled new potatoes with melting butter and plenty of fresh dill. With herring, cucumbers and sour cream."
steps:
  - "Scrub the potatoes with a brush, leaving the thin skins on."
  - "Put them in boiling salted water with a few dill stalks."
  - "Simmer for 15 to 20 minutes, until a knife slides in easily."
  - "Drain and leave in the pan uncovered for 1 minute to steam dry."
  - "Add the butter and chopped dill and shake the pan to coat the potatoes."
tips:
  - "Put new potatoes into boiling water, not cold: that way they keep their sweetness."
---

The first new potatoes at the end of June are a celebration Latvians wait for all year. They need nothing more than butter and dill.
