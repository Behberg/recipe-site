---
title: "Grilled pork neck steaks in honey mustard marinade"
description: "Juicy pork neck steaks in a honey, mustard and garlic marinade, cooked on the grill. The simplest and tastiest barbecue meat for Midsummer."
steps:
  - "Mix the mustard with the honey, crushed garlic, oil, paprika, thyme leaves and pepper."
  - "Rub the meat with the marinade and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight."
  - "Sprinkle with coarse salt just before grilling."
  - "Grill over hot coals for 4 to 6 minutes on each side, until cooked through but juicy."
  - "Rest for 5 minutes and serve."
tips:
  - "Add salt only before grilling: in the marinade it draws juice out of the meat."
---

Pork neck is the queen of barbecue meat: marbled, juicy and never dry.
