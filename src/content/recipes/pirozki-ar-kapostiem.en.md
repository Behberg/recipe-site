---
title: "Baked cabbage pirozhki (buns)"
description: "Soft yeasted pirozhki filled with braised cabbage, carrot and egg, baked in the oven. Great with broth or for packed lunches."
yield: "16 pirozhki"
steps:
  - "Mix the dough ingredients and knead for 8 minutes. Leave to rise somewhere warm for 1 hour."
  - "For the filling, fry the onion and carrot in oil, add the finely shredded cabbage and braise covered for 25 minutes. Add the tomato paste, salt and pepper. Cool and stir in the chopped eggs."
  - "Divide the dough into 16, roll each into a circle, put filling in the middle and pinch the edges firmly closed."
  - "Place seam side down on paper and leave to rise for 20 minutes."
  - "Heat the oven to 190 °C, brush with egg and bake for 20 to 25 minutes until golden."
tips:
  - "The filling must be completely cold, or the dough will turn soggy."
---

Cabbage pirozhki are cheap, filling and smell so good the neighbours come to see.
