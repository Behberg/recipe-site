---
title: Buckwheat with mushrooms and onions
description: Fluffy buckwheat with fried mushrooms, onions and butter. A simple, filling dish.
steps:
  - Rinse the buckwheat, cover with water, add salt and cook covered over a low heat for 15 minutes.
  - Take off the heat and let it stand for another 5 minutes.
  - In a pan, fry the chopped onion and sliced mushrooms in butter for 10 minutes.
  - Mix the buckwheat with the mushrooms, season with salt and pepper and sprinkle with dill.
---

Buckwheat is one of the healthiest and cheapest grains, and it grows in Latvia too.
