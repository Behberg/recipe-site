---
title: "Arepas with black beans, avocado and cheese"
description: "Venezuelan and Colombian arepas: crisp, gluten free corn cakes filled with black beans, avocado and cheese."
steps:
  - "Mix the maize flour with the salt, add the warm water and knead to a soft dough. Rest for 5 minutes."
  - "Divide into 8 and shape each into a 1.5 cm thick patty."
  - "Cook in a hot, lightly oiled pan for 5 to 6 minutes on each side, until golden and they sound hollow."
  - "Warm the beans and mash them lightly; slice the avocado."
  - "Split the arepas like pockets and fill with the beans, avocado and cheese."
tips:
  - "Only special pre cooked maize flour works for arepas; ordinary cornmeal will not hold together."
---

In Venezuela arepas are eaten for breakfast, lunch and dinner. They are like a bun you can fill with anything.
