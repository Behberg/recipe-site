---
title: "Homemade sesame bagels"
description: "Dense, chewy bagels boiled in honey water before baking. Just like the ones from New York bakeries, only from your own oven."
steps:
  - "Dissolve the yeast and 1 tbsp honey in the water. Add the flour and salt and knead for 10 minutes into a firm, smooth dough."
  - "Cover and leave to rise for 1 hour. Divide into 8 pieces, shape into balls and poke a hole in the middle with your finger, stretching into a ring."
  - "Heat the oven to 220 °C. Bring a large pot of water to the boil with the remaining honey."
  - "Boil the bagels two at a time for 1 minute on each side and lift onto a tray with a slotted spoon."
  - "Brush with egg, sprinkle with sesame and bake for 20 to 25 minutes until deep golden."
tips:
  - "It is the boiling that gives bagels their shiny crust and chewy centre."
---

Bagels travelled from the Jewish bakeries of Poland to New York and now to your breakfast table.
