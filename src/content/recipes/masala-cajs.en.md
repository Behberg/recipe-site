---
title: "Masala chai (Indian spiced milk tea)"
description: "Fragrant Indian masala chai with milk, ginger, cardamom, cinnamon and cloves. It warms you from the inside on a cold day."
steps:
  - "Lightly crush the cardamom pods and peppercorns with the flat of a knife."
  - "Bring the water to the boil in a small pan with the ginger and all the spices and simmer for 5 minutes."
  - "Add the tea and simmer for 2 minutes."
  - "Pour in the milk and sugar, bring back to the boil and simmer for 1 minute."
  - "Strain into mugs and serve hot."
tips:
  - "In India chai is boiled right in the pan rather than brewed in a pot: that makes it stronger and more aromatic."
---

In India chai is a daily ritual. On street corners it is boiled in big pots and sold in small clay cups.
