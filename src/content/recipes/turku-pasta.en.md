---
title: "Turkish pasta with yoghurt and minced beef"
description: "The TikTok-famous pasta in the style of manti: garlic yoghurt sauce, spiced minced beef and browned butter with chilli."
steps:
  - "Mix the yoghurt with the grated garlic and salt and leave it at room temperature so it is not fridge-cold."
  - "Heat the oil in a pan, fry the chopped onion for 3 minutes, add the beef and fry for 8 minutes, breaking it into crumbs."
  - "Add the tomato paste, cumin, paprika, salt and pepper and fry for 2 more minutes. If the meat looks dry, add 3 tablespoons of water."
  - "Cook the pasta in salted water as the packet says and drain."
  - "Melt the butter in a small pan and let it brown gently for 2 minutes. Take off the heat and stir in the chilli flakes and mint."
  - "Plate the pasta, top with the yoghurt sauce and the beef, pour over the hot butter and scatter with parsley."
tips:
  - "In Turkey the same toppings go on manti, tiny meat dumplings. This is the quick version with ordinary pasta."
  - "For a vegetarian version, use fried lentils or chickpeas instead of beef."
  - "Use thick yoghurt with 8 to 10% fat. Runny yoghurt makes the dish watery."
---
