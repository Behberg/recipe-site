---
title: Königsberg meatballs
description: Tender meatballs in a white cream sauce with capers and lemon.
steps:
  - Soak the bread in water and squeeze it out. Mix with the meat, egg, finely chopped onion, salt and pepper.
  - Shape into balls and simmer for 15 minutes in gently bubbling stock. Take them out and keep the stock.
  - For the sauce, melt the butter, stir in the flour and gradually add 500 ml of the stock. Cook until thickened.
  - Add the cream, capers and lemon juice. Put the meatballs into the sauce and heat through.
  - Serve with boiled potatoes and parsley.
---

A classic from Königsberg that German families have been cooking for more than two centuries.
