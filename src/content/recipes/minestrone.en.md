---
title: Minestrone
description: A hearty Italian vegetable soup with beans, small pasta and parmesan.
steps:
  - In a pan, cook the diced onion, carrots and celery in olive oil for 8 minutes.
  - Add the courgette and garlic and cook for another 3 minutes.
  - Add the tomatoes and stock, bring to the boil and simmer for 20 minutes.
  - Add the rinsed beans and the pasta and cook until the pasta is tender.
  - Season with salt and pepper. Serve with grated parmesan and basil.
---

In every region of Italy minestrone is made with whatever is growing in the garden at the time. You can do the same.
