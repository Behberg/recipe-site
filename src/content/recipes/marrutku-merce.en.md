---
title: "Horseradish and tomato relish"
description: "Fiery horseradish and tomato relish with garlic, no cooking needed. A warming condiment for meat, aspic and dumplings."
yield: "about 2 jars of 0.3 l"
steps:
  - "Peel the horseradish and grate finely or mince (ideally outdoors or by an open window)."
  - "Blend or mince the tomatoes and garlic."
  - "Mix with the horseradish and add the salt and sugar."
  - "Spoon into jars and keep in the fridge for up to a month."
tips:
  - "The heat of horseradish fades with time: it is strongest in the first few days."
---

This relish is not for the faint hearted. One spoonful with aspic clears the sinuses better than any medicine.
