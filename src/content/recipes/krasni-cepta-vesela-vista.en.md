---
title: "Roast chicken with lemon and garlic"
description: "Golden, crispy whole roast chicken with lemon, garlic and thyme, cooked on a bed of potatoes. A relaxed Sunday lunch."
steps:
  - "Take the chicken out of the fridge 30 minutes before cooking. Heat the oven to 200 °C."
  - "Pat the chicken dry. Mix the soft butter with salt, pepper and chopped thyme and rub it under and over the skin."
  - "Put the halved lemon, rosemary and half the garlic bulb inside the cavity."
  - "Cut the potatoes into wedges, toss with the oil, salt and the rest of the garlic and spread in a roasting tin. Place the chicken on top, breast side up."
  - "Roast for 80 to 90 minutes, until the juices run clear when the thigh is pierced."
  - "Let the chicken rest for 15 minutes before carving."
tips:
  - "The drier the skin before roasting, the crispier it gets. Leave the chicken uncovered in the fridge overnight."
---

Roasting a whole chicken sounds like a big occasion, but the oven does the work. All you do is carve it at the table.
