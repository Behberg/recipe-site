---
title: "Black bean burger"
description: "A hearty burger with a crisp black bean patty, avocado and tomato."
steps:
  - "Rinse the beans and dry them very well. Mash in a bowl with a fork, leaving a few whole."
  - "Add the oats, finely chopped onion and garlic, tomato paste, spices, salt and pepper. Mix and leave for 10 minutes."
  - "With wet hands shape 4 flat patties."
  - "Fry in oil over medium heat for 4 to 5 minutes on each side, until a crust forms."
  - "Toast the buns and build the burgers with lettuce, tomato and avocado slices."
---

Dry beans are the secret to patties that do not fall apart in the pan.
