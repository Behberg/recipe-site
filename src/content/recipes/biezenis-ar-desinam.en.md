---
title: Mashed potatoes with fried sausages
description: Creamy mashed potatoes with golden fried sausages, onions and pickled cucumbers.
steps:
  - Peel and cut the potatoes and boil them in salted water for 20 minutes, until soft. Drain.
  - Mash with the butter, gradually adding the warm milk, until creamy. Season with salt.
  - Make a few cuts in the sausages and fry them with the onion rings until golden.
  - Serve with the mash and pickled cucumbers.
tips:
  - "Add the milk warm: cold milk makes the mash gluey."
---

A 30 minute dinner that is always welcome.
