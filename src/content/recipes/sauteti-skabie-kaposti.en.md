---
title: Braised sauerkraut
description: Sweet and sour braised sauerkraut with bacon and carrots. The perfect side for meat and sausages.
steps:
  - Dice the bacon and render it in a pan until golden.
  - Add the chopped onion and grated carrot and fry for 5 minutes.
  - Add the sauerkraut, caraway and water. Stir.
  - Braise, covered, over a low heat for 45 to 60 minutes, stirring from time to time.
  - Finally add the sugar and cook uncovered until the cabbage is lightly caramelised.
---

At Christmas dinner it always sits next to the grey peas.
