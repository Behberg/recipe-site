---
title: "Cream of garlic soup with croutons"
description: "A mild, creamy garlic soup in which the garlic turns sweet and nutty. A surprisingly delicate winter soup with crunchy croutons."
steps:
  - "Separate and peel the garlic cloves."
  - "Soften the onion and garlic gently in butter for 10 minutes, until soft and golden but not browned."
  - "Add the diced potatoes, thyme and stock. Simmer for 20 minutes."
  - "Remove the thyme, blend until smooth and stir in the cream, salt and pepper."
  - "Serve with croutons."
tips:
  - "Slow cooking is the key: the garlic loses its bite and turns sweet."
---

Two whole heads of garlic in one pot sound like too much, but slowly cooked they turn as soft as chestnuts.
