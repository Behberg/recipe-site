---
title: "Chicken stroganoff with mushrooms"
description: "Quick chicken stroganoff: strips of chicken with mushrooms and onion in a sour cream and mustard sauce. Ready in 30 minutes, great with mash."
steps:
  - "Slice the chicken into thin strips and season with salt, pepper and paprika."
  - "Fry the chicken in oil over a high heat for 4 minutes. Set aside."
  - "In the same pan fry the onion and sliced mushrooms in butter for 7 minutes."
  - "Stir in the sour cream and mustard, return the chicken and simmer for 3 minutes without boiling."
  - "Sprinkle with parsley and serve with mash, rice or pasta."
tips:
  - "Do not boil the sour cream hard or it may split. Hot is enough."
---

Classic stroganoff uses beef, but the chicken version is quicker, cheaper and just as good on a weeknight.
