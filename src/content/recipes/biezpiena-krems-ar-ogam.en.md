---
title: Cottage cheese cream with berries
description: An airy cottage cheese and cream dessert with berries and honey. Ready in 5 minutes.
steps:
  - Blend or whisk the cottage cheese with the cream, honey and vanilla sugar until smooth.
  - Layer the cream and berries in glasses.
  - Serve straight away or chilled.
---

With strawberries in summer and with jam in winter. Always quick.
