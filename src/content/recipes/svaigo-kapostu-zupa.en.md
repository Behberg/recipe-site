---
title: "Fresh cabbage soup with pork"
description: "Classic fresh cabbage soup with pork, potatoes and carrots. Easy to make, cheap and feeds the whole family."
steps:
  - "Cut the pork into pieces, cover with the water, bring to the boil, skim and simmer for 40 minutes with the bay leaf."
  - "Shred the cabbage finely and dice the potatoes."
  - "Add the cabbage and potatoes to the broth and simmer for 15 minutes."
  - "In a frying pan, fry the onion and grated carrot, then add the tomato paste. Tip into the soup and simmer for another 10 minutes."
  - "Season with salt, pepper and dill. Serve with sour cream."
tips:
  - "Young summer cabbage cooks faster: add it together with the carrots."
---

In autumn, when the garden is full of firm cabbage heads, this soup is on the stove almost every week.
