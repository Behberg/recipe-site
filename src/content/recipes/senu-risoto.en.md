---
title: Mushroom risotto
description: A creamy Italian risotto with button or wild mushrooms, parmesan and butter.
steps:
  - Slice the mushrooms and fry them in half the butter until golden. Set aside.
  - In a pan, fry the onion and garlic in olive oil until soft.
  - Add the rice and stir for 2 minutes, until it turns translucent.
  - Pour in the wine and stir until it has evaporated.
  - Add the hot stock a ladle at a time, stirring each time until it is absorbed. This takes about 18 minutes.
  - When the rice is al dente, stir in the mushrooms, the rest of the butter and the parmesan. Cover for 2 minutes and serve.
---

The secret is patience: risotto must not be left unattended.
