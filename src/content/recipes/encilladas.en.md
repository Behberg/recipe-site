---
title: Chicken enchiladas
description: Tortillas filled with chicken and cheese, baked in a spicy tomato sauce under melted cheese.
steps:
  - For the sauce, fry half the onion and the garlic, add the tomato sauce and spices. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  - Shred the chicken and mix it with the rest of the chopped onion, half the cheese and a third of the sauce.
  - Fill each tortilla, roll it up and lay it seam side down in a dish.
  - Pour over the rest of the sauce and sprinkle with cheese.
  - Bake in the oven at 190 °C for 20 minutes. Serve with sour cream and coriander.
---

A great way to use up yesterday’s roast chicken.
