---
title: "Carrot and apple salad with raisins"
description: "Sweet, crunchy carrot and apple salad with raisins, walnuts and a yoghurt honey dressing. A vitamin bomb children happily eat."
steps:
  - "Soak the raisins in warm water for 10 minutes."
  - "Coarsely grate the carrots and apple. Sprinkle the apple with lemon juice straight away."
  - "Mix the yoghurt with the honey."
  - "Toss everything with the drained raisins and chopped nuts."
tips:
  - "Vegetarian and free of refined sugar: the sweetness comes from the apple and raisins."
---

For many children this was the first salad they actually liked. Sweet, colourful and crunchy.
