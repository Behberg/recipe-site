---
title: Chimichurri sauce
description: Argentina’s green sauce with parsley, garlic, oregano and vinegar. A must with grilled meat.
steps:
  - Chop the parsley and garlic very finely with a knife (a blender turns them into a purée).
  - Mix with the oregano, chilli, vinegar, salt and pepper.
  - Stir in the olive oil.
  - Leave to infuse for at least 30 minutes. Serve with steak, shashlik or grilled vegetables.
---

In Argentina a barbecue without chimichurri is not a real barbecue.
