---
title: Khinkali
description: Large Georgian dumplings with a juicy meat filling and broth inside. Eaten with your hands.
yield: about 20 khinkali
steps:
  - For the dough, mix the flour, salt and water and knead for 10 minutes into a firm dough. Let it rest for 30 minutes.
  - Mix the meat with the finely grated onion, coriander and spices. Gradually work in the cold water until the filling is very juicy.
  - Roll out the dough and cut out 12 cm rounds.
  - Put a tablespoon of filling in the middle, gather the edges up in pleats and pinch them tightly together to form a “cap”.
  - Boil in salted water for 10 to 12 minutes.
  - Serve with black pepper. Eat holding the cap, sucking out the broth first.
tips:
  - "Do not eat the cap: counting the caps left on the plate tells you how many khinkali were eaten."
---

Khinkali have their own rules: no forks or knives, and the broth must not spill.
