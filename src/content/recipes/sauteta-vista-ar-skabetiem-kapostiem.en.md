---
title: "Braised chicken with sauerkraut"
description: "Tender chicken thighs braised with sauerkraut, carrot, caraway and honey. A simple, warming winter dish with potatoes."
steps:
  - "Season the chicken and brown the skin in oil in a deep pan. Set aside."
  - "In the same pan fry the onion and grated carrot for 5 minutes."
  - "Add the sauerkraut, caraway, bay leaf, honey and water. Stir."
  - "Lay the chicken on top skin side up, cover and braise gently for 40 minutes."
  - "Serve with boiled potatoes."
tips:
  - "The honey softens the sourness of the sauerkraut and deepens the flavour of the sauce."
---

When there is a barrel of sauerkraut in the cellar in winter, this dish is on the table regularly. Cheap, warm and filling.
