---
title: "Vegetable stew with potatoes and courgettes"
description: "Colourful vegetable stew with potatoes, courgette, aubergine and peppers in tomato sauce. Vegan, cheap and perfect for the summer harvest."
steps:
  - "Cut all the vegetables into cubes of a similar size."
  - "In a large pan fry the onions and carrots in oil for 5 minutes."
  - "Add the potatoes and peppers, fry for 5 more minutes and add the paprika."
  - "Add the aubergine, courgette, garlic and tomatoes with their juice. Season with salt and pepper."
  - "Cover and simmer gently for 30 minutes, until the potatoes are soft. Sprinkle with parsley."
tips:
  - "The stew is even better the next day, once the flavours have come together."
---

At the end of August, when everything in the garden ripens at once, this pot brings it all together.
