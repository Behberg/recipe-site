---
title: "Rocket salad with pear, blue cheese and walnuts"
description: "An elegant salad of peppery rocket, sweet pear, salty blue cheese, toasted walnuts and a honey balsamic dressing."
steps:
  - "Toast the walnuts in a dry pan for 3 minutes."
  - "Slice the pear thinly."
  - "Whisk the oil with the balsamic and honey."
  - "Arrange the rocket, pear, crumbled cheese and nuts on a plate. Drizzle with the dressing."
tips:
  - "You can also fry the pear in butter so it caramelises."
---

Sweet, salty and bitter in one bite. This salad works for weekdays and festive tables alike.
