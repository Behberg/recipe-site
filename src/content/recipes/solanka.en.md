---
title: Solyanka
description: A rich, tangy meat soup with smoked meats, pickled cucumbers, olives and lemon.
steps:
  - Cut the smoked meats into strips and dice the pickles small.
  - In a pan, fry the onion, add the smoked meats and fry for 5 minutes.
  - Add the pickles and tomato paste and cook for 5 minutes.
  - Pour in the hot stock and pickle brine. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  - Add the olives and capers and simmer for another 5 minutes.
  - Serve with a slice of lemon, sour cream and dill.
---

The perfect way to use up the smoked meats left over after a celebration.
