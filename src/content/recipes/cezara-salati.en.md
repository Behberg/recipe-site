---
title: Chicken Caesar salad
description: Romaine lettuce with grilled chicken, croutons, parmesan and a creamy Caesar dressing.
steps:
  - Season the chicken with salt and pepper and fry in a pan for 5 to 6 minutes on each side. Let it rest and slice.
  - Cut the bread into cubes and toast in a pan with a little oil.
  - For the dressing, blend the yolk, garlic, mustard, lemon and anchovies. Pour in the oil in a thin stream until the dressing is thick.
  - Tear the lettuce leaves and toss them with the dressing.
  - Top with the chicken, croutons and shavings of parmesan.
---
