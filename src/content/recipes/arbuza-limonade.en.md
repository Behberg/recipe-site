---
title: "Watermelon lemonade with lime and mint"
description: "Refreshing watermelon lemonade with lime and mint, blended in 10 minutes. A pink summer drink with nothing extra."
steps:
  - "Blend the seedless watermelon flesh until smooth."
  - "Strain through a sieve into a jug."
  - "Stir in the lime juice and honey."
  - "Just before serving add the sparkling water, ice and mint."
tips:
  - "Freeze watermelon cubes and use them instead of ice."
---

When a watermelon is too big to eat, it becomes the best lemonade of the summer.
