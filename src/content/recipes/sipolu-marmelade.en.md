---
title: "Red onion marmalade with balsamic"
description: "Sweet, sticky red onion marmalade with red wine and balsamic vinegar. Wonderful with cheese, burgers and roast meat."
yield: "about 2 jars of 0.3 l"
steps:
  - "Slice the onions into thin half rings."
  - "Melt the butter with the oil in a wide pan, add the onions and salt. Cook gently for 25 minutes, stirring, until soft and golden."
  - "Add the sugar and thyme leaves and caramelise for 10 minutes."
  - "Pour in the wine and vinegar. Simmer gently for 15 minutes, until the liquid has almost evaporated and it is sticky."
  - "Fill sterilised jars while hot. Keeps in the fridge for up to a month."
tips:
  - "Do not rush: slow caramelising over a low heat is the whole key to the flavour."
---

A jar of onion marmalade makes a great gift. And a cheese board never feels complete without it again.
