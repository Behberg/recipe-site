---
title: "Quick kefir soda bread (no yeast)"
description: "Quick bread made with kefir and bicarbonate of soda, no yeast and no rising. From bowl to oven in 10 minutes, great with butter and soup."
yield: "1 loaf"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C and line a baking tray with paper."
  - "In a bowl mix the flour, bicarbonate of soda, salt and sugar."
  - "Pour in the kefir and bring together quickly with a spoon, then your hands, into a soft, sticky dough. Do not knead for long."
  - "Shape into a round loaf, sprinkle with oats and cut a deep cross in the top with a knife."
  - "Bake for 35 to 40 minutes, until the crust is brown and the loaf sounds hollow when tapped underneath."
  - "Cool wrapped in a tea towel so the crust stays soft."
tips:
  - "The soda starts working straight away, so get the dough into the oven without delay."
---

This is the bread for when there is no bread in the house and no time either. Half an hour later there is a warm loaf on the table.
