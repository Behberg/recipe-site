---
title: "Cream cheese and garlic stuffed mushrooms"
description: "Oven baked mushrooms stuffed with cream cheese, garlic and parmesan with a crisp topping. A party starter, ready in 35 minutes."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C. Remove the mushroom stalks and chop them finely."
  - "Fry the stalks in oil with the crushed garlic for 3 minutes."
  - "Mix with the cream cheese, half the parmesan, parsley, salt and pepper."
  - "Fill the mushroom caps and sprinkle with the rest of the parmesan and the breadcrumbs."
  - "Bake for 15 to 20 minutes, until the mushrooms are tender and the tops golden."
tips:
  - "Wipe mushrooms with damp paper rather than rinsing them, so they do not soak up water."
---

Stuffed mushrooms look festive and can be prepared ahead, then popped in the oven just before guests arrive.
