---
title: Quick ramen with egg
description: A filling noodle soup with a rich broth, marinated egg, mushrooms and spring onions.
steps:
  - Boil the eggs for exactly 6.5 minutes, cool them in iced water and peel. Soak them in a tablespoon of soy sauce with a little water.
  - Bring the stock to the boil with grated ginger and garlic and simmer for 10 minutes.
  - Add the sliced mushrooms and the rest of the soy sauce and simmer for 5 minutes.
  - Take off the heat and dissolve the miso paste in it (do not boil it again).
  - Cook the noodles separately and divide them between bowls. Pour over the broth.
  - Top with the egg halves, spring onions, strips of nori and a drop of sesame oil.
---

A real ramen broth takes 12 hours. This is the quick version for a weeknight.
