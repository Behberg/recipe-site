---
title: "Riga Black Balsam and blackcurrant cocktail"
description: "The most popular Riga Black Balsam cocktail, with blackcurrant juice and ice. A Latvian classic made in 3 minutes."
steps:
  - "Fill a glass with ice."
  - "Pour in the balsam and blackcurrant juice."
  - "Squeeze in a little lime juice and stir."
  - "Garnish with a wedge of lime or a few blackcurrants."
tips:
  - "Balsam is just as often mixed with cranberry or cherry juice, or added to hot tea with honey."
---

Riga Black Balsam has been made since the 18th century to a secret herbal recipe. With blackcurrant juice it becomes softer and easy to love.
