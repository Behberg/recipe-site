---
title: Çılbır (eggs on yogurt)
description: Poached eggs on garlic yogurt, drizzled with hot paprika butter. A Turkish breakfast treat.
steps:
  - Mix the yogurt with the crushed garlic and a pinch of salt and divide between bowls at room temperature.
  - Bring the water with the vinegar to a gentle simmer. Break each egg into a cup and slide it carefully into the water. Cook for 3 minutes.
  - Lift the eggs out with a slotted spoon and place them on the yogurt.
  - Melt the butter with the paprika and chilli until it turns red.
  - Pour over the eggs, sprinkle with dill and serve with bread.
---

A dish from the court of the Ottoman sultans that is now a favourite breakfast.
