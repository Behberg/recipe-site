---
title: Dandelion honey
description: A golden syrup made from dandelion flowers and lemon. It tastes of spring and sunshine.
yield: about 1 litre
steps:
  - Pick the flowers in the middle of a sunny day, far from roads. Pluck off only the yellow petals or cut away the green stems.
  - Cover the flowers with the water, add the sliced lemon and boil for 15 minutes.
  - Leave to infuse overnight.
  - Strain through muslin and squeeze well.
  - Add the sugar and simmer over a low heat for 60 to 90 minutes, until it is as thick as honey.
  - Pour the hot syrup into jars and seal.
---

A vegan “honey” that has been made in Latvia for generations.
