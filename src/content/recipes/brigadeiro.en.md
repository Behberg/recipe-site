---
title: Brigadeiros
description: Brazilian chocolate caramel truffles made from condensed milk. Only 4 ingredients.
yield: about 20 truffles
steps:
  - In a small pan, mix the condensed milk, cocoa and butter.
  - Heat over a low heat, stirring constantly, for 10 to 15 minutes, until the mixture thickens and comes away from the bottom of the pan.
  - Transfer to an oiled plate and let it cool.
  - With oiled hands, roll small balls and coat them in sprinkles.
  - Put them in paper sweet cases.
---

No Brazilian birthday party is complete without brigadeiros.
