---
title: "Rhubarb meringue cake"
description: "Shortcrust cake with tart rhubarb and a sweet, crisp meringue topping. The star of the spring coffee table."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C. Beat the butter with the sugar, beat in the yolks and stir in the flour with the baking powder."
  - "Press the dough into the base of a 24 cm tin with small edges."
  - "Cover with the peeled rhubarb cut into 1 cm pieces. Bake for 25 minutes."
  - "Whisk the egg whites, adding the sugar a spoonful at a time, until stiff and glossy."
  - "Spread the meringue over the rhubarb in swirls and bake for 15 to 20 minutes more, until golden."
tips:
  - "Let the cake cool in the tin: warm, it is fragile and hard to cut."
---

Rhubarb is the first thing you can harvest from the garden in spring. Sweet meringue is the perfect partner for its tartness.
