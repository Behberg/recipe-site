---
title: "Panzanella: Tuscan bread and tomato salad"
description: "A summer salad of day old bread and sweet tomatoes. The bread soaks up the tomato juice and olive oil."
steps:
  - "Tear the bread into bite sized pieces, drizzle with 2 tablespoons of the oil and toast in a 180 °C oven for 8 to 10 minutes."
  - "Cut the tomatoes into chunks, sprinkle with salt and leave for 10 minutes to release their juice."
  - "Mix the remaining oil with the vinegar, crushed garlic and pepper."
  - "In a bowl combine the bread, the tomatoes with all their juice, the chopped cucumber, thin slices of onion and the capers. Pour over the dressing."
  - "Leave for 15 minutes, add the torn basil and serve."
---

This dish needs summer tomatoes: in winter it is better to cook something else.
