---
title: "Fluffy kefir pancakes"
description: "Thick, fluffy kefir pancakes just like grandma’s. Cheap, simple and lovely with jam, honey or sour cream."
steps:
  - "Warm the kefir slightly to room temperature."
  - "Stir in the egg, sugar, salt and bicarbonate of soda. Wait 2 minutes until bubbles appear."
  - "Stir in the flour to a thick batter that slides slowly off a spoon. Do not overmix."
  - "Heat oil in a frying pan and spoon in small pancakes."
  - "Cook covered over a medium heat for 2 minutes, flip and cook for 2 minutes more."
  - "Serve warm with jam or sour cream."
tips:
  - "Warm kefir and a lid on the pan are the secret to tall, fluffy pancakes."
---

These pancakes are the way to use up kefir near its date. That is usually exactly why they get made.
