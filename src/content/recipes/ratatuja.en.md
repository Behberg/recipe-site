---
title: Ratatouille
description: A Provençal vegetable stew with aubergines, courgettes, peppers and tomatoes.
steps:
  - Cut all the vegetables into roughly 2 cm cubes.
  - In a large pan, fry the aubergine, courgettes and peppers separately in olive oil until lightly browned. Put them into a pot.
  - In the same pan, fry the onion and garlic, add the chopped tomatoes and cook for 5 minutes.
  - Add the tomatoes to the other vegetables and add the thyme, salt and pepper.
  - Simmer, covered, over a low heat for 30 minutes. Serve warm or lukewarm with basil.
tips:
  - Frying each vegetable separately keeps its own flavour and stops it stewing into a mush.
---

A peasant dish from Nice that tastes best at the end of summer.
