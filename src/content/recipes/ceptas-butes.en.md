---
title: "Pan fried Baltic flounder with new potatoes"
description: "Fresh Baltic Sea flounder dusted in flour and fried in butter until golden. A seaside summer classic with potatoes and dill."
steps:
  - "Cut off the heads and fins, gut, rinse and pat the fish dry. Score the skin in a few places."
  - "Season with salt and pepper and dredge in flour."
  - "Heat the oil with half the butter in a large frying pan."
  - "Fry the flounder for 4 to 5 minutes on each side, until golden and the flesh lifts easily off the bone."
  - "Add the remaining butter at the end and spoon it over the fish. Serve with potatoes, dill and lemon."
tips:
  - "Buy flounder from fishermen at seaside markets early in the morning."
---

Fried flounder with dill is the taste of summer in the coastal villages from Kolka to Nīca.
