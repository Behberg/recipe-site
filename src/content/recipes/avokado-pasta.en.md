---
title: "Creamy avocado pasta"
description: "A green, silky sauce of avocado, basil and lemon. Ready while the spaghetti cooks."
steps:
  - "Cook the spaghetti in salted water according to the packet. Save half a glass of the cooking water."
  - "Blend the avocado, garlic, lemon juice, basil and olive oil into a smooth sauce. Season with salt and pepper."
  - "Toss the drained spaghetti with the sauce, adding a little cooking water until the sauce is silky."
  - "Stir in the halved cherry tomatoes."
  - "Serve straight away with chilli flakes and basil leaves."
---

Eat it straight away: like the avocado itself, the sauce darkens as it stands.
