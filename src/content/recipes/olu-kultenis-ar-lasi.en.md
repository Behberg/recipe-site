---
title: Scrambled eggs with smoked salmon
description: Creamy scrambled eggs with smoked salmon, chives and toast. A special breakfast in bed.
steps:
  - Lightly beat the eggs with the cream and a pinch of salt.
  - Melt the butter in a pan over a low heat and pour in the eggs.
  - Stir slowly with a spatula from the edges to the middle until large, soft curds form. Take off the heat while the eggs are still slightly wet.
  - Toast the bread.
  - Top the toast with the eggs and salmon and sprinkle with chives and pepper.
---

The whole secret is a low heat: then the eggs will be creamy.
