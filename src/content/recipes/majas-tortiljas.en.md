---
title: "Homemade flour tortillas without yeast"
description: "Soft flour tortillas from five ingredients, cooked in a dry pan. Far tastier than shop bought ones and ready in half an hour."
steps:
  - "Mix the flour with the baking powder and salt, then add the oil and hot water."
  - "Knead for 3 minutes until smooth and soft. Cover and rest for 15 minutes."
  - "Divide into 8 balls and roll each thinly into a 20 cm round."
  - "Cook in a hot dry pan for 30 to 40 seconds on each side until brown spots appear."
  - "Stack the finished tortillas under a tea towel to keep them soft."
tips:
  - "Cooled tortillas can be frozen with a sheet of parchment between each one."
---

When you make tortillas yourself, they suddenly become the star of dinner rather than just the wrapping.
