---
title: Greek lemon potatoes
description: Oven roasted potatoes with lemon, garlic and oregano. Soft inside, golden outside.
steps:
  - Peel the potatoes and cut them into large wedges.
  - Mix the olive oil, lemon juice, crushed garlic, oregano and salt.
  - Put the potatoes in a dish and pour over the dressing and the stock.
  - Roast in the oven at 200 °C for 50 to 60 minutes, turning halfway, until the liquid has been absorbed and the potatoes are golden.
---

The potatoes soak up the lemony stock and become unusually juicy. A great side for chicken and fish.
