---
title: Pizza Margherita
description: The classic Neapolitan pizza with tomato sauce, mozzarella and fresh basil on a thin, crisp base.
yield: 2 large pizzas
steps:
  - Mix the flour with the yeast and salt and add the water and olive oil. Knead for 10 minutes, until the dough is smooth and elastic.
  - Cover and leave to rise in a warm place for 1 hour, until doubled.
  - Heat the oven to its highest temperature (250 °C) with a tray or pizza stone inside.
  - Divide the dough in two and stretch each piece by hand into a thin round, leaving a thicker rim.
  - Spread with tomato sauce, salt, scatter over torn mozzarella and drizzle with a little olive oil.
  - Bake for 8 to 12 minutes, until the edges are brown and the cheese is bubbling. Scatter with basil before serving.
tips:
  - "You can make the dough the night before and keep it in the fridge: the flavour will be even better."
---

The pizza was named in honour of Queen Margherita: its red, white and green recall the Italian flag.
