---
title: "Sheet pan baked salmon with vegetables"
description: "Juicy salmon with potatoes, broccoli and tomatoes, roasted in one tray with lemon and dill. A healthy dinner with hardly any washing up."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 210 °C."
  - "Halve the potatoes, toss with half the oil and some salt, and roast for 15 minutes."
  - "Cut the broccoli into florets and the onion into wedges. Add to the tray with the tomatoes."
  - "Cut the salmon into 4 pieces and nestle among the vegetables. Drizzle with the rest of the oil, crush over the garlic and season with salt, pepper and lemon zest."
  - "Roast for 12 to 14 minutes, until the salmon flakes easily."
  - "Squeeze over the lemon juice and scatter with dill."
tips:
  - "Take the salmon out while the centre is still slightly translucent so it stays juicy."
---

One tray, half an hour and almost no washing up. This recipe is made for tired weekday evenings.
