---
title: "Apple charlotte (easy egg sponge apple cake)"
description: "Airy apple charlotte made from eggs, sugar and flour with lots of apples and cinnamon. A classic that works with 4 ingredients and 15 minutes of work."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C and butter a 24 cm round tin."
  - "Peel, core and slice the apples. Arrange them in the tin and sprinkle with cinnamon."
  - "Whisk the eggs with the sugar and vanilla sugar for 7 minutes, until pale and tripled."
  - "Gently fold in the sifted flour so the mixture does not deflate."
  - "Pour over the apples and bake for 40 to 45 minutes until golden. Dust with icing sugar once cool."
tips:
  - "There is no butter or baking powder in the batter: the lightness comes only from well whisked eggs."
---

Charlotte is the cake you bake when apples need using and time is short. It always works and always disappears.
