---
title: "Berry smoothie with banana and yoghurt"
description: "A thick, creamy berry smoothie with banana and yoghurt in 5 minutes. A vitamin rich breakfast or snack you can take with you."
steps:
  - "Put the frozen berries, banana, yoghurt, milk and honey in a blender."
  - "Blend for 1 minute until completely smooth."
  - "If it is too thick, add a little more milk."
  - "Pour into glasses and drink straight away."
tips:
  - "Frozen berries make the smoothie cold and thick without ice."
---

In winter, frozen forest berries are a small piece of summer. In a smoothie they come back in full colour.
