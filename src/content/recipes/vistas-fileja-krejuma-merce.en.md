---
title: "Creamy garlic chicken breast"
description: "Juicy chicken breast in a silky cream, garlic and cheese sauce with spinach. A quick one pan dinner in 30 minutes."
steps:
  - "Cut the chicken into bite sized pieces and season with salt and pepper."
  - "Heat the oil and butter in a frying pan and brown the chicken for 6 to 7 minutes until golden. Set aside on a plate."
  - "In the same pan fry the chopped onion for 3 minutes, add the garlic and cook for 1 more minute."
  - "Pour in the cream, add the cheese and simmer for 3 minutes until the sauce thickens."
  - "Return the chicken, add the spinach and simmer for 3 minutes. Serve with pasta, rice or potatoes."
tips:
  - "Sun dried tomatoes or mushrooms are good additions to the sauce."
---

This is the dinner that saves an ordinary weekday. The sauce is so good that bread gets used to wipe the plate clean.
