---
title: "Banana muffins with nuts or chocolate"
description: "Moist, fragrant banana muffins from overripe bananas with cinnamon and nuts or chocolate. One bowl, 30 minutes."
yield: "12 muffins"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C and line a muffin tin with paper cases."
  - "Mash the bananas with a fork and whisk in the eggs, sugar and oil."
  - "Stir in the flour, bicarbonate of soda and cinnamon until no flour is visible."
  - "Fold in the nuts or chocolate and divide between the cases."
  - "Bake for 20 to 22 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean."
tips:
  - "The darker the banana skins, the sweeter and more fragrant the muffins."
---

Brown bananas in the fruit bowl are not a problem but a reason to bake. These muffins are that reason.
