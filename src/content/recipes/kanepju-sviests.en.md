---
title: "Latvian hemp butter on rye bread"
description: "Traditional Latvian hemp butter made from toasted hemp seeds and butter. A nutty, filling spread for rye bread and potatoes."
steps:
  - "Toast the hemp seeds in a dry pan over a medium heat for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring, until they crackle and smell nutty."
  - "While still warm, grind them in a mortar or blender until they release their oil and become a fine, moist paste."
  - "Add the salt and soft butter and mix to an even spread."
  - "Serve on rye bread or with boiled potatoes."
tips:
  - "In the old days hemp butter was made with milk or water rather than butter. Butter makes it milder."
---

Hemp was one of the most important plants in old Latvian cooking. Hemp butter is back in fashion and no longer just grandma’s food.
