---
title: Fresh rice paper rolls
description: Translucent rice paper rolls with prawns, noodles, vegetables and a peanut dipping sauce.
yield: 12 rolls
steps:
  - Cook the noodles and rinse them in cold water. Cut the carrot and cucumber into thin strips. Halve the prawns.
  - Dip a rice paper sheet in warm water for 5 seconds and lay it on a damp board.
  - Put the prawns on one side, then top with lettuce, noodles, vegetables and herbs.
  - Fold in the sides and roll up tightly.
  - For the sauce, mix the peanut butter, soy sauce, lime juice, honey and a little water.
---

The freshest starter you can imagine. Perfect on a hot summer day.
