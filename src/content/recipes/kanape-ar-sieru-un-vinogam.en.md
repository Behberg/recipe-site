---
title: "Cheese and grape canapés with honey and walnuts"
description: "The simplest party canapés: cubes of cheese with grapes, walnuts and honey on sticks. A no cook starter for New Year and parties."
steps:
  - "Cut the cheese into 2 cm cubes."
  - "Thread a grape, a basil leaf and a cube of cheese onto each cocktail stick."
  - "Arrange on a plate."
  - "Just before serving drizzle with honey and sprinkle with chopped walnuts."
tips:
  - "Try different cheeses: gouda, cheddar, brie or blue cheese with red and green grapes."
---

Canapés are a starter you can make with children in 15 minutes, and they look like they came from a caterer.
