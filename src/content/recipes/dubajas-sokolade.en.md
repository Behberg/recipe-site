---
title: "Dubai chocolate with pistachio and kataifi"
description: "The famous chocolate bar with a crunchy kataifi and pistachio cream filling. You can make it at home without special moulds."
yield: "2 large bars"
steps:
  - "Cut or tear the kataifi into 1 cm pieces. Melt the butter in a pan and toast the kataifi over medium heat for 6 minutes, stirring all the time, until golden and crisp. Leave to cool."
  - "Mix the cooled kataifi with the pistachio cream, tahini and salt."
  - "Melt half the chocolate over a pan of simmering water or in the microwave in 20 second bursts, stirring each time."
  - "Spread the chocolate thinly with a spoon in a silicone mould or on baking paper as two rectangles. Chill for 10 minutes."
  - "Spread the filling over the set chocolate, leaving a 5 mm border. Melt the rest of the chocolate, pour it over the filling and smooth it out."
  - "Chill for 1 hour until completely set. Cut with a hot knife."
tips:
  - "Kataifi is finely shredded filo pastry. Look for it in Middle Eastern shops or in the freezer section of large supermarkets."
  - "No pistachio cream? Blend 120 g pistachios with 2 tablespoons of oil and 2 tablespoons of icing sugar."
  - "With dark chocolate the bar is less sweet and goes very well with the filling."
---

Dubai chocolate is one of the biggest internet food trends of recent years. It is all about contrast: smooth chocolate around a crunchy, salty-sweet pistachio filling.
