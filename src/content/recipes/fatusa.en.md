---
title: Fattoush salad
description: A Lebanese vegetable salad with crisp pieces of pitta, mint and a lemon dressing.
steps:
  - Tear the pitta into pieces, drizzle with olive oil and toast in the oven or a pan until crisp.
  - Cut the vegetables into largish pieces and the radishes into slices, and roughly tear the herbs.
  - For the dressing, whisk the lemon juice, olive oil, crushed garlic, sumac and salt.
  - Toss the vegetables with the dressing and stir in the pitta pieces just before serving.
---

A salad born from the wish to use up yesterday’s bread.
