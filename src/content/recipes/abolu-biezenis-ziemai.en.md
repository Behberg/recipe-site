---
title: "Homemade apple purée for winter"
description: "Smooth homemade apple purée with very little sugar, preserved in jars for winter. A children’s favourite with pancakes, curd cheese and porridge."
yield: "about 6 jars of 0.5 l"
steps:
  - "Peel, core and chop the apples."
  - "Put in a pan with the water, cover and simmer for 20 minutes until completely soft."
  - "Blend until smooth or press through a sieve."
  - "Add the sugar and cinnamon and cook for 10 more minutes, stirring often as it splutters."
  - "Fill sterilised jars while hot, seal and turn upside down."
tips:
  - "With sweet apples you can leave out the sugar entirely: the purée is naturally sweet."
---

In autumn, when apples fall faster than you can gather them, jars of purée are the best way to keep the harvest.
