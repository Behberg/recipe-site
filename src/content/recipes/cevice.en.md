---
title: Ceviche
description: A Peruvian fresh fish starter, “cooked” in lime juice with red onion, chilli and coriander.
steps:
  - Boil the sweet potatoes, peel and slice them into rounds.
  - Cut the fish into 1.5 cm cubes, salt and put them in a cold glass bowl.
  - Pour over freshly squeezed lime juice so the fish is covered.
  - Add the thinly sliced onion and finely chopped chilli. Leave in the fridge for 10 to 15 minutes, until the fish turns opaque.
  - Sprinkle with coriander and serve straight away with the sweet potatoes.
tips:
  - Use only the freshest fish from a seller you trust.
---

Fresh, tangy and spicy. In Peru it is eaten for lunch by the sea.
