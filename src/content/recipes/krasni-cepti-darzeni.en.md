---
title: "Oven roasted vegetables with garlic and rosemary"
description: "Caramelised oven roasted vegetables with sweet potato, carrots, pepper and garlic. A simple, colourful side or vegetarian dinner."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 210 °C."
  - "Cut the sweet potato and carrots into chunks, the pepper and courgette into pieces and the onions into wedges."
  - "In a large tray toss the vegetables with the oil, salt, pepper and rosemary. Add the garlic cloves in their skins."
  - "Spread in one layer and roast for 20 minutes. Add the tomatoes, stir and roast for 15 minutes more."
  - "Squeeze the soft garlic out of its skins and toss with the vegetables."
tips:
  - "Vegetables need space: if the tray is crowded they steam instead of browning."
---

Roasting turns even carrots into something children ask for more of.
