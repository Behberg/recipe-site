---
title: German potato salad
description: A warm potato salad with onion, bacon and a vinegar dressing. No mayonnaise.
steps:
  - Boil the potatoes in their skins, peel them while still warm and slice.
  - Fry the bacon until crisp. In the same pan, fry the chopped onion.
  - Add the stock, vinegar, mustard and sugar and bring to the boil.
  - Pour the hot dressing over the warm potatoes, add the oil and mix. Leave for 20 minutes to soak in.
  - Add the bacon and spring onions and season with salt and pepper.
---

In southern Germany potato salad is made with stock, not mayonnaise. It is lighter and juicier.
