---
title: "Kimbap (Korean seaweed rice rolls)"
description: "Korean kimbap: nori rolls with sesame rice, egg omelette, carrot, spinach and cucumber. A colourful snack for picnics and lunchboxes."
steps:
  - "Cook the rice and while still warm mix with the sesame oil and salt."
  - "Beat the eggs and cook into a thin omelette, then cut into strips. Fry the carrot strips for 2 minutes. Blanch the spinach for 30 seconds and squeeze dry. Cut the cucumber into long strips."
  - "Spread a thin layer of rice over a nori sheet, leaving 3 cm clear at the top."
  - "Lay a row of egg, carrot, spinach, cucumber and crab sticks along the middle. Roll up tightly."
  - "Brush the rolls with sesame oil, sprinkle with sesame and slice with a sharp, wet knife."
tips:
  - "Dip the knife in water before every cut for neat slices."
---

In Korea kimbap goes along on picnics and school trips. It is like a sandwich, only more colourful.
