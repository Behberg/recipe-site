---
title: Bruschetta with tomato and basil
description: Toasted bread with garlic, juicy tomatoes, basil and olive oil.
steps:
  - Dice the tomatoes finely and mix with the chopped basil, olive oil, salt and pepper.
  - Slice the bread and toast it in a pan or under the grill until golden.
  - Rub the hot slices of bread with a cut clove of garlic.
  - Spoon the tomato mixture on top just before serving, so the bread stays crisp.
---

The simplest Italian starter, whose flavour depends only on the quality of the tomatoes.
