---
title: "Nettle soup with egg and sour cream"
description: "Spring nettle soup made with young nettle tops, potatoes, boiled egg and sour cream. Green, cheap and full of vitamins."
steps:
  - "Pick the nettles wearing gloves, taking only the young tops. Wash them and pour boiling water over them for 1 minute so they no longer sting. Chop."
  - "Fry the onion and grated carrot in butter in a pan for 5 minutes."
  - "Add the stock and diced potatoes and simmer for 15 minutes."
  - "Add the nettles and simmer for another 5 minutes. Season with salt and pepper."
  - "Serve with halves of boiled egg and a spoonful of sour cream."
tips:
  - "Pick nettles in May while they are small and tender, away from roads."
---

After the long winter, nettle soup was the first fresh green food on the table. Today people make it for the joy of it.
