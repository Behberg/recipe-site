---
title: "Broccoli and cheese soup"
description: "Velvety, bright green broccoli soup with melted cheese and cream. Simple, healthy and ready in 35 minutes."
steps:
  - "In a pan soften the chopped onion in butter for 5 minutes, then add the garlic."
  - "Add the diced potatoes and stock and simmer for 10 minutes."
  - "Add the broccoli florets and chopped stalks and simmer for 8 minutes more. Do not overcook so it stays green."
  - "Blend until smooth, then stir in the grated cheese and cream. Season with salt and pepper."
  - "Serve with croutons and a little extra cheese on top."
tips:
  - "Peel and chop the broccoli stalk: it is just as tasty as the florets."
---

Broccoli and cheese soup is a way to get children eating vegetables without an argument.
