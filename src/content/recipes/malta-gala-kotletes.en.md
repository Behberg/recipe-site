---
title: Meat patties
description: Juicy homemade patties with onion and bread. Serve with mashed potatoes and cucumbers.
steps:
  - Pour the milk over the bread and leave to soak for 5 minutes, then squeeze it out.
  - Grate the onion finely. Mix the mince, bread, onion, egg, garlic, salt and pepper.
  - "Knead the mixture well: this makes the patties juicier and stops them falling apart."
  - With wet hands, shape into patties and coat them in breadcrumbs.
  - Fry in hot oil over a medium heat for 5 to 6 minutes on each side.
  - Serve with mashed potatoes, braised cabbage or a fresh salad.
---

Grandma’s secret: bread soaked in milk keeps the patties soft every time.
