---
title: "Mujadara: lentils and rice with caramelised onions"
description: "A Middle Eastern everyday dish made from three cheap ingredients. The darkly fried onions give all the flavour."
steps:
  - "Boil the lentils in water for 15 minutes, until half tender. Drain."
  - "Slice the onions into thin half rings and fry in the olive oil over medium heat for 20 to 25 minutes, until deep brown. Set half aside."
  - "Add the cumin, cinnamon, rice and lentils to the onions in the pan and stir."
  - "Pour in 500 ml of water, season with salt and cook covered over low heat for 15 minutes. Leave covered for another 10 minutes."
  - "Serve with the reserved onions, parsley and a wedge of lemon."
---

Do not rush the onions: they turn a simple dish into a feast.
