---
title: "Matcha latte"
description: "A creamy green tea latte that is easy to make at home: hot or iced, with dairy or plant milk."
steps:
  - "Sift the matcha into a mug or small bowl so there are no lumps."
  - "Pour in water at 80 °C (not boiling, or the tea turns bitter) and whisk quickly with a bamboo whisk or small frother for 30 seconds until there is a fine foam on top."
  - "Heat the milk and froth it."
  - "Stir the honey into the matcha and top with the frothy milk."
  - "For an iced latte, fill a glass with ice, pour in cold milk and pour the whisked matcha over the top."
tips:
  - "For drinks choose ceremonial or latte grade matcha: it is bright green, not olive green."
  - "Strawberry matcha latte: put 2 tablespoons of crushed strawberries with honey in the bottom of the glass."
  - "Keep opened matcha in the fridge so it keeps its colour."
---

Matcha latte is still one of the most popular drinks in the world, and in 2025 Google searches were all about strawberry and banana matcha variations.
