---
title: Avgolemono soup
description: A gentle Greek chicken soup with rice, thickened with eggs and lemon.
steps:
  - Simmer the chicken in the stock for 15 minutes. Take it out and shred it.
  - Cook the rice in the stock for 15 minutes, until soft.
  - In a bowl, whisk the eggs with the lemon juice until frothy.
  - Whisking all the time, add 2 ladles of the hot stock to the eggs, one ladle at a time.
  - Pour the egg mixture into the soup and heat gently, stirring, until the soup thickens. Do not let it boil or the eggs will curdle.
  - Return the chicken, season with salt and pepper and serve with dill.
---

Greek grandmothers’ cure for a cold: filling, tangy and very comforting.
