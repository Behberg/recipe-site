---
title: "Creamy potato soup with crispy bacon"
description: "Thick, velvety potato soup with crispy bacon, cheese and chives. Cheap, filling and comforting on cold evenings."
steps:
  - "Cut the bacon into strips and fry in a pan until crisp. Set half aside for garnish."
  - "Fry the onion in the bacon fat for 5 minutes, then add the garlic."
  - "Add the diced potatoes and stock and simmer for 20 minutes."
  - "Blend partly or completely smooth and stir in the cream, salt and pepper."
  - "Serve topped with cheese, the reserved bacon and chives."
tips:
  - "Leave a few chunks of potato unblended for a nicer texture."
---

Potatoes, bacon and cheese are three things that always work together. This soup proves it.
