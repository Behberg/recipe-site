---
title: Chicken biryani
description: Fragrant basmati rice layered with spiced chicken, fried onions and herbs.
steps:
  - Marinate the chicken in the yogurt with the spices, garlic, ginger and salt for at least 1 hour.
  - Slice the onions thinly and fry in oil until deep golden. Soak the saffron in the warm milk.
  - Boil the rice in salted water with the cardamom and cinnamon for 6 minutes (half cooked). Drain.
  - Cook the chicken with its marinade in a heavy based pan for 10 minutes.
  - Layer the rice, onions and herbs on top and pour over the saffron milk and melted butter.
  - Seal tightly with a lid and cook over a very low heat for 25 minutes. Mix gently before serving.
---

Biryani is a festive dish, and every region of India believes its own version is the best.
