---
title: "German beef rouladen with pickle and bacon"
description: "Classic German rouladen: thin slices of beef with mustard, bacon, onion and pickle, slowly braised in a dark red wine gravy."
steps:
  - "Pound the beef slices thin, season with salt and pepper and spread with mustard."
  - "Top each with a slice of bacon, onion slices and a strip of pickle. Roll up tightly and secure with cocktail sticks."
  - "Brown the rolls on all sides in oil in a heavy pan. Set aside."
  - "In the same pan fry the rest of the onion and the carrot, add the tomato paste, then pour in the wine and stock."
  - "Return the rolls, cover and simmer gently for 1.5 to 2 hours. Blend the sauce and serve with potatoes."
tips:
  - "The thinner you pound the meat, the more tender the rolls."
---

In Germany rouladen are Sunday lunch, served with potato dumplings and red cabbage.
