---
title: "Pork stew in paprika and sour cream sauce"
description: "Tender, slow braised pork in a thick paprika, tomato and sour cream sauce. A hearty family dish with potatoes, rice or pasta."
steps:
  - "Cut the pork into 3 cm cubes and brown in batches in oil in a heavy pan."
  - "Add the onions and pepper strips and fry for 5 minutes. Add the garlic."
  - "Stir in the paprika and tomato paste for 1 minute."
  - "Pour in the hot water and season with salt and pepper. Cover and simmer gently for 45 to 50 minutes."
  - "Stir in the sour cream and heat for 5 more minutes without boiling."
tips:
  - "Do not burn the paprika: fry it only briefly, or it turns bitter."
---

This stew is made in a big pot, and the next day it is even better.
