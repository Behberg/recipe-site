---
title: "Chicken and broccoli pasta in cheese sauce"
description: "Hearty pasta with chicken breast, broccoli and a creamy cheese sauce. A quick family dinner that children eat without a fuss."
steps:
  - "Cook the pasta in salted water, adding the broccoli florets for the last 3 minutes. Drain."
  - "Cut the chicken into pieces, season with salt and pepper and fry in oil for 6 minutes."
  - "Add the garlic, then after a minute pour in the cream and add the cheese. Simmer for 2 minutes."
  - "Stir in the pasta and broccoli and add the lemon zest."
  - "Serve straight away, sprinkled with a little more cheese."
tips:
  - "Swap the broccoli for peas or spinach."
---

One pot, one pan and the whole family is fed. That is the real value of this pasta.
