---
title: "Pasta salad with ham and vegetables"
description: "Hearty pasta salad with ham, pickles, pepper and peas. Great for parties, picnics and packed lunches."
steps:
  - "Cook the pasta in salted water, rinse with cold water and drain well."
  - "Dice the ham, pickles and pepper."
  - "Mix the mayonnaise with the mustard, salt and pepper."
  - "Toss everything with the pasta and peas and add the dill."
  - "Chill for 30 minutes before serving."
tips:
  - "If the salad dries out as it stands, stir in a spoonful of pickle brine."
---

Pasta salad is one of the first things students learn to make. Cheap, quick and enough for everyone.
