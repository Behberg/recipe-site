---
title: "Chicken skewers in yoghurt and lemon marinade"
description: "Juicy chicken skewers marinated in yoghurt, garlic and lemon, grilled or pan fried. They never dry out and cook in 15 minutes."
steps:
  - "Cut the chicken into 4 cm pieces."
  - "Mix the yoghurt with crushed garlic, lemon juice and zest, paprika, cumin, oil, salt and pepper."
  - "Toss the chicken in the marinade and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or overnight."
  - "Thread onto skewers, alternating with onion wedges."
  - "Grill over a medium heat for 12 to 15 minutes, turning often, until cooked through and charred at the edges."
tips:
  - "Thigh fillet is juicier than breast and does not dry out on the grill."
---

Yoghurt makes the chicken so tender that even people who only grill pork start to change their minds.
