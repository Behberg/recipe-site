---
title: "Pumpkin and chickpea curry"
description: "An autumn curry with pumpkin, chickpeas and spinach in a coconut milk sauce."
steps:
  - "Fry the chopped onion in the oil for 4 minutes. Add the garlic, grated ginger and curry powder and fry for 1 minute."
  - "Add the diced pumpkin, tomatoes and coconut milk."
  - "Simmer covered for 20 minutes, until the pumpkin is soft."
  - "Add the rinsed chickpeas and the spinach and heat for 5 minutes."
  - "Season with salt and serve with rice."
---

The curry tastes even better the next day.
