---
title: Banh mi sandwich
description: A Vietnamese baguette with marinated pork, quick pickled vegetables, coriander and chilli.
steps:
  - Cut the carrots and cucumber into strips and soak them in vinegar with sugar and a pinch of salt for at least 20 minutes.
  - Slice the meat thinly and marinate it in the soy sauce, fish sauce, honey and garlic for 20 minutes.
  - Fry in a very hot pan for 2 to 3 minutes on each side, until caramelised.
  - Cut the baguettes into portions, split and toast them. Spread with mayonnaise.
  - Fill with the meat, pickled vegetables, coriander and chilli.
---

The French baguette meets Vietnamese flavours. The result is one of the best sandwiches in the world.
