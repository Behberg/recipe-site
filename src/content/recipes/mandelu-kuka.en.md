---
title: "Flourless almond cake (gluten free)"
description: "A moist, nutty almond cake with no wheat flour, with lemon zest and toasted flaked almonds. Simple and gluten free."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 175 °C and line a 22 cm round tin with paper."
  - "Whisk the eggs with the sugar until pale and fluffy."
  - "Fold in the ground almonds, lemon zest and melted butter."
  - "Pour into the tin and scatter with the flaked almonds."
  - "Bake for 35 to 40 minutes. Dust with icing sugar once cool."
tips:
  - "The cake keeps well in the fridge for 4 days and only gets moister."
---

This cake is inspired by Spanish almond tarts. It is so simple you can remember the recipe by heart.
