---
title: "Baked beans in tomato sauce (gigantes)"
description: "A Greek classic: large white beans baked slowly in a thick sauce of tomato, olive oil and herbs."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 190 °C."
  - "Fry the chopped onion, carrot and garlic in the olive oil for 5 minutes."
  - "Add the tomatoes, tomato paste, oregano, sugar, salt and pepper. Simmer for 5 minutes."
  - "Stir in the rinsed beans and half the herbs and transfer to a baking dish."
  - "Bake for 30 minutes, until the sauce thickens and the edges brown lightly. Scatter with the remaining herbs."
---

In Greece this is eaten lukewarm, with bread to mop up the sauce.
