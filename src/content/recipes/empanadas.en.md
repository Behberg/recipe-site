---
title: Argentinian empanadas
description: Baked pasties with beef, onions, olives and egg. A classic of the Argentinian festive table.
yield: about 16 empanadas
steps:
  - Rub the flour, salt and butter into crumbs, add the water and knead into a dough. Chill for 30 minutes.
  - In a pan, fry the onions, add the meat and spices and fry for 10 minutes. Let it cool, then stir in the chopped eggs and olives.
  - Roll the dough out thinly and cut out 12 cm rounds.
  - Put a spoonful of filling on each, fold in half and crimp the edge into a braid or press it with a fork.
  - Brush with egg and bake in the oven at 200 °C for 20 to 25 minutes.
---

In every Argentinian province empanadas look and taste a little different.
