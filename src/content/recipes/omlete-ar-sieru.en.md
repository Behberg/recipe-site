---
title: Cheese and tomato omelette
description: A soft, fluffy omelette with cheese, tomatoes and spring onions. Breakfast in 10 minutes.
steps:
  - Beat the eggs with the milk, salt and pepper.
  - Melt the butter in a pan over a medium heat and pour in the eggs.
  - When the edges start to set, pull them towards the centre with a spatula so the uncooked mixture runs to the edges.
  - While the top is still slightly wet, scatter the cheese, diced tomatoes and spring onions over half the omelette.
  - Fold in half and serve straight away.
---

An omelette does not like a hot pan. A medium heat and patience make it tender.
