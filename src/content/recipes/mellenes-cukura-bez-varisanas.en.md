---
title: "Uncooked bilberries in sugar"
description: "Fresh wild bilberries crushed with sugar without cooking, to keep all their vitamins. Store in the fridge or freezer."
yield: "about 3 jars of 0.5 l"
steps:
  - "Pick over, wash and dry the berries thoroughly."
  - "Crush or blend half the berries with the sugar."
  - "Stir in the remaining whole berries."
  - "Leave for 2 hours, stirring often, until the sugar dissolves."
  - "Fill sterilised jars, sprinkle a spoonful of sugar on top and keep in the fridge or freeze."
tips:
  - "The berries must be completely dry or they will start to ferment."
---

Bilberries in sugar are the taste of the summer forest in winter, with all their vitamins intact.
