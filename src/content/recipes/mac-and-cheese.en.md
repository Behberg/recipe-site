---
title: Mac and cheese
description: Creamy macaroni in a rich cheddar sauce with a crisp topping.
steps:
  - Cook the macaroni for 2 minutes less than it says on the packet.
  - Melt the butter in a pan, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  - Gradually add the milk, stirring, until the sauce thickens.
  - Take off the heat and stir in three quarters of the grated cheese and the mustard. Season with salt and pepper.
  - Mix with the macaroni, put in a dish and top with the rest of the cheese and the breadcrumbs.
  - Bake in the oven at 200 °C for 15 minutes, until the top is golden.
---

America’s favourite comfort food.
