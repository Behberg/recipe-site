---
title: Tiramisu
description: A classic Italian dessert of savoiardi biscuits dipped in coffee with a mascarpone cream.
steps:
  - Whisk the yolks with the sugar into a pale, thick mixture. Stir in the mascarpone.
  - Whisk the whites to stiff peaks and gently fold them into the mascarpone mixture.
  - Cool the coffee and mix it with the liqueur.
  - Dip the biscuits one at a time briefly into the coffee and lay them in a dish.
  - Cover with half the cream, then repeat the layers.
  - Put in the fridge for at least 6 hours. Dust with cocoa before serving.
---

Tiramisu means “pick me up”. Coffee and cream do that beautifully.
