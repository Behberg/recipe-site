---
title: "Garlic green beans with almonds"
description: "Crisp green beans with garlic, toasted almonds and lemon. A healthy side dish ready in 15 minutes."
steps:
  - "Trim the beans. Boil in salted water for 3 minutes, then plunge straight into iced water."
  - "Chop the almonds and toast them in a dry pan."
  - "Heat the oil in a frying pan, add the thinly sliced garlic and cook for 30 seconds."
  - "Add the beans and fry for 3 minutes, stirring."
  - "Squeeze over the lemon juice and add salt, pepper and the almonds."
tips:
  - "The iced water keeps the beans bright green and crisp."
---

This side looks lovely on any plate and is simple enough to make while the meat cooks.
