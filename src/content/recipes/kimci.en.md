---
title: Kimchi
description: Korean fermented Chinese cabbage with chilli, garlic and ginger. Spicy and healthy.
yield: about 2 litres
steps:
  - Cut the cabbage into large pieces, sprinkle with salt and leave for 2 hours, stirring from time to time.
  - Rinse the cabbage thoroughly 3 times in cold water and let it drain.
  - For the paste, blend the garlic, ginger, fish sauce and sugar. Stir in the chilli flakes.
  - Add the sliced spring onions and carrot strips.
  - Wearing gloves, massage the paste into the cabbage.
  - Pack tightly into jars, leaving space at the top. Ferment at room temperature for 2 days, then keep in the fridge.
---

Kimchi gets tastier with every week in the fridge.
