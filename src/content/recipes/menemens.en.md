---
title: Menemen
description: Turkish scrambled eggs with tomatoes, peppers and onions. A breakfast eaten with fresh bread.
steps:
  - Fry the chopped onion and pepper in a pan for 5 minutes.
  - Add the grated or finely chopped tomatoes and cook until most of the liquid has evaporated.
  - Crack in the eggs and stir gently until they are only just set and creamy.
  - Sprinkle with chilli, salt and parsley. Serve straight from the pan with bread.
---

In Turkey there is an eternal debate about whether onion belongs in menemen. We put it in.
