---
title: Apple strudel
description: Thin pastry with apples, raisins, cinnamon and toasted breadcrumbs. A Viennese café classic.
steps:
  - Peel the apples and slice them thinly. Mix with the sugar, cinnamon, raisins and lemon juice.
  - Toast the breadcrumbs in 30 g of the butter until golden. Melt the rest of the butter.
  - Lay the filo sheets on a clean tea towel, brushing each one with butter.
  - Scatter the breadcrumbs along one long edge and put the apples on top. Roll up tightly using the towel.
  - Place on a baking tray seam side down, brush with butter and bake in the oven at 190 °C for 35 to 40 minutes.
  - Dust with icing sugar and serve warm with vanilla sauce or ice cream.
tips:
  - The toasted breadcrumbs soak up the apple juice, so the pastry stays crisp.
---

In Vienna they say that real strudel dough should be so thin you could read a newspaper through it.
