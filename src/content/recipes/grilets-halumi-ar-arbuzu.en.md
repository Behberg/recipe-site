---
title: "Grilled halloumi with watermelon and mint"
description: "Salty grilled halloumi with juicy watermelon, mint and a lime honey dressing. An unexpected but brilliant summer duo."
steps:
  - "Cut the halloumi into 1 cm slices and pat dry."
  - "Cook in a griddle pan or on the grill for 1 to 2 minutes on each side, until golden stripes appear."
  - "Cut the watermelon into cubes or triangles."
  - "Mix the lime juice with the oil and honey."
  - "Arrange the watermelon and halloumi on a plate, drizzle with the dressing and scatter with mint."
tips:
  - "Serve halloumi straight away: it turns rubbery as it cools."
---

Hot salty cheese and cold sweet watermelon sounds odd until you taste it for the first time.
