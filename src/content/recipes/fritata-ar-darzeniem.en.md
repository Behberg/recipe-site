---
title: "Vegetable frittata with feta"
description: "A thick, juicy egg frittata with pepper, spinach and feta, started on the hob and finished in the oven. A great brunch for the family."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 190 °C."
  - "Whisk the eggs with the milk, salt and pepper."
  - "In an ovenproof frying pan fry the onion and pepper in oil for 5 minutes. Add the spinach and let it wilt."
  - "Pour in the eggs and crumble the feta on top. Cook over a medium heat for 3 minutes until the edges start to set."
  - "Transfer to the oven for 12 to 15 minutes, until the centre is set."
  - "Cool for 5 minutes and cut into wedges like a cake."
tips:
  - "A frittata is a good way to use up leftover vegetables and cheese in the fridge."
---

A frittata is an omelette you never have to flip. It is just as good cold and travels well.
