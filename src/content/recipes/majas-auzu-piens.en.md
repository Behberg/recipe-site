---
title: "Homemade oat milk"
description: "Plant milk from oats and water for a few cents. Ready in five minutes, no soaking."
yield: "1 litre"
steps:
  - "Put the oats, ice cold water, dates, salt and vanilla in a blender."
  - "Blend for only 30 to 40 seconds. Blending longer makes the milk slimy."
  - "Strain through a fine sieve or a clean cotton cloth. Do not squeeze, let it drip through."
  - "Pour into a clean bottle and keep in the fridge for up to 4 days."
  - "Shake before use: the sediment is natural."
---

Cold water and a short blend are the two secrets to milk that is not slimy.
