---
title: "Vegetarian bean chilli with sweetcorn"
description: "Thick, spicy vegetarian chilli with two kinds of beans, peppers and sweetcorn. Vegan, cheap, filling and even better the next day."
steps:
  - "In a large pan fry the onion and peppers in oil for 5 minutes."
  - "Add the garlic and spices and cook for 1 minute."
  - "Add the tomatoes, the rinsed beans and the sweetcorn. Season with salt."
  - "Simmer gently for 25 to 30 minutes, stirring, until thick."
  - "Serve with rice, coriander and, if you like, sour cream and avocado."
tips:
  - "A spoonful of cocoa or a square of dark chocolate deepens the flavour."
---

This chilli proves that hearty food can be meat free. Even meat lovers eat it happily.
