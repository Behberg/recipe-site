---
title: Crème brûlée
description: A silky vanilla custard with a crisp caramelised sugar top.
steps:
  - Heat the cream with the vanilla seeds and pod until it just starts to boil. Take off the heat and leave to infuse for 15 minutes.
  - Stir the yolks lightly with the sugar (do not whisk). Pour in the warm cream in a thin stream, stirring.
  - Strain the mixture and pour it into ramekins.
  - Put the ramekins in a roasting tin and pour in hot water halfway up their sides. Bake in the oven at 150 °C for 35 to 40 minutes, until the edges have set but the middle still wobbles slightly.
  - Chill in the fridge for at least 4 hours.
  - Before serving, sprinkle the tops with brown sugar and caramelise with a blowtorch or under a very hot grill.
---

Cracking the sugar crust with a spoon is half the fun.
