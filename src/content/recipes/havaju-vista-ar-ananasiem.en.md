---
title: "Hawaiian chicken with pineapple and cheese"
description: "Chicken breast topped with ham, a pineapple ring and melting mozzarella, baked in the oven. A sweet and savoury family favourite with 10 minutes of work."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C."
  - "Slice the breasts in half horizontally to make thinner fillets. Pound lightly and season with salt, pepper and paprika."
  - "Lay in an oiled dish. Top each with a slice of ham and a pineapple ring."
  - "Scatter with grated or sliced mozzarella."
  - "Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until the chicken is cooked and the cheese golden. Serve with rice."
tips:
  - "Drain the pineapple well, otherwise the dish fills with juice."
---

Pineapple on chicken sounds odd to some of the family, but the argument usually ends after the first bite.
