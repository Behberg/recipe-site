---
title: "Sugar free banana oat pancakes"
description: "Fluffy oat pancakes with banana and cinnamon, no flour and no sugar, blended in 5 minutes. A healthy breakfast with berries."
steps:
  - "Put the oats, banana, eggs, milk, baking powder and cinnamon in a blender. Blend to a smooth batter."
  - "Leave for 5 minutes so the oats swell."
  - "Heat a little oil in a frying pan and pour in small pancakes."
  - "Cook over a medium heat for 2 minutes, flip and cook for 1 to 2 minutes more."
  - "Serve with berries and yoghurt if you like."
tips:
  - "Do not let the heat get too high: the banana caramelises and burns quickly."
---

These pancakes are sweet with no sugar, because all the sweetness comes from the banana. Children do not even notice.
