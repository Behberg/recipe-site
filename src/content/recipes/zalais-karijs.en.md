---
title: Green chicken curry
description: Thai green curry in coconut milk with chicken, aubergine and basil.
steps:
  - Heat a third of the coconut milk in a pan and fry the curry paste for 2 minutes, until fragrant.
  - Add the thinly sliced chicken and fry for 3 minutes.
  - Pour in the rest of the coconut milk and the stock and add the vegetables.
  - Simmer for 10 minutes, then add the fish sauce and sugar.
  - Stir in the basil, add the lime juice and serve with rice.
tips:
  - "Adjust the amount of curry paste to your taste: 3 spoonfuls is medium hot."
---

Green curry is the hottest of the Thai curries. The coconut milk softens it.
