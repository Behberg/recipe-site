---
title: "Buckwheat salad with roasted vegetables"
description: "A warm autumn salad with buckwheat, roasted pumpkin and beetroot, rocket and walnuts."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C. Cut the pumpkin and beetroot into 2 cm cubes and the onion into wedges."
  - "Toss with 2 tablespoons of the oil, salt and pepper and roast for 25 to 30 minutes, until tender and browned at the edges."
  - "Cook the buckwheat in salted water for 12 to 15 minutes and drain."
  - "Whisk the remaining oil with the balsamic vinegar and mustard."
  - "Toss the buckwheat with the vegetables and dressing. Add the rocket and roughly chopped walnuts."
---

Good warm, and just as good the next day from a lunchbox at work.
