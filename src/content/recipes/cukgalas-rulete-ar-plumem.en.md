---
title: "Pork loin roll with prunes and apples"
description: "A festive rolled pork loin stuffed with prunes and apples, roasted with a honey mustard glaze. Impressive yet simple."
steps:
  - "Cut the loin in a spiral to open it into a wide sheet about 2 cm thick. Pound lightly and season with salt, pepper, marjoram and crushed garlic."
  - "Lay the prunes and thinly sliced apples over it, roll up tightly and tie with string."
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C. Rub the roll with oil and brown all over in a frying pan."
  - "Roast for 70 minutes. Mix the honey with the mustard, brush over the roll and roast for 15 minutes more."
  - "Rest for 15 minutes, remove the string and slice."
tips:
  - "A meat thermometer is your best helper: the roll is done at 68 °C."
---

When the roll is sliced and each piece shows a spiral of prunes and apple, the table instantly becomes festive.
