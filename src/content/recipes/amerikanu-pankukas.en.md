---
title: American pancakes
description: Thick, fluffy pancakes with maple syrup and berries. A real Sunday breakfast.
yield: about 12 pancakes
steps:
  - Mix the dry ingredients in one bowl.
  - In another bowl, whisk the buttermilk, eggs and melted butter.
  - "Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ones and stir only lightly. Small lumps are fine: they are exactly what makes the pancakes fluffy."
  - Let the batter rest for 5 minutes.
  - Cook in a dry or lightly oiled pan over a medium heat. When bubbles appear on top, flip.
  - Serve in a stack with butter, maple syrup and berries.
---

Kefir is a great substitute for buttermilk, so these pancakes turn out very well in Latvia too.
