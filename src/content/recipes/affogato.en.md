---
title: Affogato
description: Vanilla ice cream drowned in hot espresso. An Italian dessert in two minutes.
steps:
  - Chill the glasses or cups in the freezer for 10 minutes.
  - Put two scoops of ice cream in each.
  - Make the espresso and pour it over the ice cream straight away.
  - Sprinkle with grated chocolate and eat at once, while the hot and the cold have not yet mixed.
---

Affogato means “drowned” in Italian: the ice cream drowns in coffee.
