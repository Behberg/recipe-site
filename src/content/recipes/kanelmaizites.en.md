---
title: Cinnamon buns
description: Soft, fragrant Swedish cinnamon buns with cardamom and pearl sugar.
yield: about 20 buns
steps:
  - Dissolve the yeast in the warm milk. Add the melted butter, sugar, cardamom and flour. Knead for 10 minutes.
  - Let the dough rise for 45 minutes.
  - For the filling, mix the soft butter, brown sugar and cinnamon.
  - Roll the dough out into a rectangle, spread with the filling and roll up. Cut into 2 cm slices.
  - Put them in paper cases, leave to rise for 20 minutes, brush with egg and sprinkle with pearl sugar.
  - Bake in the oven at 220 °C for 10 to 12 minutes.
---

In Sweden they have their own day: 4 October. We bake them any day.
