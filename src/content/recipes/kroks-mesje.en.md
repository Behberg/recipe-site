---
title: Croque monsieur
description: A French hot sandwich with ham, cheese and béchamel sauce, baked in the oven until golden.
steps:
  - For the sauce, melt the butter, stir in the flour, pour in the milk and cook, stirring, until thickened. Add salt and nutmeg.
  - Spread two slices of bread thinly with mustard, add the ham and half the cheese and cover with another slice.
  - Spread the top of the sandwich with the sauce and sprinkle over the rest of the cheese.
  - Bake in the oven at 200 °C for 10 to 15 minutes, until the cheese has melted and turned golden.
tips:
  - With a fried egg on top it becomes a croque madame.
---

A Parisian café classic, perfect for a late Sunday breakfast.
