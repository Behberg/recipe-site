---
title: "Chocolate avocado mousse"
description: "A silky chocolate cream in five minutes. The avocado gives the creaminess, but you cannot taste it."
steps:
  - "Put the avocado flesh in a blender with the cocoa, maple syrup, plant milk, vanilla and salt."
  - "Blend for 1 to 2 minutes, until completely smooth. If it is too thick, add a little more milk."
  - "Taste and add more syrup if needed."
  - "Spoon into glasses and chill in the fridge for at least 30 minutes."
  - "Serve with fresh berries."
---

Use fully ripe avocados: hard ones leave a green aftertaste.
