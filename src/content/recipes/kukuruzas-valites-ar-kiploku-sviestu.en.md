---
title: "Corn on the cob with garlic butter"
description: "Juicy boiled or grilled corn on the cob with garlic and parsley butter and coarse salt. A summer side for the barbecue."
steps:
  - "Mix the soft butter with crushed garlic and finely chopped parsley."
  - "Remove the husks and silk from the corn."
  - "Boil in unsalted water for 8 to 10 minutes, or grill for 12 minutes, turning."
  - "Spread the hot cobs generously with the garlic butter."
  - "Sprinkle with coarse salt and serve straight away."
tips:
  - "Do not boil corn in salted water: salt toughens the kernels."
---

In August, when corn is at its sweetest, a cob with butter is a simple joy that nothing replaces.
