---
title: "Chicken and rice bake with mushrooms and cheese"
description: "Creamy oven baked chicken and rice with mushrooms, carrots and a golden cheese crust. All in one dish with no pre cooking."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 190 °C."
  - "Fry the onion, grated carrot and mushrooms in oil for 7 minutes."
  - "In a baking dish mix the uncooked, rinsed rice with the vegetables. Lay the chicken pieces on top and season with salt, pepper and paprika."
  - "Pour over the hot stock and cream. Cover tightly with foil and bake for 35 minutes."
  - "Remove the foil, scatter with cheese and bake for 10 minutes more, until golden."
tips:
  - "The foil must be sealed tightly: the steam is what cooks the rice."
---

Putting the rice straight into the dish uncooked saves a pan and effort. The result tastes like a café.
