---
title: Thin pancakes
description: Classic thin Latvian pancakes made with milk. Perfect with jam, cottage cheese or sour cream.
yield: about 15 pancakes
steps:
  - Whisk the eggs with the sugar and salt. Add half the milk and the flour and mix until smooth.
  - Gradually add the rest of the milk and the oil. The batter should be as thin as cream.
  - Let the batter rest for 15 minutes.
  - Pour a thin layer of batter into a hot, lightly oiled pan and cook for 1 minute, flip and cook for another 30 seconds.
  - Stack the pancakes and serve with jam or sour cream.
---

The first pancake always goes to the cook.
