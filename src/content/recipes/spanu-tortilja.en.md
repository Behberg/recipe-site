---
title: Spanish tortilla
description: A thick potato and egg omelette with onions. A Spanish bar classic, good warm or cold.
steps:
  - Peel the potatoes and slice them thinly, and cut the onion into thin strips.
  - Heat the olive oil in a pan and cook the potatoes with the onion over a medium low heat for 20 minutes, until soft but not browned.
  - Drain the potatoes (keep the oil) and mix them with the beaten, salted eggs. Leave for 5 minutes.
  - Heat 2 tablespoons of the oil in a small pan, pour in the mixture and cook over a low heat for 6 to 8 minutes.
  - Cover the pan with a plate, flip it over and slide the omelette back into the pan. Cook for another 3 to 4 minutes.
---

In Spain they argue about whether onion belongs in a tortilla. We think it does.
