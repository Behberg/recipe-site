---
title: Eton mess
description: Crushed meringue, whipped cream and strawberries in one glass. A no bake summer dessert.
steps:
  - Slice half the strawberries and mash the other half with the sugar into a sauce.
  - Whip the cream to soft peaks.
  - Roughly crush the meringues.
  - Gently fold together the cream, meringue, strawberries and sauce so red streaks remain. Serve straight away.
---

A dessert from Eton College, where it is served at the annual cricket match. The mess is intentional.
