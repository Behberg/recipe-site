---
title: Avocado toast with egg
description: Crisp toast with smashed avocado, lemon, chilli and a soft boiled egg.
steps:
  - Boil the eggs for 6.5 minutes, cool them in cold water and peel.
  - Toast the bread.
  - Mash the avocado with the lemon juice, salt and pepper.
  - Spread the avocado on the toast, top with a halved egg and sprinkle with chilli flakes.
---

A breakfast that has become a modern classic.
