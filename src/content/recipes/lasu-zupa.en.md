---
title: Creamy salmon soup
description: A gentle fish soup with salmon, potatoes, cream and dill. Ready in 30 minutes.
steps:
  - Bring the water or stock to the boil. Add the diced potatoes and sliced carrot.
  - Slice the leek thinly and add it to the soup. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  - Cut the salmon into 2 cm pieces and add them to the soup. Simmer for only 4 to 5 minutes, so the fish stays juicy.
  - Stir in the cream and heat through, but do not boil.
  - Add salt, pepper and chopped dill. Serve with a slice of lemon.
---

A light, elegant lunch that always turns out well.
