---
title: "Herring forshmak (herring pâté)"
description: "Smooth herring pâté with apple, egg and butter, spread on dark rye bread. A good old party starter and sandwich spread."
steps:
  - "Soak the white bread, crusts removed, in the milk."
  - "Check the herring for bones and cut it into pieces. Peel and core the apple."
  - "Mince or blend the herring, apple, onion, eggs and squeezed bread together."
  - "Beat in the soft butter until smooth. Chill for 1 hour."
  - "Serve on slices of rye bread with chopped chives."
tips:
  - "If the herring is very salty, soak it in milk for 30 minutes first."
---

Forshmak turns a humble herring into something festive. On rye bread it is unbeatable.
