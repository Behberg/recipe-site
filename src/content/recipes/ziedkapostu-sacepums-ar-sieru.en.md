---
title: "Cauliflower cheese bake"
description: "Tender cauliflower in a rich cheese sauce, baked until golden and bubbling. A great side dish or a vegetarian dinner."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C. Break the cauliflower into florets and boil in salted water for 5 minutes. Drain."
  - "For the sauce, melt the butter in a pan, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute."
  - "Gradually pour in the milk, stirring constantly, and cook until the sauce thickens."
  - "Take off the heat and stir in two thirds of the cheese, the mustard, nutmeg, salt and pepper."
  - "Put the cauliflower in a dish, pour over the sauce and scatter with the rest of the cheese. Bake for 25 minutes until golden."
tips:
  - "Only parboil the cauliflower, otherwise it turns watery in the oven."
---

With cheese sauce even cauliflower sceptics ask for more. It is the best way to fall for it.
