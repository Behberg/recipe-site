---
title: "Penne arrabbiata (spicy tomato pasta)"
description: "The Italian classic penne arrabbiata: pasta in a fiery tomato sauce with garlic, chilli and olive oil. From storecupboard ingredients in 25 minutes."
steps:
  - "Cook the pasta in salted water according to the packet."
  - "In a frying pan gently heat the thinly sliced garlic and chilli flakes in the oil for 2 minutes, until the garlic is golden."
  - "Add the crushed tomatoes and salt and simmer for 15 minutes until thickened."
  - "Toss in the pasta with a splash of the cooking water."
  - "Sprinkle with parsley and, if you like, cheese."
tips:
  - "Arrabbiata means “angry”: the amount of chilli decides how angry it gets."
---

This is what Romans cook when the fridge is empty. A tin of tomatoes, garlic and chilli, and dinner is ready.
