---
title: "Lamb stew with potatoes and carrots"
description: "Slow cooked, tender lamb with potatoes, carrots and herbs. A simple, hearty dish for Easter and cold evenings."
steps:
  - "Cut the lamb into large pieces, season and brown in batches in oil in a heavy pan."
  - "Add the onions and garlic and cook for 5 minutes."
  - "Pour in the stock and add the thyme and rosemary. Cover and simmer gently for 1 hour."
  - "Add the potatoes and carrots in large chunks. Simmer for another 45 minutes."
  - "Remove the herb stalks and serve with bread."
tips:
  - "Lamb shoulder is perfect for stewing: it turns tender and never dries out."
---

Lamb stew is one of the oldest dishes in Europe. All it needs is time and good meat.
