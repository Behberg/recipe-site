---
title: Vegetable tempura
description: Vegetables fried in a light, crisp batter with a soy and ginger dipping sauce.
steps:
  - Cut the vegetables into thin slices or strips and pat them dry.
  - Heat the oil to 175 °C.
  - "For the batter, whisk the ice cold water with the egg, then add the flour and starch. Stir just a few times: lumps are fine."
  - Dip the vegetables in the batter and fry in batches for 2 to 3 minutes, until pale golden.
  - Drain on kitchen paper and serve straight away with the soy and ginger sauce.
tips:
  - The colder the batter, the crisper the tempura. Keep the bowl on ice.
---

Tempura was brought to Japan by Portuguese missionaries in the 16th century.
