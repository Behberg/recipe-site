---
title: "Peanut stew with sweet potato"
description: "A West African inspired stew: sweet potato, chickpeas and spinach in a thick peanut and tomato sauce."
steps:
  - "Fry the onion in the oil for 4 minutes, add the garlic, ginger, cumin and chilli and fry for 1 minute."
  - "Add the diced sweet potato, tomatoes and stock. Simmer for 15 minutes."
  - "Stir the peanut butter with a ladle of the hot liquid and pour it into the pot."
  - "Add the chickpeas and simmer for another 10 minutes, until the stew thickens."
  - "Stir in the spinach, season with salt and serve with chopped peanuts."
---

Peanut butter makes the sauce thick and velvety without any cream.
