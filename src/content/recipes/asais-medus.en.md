---
title: "Hot honey"
description: "Chilli-infused honey that has taken over the world. Drizzle it on pizza, fried chicken, cheese and roast vegetables."
yield: "1 small jar (250 ml)"
steps:
  - "Slice the fresh chilli in half lengthways. Keep the seeds if you like it very hot."
  - "Put the honey in a small pan with the chilli flakes and fresh chilli and warm over very low heat for 5 minutes. The honey must not boil, only turn runny and fragrant."
  - "Take off the heat, stir in the vinegar and salt and leave to infuse for 30 minutes."
  - "Strain through a sieve or leave the flakes in, and pour into a clean jar."
tips:
  - "In a closed jar at room temperature hot honey keeps for several months."
  - "Best on salami pizza, fried chicken, goat's cheese or hot roast potatoes."
  - "If the honey crystallises, stand the jar in warm water."
---
