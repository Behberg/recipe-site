---
title: "Moist chocolate cherry cake"
description: "A moist, rich chocolate cake with juicy cherries, somewhere near a brownie. A simple recipe with frozen or jarred cherries."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 175 °C and line a 24 cm round tin with paper."
  - "Melt the chocolate with the butter, stir in the sugar and then the eggs one at a time."
  - "Sift in the flour, cocoa and baking powder and mix."
  - "Pour into the tin and scatter with the well drained cherries."
  - "Bake for 35 to 40 minutes. The centre should stay slightly moist."
tips:
  - "Pat the cherries dry on kitchen paper first so the cake does not get soggy."
---

Chocolate and cherries are a classic pair. This cake is like a Black Forest gateau without the cream or the effort.
