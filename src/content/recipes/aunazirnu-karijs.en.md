---
title: Chickpea curry (chana masala)
description: Chickpeas in a spicy tomato and onion sauce with Indian spices. Vegan and filling.
steps:
  - Fry the onion in oil in a pan until golden.
  - Add the garlic, ginger and all the spices and fry for 1 minute.
  - Add the tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes.
  - Add the rinsed chickpeas and a little water and simmer for 10 minutes. Mash some of the chickpeas to thicken the sauce.
  - Add the lemon juice and salt. Serve with rice and coriander.
---

A South Asian street food, eaten with fried breads or rice.
