---
title: Buffalo chicken wings
description: Crispy oven baked wings in a spicy butter sauce with blue cheese dip and celery.
steps:
  - "Pat the wings dry and toss them with the baking powder and salt: this makes the skin crispy."
  - Arrange them on a rack over a baking tray and bake in the oven at 220 °C for 45 to 50 minutes, turning halfway.
  - For the sauce, melt the butter with the chilli sauce and honey.
  - For the blue cheese dip, mix the sour cream, crumbled cheese and lemon juice.
  - Toss the hot wings in the spicy sauce. Serve with the cheese dip and celery sticks.
---

Invented in Buffalo in 1964 and now part of every American football night.
