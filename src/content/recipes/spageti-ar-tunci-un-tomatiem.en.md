---
title: "Spaghetti with tuna, tomatoes and olives"
description: "Quick spaghetti with tuna, tomato sauce, olives, capers and garlic. Dinner from the storecupboard in 25 minutes."
steps:
  - "Cook the spaghetti in salted water according to the packet."
  - "Heat the sliced garlic and chilli in the oil in a frying pan for 1 minute."
  - "Add the crushed tomatoes and simmer for 10 minutes."
  - "Add the drained tuna, olives and capers and warm through for 2 minutes."
  - "Toss in the spaghetti with a splash of cooking water and sprinkle with parsley."
tips:
  - "Tuna in oil gives a richer flavour: you can use some of the oil in the sauce."
---

This is what you can make when it seems there is nothing in the house. A tin of tuna and tomatoes is always somewhere.
