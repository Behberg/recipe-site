---
title: Baked apples with honey and nuts
description: Oven baked apples filled with nuts, raisins, honey and cinnamon.
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 180 °C.
  - Core the apples without cutting all the way through to the bottom.
  - Mix the chopped nuts, raisins, honey and cinnamon and fill the apples.
  - Put a knob of butter on each. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, until the apples are soft.
  - Serve warm with yogurt or vanilla sauce.
---

An autumn dessert whose scent fills the whole house.
