---
title: "Homemade pita bread with a pocket"
description: "Soft pita that puffs up in the oven to make a pocket for filling. Great for falafel, gyros and all your favourite stuffed breads."
steps:
  - "Dissolve the yeast and sugar in the water. Add the flour, salt and oil and knead for 8 minutes into a smooth dough."
  - "Cover and leave to rise in a warm place for 1 hour until doubled."
  - "Heat the oven to 250 °C with a baking tray inside."
  - "Divide the dough into 8 balls and roll each into a 5 mm thick round."
  - "Place on the hot tray and bake for 4 to 5 minutes until puffed. Wrap straight away in a tea towel to keep them soft."
tips:
  - "A hot tray and a hot oven are the main secret to getting the pita to puff."
---

When a pita suddenly puffs up like a balloon in the oven, it is a small miracle that children watch through the oven door.
