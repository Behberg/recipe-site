---
title: Pickled chanterelles
description: Small chanterelles in a sweet and sour marinade with onion and spices. A festive starter.
yield: about 3 jars of 0.5 l
steps:
  - Clean the chanterelles carefully and wash them in several changes of water.
  - Boil in salted water for 15 minutes and drain.
  - For the marinade, boil the water with the sugar, salt and spices.
  - Add the mushrooms and onion rings and boil for 5 minutes. Add the vinegar at the end.
  - Pack the hot mushrooms into sterilised jars with the marinade and seal.
---

A jar of pickled chanterelles in winter is like a little corner of the forest on the table.
