---
title: "Chickpea flour omelette"
description: "A golden egg free omelette made from chickpea flour with tomato, spring onions and spinach."
steps:
  - "Whisk the chickpea flour with the water, turmeric, baking powder, salt and pepper into a smooth batter. Leave for 5 minutes."
  - "Stir in the finely chopped tomato, spring onions and spinach."
  - "Heat half the oil in a pan over medium heat and pour in half the batter."
  - "Fry for 3 minutes, until the edges are firm, turn and fry for 2 more minutes."
  - "Make the second omelette the same way. Serve with fresh vegetables or bread."
---

In India this omelette is called chilla and eaten for breakfast with a hot sauce.
