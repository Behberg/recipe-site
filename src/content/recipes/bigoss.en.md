---
title: Bigos
description: A Polish hunter’s stew with sauerkraut, several kinds of meat, sausage and prunes.
steps:
  - Lightly rinse the sauerkraut and shred the fresh cabbage. Stew both with a little water for 30 minutes.
  - In a pan, fry the bacon, the diced meat and the sausage, then add the onion.
  - Add the meat to the cabbage together with the prunes, tomato paste, wine and spices.
  - Simmer over a low heat for at least 2 hours, stirring from time to time.
  - Bigos is best reheated the next day, or even the day after.
---

In the old days bigos was cooked for several days, reheated every day with fresh meat added.
