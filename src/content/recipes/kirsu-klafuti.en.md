---
title: "Cherry clafoutis"
description: "French clafoutis: juicy cherries baked in a soft, pancake like egg batter. A simple summer dessert with 15 minutes of work."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 190 °C. Butter a baking dish and sprinkle with a spoonful of sugar."
  - "Arrange the cherries in the dish in a single layer."
  - "Whisk the eggs with the sugar and vanilla sugar, stir in the flour, then the milk to a smooth, thin batter."
  - "Pour over the cherries and bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until puffed and golden."
  - "Cool slightly and dust with icing sugar. Serve warm."
tips:
  - "In France the cherries are traditionally left unpitted for an almond aroma. Just take care when eating."
---

Clafoutis is a French country dessert baked in cherry season. It sits somewhere between a pancake and a pudding.
