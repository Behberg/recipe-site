---
title: "Fruit salad with vanilla yoghurt"
description: "Colourful fresh fruit salad with a vanilla honey yoghurt and mint. A simple, healthy dessert for children and parties."
steps:
  - "Wash, peel and cut the fruit into even pieces."
  - "Sprinkle bananas and apples with lemon juice so they do not brown."
  - "Mix the yoghurt with the honey and vanilla."
  - "Spoon the fruit into bowls, top with the yoghurt and sprinkle with mint."
tips:
  - "Cut the fruit just before serving so it stays fresh and crisp."
---

When children say they do not want fruit, fruit salad with yoghurt always works.
