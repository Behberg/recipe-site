---
title: "Christmas roast ham with honey mustard glaze"
description: "A festive pork leg slow roasted and glazed with honey, mustard and cloves. Juicy inside with a glossy, sweet crust."
steps:
  - "Score the skin in a diamond pattern without cutting into the meat. Rub with salt, pepper and crushed garlic. Leave overnight in the fridge."
  - "Heat the oven to 160 °C. Put the ham in a roasting tin, pour in the water or beer and cover with foil."
  - "Roast for 2.5 hours, basting now and then."
  - "For the glaze, mix the honey, mustard and brown sugar. Stud each diamond with a clove."
  - "Remove the foil, raise the oven to 200 °C, brush with glaze and roast for 30 more minutes, glazing twice more."
  - "Rest for 20 minutes before carving."
tips:
  - "A meat thermometer is your best friend: the ham is done at 70 °C in the centre."
---

When the Christmas ham is in the oven, the whole house smells of celebration. It is the dish the family gathers around.
