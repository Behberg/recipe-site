---
title: Raspberry jam
description: Fragrant raspberry jam with a vivid colour. Best with pancakes and with tea.
yield: about 3 jars of 0.5 l
steps:
  - Do not wash the raspberries, just pick them over carefully. Layer them in a pan with the sugar and leave for 3 hours.
  - Bring to the boil over a medium heat, stirring gently so the berries do not break up.
  - Boil for 10 to 15 minutes, skimming off the foam.
  - Add the lemon juice and boil for 2 more minutes.
  - Pour the hot jam into sterilised jars and seal tightly.
tips:
  - If you do not like the seeds, push half of the berries through a sieve.
---

A spoonful of raspberry jam in tea is grandma’s cure for a cold.
