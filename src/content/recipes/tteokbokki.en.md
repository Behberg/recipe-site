---
title: "Tteokbokki (Korean spicy rice cakes)"
description: "Korean street food tteokbokki: chewy rice cakes in a spicy, sweet gochujang sauce with eggs and spring onions. Ready in 25 minutes."
steps:
  - "If the rice cakes are hard, soak them in warm water for 10 minutes."
  - "Bring the water to the boil in a pan and stir in the gochujang, chilli flakes, soy sauce and sugar."
  - "Add the rice cakes and simmer over a medium heat for 10 to 12 minutes, stirring, until the sauce thickens and the cakes are soft."
  - "Add the halved eggs and spring onions and cook for 1 minute more."
  - "Sprinkle with sesame and serve straight away."
tips:
  - "Rice cakes are sold in Asian grocery stores, usually frozen or vacuum packed."
---

In Seoul tteokbokki is sold on every corner. Spicy, sweet and so chewy it is hard to stop.
