---
title: Caprese salad
description: Ripe tomatoes, soft mozzarella and fresh basil with olive oil. A summer salad in 5 minutes.
steps:
  - Slice the tomatoes and mozzarella to the same thickness.
  - Arrange them alternately on a plate, tucking basil leaves between the slices.
  - Drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with coarse salt and pepper. Serve straight away.
tips:
  - "Do not keep tomatoes in the fridge: at room temperature they taste much better."
---

A salad from the island of Capri, whose colours recall the Italian flag.
