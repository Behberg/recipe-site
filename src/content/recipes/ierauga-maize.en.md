---
title: "Sourdough bread"
description: "Homemade wheat sourdough with a crackling crust and a moist, open crumb, baked in a cast iron pot. With notes on growing your own starter."
yield: "1 loaf"
steps:
  - "Feed the starter 4 to 6 hours before making the dough. It is ready when it has doubled and a spoonful floats in a glass of water."
  - "In a large bowl, mix both flours with 320 ml of the water until no dry flour is left. Cover and leave for 1 hour."
  - "Add the starter, salt and the rest of the water and squeeze and knead with wet hands for 5 minutes until evenly mixed."
  - "Over the next 2 hours, stretch and fold the dough 4 times, every 30 minutes. Then leave it covered until it has grown by half and is bubbly, 4 to 6 hours in total at room temperature."
  - "Tip the dough onto a lightly floured worktop, shape it into a tight ball and place it seam side up in a floured basket or a bowl lined with a tea towel. Cover and refrigerate overnight, 12 to 16 hours."
  - "Heat the oven with a cast iron pot and its lid inside to 250 °C for 45 minutes."
  - "Turn the dough out onto baking paper, slash the top with a sharp knife and lower it into the hot pot. Bake with the lid on for 20 minutes."
  - "Remove the lid, lower the heat to 230 °C and bake for 25 more minutes until the crust is deep brown."
  - "Let the bread cool on a rack for at least 1 hour before slicing."
tips:
  - "Start the starter 7 days ahead: mix 50 g wholemeal flour with 50 g water. Each day discard half and feed it with 50 g flour and 50 g water. Once it reliably doubles within 4 to 6 hours of feeding, it is ready."
  - "If you have a rye bread starter, feed it with wheat flour for a few days and it will work for this loaf."
  - "Without a cast iron pot, bake on a hot tray with a dish of boiling water on the oven floor."
---
