---
title: "Honey cookies with cinnamon and ginger"
description: "Soft, fragrant honey cookies with cinnamon and ginger, cut out with cutters. Easier than gingerbread and great for decorating with children."
yield: "about 40 cookies"
steps:
  - "Gently melt the honey, butter and sugar in a small pan. Cool to lukewarm."
  - "Beat in the egg and stir in the flour with the bicarbonate of soda and spices. Knead to a soft dough."
  - "Wrap in cling film and chill for 1 hour."
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C. Roll the dough out 4 mm thick and cut out shapes."
  - "Bake on paper for 8 to 10 minutes until light brown. Cool and decorate with icing if you like."
tips:
  - "The cookies are soft straight out of the oven and firm up as they cool."
---

Honey cookies are older than gingerbread. They smell of beeswax and waiting for Christmas.
