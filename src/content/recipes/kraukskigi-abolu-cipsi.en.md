---
title: "Crispy oven baked apple chips with cinnamon"
description: "Thin, crispy oven dried apple chips with cinnamon and no sugar or oil. A healthy snack for children and a great way to use up apples."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 110 °C and line two baking trays with paper."
  - "Leave the apples unpeeled and slice them 2 mm thin with a sharp knife or mandoline. Remove the seeds."
  - "Brush the slices lightly with lemon juice and lay them in one layer on the paper."
  - "Sprinkle with cinnamon and dry for 1.5 to 2 hours, turning after an hour."
  - "Turn off the oven and let the chips cool inside: they turn crisp as they cool."
tips:
  - "Keep them in a closed jar: in humid air the chips soon lose their crunch."
---

Apple chips are crisps that parents are happy to give children. And children eat them just as happily as the real thing.
