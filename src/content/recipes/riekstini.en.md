---
title: “Nuts” cookies
description: Walnut shaped cookies filled with boiled condensed milk. A nostalgic festive treat.
yield: about 30 nuts
steps:
  - Beat the butter with the sugar, add the eggs, then the flour with the baking powder. Knead into a soft dough.
  - Grease the special nut moulds. Press a small ball of dough into each half.
  - Bake on the stove or in the oven until the halves are golden, about 3 to 4 minutes per batch.
  - Break off the edges from the cooled halves.
  - Mix the caramel with the chopped nuts, fill the halves and stick them together.
tips:
  - You can boil the condensed milk in its can in water for 2.5 hours, or buy it ready boiled.
---

If you have an old nut mould at home, this is the recipe to finally take it out of the cupboard.
