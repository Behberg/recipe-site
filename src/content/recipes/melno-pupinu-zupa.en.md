---
title: "Black bean soup"
description: "A thick, smoky soup with cumin and lime. As filling as a stew and ready in half an hour."
steps:
  - "Fry the chopped onion, pepper and carrot in the oil for 5 minutes."
  - "Add the garlic, cumin, smoked paprika and tomato paste and fry for 1 minute."
  - "Add the beans with their liquid and the stock. Simmer for 15 minutes."
  - "Blend about half the soup and pour it back, so it is thick but still chunky."
  - "Add the lime juice, salt and pepper. Serve with diced avocado and coriander."
---

The liquid from the tin thickens the soup, so do not pour it away.
