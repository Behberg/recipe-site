---
title: Rosemary focaccia
description: A soft, airy Italian flatbread with olive oil, coarse salt and rosemary.
steps:
  - "Mix the flour, yeast, salt and water. The dough will be very wet: that is right. Stir in 2 tablespoons of olive oil."
  - Cover and leave to rise for 1 hour. During this time, stretch and fold the dough 3 times with wet hands.
  - Oil a deep baking tray generously, put in the dough and push it out to the edges with your fingers. Leave to rise for another 30 minutes.
  - Press deep dimples into the dough with your fingers, drizzle with the rest of the olive oil and scatter over the rosemary and coarse salt.
  - Bake in the oven at 220 °C for 20 to 25 minutes, until the top is golden.
---

A wet dough and generous olive oil are the whole secret of focaccia.
