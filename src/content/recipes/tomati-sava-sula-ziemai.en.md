---
title: "Home preserved tomatoes in their own juice"
description: "Homemade tomatoes preserved in their own juice: the base for soups, sauces, stews and pizza all winter. Just tomatoes, salt and a little basil."
yield: "about 5 jars of 0.5 l"
steps:
  - "Score a cross in the tomatoes, pour boiling water over them for 30 seconds and cool in cold water. Peel."
  - "Blend a third of the softest tomatoes, add the salt and sugar and bring to the boil."
  - "Quarter the rest of the tomatoes and pack tightly into sterilised jars with a basil leaf in each."
  - "Pour the boiling tomato juice over them to the top."
  - "Put the lids on and process the jars in a boiling water bath for 20 minutes. Seal and cool."
tips:
  - "Processing in a water bath is essential because tomatoes are low in acid."
---

A jar of garden tomatoes in February smells of August. No shop tin can replace it.
