---
title: "Watermelon and feta salad with mint"
description: "Refreshing summer salad with juicy watermelon, salty feta, cucumber, mint and olives. Perfect on a hot day and at barbecues."
steps:
  - "Cube the watermelon and cucumber."
  - "Slice the onion into thin half rings and soak in the lime juice for 5 minutes."
  - "Put the watermelon, cucumber, onion and olives in a bowl."
  - "Scatter with crumbled feta and mint and drizzle with oil."
tips:
  - "Make it just before serving, otherwise the watermelon releases a lot of juice."
---

This salad is summer on a plate: sweet, salty and cool all at once.
