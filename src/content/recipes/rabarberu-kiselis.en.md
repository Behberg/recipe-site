---
title: Rhubarb kissel
description: A tart and sweet rhubarb kissel with vanilla. Serve with milk or whipped cream.
steps:
  - Wash the rhubarb and cut it into 1 cm pieces.
  - Bring the water to the boil with the sugar, add the rhubarb and cook for 5 minutes, until soft.
  - Dissolve the starch in 100 ml of cold water.
  - Pour the starch into the kissel in a thin stream, stirring constantly, and bring back to the boil. As soon as it thickens, take it off the heat.
  - Stir in the vanilla sugar, pour into bowls and sprinkle with sugar to stop a skin forming.
---

The first spring dessert from your own garden.
