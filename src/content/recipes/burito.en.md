---
title: Beef and bean burrito
description: A large wheat tortilla with spiced meat, rice, beans, cheese and salsa.
steps:
  - Fry the onion in a pan, add the meat and spices and fry until the meat is browned.
  - Add the rinsed beans and simmer for 5 minutes. Season with salt.
  - Mix the rice with the lime juice.
  - Warm the tortillas. Put rice, meat, cheese, avocado and sour cream in the middle of each.
  - Fold in the sides, roll up tightly and toast in a dry pan seam side down.
---

The burrito was born in northern Mexico but became famous in California.
