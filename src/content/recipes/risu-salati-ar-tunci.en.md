---
title: "Rice salad with tuna, eggs and sweetcorn"
description: "Filling rice salad with tuna, eggs, sweetcorn and cucumber in a light dressing. Cheap, quick and great for packed lunches."
steps:
  - "Cook the rice and let it cool completely."
  - "Dice the eggs and cucumber and chop the spring onions."
  - "Drain the tuna and flake with a fork."
  - "Mix everything with the sweetcorn, mayonnaise or yoghurt, lemon juice, salt and pepper."
tips:
  - "The salad keeps well in the fridge for 2 days: ideal for making ahead."
---

This salad is how yesterday’s rice becomes today’s lunch.
