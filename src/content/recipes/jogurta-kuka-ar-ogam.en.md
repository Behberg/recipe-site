---
title: "Yoghurt cake with berries"
description: "Moist, fluffy yoghurt cake with berries and lemon zest, mixed in one bowl. The easiest summer cake with fresh or frozen berries."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 175 °C and line a 24 cm round tin with paper."
  - "Whisk the eggs with the sugar, then stir in the yoghurt, oil and lemon zest."
  - "Sift in the flour with the baking powder and mix until smooth."
  - "Pour into the tin and scatter the berries on top."
  - "Bake for 40 to 45 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean. Dust with icing sugar once cool."
tips:
  - "You can use the yoghurt pot as a measure: 1 of yoghurt, 2 of sugar, 3 of flour."
---

This cake is so simple you can bake it even when the guests are already on their way.
