---
title: Apple crumble
description: Warm cinnamon apples under a crisp buttery crumble. Serve with vanilla ice cream.
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 190 °C.
  - Peel the apples, cut them into chunks and mix with the sugar and cinnamon. Put them in a baking dish.
  - Rub the flour, sugar and cold butter together with your fingertips into crumbs, then stir in the oats.
  - Scatter the crumble over the apples.
  - Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until the top is golden and the apples are bubbling.
---

A British dessert that always works, even if you are baking it for the first time.
