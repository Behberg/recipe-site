---
title: "Classic mojito cocktail"
description: "The classic Cuban mojito with white rum, lime, mint and soda water. A summer terrace cocktail ready in 5 minutes."
steps:
  - "Cut the lime into wedges and put in a tall glass with the sugar and mint leaves."
  - "Muddle gently to release the juice and aroma."
  - "Fill the glass with crushed ice and pour in the rum."
  - "Top up with soda water, stir from the bottom up and garnish with mint."
tips:
  - "Do not crush the mint too hard or the cocktail turns bitter."
---

The mojito was born in Havana, but in summer it feels at home on any terrace, Latvia included.
