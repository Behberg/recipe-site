---
title: Cream of tomato soup
description: A velvety tomato soup with basil and cream. Can be made from tinned tomatoes all year round.
steps:
  - In a pan, cook the onion in olive oil until soft and add the garlic.
  - Add the tomatoes, stock and sugar. Simmer for 20 minutes.
  - Add the basil and blend until smooth.
  - Stir in the cream and season with salt and pepper.
  - Serve with a grilled cheese sandwich or croutons.
---

A pinch of sugar balances the acidity of the tomatoes.
