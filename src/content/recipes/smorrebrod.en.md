---
title: Danish open sandwiches (smørrebrød)
description: "Rye bread with a variety of toppings: herring, eggs, prawns or roast beef. Beautiful and filling."
steps:
  - Spread the slices of bread thinly with butter.
  - "Egg sandwiches: slices of egg, a spoonful of mayonnaise, dill and pepper."
  - "Herring sandwiches: herring, thin onion rings and slices of cucumber."
  - "Prawn sandwiches: mayonnaise, prawns, a slice of lemon and dill."
  - Arrange on a board and serve straight away.
---

In Copenhagen one sandwich can be piled so high that you eat it with a knife and fork.
