---
title: "Pasta with broccoli and garlic"
description: "A quick southern Italian supper: the broccoli cooks with the pasta and turns into a sauce with garlic and chilli."
steps:
  - "Toast the breadcrumbs in a pan with 1 tablespoon of the oil until golden. Set aside."
  - "Cook the pasta in salted water. Add the broccoli florets 5 minutes before it is done."
  - "In a pan warm the sliced garlic and chilli in the remaining oil over low heat for 2 minutes."
  - "Add the drained pasta and broccoli to the pan, pour in a ladle of cooking water and stir, crushing some of the broccoli, until a sauce forms."
  - "Season with salt, pepper and lemon zest. Serve with the crisp breadcrumbs."
---

In southern Italy toasted breadcrumbs are the poor man's parmesan.
