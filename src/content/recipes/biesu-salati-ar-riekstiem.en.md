---
title: Beetroot salad with garlic and walnuts
description: A simple, hearty beetroot salad with garlic, walnuts, prunes and mayonnaise.
steps:
  - Boil the beetroot until tender (about 40 minutes), or use ready cooked ones. Peel.
  - Coarsely grate the beetroot.
  - Lightly toast the walnuts in a dry pan and chop them. Cut the prunes into strips.
  - Mix the beetroot, crushed garlic, walnuts, prunes and mayonnaise. Salt to taste.
---

A great salad with meat and potatoes, and on dark rye bread too.
