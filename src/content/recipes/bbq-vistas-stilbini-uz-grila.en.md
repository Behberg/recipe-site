---
title: "BBQ chicken drumsticks on the grill"
description: "Sticky, smoky chicken drumsticks with a smoked paprika rub and barbecue sauce glaze from the grill. A barbecue party favourite."
steps:
  - "Mix the paprika, garlic powder, sugar, salt and pepper. Rub the chicken with oil and the spice mix."
  - "Set up a hot and a cooler zone on the grill. Cook the chicken in the cooler zone with the lid closed for 25 minutes, turning."
  - "Move to the hot zone, brush with barbecue sauce and grill for 5 minutes, turning and brushing again."
  - "The meat is done when it reaches 75 °C near the bone."
  - "Serve with the rest of the sauce and a fresh salad."
tips:
  - "Add the sauce only at the end: the sugar in it burns quickly."
---

Drumsticks are the cheapest barbecue meat, and with this glaze they are the first to go.
