---
title: "Tomato and cucumber salad with onion and dill"
description: "The simplest summer salad: ripe tomatoes, crunchy cucumbers, red onion and dill with oil. Ready in 10 minutes."
steps:
  - "Cut the tomatoes into wedges and the cucumbers into half moons."
  - "Slice the onion into thin half rings."
  - "Put everything in a bowl and scatter with dill."
  - "Drizzle with oil and season with salt and pepper just before serving."
tips:
  - "This salad is only as good as the tomatoes: choose ripe, fragrant ones."
---

In August, tomatoes and cucumbers from the garden need nothing more. This salad is on the table every summer day.
