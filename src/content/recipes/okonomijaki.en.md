---
title: Okonomiyaki
description: A Japanese cabbage pancake with bacon, mayonnaise and a sweet and savoury sauce. Osaka street food.
steps:
  - Shred the cabbage very finely.
  - Mix the flour, eggs and stock, then stir in the cabbage and spring onions.
  - Heat oil in a pan, spread half the mixture into a 2 cm thick pancake and lay half the bacon on top.
  - Cook for 5 minutes, flip and cook for another 5 minutes, until the bacon is crisp.
  - For the sauce, mix the ketchup, soy sauce and honey. Drizzle the pancake with the sauce and mayonnaise.
---

Okonomi means “whatever you like”: change the filling as you please.
