---
title: Sauerkraut soup
description: A hearty, tangy cabbage soup with smoked meat. On the second day it tastes even better.
steps:
  - Cover the ribs with water, bring to the boil, skim off the foam and simmer gently for 1 hour.
  - Add the sauerkraut and bay leaves and simmer for another 20 minutes.
  - Add the diced potatoes.
  - Fry the onion and grated carrot in a pan and add them to the soup.
  - Simmer for 15 minutes, until the potatoes are soft. Take the meat off the bones and return it to the soup.
  - Season with salt and pepper. Serve with sour cream.
---

A classic winter soup that warms you up after a long walk in the snow.
