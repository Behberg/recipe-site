---
title: Spanakopita
description: A Greek pie with spinach, feta and dill between crisp layers of filo.
steps:
  - Briefly wilt the spinach and squeeze out all the liquid. Chop.
  - Mix the spinach with the crumbled feta, eggs, chopped spring onions, dill, pepper and nutmeg.
  - Butter a dish. Lay in 5 filo sheets, brushing each with butter.
  - Spread the filling, cover with the rest of the sheets, brushing each with butter. Tuck the edges into the dish.
  - Score the top into portions and bake in the oven at 180 °C for 40 to 45 minutes, until golden.
---

In Greece spanakopita is eaten for breakfast, as a starter and for lunch.
