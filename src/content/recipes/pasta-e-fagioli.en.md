---
title: "Pasta e fagioli (Italian bean and pasta soup)"
description: "The Italian home soup pasta e fagioli with white beans, small pasta, tomatoes and rosemary. Thick, cheap and as filling as a stew."
steps:
  - "Finely chop the onion, carrot and celery and soften in oil in a pan for 8 minutes. Add the garlic and rosemary."
  - "Add the tomatoes and half the beans; mash the other half with a fork and add them too."
  - "Pour in the stock and simmer for 15 minutes."
  - "Add the pasta and cook until tender, about 10 minutes. Season with salt and pepper."
  - "Serve with parmesan and a drizzle of olive oil."
tips:
  - "The mashed beans make the soup thick and creamy without any cream."
---

Italian grandmothers make this soup from whatever is in the cupboard. It is so thick a spoon almost stands up in it.
