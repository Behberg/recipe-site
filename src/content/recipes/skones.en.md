---
title: Scones
description: Soft British tea scones served with jam and clotted cream.
yield: about 8 scones
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 220 °C.
  - Mix the flour, baking powder and a pinch of salt, then rub in the cold butter until the mixture looks like crumbs. Stir in the sugar.
  - Mix the milk with the lemon juice, pour it into the flour and quickly bring together into a soft dough with a knife. Do not knead for long.
  - Pat the dough into a 3 cm thick slab and cut out rounds with a glass.
  - Brush with egg and bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until risen and golden.
  - Serve warm with strawberry jam and whipped cream.
---

In Cornwall the jam goes on first, in Devon the cream. We stay out of it.
