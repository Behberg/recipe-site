---
title: "Dutch baby (oven puffed pancake)"
description: "An oven baked pancake that puffs up like a cloud with crisp edges. Served with berries and icing sugar. An impressive breakfast in 25 minutes."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 220 °C with a cast iron pan or baking dish inside."
  - "Whisk or blend the eggs, milk, flour, sugar and salt into a smooth batter. Rest for 5 minutes."
  - "Melt the butter in the hot pan and quickly pour in the batter."
  - "Bake for 18 to 20 minutes without opening the oven, until tall, puffed and golden."
  - "Top straight away with berries and dust with icing sugar. Serve immediately, as it deflates quickly."
tips:
  - "The pan must heat up with the oven: the heat is what makes the pancake rise."
---

When it comes out of the oven it looks like a magic trick. A Mother’s Day breakfast that surprises.
