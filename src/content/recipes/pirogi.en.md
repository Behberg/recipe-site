---
title: Pierogi with potato and cottage cheese
description: Polish half moon dumplings with a filling of potato, cottage cheese and fried onion.
yield: about 40 pierogi
steps:
  - Boil and mash the potatoes. Fry the onions in butter until golden.
  - Mix the potatoes with the cottage cheese and half the onions. Season with salt and plenty of pepper.
  - For the dough, mix the flour, salt, water and oil, knead for 8 minutes and let it rest for 20 minutes.
  - Roll out thinly, cut out rounds with a glass, put a spoonful of filling on each and press the edges firmly together.
  - Boil in salted water for 2 to 3 minutes after they float.
  - Serve with the rest of the onions and sour cream. The next day they can be fried in butter.
---

In Poland these pierogi are called “ruskie”, and almost every family makes them.
