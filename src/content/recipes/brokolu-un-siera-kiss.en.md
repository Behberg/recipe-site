---
title: "Broccoli and cheese quiche"
description: "A crisp shortcrust tart with broccoli and a creamy egg and cheese filling. A vegetarian dish that is good warm or cold."
steps:
  - "Rub the flour, salt and cold butter into crumbs, add the egg and water and quickly bring together. Wrap and chill for 30 minutes."
  - "Heat the oven to 190 °C. Roll out the pastry, line a 24 cm tin, prick with a fork and bake for 12 minutes."
  - "Cut the broccoli into small florets and blanch in boiling water for 2 minutes."
  - "Whisk the eggs with the cream, half the cheese, nutmeg, salt and pepper."
  - "Arrange the broccoli in the case, pour over the egg mixture and scatter with the rest of the cheese. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, until set and golden."
tips:
  - "Blind baking the base prevents a soggy bottom."
---

A quiche can be baked in the evening and taken to work or a picnic the next day.
