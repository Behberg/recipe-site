---
title: "Samogitian pancakes (potato cakes stuffed with meat)"
description: "Lithuanian Samogitian blynai: crisp mashed potato cakes with a juicy minced meat filling. Served with sour cream."
steps:
  - "Boil and mash the potatoes and let cool. Mix in the egg, flour and salt."
  - "For the filling, mix the mince with finely chopped onion, salt and pepper."
  - "With floured hands flatten some potato, put meat in the middle and cover with more potato to form a cake."
  - "Heat oil in a pan and fry over a medium low heat for 6 to 8 minutes on each side, until golden and the meat is cooked."
  - "Serve with sour cream."
tips:
  - "Fry slowly: the meat must cook through before the potato burns."
---

In Samogitia these pancakes were made from yesterday’s potatoes. Now people drive to Lithuania just to eat them.
