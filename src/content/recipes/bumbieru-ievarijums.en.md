---
title: "Pear jam with lemon and vanilla"
description: "Golden pear preserve with translucent slices, lemon and vanilla. A delicate taste with pancakes, curd cheese and cheeses."
yield: "about 3 jars of 0.5 l"
steps:
  - "Peel and core the pears and cut into slices."
  - "Sprinkle with the sugar and leave for 4 hours or overnight until the juices run."
  - "Add the lemon juice and zest and the split vanilla pod. Bring to the boil, simmer for 10 minutes, then leave for 4 hours."
  - "Repeat the boiling 2 more times, until the slices are translucent and the syrup is thick."
  - "Remove the vanilla, fill sterilised jars while hot and seal."
tips:
  - "Cooking in several stages keeps the slices whole and clear."
---

Old pear varieties give more fruit than anyone can eat. This jam is where they belong in winter.
