---
title: "Beetroot soup with apple and sour cream"
description: "Deep pink cream of beetroot soup with apple for a fresh sweet and sour note. Colourful, healthy and lovely with sour cream and dill."
steps:
  - "Peel and dice the beetroot, apple and potato."
  - "Soften the onion in oil in a pan for 5 minutes."
  - "Add the beetroot, potato, apple and stock. Simmer for 30 to 35 minutes, until the beetroot is tender."
  - "Blend until smooth and add the lemon juice, salt and pepper."
  - "Serve with a spoonful of sour cream and dill."
tips:
  - "To avoid stained hands, peel beetroot with gloves or roast it in its skin first."
---

The colour of this soup is so bright it looks like a magazine photo. It tastes of an autumn garden.
