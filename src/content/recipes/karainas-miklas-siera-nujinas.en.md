---
title: "Puff pastry cheese straws"
description: "Crisp, flaky cheese straws made from ready rolled puff pastry with caraway. A party or beer snack in 25 minutes."
yield: "about 30 straws"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C and line a baking tray with paper."
  - "Roll the pastry into a rectangle and brush with beaten egg."
  - "Sprinkle half the pastry with cheese, paprika and caraway, fold in half and roll lightly."
  - "Cut into 1.5 cm strips and twist each a few times into a spiral. Brush with egg and sprinkle with the rest of the cheese."
  - "Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until golden and puffed."
tips:
  - "Keep the pastry cold: the layers separate better and the straws are lighter."
---

Cheese straws are the snack that always goes first. They are cheap, quick and everybody likes them, children included.
