---
title: "Creamy mushroom sauce"
description: "Creamy sauce with button mushrooms, onion and dill. A classic to serve with potatoes, patties, pork chops and buckwheat."
steps:
  - "Slice the mushrooms and finely chop the onion."
  - "Fry the onion in butter for 3 minutes, add the mushrooms and cook for 7 minutes until the juices evaporate."
  - "Sprinkle in the flour and stir for 1 minute."
  - "Pour in the cream and simmer for 3 minutes until thickened."
  - "Season with salt, pepper and dill."
tips:
  - "If the sauce is too thick, add a little milk or stock."
---

Mushroom sauce turns the plainest potatoes into a favourite meal. It is on many family tables every week.
