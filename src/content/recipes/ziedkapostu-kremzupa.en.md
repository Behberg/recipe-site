---
title: "Cauliflower soup with nutmeg"
description: "Soft, creamy cauliflower soup with butter, cream and nutmeg. A light lunch soup served with crunchy croutons."
steps:
  - "In a pan soften the onion and garlic in butter for 5 minutes."
  - "Add the cauliflower florets, diced potato and stock."
  - "Simmer for 15 to 18 minutes, until the vegetables are soft."
  - "Blend until smooth and stir in the cream, nutmeg, salt and pepper."
  - "Serve with croutons and fresh herbs."
tips:
  - "Roast a few florets in the oven to scatter on top for a crunchy finish."
---

Cauliflower is often overlooked, but in this soup it turns as smooth as velvet.
