---
title: Roast pork with caraway
description: Juicy roast pork with crackling, caraway and garlic. The centrepiece of a Latvian festive table.
steps:
  - Score the skin in a diamond pattern. Make small holes in the meat with a knife and push in the garlic cloves.
  - Rub with salt, pepper and caraway and leave for at least 2 hours.
  - Put onion slices in a roasting tin and the meat on top, skin side up. Pour in water or beer.
  - Roast in the oven at 170 °C for 1.5 hours, basting with the juices from time to time.
  - Raise the temperature to 220 °C and roast for another 20 minutes, until the crackling is crisp.
  - Let it rest for 15 minutes, then carve. The juices in the tin make a wonderful gravy.
---

A roast whose smell means the celebration has begun.
