---
title: "Pearl barley risotto with mushrooms and parmesan"
description: "Creamy pearl barley risotto with mushrooms, thyme and parmesan. A local, cheaper and heartier take on the Italian classic."
steps:
  - "Rinse the barley. Fry the onion in half the butter for 5 minutes, then add the garlic."
  - "Add the barley and toast for 2 minutes, stirring."
  - "Pour in a third of the hot stock, add the thyme and simmer, stirring. When absorbed, add the next part. 35 to 40 minutes in all."
  - "Meanwhile fry the mushrooms in the rest of the butter until browned."
  - "Stir the mushrooms and parmesan into the barley and season with salt, pepper and parsley."
tips:
  - "Barley keeps a slight chew: it never gets as soft as rice, and that is a good thing."
---

Our grandparents ate pearl barley every day. Cooked like risotto, it suddenly becomes restaurant food.
