---
title: "Lemon drizzle loaf cake"
description: "Soft, moist lemon loaf with a tangy lemon syrup and white icing. A simple cake for tea or coffee."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 170 °C and line a loaf tin with baking paper."
  - "Beat the butter with the sugar until pale, then beat in the eggs one at a time."
  - "Stir in the zest of both lemons, the flour with the baking powder, and the milk."
  - "Pour into the tin and bake for 45 to 50 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean."
  - "Prick the hot cake all over with a skewer and spoon over 2 tablespoons of lemon juice."
  - "When cool, pour over an icing made from the icing sugar and the rest of the lemon juice."
tips:
  - "Add the juice to the icing a teaspoon at a time: it should be thick like honey."
---

Lemon loaf is the cake you take when visiting. It survives the journey and is even better the next day.
