---
title: "Cheesy garlic pull apart bread rolls"
description: "Soft yeasted rolls with garlic butter and melted cheese, baked together in one tin. Tear them apart with soup or at a party."
yield: "16 rolls"
steps:
  - "Mix the dough ingredients and knead for 8 minutes until smooth. Leave to rise for 1 hour."
  - "Mix the melted butter with crushed garlic and chopped parsley."
  - "Divide the dough into 16 pieces, roll each into a ball, dip in the garlic butter and roll in cheese."
  - "Pack the balls tightly into a round tin and pour over the rest of the butter and cheese. Leave to rise for 30 minutes."
  - "Heat the oven to 190 °C and bake for 22 to 25 minutes until golden."
  - "Serve warm, pulling them off one by one."
tips:
  - "If the cheese browns too quickly, cover the tin with foil for the last 10 minutes."
---

Nobody needs to be called to the table for these. They disappear from the tin before they have time to cool.
