---
title: "Bun cha (Vietnamese grilled pork with noodles)"
description: "Hanoi bun cha: grilled pork patties in a sweet and sour fish sauce dressing with rice noodles, lettuce and fresh herbs. Summer street food."
steps:
  - "Mix the pork with 1 spoonful of fish sauce, 1 spoonful of sugar and crushed garlic. Shape into small patties."
  - "For the dipping sauce, mix the water, remaining fish sauce, sugar, lime juice, garlic and sliced chilli."
  - "Grill the patties or cook in a hot pan for 3 minutes on each side, until charred at the edges."
  - "Put the hot patties into the sauce."
  - "Cook the noodles according to the packet. Serve the noodles, lettuce and herbs separately to dip into the sauce with the pork."
tips:
  - "The sauce should be sweet, sour and salty at once: taste and adjust."
---

Bun cha is a Hanoi lunch dish, and it is what a famous chef once ate with a US president in a simple street café.
