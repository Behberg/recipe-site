---
title: Chicken tacos
description: Soft corn tortillas with spiced chicken, pico de gallo, avocado and lime.
steps:
  - Rub the chicken with the spices, salt and half the lime juice. Leave for 15 minutes.
  - For the pico de gallo, chop the tomatoes, onion and coriander and add the lime juice and salt.
  - Fry the chicken in a hot pan for 5 to 6 minutes on each side, then slice into strips.
  - Warm the tortillas in a dry pan.
  - Top the tortillas with the chicken, pico de gallo, avocado slices and sour cream. Serve with lime.
---
