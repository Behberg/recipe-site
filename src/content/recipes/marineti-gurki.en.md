---
title: Pickled cucumbers with dill
description: Crunchy sweet and sour pickled cucumbers with dill, garlic and horseradish for the winter table.
yield: 4 jars of 1 l
steps:
  - Wash the cucumbers and soak them in cold water for 3 to 4 hours. This keeps them crunchy.
  - Put the dill, garlic, pieces of horseradish, leaves and peppercorns in the bottom of sterilised jars.
  - Pack the cucumbers tightly into the jars, standing upright.
  - For the brine, boil the water with the salt and sugar, take off the heat and add the vinegar.
  - Pour the hot brine into the jars right to the top.
  - Sterilise the jars in a boiling water bath for 10 minutes, then seal, turn upside down and wrap in a blanket until cool.
---

A jar of your own pickles in winter is like a greeting from summer.
