---
title: "Egg muffins with ham and vegetables"
description: "Baked egg muffins with ham, pepper, spinach and cheese. Handy, high protein breakfasts to grab and go."
yield: "12 muffins"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C and grease a muffin tin with oil."
  - "Finely chop the ham, pepper and spinach and divide between the cups."
  - "Whisk the eggs with the milk, salt and pepper. Pour into the cups until three quarters full."
  - "Sprinkle with cheese and bake for 18 to 20 minutes, until the eggs are set."
  - "Cool for 5 minutes and turn out of the tin."
tips:
  - "They keep for 4 days in the fridge: just warm them in the microwave for 30 seconds in the morning."
---

Bake 12 muffins on Sunday and the working week’s breakfasts are sorted.
