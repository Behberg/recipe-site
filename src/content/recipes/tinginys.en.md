---
title: Tinginys (lazy cake)
description: A chocolate and biscuit dessert with condensed milk. No baking, ready after a night in the fridge.
steps:
  - Break the biscuits into small pieces, not crumbs.
  - Melt the butter in a small pan and stir in the condensed milk and cocoa. Heat until smooth.
  - Pour over the biscuits and mix well.
  - Put the mixture on cling film, shape into a brick and wrap tightly.
  - Chill in the fridge for at least 6 hours, then slice.
---

Tinginys means “lazybones” in Lithuanian: even the laziest person can make it.
