---
title: "Homemade granola with nuts and honey"
description: "Crunchy homemade granola with oats, almonds, seeds and honey. Healthier and cheaper than shop bought, keeps for 3 weeks."
yield: "about 700 g"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 160 °C and line a baking tray with baking paper."
  - "In a bowl mix the oats, roughly chopped almonds, seeds, coconut, cinnamon and salt."
  - "Warm the honey with the oil until runny and pour over the dry ingredients. Stir well."
  - "Spread thinly on the tray and bake for 25 to 30 minutes, stirring after 15 minutes, until golden."
  - "Let it cool completely on the tray, then stir in the raisins. Store in an airtight jar."
tips:
  - "For big crunchy clusters, do not stir in the last 10 minutes and let it cool untouched."
---

Once you bake granola at home, shop bought packets lose their appeal. And the whole kitchen smells of honey and cinnamon.
