---
title: One pan chicken and rice
description: Chicken, rice and vegetables cooked in one pan with paprika and stock.
steps:
  - Dice the chicken, salt it and brown it in oil in a large pan. Take it out.
  - In the same pan, fry the onion, pepper and garlic for 5 minutes.
  - Add the rice and paprika and stir so the rice is coated in oil.
  - Pour in the stock, return the chicken and simmer, covered, over a low heat for 18 minutes.
  - Stir in the peas, cover and leave to stand for 5 minutes.
---

Dinner with only one dirty pan.
