---
title: Courgette caviar
description: A spread of stewed courgettes, carrots and onions. Tasty on bread and ideal for preserving.
yield: about 3 jars of 0.5 l
steps:
  - Dice the courgettes, grate the carrots and chop the onions.
  - Fry the onions and carrots in oil in a pan for 10 minutes.
  - Add the courgettes and stew, covered, for 30 minutes.
  - Add the tomato paste, garlic, sugar, salt and pepper. Stew uncovered for another 15 minutes.
  - Blend until smooth, add the vinegar and bring to the boil.
  - Pour the hot mixture into sterilised jars and seal.
---

When the garden has more courgettes than you can eat, this is the best way out.
