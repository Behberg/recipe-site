---
title: Layered rye bread dessert
description: "The legendary Latvian dessert: layers of sweet rye bread crumbs, whipped cream and cranberry or lingonberry jam."
steps:
  - Grate the rye bread or blitz it into crumbs.
  - Melt the butter in a pan and add the crumbs, sugar and cinnamon. Toast for 5 to 8 minutes, stirring often, until the crumbs are crisp. Let them cool.
  - Whip the cream with the icing sugar and vanilla to soft peaks.
  - "Layer in glasses or a bowl: crumbs, jam, whipped cream. Repeat the layers once more."
  - Sprinkle grated chocolate and a few crumbs on top.
  - Before serving, put it in the fridge for at least 1 hour so the flavours blend.
tips:
  - The crumbs can be made ahead and kept in a closed jar for up to a week.
---

This is the dessert foreign guests remember longest. Sweet, tart, crunchy and creamy all at once.
