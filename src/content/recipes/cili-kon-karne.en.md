---
title: Chilli con carne
description: A spicy stew of beef and beans with tomatoes and spices.
steps:
  - In a pan, fry the onion, garlic and pepper for 5 minutes.
  - Add the meat and fry until browned.
  - Stir in the spices and fry for 1 minute.
  - Add the tomatoes and salt and simmer over a low heat for 40 minutes.
  - Add the rinsed beans and simmer for another 15 minutes.
  - Serve with rice, sour cream and tortilla chips.
---

One pot, little work and food for several days.
