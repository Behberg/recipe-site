---
title: "Cranberry sauce with orange"
description: "Sweet and sour cranberry sauce with orange and cinnamon for roast poultry, duck and pork. Ready in 20 minutes and keeps for 2 weeks."
yield: "about 400 ml"
steps:
  - "Put the cranberries, sugar, water, orange juice and zest and cinnamon in a small pan."
  - "Bring to the boil and simmer over a medium heat for 10 to 12 minutes, until most of the berries have burst."
  - "Remove the cinnamon. The sauce thickens as it cools."
  - "Serve at room temperature or cold. Store in a jar in the fridge."
tips:
  - "Frozen cranberries work just as well and do not need thawing."
---

Cranberries are the treasure of Latvian bogs, and with a holiday roast they are the best companion.
