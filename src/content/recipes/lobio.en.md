---
title: Lobio
description: A Georgian red bean stew with walnuts, coriander and spices.
steps:
  - Fry the onion in oil until soft.
  - Add the beans with some of their liquid and simmer for 10 minutes. Mash some of the beans.
  - Grind the walnuts with the garlic, spices and salt.
  - Stir the walnut mixture and vinegar into the beans and simmer for another 5 minutes.
  - Add the chopped coriander. Serve warm or cold with bread.
---

In Georgia lobio is served in clay pots with cornbread and pickled vegetables.
