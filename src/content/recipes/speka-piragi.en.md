---
title: Bacon pīrāgi
description: Soft yeast buns filled with smoked bacon and onions. Essential on a festive table and on Midsummer night.
yield: about 40 pīrāgi
steps:
  - Warm the milk (not hot) and melt the butter in it. Dissolve the yeast with the sugar.
  - Add the egg, salt and gradually the flour. Knead for 10 minutes, until the dough is smooth and no longer sticks to your hands.
  - Cover with a tea towel and leave to rise in a warm place for 1 hour, until doubled.
  - For the filling, dice the bacon, ham and onion very small and mix with pepper.
  - Shape the dough into balls, flatten into thin rounds, add a teaspoon of filling and fold into a half moon.
  - Put them on a tray seam side down, leave to rise for 15 minutes and brush with egg.
  - Bake in the oven at 200 °C for 12 to 15 minutes, until golden brown. Cover with a tea towel so they stay soft.
tips:
  - The filling is best made a day ahead so the flavours come together.
  - Pīrāgi can be frozen and reheated in the oven for 5 minutes.
---

Baking pīrāgi is a family event. Someone kneads, someone chops the bacon, and someone always eats the first tray while it is still warm.
