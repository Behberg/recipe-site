---
title: "Teriyaki tofu"
description: "Crisp tofu cubes in a glossy, sweet and salty teriyaki sauce. With rice it makes a complete dinner."
steps:
  - "Wrap the tofu in kitchen paper, put a weight on top and press out the water for 10 minutes. Cut into cubes and coat in the cornflour."
  - "Fry in the oil for 8 to 10 minutes, turning, until golden and crisp on all sides."
  - "Mix the soy sauce with the mirin, sugar, water, grated garlic and ginger."
  - "Pour the sauce into the pan with the tofu and simmer for 1 to 2 minutes, until it thickens and coats the cubes."
  - "Serve with rice, sprinkled with sesame seeds and spring onions."
---

The more water you press out of the tofu, the crisper it fries.
