---
title: Ginger tea with lemon and honey
description: A strong, warming fresh ginger tea with lemon and honey. For colds and cold weather.
steps:
  - Peel the ginger and slice it thinly.
  - Bring the water to the boil, add the ginger and simmer for 10 minutes.
  - Take off the heat and add half the lemon juice.
  - When the tea has cooled a little, stir in the honey (honey does not like boiling water).
  - Serve with a slice of lemon.
