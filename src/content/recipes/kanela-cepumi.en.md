---
title: "Soft cinnamon sugar cookies"
description: "Soft, chewy cookies rolled in cinnamon sugar with crisp edges. A simple one bowl recipe that smells of Christmas."
yield: "about 24 cookies"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C and line a baking tray with paper."
  - "Beat the butter with the sugar, then beat in the egg and vanilla sugar."
  - "Stir in the flour with the baking powder and salt."
  - "Roll into balls, coat in the cinnamon sugar and space them out on the paper."
  - "Bake for 10 to 12 minutes, until the edges are golden but the centres still soft. Let them cool on the tray."
tips:
  - "Take them out while the middles still look underdone: they stay soft as they cool."
---

These are the cookies children eat before they have had time to cool.
