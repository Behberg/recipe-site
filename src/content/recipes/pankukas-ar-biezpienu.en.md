---
title: Crêpes with cottage cheese filling
description: Thin crêpes filled with sweet vanilla cottage cheese and fried in butter.
steps:
  - For the crêpes, whisk the eggs with the sugar, then add the milk, flour and oil. Cook thin crêpes.
  - Mash the cottage cheese with the yolk, sugar and vanilla sugar and stir in the raisins.
  - Put a tablespoon of filling on each crêpe and fold into a parcel.
  - Melt the butter in a pan and fry the parcels for 1 to 2 minutes on each side.
  - Serve with sour cream or jam.
---

Crêpes you can make ahead and fry just before eating.
