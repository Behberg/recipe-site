---
title: "Cabbage, carrot and apple vitamin salad"
description: "Crunchy fresh cabbage salad with carrot, apple and lemon, like the canteen classic but fresher. Cheap, vegan and ready in 15 minutes."
steps:
  - "Shred the cabbage very finely, add the salt and sugar and squeeze it with your hands for 1 minute until it releases juice."
  - "Add the coarsely grated carrot and apple."
  - "Dress with the lemon juice and oil and stir in chopped dill."
  - "Leave for 10 minutes to soften slightly, then serve."
tips:
  - "Squeezing the cabbage is the secret: it becomes juicy but stays crunchy."
---

This is the same salad every school canteen served. Made at home, it finally tastes the way it should.
