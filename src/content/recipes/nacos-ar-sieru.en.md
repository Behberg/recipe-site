---
title: "Loaded cheese nachos with beans and avocado"
description: "Oven baked nachos with melting cheese, black beans, jalapeño, fresh tomato salsa and avocado. The movie night and party snack."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C."
  - "Spread half the tortilla chips on a baking tray and scatter with half the cheese and the beans. Repeat the layers."
  - "Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, until the cheese has melted and is bubbling."
  - "Meanwhile chop the tomatoes and onion and mix with coriander and a little lime juice. Mash the avocado with the rest of the lime juice and salt."
  - "Top the hot nachos with salsa, sliced jalapeño, avocado and sour cream. Serve straight away."
tips:
  - "Layering in two tiers makes sure every chip gets cheese."
---

Nachos are eaten with your hands from one tray, all together. That alone makes them party food.
