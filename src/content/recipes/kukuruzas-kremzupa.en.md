---
title: "Sweetcorn soup with bacon"
description: "Sweet, creamy sweetcorn soup with potatoes, milk and crispy bacon. Children love its colour and flavour."
steps:
  - "Soften the onion in butter in a pan for 5 minutes."
  - "Add the diced potatoes, the sweetcorn (keeping back a few spoonfuls) and the stock. Simmer for 15 minutes."
  - "Add the milk and blend until smooth. Stir in the reserved corn and season with salt and pepper."
  - "Fry the bacon in a pan until crisp and crumble it."
  - "Serve topped with the bacon and chives."
tips:
  - "For a vegetarian version, use smoked paprika and toasted seeds instead of bacon."
---

Yellow soup with a crunchy topping is what children choose for themselves. And it is ready in half an hour.
