---
title: "Homemade eggnog"
description: "Creamy homemade eggnog with vanilla, nutmeg and rum. A Christmas drink you can also make without alcohol."
yield: "about 1 l"
steps:
  - "Whisk the yolks with the sugar until pale and thick."
  - "Heat the milk and cream until hot but not boiling."
  - "Slowly pour the hot milk into the yolks, whisking constantly."
  - "Return to the pan and heat gently, stirring, until it coats a spoon (80 °C). Do not boil."
  - "Cool, then stir in the vanilla, nutmeg and rum. Keep in the fridge for up to 5 days."
tips:
  - "For an alcohol free version just leave out the rum: children will love it."
---

Eggnog is an old winter drink. A glass by the Christmas tree makes an evening especially cosy.
