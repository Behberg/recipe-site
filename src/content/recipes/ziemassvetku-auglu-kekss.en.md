---
title: "Christmas fruit cake with nuts and spices"
description: "Dark, moist Christmas cake with dried fruit, nuts, orange and gingerbread spices. It keeps for weeks and only gets better."
steps:
  - "Chop the dried fruit and soak overnight in the orange juice and zest."
  - "Heat the oven to 160 °C and line a loaf tin with paper."
  - "Beat the butter with the brown sugar, then beat in the eggs one at a time."
  - "Stir in the flour with the baking powder and spices, then the fruit with its juice and the chopped nuts."
  - "Spoon into the tin and bake for 60 to 70 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean."
  - "Cool in the tin, then wrap in paper and foil. Let it mature for at least 2 days."
tips:
  - "Every few days you can drizzle the cake with a spoonful of rum or orange juice to keep it moist."
---

Fruit cake is the taste of waiting for Christmas. Bake it in Advent and it is ready just in time.
