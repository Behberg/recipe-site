---
title: "Chicken piccata with lemon and capers"
description: "Italian chicken piccata: thin chicken cutlets in a butter, lemon, white wine and caper sauce. An elegant dinner in 25 minutes."
steps:
  - "Halve the breasts and pound thin. Season and dredge in flour."
  - "Heat the oil with half the butter and cook the chicken for 3 minutes on each side. Set aside."
  - "Pour the wine into the pan and simmer for 1 minute, scraping the bottom."
  - "Add the stock, lemon juice and capers and simmer for 3 minutes."
  - "Stir in the rest of the butter, return the chicken and sprinkle with parsley."
tips:
  - "Cold butter at the end makes the sauce glossy and thicker."
---

Piccata is an Italian trattoria classic. The tangy, buttery sauce is what makes it memorable.
