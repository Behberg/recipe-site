---
title: Chanterelle sauce with new potatoes
description: A creamy chanterelle sauce with onions and dill. A Latvian late summer delicacy.
steps:
  - Clean the chanterelles carefully of sand and cut up the large ones. Let them drain.
  - Boil the potatoes in salted water with a sprig of dill.
  - Melt the butter in a pan and fry the chopped onion until soft.
  - Add the chanterelles and fry over a medium heat for 10 minutes, until the juices evaporate.
  - Add the sour cream, salt and pepper. Simmer for 5 minutes, until the sauce thickens.
  - Sprinkle with dill and serve with the potatoes.
---

After a good day of mushroom picking, there is nothing better.
