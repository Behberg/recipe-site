---
title: "No bake date and nut energy balls"
description: "Chocolatey energy balls made from dates, walnuts, almonds and cocoa, rolled in coconut. A healthy no bake treat with no added sugar."
yield: "about 18 balls"
steps:
  - "If the dates are dry, soak them in warm water for 10 minutes and pat dry."
  - "Blitz the nuts in a blender to coarse crumbs."
  - "Add the dates, cocoa, honey and salt and blend to a sticky mixture."
  - "With damp hands roll into walnut sized balls."
  - "Roll in the coconut and chill for 30 minutes."
tips:
  - "Keep in a closed box in the fridge for up to 2 weeks."
---

These are what to eat when you want something sweet but not cake. They taste like truffles but are made of fruit and nuts.
