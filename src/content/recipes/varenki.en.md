---
title: Potato varenyky
description: Ukrainian half moon dumplings with a potato and onion filling and sour cream.
yield: about 50 varenyky
steps:
  - Boil and mash the potatoes. Fry the onions in butter until golden and stir half into the mash. Season with salt and pepper.
  - For the dough, mix the flour, salt, water and oil. Knead for 10 minutes and let it rest for 30 minutes.
  - Roll out the dough thinly and cut out rounds.
  - Put a spoonful of filling on each round, fold over and press the edges firmly together.
  - Boil in salted water for 3 to 4 minutes after they float.
  - Serve with the rest of the fried onions and sour cream.
---

In Ukraine varenyky are also filled with cherries or cottage cheese as a sweet dish.
