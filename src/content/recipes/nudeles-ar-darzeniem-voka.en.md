---
title: "Vegetable chow mein"
description: "Fried noodles with crisp vegetables and soy sauce. Faster than ordering a takeaway."
steps:
  - "Cook the noodles according to the packet, rinse with cold water and drain."
  - "Cut the vegetables into thin strips. Mix the soy sauce with the sesame oil and sugar."
  - "Heat the oil in a wok or large pan over very high heat. Fry the garlic and ginger for 1 minute."
  - "Add the carrot, pepper, cabbage and mushrooms. Fry for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring constantly, so the vegetables stay crisp."
  - "Add the noodles and sauce and fry for 2 more minutes. Scatter with spring onions."
---

Cut everything before you start frying: there is no time once the wok is hot.
