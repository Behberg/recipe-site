---
title: Borscht
description: A rich beetroot soup with beef, cabbage and sour cream. Loved all over Eastern Europe.
steps:
  - Cover the meat with water, bring to the boil, skim off the foam and simmer gently for 1.5 hours with a bay leaf.
  - Take out the meat, remove it from the bone, cut it up and return it to the stock.
  - Add the diced potatoes and finely shredded cabbage. Boil for 10 minutes.
  - In a pan, fry the onion and carrot, then add the beetroot cut into strips, the tomato paste and vinegar. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  - Add the vegetables to the soup and boil for 10 more minutes. Finally stir in the crushed garlic, salt and pepper.
  - Let the soup stand for 15 minutes. Serve with sour cream and dill.
tips:
  - The vinegar helps keep the bright red colour.
  - Borscht tastes even better the next day.
---

Every family has its own borscht. This is the classic version with beef.
