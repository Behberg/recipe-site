---
title: Fish and chips
description: Cod fillet in a crisp beer batter with chips and mushy peas.
steps:
  - Cut the potatoes into thick chips, rinse and pat dry. Fry in oil at 160 °C for 5 minutes and take them out.
  - Boil the peas and mash them with butter and salt.
  - For the batter, mix the flour, baking powder and salt, pour in the cold beer and stir until smooth.
  - Cut the fish into portions, pat dry, coat in flour and dip into the batter.
  - Fry in oil at 180 °C for 5 to 6 minutes until golden. Then fry the chips again for 2 minutes until crisp.
  - Serve with the mushy peas, lemon and vinegar.
---

In England it is traditionally eaten on Fridays, wrapped in paper, on a harbour bench.
