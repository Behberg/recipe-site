---
title: Chakhokhbili
description: A Georgian chicken stew in tomatoes with onions, garlic and lots of fresh herbs.
steps:
  - Brown the chicken skin side down in a dry pan until the fat has melted and the skin is golden.
  - Add the onions, cut into thin half rings, and cook for 10 minutes.
  - Scald, peel and chop the tomatoes and add them to the chicken with the spices and salt.
  - Stew, covered, for 30 minutes, until the chicken is tender.
  - Finally stir in the crushed garlic and chopped herbs, wait 5 minutes and serve.
---

The name comes from the pheasant it was once made with. Today it is cooked with chicken.
