---
title: "Latvian chicken soup with dumplings"
description: "Grandma’s dumpling soup: clear chicken broth, potatoes and soft flour dumplings. Simple, cheap and deeply comforting."
steps:
  - "Cover the chicken with cold water, bring to the boil, skim off the foam and simmer gently for 40 minutes with a whole onion and the bay leaf."
  - "Lift out the chicken, pull the meat off the bones and chop it. Discard the onion and bay leaf."
  - "Add diced potatoes and grated carrot to the broth and simmer for 15 minutes."
  - "For the dumplings, whisk the egg with the milk and salt, then stir in the flour. The batter should be thick like sour cream and drip slowly off a spoon."
  - "Drop the batter into the boiling soup in small dumplings using a wet teaspoon. Simmer for 5 minutes, until they float and are cooked through."
  - "Return the chicken, season with salt and pepper and add chopped dill."
tips:
  - "Dip the spoon in the soup each time so the batter does not stick."
---

This is the soup mum made when someone was ill or it was simply cold outside. Most Latvians remember it with tiny, uneven dumplings.
