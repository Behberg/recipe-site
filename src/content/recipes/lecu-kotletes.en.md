---
title: "Red lentil patties with carrot and spices"
description: "Crisp, filling red lentil patties with carrot, onion and cumin. Vegan, cheap and great in a burger bun too."
steps:
  - "Rinse the lentils and simmer in the water for 12 to 15 minutes, until the water is absorbed and they are soft. Let cool."
  - "Fry the onion and grated carrot in oil for 5 minutes, then add the garlic and spices."
  - "Mix the lentils with the vegetables, oats, parsley, salt and pepper. Leave for 10 minutes."
  - "With wet hands shape into patties."
  - "Fry in oil for 3 to 4 minutes on each side until crisp. Serve with yoghurt sauce and salad."
tips:
  - "If the mixture is too soft, add another spoonful of oats: they soak up the moisture."
---

Lentil patties are surprisingly filling. Red lentils cook quickly and bind the mixture by themselves.
