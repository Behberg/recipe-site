---
title: Navy pasta
description: Pasta with fried minced meat and onions. A quick, cheap and very filling dish.
steps:
  - Cook the pasta in salted water and drain.
  - Fry the chopped onions in oil in a pan until golden.
  - Add the mince and fry, breaking it up with a spatula, until browned and slightly crisp. Season with salt and pepper.
  - Add the pasta and butter, stir and fry for another 2 minutes.
  - Serve with ketchup or pickled cucumbers.
---

The name comes from the galleys of the Soviet navy, but the dish is still a favourite in many homes.
