---
title: Pork shashlik
description: Pork shashlik marinated in onions and vinegar and cooked over charcoal. A classic of Midsummer night and summer barbecues.
steps:
  - Cut the meat into large 5 cm pieces.
  - Slice the onions into rings and squeeze them well with salt so they release their juice.
  - Mix the meat with the onions, spices, vinegar and water. Marinate in the fridge for at least 6 hours or overnight.
  - Thread the meat onto skewers, alternating with onion rings.
  - Cook over hot coals (with no flames) for 15 to 20 minutes, turning often.
  - Serve with fresh vegetables, bread and tomato sauce.
---

A bonfire, songs and shashlik are an essential tradition on Midsummer night.
