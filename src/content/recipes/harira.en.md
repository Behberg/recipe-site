---
title: Harira soup
description: A Moroccan tomato, lentil and chickpea soup with spices and lemon. Filling and warming.
steps:
  - In a pan, fry the onion, celery and garlic in olive oil.
  - Add the spices and fry for 1 minute.
  - Add the tomatoes, lentils and stock. Simmer for 25 minutes.
  - Add the chickpeas and simmer for another 10 minutes.
  - Stir in the herbs and lemon juice. Serve with dates and bread on the side.
---

During Ramadan, harira is how Moroccans break the day’s fast.
