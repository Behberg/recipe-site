---
title: "Easy homemade white bread"
description: "Simple homemade white bread with a crisp crust and soft crumb. Just 6 ingredients, no bread machine needed."
yield: "1 loaf"
steps:
  - "In a bowl mix the flour, yeast, sugar and salt."
  - "Add the warm water and oil and knead for 8 to 10 minutes, until smooth and elastic."
  - "Cover and leave to rise somewhere warm for 1 hour, until doubled."
  - "Knock back gently, shape into a loaf and put into an oiled loaf tin. Leave to rise for another 40 minutes."
  - "Heat the oven to 220 °C. Slash the top and spray with water."
  - "Bake for 30 to 35 minutes. It is done when it sounds hollow when tapped underneath. Cool on a rack."
tips:
  - "A tray of hot water on the bottom of the oven gives a shiny, crisp crust."
---

Nothing smells better than bread just out of the oven. And nothing is harder than waiting for it to cool.
