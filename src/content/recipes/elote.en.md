---
title: Elote (grilled corn)
description: Mexican street corn with mayonnaise, cheese, chilli and lime.
steps:
  - Grill the corn or cook it in a very hot griddle pan for 10 to 15 minutes, turning, until charred.
  - Mix the mayonnaise and sour cream.
  - Brush the hot corn with the sauce and roll it in crumbled cheese.
  - Sprinkle with chilli and coriander and serve with lime wedges.
---

In Mexico it is sold at street stalls, stuck on a wooden skewer.
