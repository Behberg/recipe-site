---
title: Gyoza
description: Japanese dumplings with a pork and cabbage filling. Crisp underneath, steamed on top.
yield: about 30 gyoza
steps:
  - Finely chop the cabbage, salt it, leave for 10 minutes and squeeze out the liquid.
  - Mix the meat, cabbage, spring onions, ginger, garlic, soy sauce and sesame oil.
  - Put a teaspoon of filling in the middle of each wrapper, wet the edge, fold over and pleat one side.
  - Heat the oil in a pan and fry the gyoza flat side down for 2 minutes, until the bottoms are golden.
  - Pour in water, cover and steam for 5 minutes, until the water has evaporated.
  - Serve with a soy sauce and vinegar dip.
---

This “steam frying” makes gyoza soft and crisp at the same time.
