---
title: Panna cotta with berry sauce
description: A silky vanilla cream dessert with a fresh berry sauce. Made without baking.
steps:
  - Soak the gelatine in cold water for 5 minutes.
  - Heat the cream with the sugar and vanilla seeds until it just starts to boil.
  - Take off the heat and stir in the squeezed gelatine until it dissolves.
  - Pour into glasses or moulds and put in the fridge for at least 4 hours.
  - For the sauce, bring the berries and sugar to the boil and mash them. Cool and pour over the portions.
---

An elegant dessert you can make the evening before.
