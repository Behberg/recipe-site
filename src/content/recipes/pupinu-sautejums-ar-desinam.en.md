---
title: "Sausage and bean stew in tomato sauce"
description: "Hearty bean stew with browned sausages in a smoky tomato sauce. One pan, 45 minutes and a cheap, warming dinner."
steps:
  - "Brown the sausages on all sides in oil in a frying pan, then remove and slice."
  - "In the same pan fry the onion for 5 minutes, then add the garlic and smoked paprika."
  - "Add the tomatoes, rinsed beans and honey. Simmer for 15 minutes."
  - "Return the sausages and simmer for 10 minutes more, until the sauce is thick."
  - "Season with salt, pepper and parsley. Serve with bread."
tips:
  - "Any sausages work: pork, chicken or smoked."
---

A simple dish that tastes like something far more complicated. Bread gets dipped until the last drop.
