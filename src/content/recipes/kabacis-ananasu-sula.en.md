---
title: "Courgettes in pineapple juice (mock pineapple)"
description: "Courgette cubes preserved in a sweet pineapple juice syrup that tastes almost like real pineapple. A nostalgic winter store and dessert."
yield: "about 4 jars of 0.7 l"
steps:
  - "Peel and deseed the courgettes and cut into 1.5 cm cubes, or into rings with a hole like pineapple."
  - "Bring the pineapple juice to the boil with the sugar and lemon juice."
  - "Add the courgettes to the syrup and simmer for 10 to 12 minutes, until translucent."
  - "Pack into sterilised jars while hot, cover with syrup and seal."
tips:
  - "Older courgettes with firm flesh work best: they do not fall apart."
---

Back when pineapples were rare, home cooks invented this trick. Even today it surprises everyone who tastes it.
