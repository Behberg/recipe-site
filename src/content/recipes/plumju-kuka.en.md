---
title: "Plum cake with cinnamon"
description: "Juicy butter cake with a thick layer of plums, cinnamon and a caramelised top. A late summer and autumn cake from garden plums."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C and line a 26 cm round tin with paper."
  - "Halve and stone the plums."
  - "Beat the butter with the sugar, beat in the eggs one at a time and stir in the flour with the baking powder."
  - "Spread the batter in the tin. Arrange the plums tightly on top, cut side up."
  - "Sprinkle with cinnamon and brown sugar. Bake for 40 to 45 minutes."
tips:
  - "Dark, slightly tart plums work best: they hold their shape when baked."
---

At the end of August, when the plum tree bends to the ground, this cake is the best way to eat them.
