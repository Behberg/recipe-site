---
title: "Pork medallions with mushroom sauce"
description: "Juicy pork tenderloin medallions with a creamy mushroom sauce. A special occasion dinner on the table in 30 minutes."
steps:
  - "Cut the tenderloin into 3 cm thick medallions, flatten lightly with your palm and season with salt and pepper."
  - "Heat the oil and half the butter in a frying pan. Cook the medallions for 3 to 4 minutes on each side. Remove and cover with foil."
  - "In the same pan with the rest of the butter fry the onion and sliced mushrooms for 7 minutes."
  - "Pour in the cream and add the mustard and thyme. Simmer for 3 minutes."
  - "Return the medallions with their juices and warm through in the sauce for 2 minutes. Serve with potatoes."
tips:
  - "Pork tenderloin is very lean: do not overcook it, it should reach 63 °C inside."
---

Pork tenderloin is one of the most tender cuts and needs only a few minutes in the pan.
