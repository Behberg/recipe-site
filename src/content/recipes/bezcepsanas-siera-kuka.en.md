---
title: "No bake strawberry cheesecake"
description: "Creamy no bake cheesecake without gelatine on a biscuit base, topped with fresh strawberries. A summer dessert you make the night before."
steps:
  - "Crush the biscuits to crumbs, mix with the melted butter and press firmly into the base of a 22 cm tin. Chill."
  - "Beat the cream cheese with the icing sugar, vanilla sugar and lemon juice until smooth."
  - "Whip the cream to stiff peaks and gently fold into the cheese mixture."
  - "Spread over the base and smooth the top. Chill for at least 6 hours."
  - "Before serving, top with strawberries and brush with slightly warmed jam."
tips:
  - "Both the cream cheese and cream must be very cold so the cake sets without gelatine."
---

A no bake cheesecake is the answer for hot summer days: the oven stays off and dessert waits in the fridge.
