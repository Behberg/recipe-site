---
title: Herring under a fur coat
description: A festive layered salad with herring, potatoes, carrots, eggs and beetroot.
steps:
  - Boil the potatoes, carrots and beetroot in their skins, cool and peel. Hard boil the eggs.
  - Dice the herring small and chop the onion finely.
  - "Layer in a dish: herring with onion, grated potatoes, a thin layer of mayonnaise."
  - "Then: grated carrots, mayonnaise, grated eggs, mayonnaise."
  - Top with grated beetroot spread with mayonnaise.
  - Let it set in the fridge for at least 6 hours or overnight.
---

A New Year table classic throughout the former USSR.
