---
title: "Garlic butter mushrooms"
description: "Browned, juicy mushrooms fried in butter with garlic, thyme and parsley. A great side for steak, on toast or with eggs."
steps:
  - "Clean the mushrooms and cut into large pieces or halves."
  - "Melt half the butter in a large pan over a high heat, add the mushrooms in one layer and fry for 5 minutes without stirring."
  - "Stir and fry for 3 more minutes until browned."
  - "Lower the heat, add the rest of the butter, the chopped garlic and thyme. Cook for 1 minute."
  - "Season with salt, pepper and parsley."
tips:
  - "Add salt only at the end, otherwise the mushrooms release liquid and will not brown."
---

Butter fried mushrooms are the simplest and tastiest way to bring out their flavour. With wild mushrooms in autumn they are even better.
