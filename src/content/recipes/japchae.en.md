---
title: "Japchae (Korean glass noodles with vegetables)"
description: "The Korean celebration dish japchae: glass noodles with beef, spinach, mushrooms and vegetables in a sweet soy and sesame sauce."
steps:
  - "For the sauce, mix the soy sauce, sesame oil, sugar and crushed garlic."
  - "Slice the beef into thin strips and mix with 2 spoonfuls of the sauce."
  - "Boil the noodles for 6 minutes, rinse in cold water and snip them shorter with scissors."
  - "In a wok fry the onion, carrot strips, mushrooms and spinach separately in oil, 2 minutes each. Then fry the beef."
  - "Toss everything with the noodles and the rest of the sauce and heat for 2 minutes. Sprinkle with sesame."
tips:
  - "Glass noodles are made from sweet potato starch, so the dish is gluten free with gluten free soy sauce."
---

In Korea japchae is on every celebration table. It is colourful, and each vegetable is cooked separately to keep its own flavour.
