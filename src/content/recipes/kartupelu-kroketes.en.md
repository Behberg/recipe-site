---
title: "Cheesy potato croquettes"
description: "Crisp golden potato croquettes with cheese and a soft mashed centre. A festive side for meat and a great way to use up mash."
steps:
  - "Boil and drain the potatoes and dry them in the pan over the heat for 1 minute. Mash without milk."
  - "Stir in the butter, yolks, cheese, nutmeg, salt and pepper. Let cool."
  - "Shape the mixture into 5 cm long cylinders."
  - "Coat in flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs."
  - "Fry in hot oil at 175 °C for 3 to 4 minutes until golden. Drain on paper."
tips:
  - "The mash must be completely cold and dry, or the croquettes fall apart when fried."
---

Croquettes turn ordinary potatoes into the jewel of a festive table.
