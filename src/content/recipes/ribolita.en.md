---
title: "Ribollita: Tuscan bean and kale soup"
description: "A thick peasant soup with white beans, kale and day old bread. A spoon stands up in it."
steps:
  - "Fry the chopped onion, carrots and celery in the olive oil for 8 minutes."
  - "Add the garlic and thyme, and after a minute the tomatoes and stock."
  - "Mash half the beans with a fork and add them to the pot with the whole ones. Simmer for 15 minutes."
  - "Add the chopped kale without its tough stalks and simmer for another 10 minutes."
  - "Stir in the torn bread and leave for 5 minutes for the soup to thicken. Serve with a drizzle of olive oil."
---

The name means "reboiled": it is at its best reheated on the second day.
