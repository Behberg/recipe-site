---
title: "Easy one bowl chocolate cake"
description: "A moist, chocolatey cake mixed in one bowl, with a glossy chocolate ganache. The safest birthday cake for beginners."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C and line a 22 cm round tin with paper."
  - "In a bowl mix the flour, sugar, cocoa, bicarbonate of soda, baking powder and salt."
  - "Add the eggs, milk and oil and whisk to a smooth batter. Finally stir in the boiling water: the batter will be thin."
  - "Pour into the tin and bake for 32 to 35 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean."
  - "For the ganache, pour the hot cream over the broken chocolate, wait a minute and stir until smooth."
  - "Pour the ganache over the cooled cake."
tips:
  - "The boiling water makes the cake extra moist and deepens the cocoa flavour."
---

This cake works even if it is the first cake you ever bake. That makes it the best first cake to learn.
