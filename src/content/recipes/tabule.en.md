---
title: Tabbouleh
description: A Lebanese herb salad with bulgur, parsley, mint, tomatoes and lemon.
steps:
  - Pour boiling water over the bulgur to cover it and leave for 15 minutes. Drain.
  - Chop the parsley and mint very finely.
  - Dice the tomatoes small and slice the spring onions thinly.
  - Mix everything and add the lemon juice, olive oil and salt.
---

In Lebanon tabbouleh is a green salad with a little bulgur, not the other way round.
