---
title: "Tuna melt open sandwiches"
description: "Oven baked open sandwiches with a creamy tuna salad topping and melted cheese. A quick lunch or dinner in 20 minutes."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C or turn on the grill."
  - "Drain the tuna and mix with the mayonnaise, finely chopped onion, pickle, lemon juice, salt and pepper."
  - "Lightly toast the bread."
  - "Spread the tuna mixture over each slice and scatter generously with cheese."
  - "Bake for 6 to 8 minutes, until the cheese has melted and is bubbling."
tips:
  - "Add a slice of tomato before the cheese for an even juicier sandwich."
---

These sandwiches are the best way to turn a tin of tuna into a warm, filling meal.
