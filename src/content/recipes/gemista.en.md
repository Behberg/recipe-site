---
title: Stuffed tomatoes and peppers (gemista)
description: Tomatoes and peppers stuffed with rice, herbs and tomato pulp, baked with potatoes.
steps:
  - Cut the tops off the tomatoes and scoop out the pulp. Remove the seeds from the peppers. Blend the tomato pulp.
  - In a pan, fry the onion and garlic, add the rice, half the tomato pulp, the herbs, salt and pepper. Cook for 5 minutes.
  - Fill the vegetables three quarters full with the stuffing and put the lids back on.
  - Put them in a dish and arrange potato wedges in between. Pour over the rest of the tomato pulp and some olive oil.
  - Bake in the oven at 180 °C for 60 to 70 minutes, until the rice is tender and the vegetables are browned.
---

A Greek summer dish that tastes best lukewarm, the next day.
