---
title: "Chokeberry (aronia) jam with apples"
description: "Dark, rich chokeberry jam with apples and lemon to soften the berries’ astringent taste. A very vitamin rich autumn preserve."
yield: "about 4 jars of 0.5 l"
steps:
  - "Wash the chokeberries, pour boiling water over them and leave for 5 minutes to soften. Drain."
  - "Boil the water with the sugar to make a syrup."
  - "Add the berries to the syrup, bring to the boil and leave to steep for 4 hours."
  - "Add the peeled, diced apples and the lemon juice. Simmer gently for 20 to 25 minutes."
  - "Fill sterilised jars while hot and seal."
tips:
  - "Pick chokeberries after the first frosts: they are sweeter then."
---

Chokeberries grow in almost every Latvian farmyard, but few people know what to do with them. This jam solves that.
