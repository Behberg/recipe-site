---
title: Cottage cheese pancakes
description: Tender, golden cottage cheese pancakes for breakfast, with sour cream and jam.
steps:
  - Mash the cottage cheese with a fork so there are no big lumps.
  - Add the egg, sugar, vanilla sugar, salt and flour. Mix into a smooth mixture.
  - With wet hands, shape small patties and coat them in flour.
  - Fry in hot oil over a medium heat for 3 minutes on each side, until golden.
  - Serve with sour cream and your favourite jam.
---

A breakfast that takes 25 minutes to make and even less to eat.
