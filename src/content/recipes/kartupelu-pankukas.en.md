---
title: Potato pancakes
description: Crisp grated potato pancakes with onion. Serve with sour cream or lingonberry jam.
steps:
  - "Peel the potatoes and grate them finely. Grate the onion into the same bowl: it stops the potatoes turning dark."
  - Put the mixture in a sieve and gently squeeze out the excess liquid.
  - Add the eggs, flour and salt and mix well.
  - Heat a generous layer of oil in a pan. Spoon in thin pancakes and fry over a medium heat for 3 to 4 minutes on each side, until golden and crisp.
  - Drain the cooked pancakes on kitchen paper. Serve hot with sour cream.
tips:
  - The thinner the pancakes, the crispier they will be.
---

One of the cheapest and best loved Latvian lunches. Lingonberry jam on the side is a must.
