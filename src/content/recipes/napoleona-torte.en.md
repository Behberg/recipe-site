---
title: "Napoleon cake with custard cream"
description: "The classic Napoleon cake with thin, flaky pastry layers and a smooth vanilla custard cream. A celebration cake that turns melt in the mouth overnight."
steps:
  - "For the layers, rub the flour, salt and cold butter into crumbs. Whisk the egg with the water and vinegar, add and quickly bring together. Divide into 8 and chill for 1 hour."
  - "For the cream, whisk the eggs with the sugar, vanilla sugar and flour and add half the milk. Bring the rest of the milk to the boil and pour in the egg mixture, stirring."
  - "Cook over a low heat, stirring constantly, until the custard thickens. Cool, then beat in the soft butter."
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C. Roll each piece of dough thinly on paper, cut out a 24 cm circle and prick with a fork. Bake for 6 to 8 minutes until golden. Bake the trimmings too."
  - "Spread the layers with cream and stack them. Cover the sides and top with cream too."
  - "Crush the trimmings into crumbs and cover the whole cake. Chill for at least 8 hours."
tips:
  - "The longer it stands, the more tender it gets. It is best on the second day."
---
