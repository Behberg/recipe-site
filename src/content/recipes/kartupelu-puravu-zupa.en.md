---
title: "Potato and leek soup"
description: "Soft, filling potato and leek soup with butter and milk. One of the cheapest and most comforting soups you can make."
steps:
  - "Slice the leeks and rinse them well to remove any grit."
  - "Gently soften the leeks in butter in a pan for 8 minutes, until soft but not browned."
  - "Add the diced potatoes, stock and bay leaf. Simmer for 20 minutes."
  - "Remove the bay leaf, add the milk and blend until smooth. Season with salt and pepper."
  - "Serve with chopped chives and croutons."
tips:
  - "Only the white and pale green part of the leek is tender; use the dark green leaves for stock."
---

Potatoes and leeks are the cheapest things in the shop, but together they make a soup that tastes like a restaurant.
