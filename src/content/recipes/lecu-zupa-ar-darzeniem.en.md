---
title: "Lentil soup with vegetables and spinach"
description: "Hearty green lentil soup with carrots, celery, tomatoes and spinach. Vegan, cheap and high in protein."
steps:
  - "Rinse the lentils. Soften the onion, carrots and celery in oil in a pan for 7 minutes."
  - "Add the garlic and cumin and cook for 1 minute."
  - "Add the lentils, tomatoes and stock. Simmer gently for 30 to 35 minutes, until the lentils are tender."
  - "Stir in the spinach and simmer for 2 minutes."
  - "Season with salt and pepper and squeeze in the lemon juice. Serve with bread."
tips:
  - "The lemon juice at the end brightens the soup: do not skip it."
---

Lentil soup is a way to eat heartily without meat. It lasts the whole week and freezes well.
