---
title: Banana bread
description: Moist, sweet banana bread made from overripe bananas. For breakfast or with coffee.
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 175 °C and line a loaf tin with paper.
  - Mash the bananas with a fork.
  - Stir in the butter, sugar, egg and vanilla.
  - Add the flour, baking soda, salt and cinnamon. Stir only until there is no dry flour left.
  - Pour into the tin and bake for 50 to 55 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
---

The blacker the bananas, the sweeter the bread.
