---
title: Plum butter (povidlo)
description: A slowly cooked, thick plum butter with only a little sugar. A filling for pies.
yield: about 4 jars of 0.5 l
steps:
  - Wash, halve and stone the plums.
  - Put them in a heavy based pan and heat gently until the juice comes out (about 20 minutes).
  - Blend the plums or push them through a sieve.
  - Add the sugar and cook over a very low heat for 1.5 hours, stirring often, until very thick and dark.
  - Add the cinnamon. Pour the hot plum butter into jars and seal.
tips:
  - It is ready when a spoon drawn across the bottom leaves a trail that does not close straight away.
---

Povidlo is the basis for the best plum pies and pancake fillings.
