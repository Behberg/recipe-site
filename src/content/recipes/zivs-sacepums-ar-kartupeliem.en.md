---
title: "Fish and potato bake with cheese"
description: "Creamy fish and potato bake with dill, a cream and egg custard and a cheese topping. A simple family dish that children like too."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 190 °C. Peel the potatoes, slice thinly and boil for 5 minutes. Drain."
  - "Butter a baking dish. Layer half the potatoes, then the fish pieces, onion half rings and dill, then the rest of the potatoes."
  - "Whisk the eggs with the cream, salt and pepper. Pour over the bake."
  - "Scatter with grated cheese."
  - "Bake for 35 to 40 minutes, until golden on top and the potatoes are tender."
tips:
  - "Parboiling the potatoes makes sure they are done at the same time as the fish."
---

Fish bake is a way to feed fish even to those who say they do not want it. Cheese and cream do the rest.
