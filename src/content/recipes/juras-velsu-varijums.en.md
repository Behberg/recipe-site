---
title: "Seafood boil with Cajun butter"
description: "A Southern US party dish: prawns, mussels, potatoes, corn and sausage boiled in spices and drenched in spicy garlic butter."
steps:
  - "Bring the water to the boil in a large pot with the salt, Cajun seasoning, bay leaves, the halved onion, the halved garlic bulb and wedges of one lemon."
  - "Add the potatoes and boil for 12 minutes."
  - "Add the corn, cut into 3 pieces, and the sliced sausage and boil for 8 minutes."
  - "Add the mussels and prawns and boil for 4 minutes until the prawns turn pink and the mussels open. Throw away any that stay closed."
  - "Meanwhile, melt the butter in a small pan, add the chopped garlic, spices and the juice of the second lemon and heat for 2 minutes."
  - "Drain everything, tip it onto a large platter or a table covered with baking paper, pour over the butter sauce and scatter with parsley."
tips:
  - "This is eaten with your hands, so cover the table with baking paper and put out plenty of napkins."
  - "Make your own Cajun mix: sweet and smoked paprika, garlic powder, oregano, thyme, chilli and black pepper."
  - "Crayfish work instead of prawns and mussels, and in Latvia they are best in August."
---
