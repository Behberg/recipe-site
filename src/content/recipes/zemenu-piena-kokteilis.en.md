---
title: "Strawberry milkshake with ice cream"
description: "A thick, pink strawberry milkshake with vanilla ice cream, like the café treat from childhood. Ready in 5 minutes."
steps:
  - "Hull the strawberries, keeping a few to decorate."
  - "Put the strawberries, ice cream, cold milk and sugar in a blender."
  - "Blend for 1 minute until frothy."
  - "Pour into tall glasses and decorate with a strawberry."
tips:
  - "The milk must be ice cold, otherwise the ice cream melts and the shake is thin."
---

A milkshake with a straw was the greatest joy of childhood. At home you can have one any time.
