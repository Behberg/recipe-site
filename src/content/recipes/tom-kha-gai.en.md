---
title: Tom kha gai (coconut chicken soup)
description: A gentle Thai coconut soup with chicken, ginger, lemongrass and lime.
steps:
  - In a pan, heat the coconut milk with the stock, bruised lemongrass and slices of ginger.
  - Simmer gently for 10 minutes to let the flavours infuse.
  - Add the thinly sliced chicken and the mushrooms and simmer for 8 minutes.
  - Add the fish sauce, lime juice and chilli.
  - Serve with coriander. Do not eat the lemongrass and ginger.
---

The mildest Thai soup, good even for those who do not like heat.
