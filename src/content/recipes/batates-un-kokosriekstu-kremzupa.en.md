---
title: "Sweet potato and coconut soup"
description: "A velvety, gently sweet soup with ginger and curry. It warms you up on a cold autumn evening."
steps:
  - "Peel and dice the sweet potato and carrots, and chop the onion."
  - "Fry the onion in the oil for 3 minutes, add the garlic, grated ginger and curry powder and fry for 1 more minute."
  - "Add the vegetables and stock. Simmer for 20 minutes, until the vegetables are soft."
  - "Pour in the coconut milk and blend until smooth."
  - "Add the lime juice, salt and pepper. Serve with pumpkin seeds."
---

Lime juice at the end balances the sweetness of the sweet potato.
