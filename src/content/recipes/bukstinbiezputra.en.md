---
title: "Bukstiņbiezputra (potato and barley porridge with bacon)"
description: "Kurzeme’s bukstiņbiezputra made from potatoes and barley groats with a smoked bacon and onion sauce and soured milk. A hearty traditional country dish."
steps:
  - "Rinse the barley and simmer in the salted water for 20 minutes."
  - "Add the peeled potatoes cut into large pieces and simmer for 25 more minutes, until everything is very soft."
  - "Pour off any excess water and mash the potatoes and barley into a thick, slightly grainy porridge."
  - "Dice the bacon and render it in a frying pan, add the onion and fry until golden."
  - "Spoon the porridge onto plates, make a well in the middle and pour in the bacon sauce. Serve with cold soured milk."
tips:
  - "The soured milk is essential: its tang balances the rich bacon sauce."
---

Bukstiņbiezputra is the heart of the Kurzeme countryside. It was everyday food, but with bacon sauce it tastes like a feast.
