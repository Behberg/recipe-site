---
title: Chocolate fondant
description: Little chocolate cakes with a hot, runny middle. A romantic dessert for two.
steps:
  - Butter two ramekins and dust them with cocoa.
  - Melt the chocolate with the butter over a water bath.
  - Whisk the eggs with the sugar until pale, stir in the chocolate, then sift in the flour.
  - Pour into the ramekins. You can keep them in the fridge until baking.
  - Bake in the oven at 200 °C for 10 to 12 minutes. The edges should be firm and the middle should wobble.
  - Wait 1 minute, turn out onto a plate and serve straight away with ice cream or raspberries.
tips:
  - Test with one ramekin first to find the perfect time for your oven.
---

The moment the spoon cuts into the cake and chocolate pours out is worth all the effort.
