---
title: "Oven baked pelmeni in sour cream with cheese"
description: "Frozen pelmeni dumplings baked in a sour cream sauce with onion under a golden cheese crust. The laziest and tastiest dinner in 40 minutes."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C."
  - "Fry the onion in butter until golden."
  - "Mix the sour cream with the water, salt and pepper."
  - "Lay the frozen dumplings in a dish in one layer, top with the onion and pour over the sour cream."
  - "Cover with foil and bake for 20 minutes. Uncover, scatter with cheese and bake for 12 to 15 minutes more. Sprinkle with dill."
tips:
  - "No need to thaw the dumplings: they cook in the sour cream sauce right in the oven."
---

This student recipe grew into a family classic. A bag of pelmeni becomes a proper dinner.
