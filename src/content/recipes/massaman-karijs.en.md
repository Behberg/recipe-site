---
title: Beef massaman curry
description: A mild, sweet Thai curry with beef, potatoes and peanuts.
steps:
  - Heat some of the coconut milk in a pan and fry the curry paste for 3 minutes.
  - Add the diced beef and brown on all sides.
  - Pour in the rest of the coconut milk and the stock and simmer, covered, for 1.5 hours.
  - Add the potatoes, onion and peanuts and simmer for another 30 minutes.
  - Add the fish sauce, sugar and tamarind. Serve with rice.
---

Massaman spices show the influence of Persian traders: cinnamon, cardamom and cloves.
