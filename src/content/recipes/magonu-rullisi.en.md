---
title: "Poppy seed swirl buns"
description: "Soft yeasted buns with a sweet poppy seed and lemon filling. A festive coffee table classic that tastes of childhood."
yield: "12 buns"
steps:
  - "Mix the dough ingredients and knead for 8 minutes. Cover and leave to rise somewhere warm for 1 hour."
  - "For the filling, simmer the poppy seeds with the milk and sugar over a low heat for 10 minutes until the milk is absorbed. Cool and stir in the lemon zest."
  - "Roll the dough into a rectangle of about 40 × 30 cm and spread the filling evenly over it."
  - "Roll up from the long side and cut into 12 pieces. Place cut side up in a tin and leave to rise for 30 minutes."
  - "Heat the oven to 190 °C, brush with egg and bake for 20 to 25 minutes until golden."
tips:
  - "Grinding the poppy seeds in a coffee grinder first makes a darker, creamier filling."
---

Poppy seed buns are the taste of old fashioned bakeries. Homemade, they are softer and packed with far more poppy seeds.
