---
title: "Pumpkin bread with cinnamon and walnuts"
description: "Moist, fragrant pumpkin bread with cinnamon, ginger and walnuts. An autumn loaf cake for coffee that smells of the holidays."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 175 °C and line a loaf tin with paper."
  - "Whisk the pumpkin purée with the sugar, eggs and oil."
  - "In another bowl mix the flour, bicarbonate of soda and spices and stir into the pumpkin mixture."
  - "Fold in the chopped walnuts and pour into the tin."
  - "Bake for 50 to 55 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean."
tips:
  - "For the purée, roast the pumpkin for 40 minutes and blend: it is sweeter than boiled."
---

When pumpkins are in every garden and market in autumn, this bread is the reason to buy one more.
