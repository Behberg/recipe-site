---
title: Churros with chocolate sauce
description: Crisp fried sticks of dough with cinnamon sugar and a thick hot chocolate sauce.
steps:
  - In a small pan, bring the water to the boil with the butter, sugar and salt. Tip in all the flour and stir until the dough comes away from the sides.
  - Cool slightly and beat in the eggs one at a time.
  - Put the dough into a piping bag with a star nozzle.
  - Heat the oil to 180 °C. Pipe 10 cm lengths and fry for 2 to 3 minutes, until golden.
  - Roll the hot churros in cinnamon sugar.
  - For the sauce, heat the cream and melt the chocolate in it. Serve together.
---

In Spain they are eaten for breakfast; we eat them whenever we feel like it.
