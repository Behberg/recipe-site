---
title: Coleslaw
description: Crunchy cabbage and carrot salad in a creamy dressing. A must with barbecue.
steps:
  - Shred the cabbage very finely and grate the carrots.
  - Mix the mayonnaise, sour cream, vinegar, sugar and mustard.
  - Toss the vegetables with the dressing and season with salt and pepper.
  - Let it sit in the fridge for at least 30 minutes.
---

The name comes from the Dutch “koolsla”, meaning cabbage salad.
