---
title: "Thick hot chocolate made with real chocolate"
description: "Thick, velvety hot chocolate made with milk and dark chocolate, topped with whipped cream. The best winter evening drink, ready in 10 minutes."
steps:
  - "In a small pan mix the cocoa, sugar and salt with a few spoonfuls of milk to a smooth paste."
  - "Add the rest of the milk and heat, stirring, until hot but not boiling."
  - "Add the broken chocolate and stir until completely melted."
  - "Stir in the vanilla. Pour into mugs and top with whipped cream."
tips:
  - "The darker the chocolate, the richer the flavour. For children use milk chocolate and less sugar."
---

After the ice rink or a snowball fight nothing warms you up like a mug of thick hot chocolate.
