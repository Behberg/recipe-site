---
title: Couscous with vegetables and chickpeas
description: Fluffy couscous with roasted vegetables, chickpeas, raisins and spices.
steps:
  - Dice the vegetables and toss them with olive oil, spices and salt. Roast in the oven at 200 °C for 25 minutes.
  - Add the rinsed chickpeas for the last 10 minutes.
  - Pour the hot stock over the couscous and raisins, cover and leave for 5 minutes. Fluff up with a fork.
  - Mix the couscous with the vegetables and add the lemon juice and mint.
---

In Morocco couscous is traditionally made on Fridays for the whole family.
