---
title: Mango sticky rice
description: "A Thai dessert: sticky rice cooked in coconut milk with ripe mango and sesame."
steps:
  - Soak the rice in water for at least 1 hour, then rinse.
  - Steam the rice for 20 to 25 minutes, until soft and translucent.
  - Heat the coconut milk with the sugar and salt until the sugar dissolves.
  - Pour two thirds of the coconut milk over the hot rice, stir and leave to soak for 15 minutes.
  - Peel and slice the mango. Serve with the rice, pour over the rest of the coconut sauce and sprinkle with sesame.
---

A Bangkok street dessert that wins you over with the first spoonful.
