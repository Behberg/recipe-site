---
title: Creamy hummus
description: Silky smooth hummus with tahini, lemon and garlic. Serve with pitta and vegetables.
steps:
  - "Rinse the chickpeas. If you have time, rub off the skins by hand: the hummus will be much smoother."
  - In a blender, blend the tahini with the lemon juice and garlic until the mixture turns pale.
  - Add the chickpeas, cumin and salt. Blend for 2 to 3 minutes.
  - Add ice cold water a spoonful at a time until the mixture is silky and creamy.
  - Spread on a plate, make a hollow with a spoon, drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with paprika.
---

The secret is ice cold water and patience while blending.
