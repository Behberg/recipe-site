---
title: "Banana oat cookies"
description: "Soft cookies made from three ingredients, with no flour, sugar or eggs. A good way to use up overripe bananas."
yield: "12 cookies"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C and line a baking tray with baking paper."
  - "Mash the bananas with a fork until smooth."
  - "Stir in the oats, cinnamon, salt and chopped chocolate. Leave for 5 minutes for the oats to swell."
  - "Spoon 12 mounds onto the tray and flatten them slightly."
  - "Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until the edges are golden."
---

The cookies do not spread, so give them their final shape on the tray.
