---
title: Aubergine parmigiana
description: Layers of roasted aubergine with tomato sauce, mozzarella and parmesan. An Italian vegetable bake.
steps:
  - Cut the aubergines into 1 cm slices, salt them and leave for 20 minutes. Pat dry with kitchen paper.
  - Brush the slices with olive oil and bake in the oven at 200 °C for 20 minutes, until soft and browned.
  - Simmer the tomato sauce with crushed garlic and salt for 10 minutes.
  - Layer the sauce, aubergines, mozzarella, basil and parmesan in a dish. Repeat, finishing with sauce and parmesan.
  - Bake in the oven at 190 °C for 25 to 30 minutes. Let it stand for 10 minutes before cutting.
---

Parmigiana is even better the next day, once the layers have had time to settle.
