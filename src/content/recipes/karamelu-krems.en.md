---
title: "Crème caramel"
description: "Silky smooth crème caramel with runny caramel, baked in a water bath. A classic dessert made from eggs, milk and sugar."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 160 °C. For the caramel, boil the sugar with the water in a small pan, without stirring, until golden brown."
  - "Immediately pour the caramel into 6 ramekins and swirl to coat the bases."
  - "Heat the milk until hot. Whisk the eggs and yolks with the sugar and vanilla and slowly pour in the milk, stirring."
  - "Strain the mixture and divide between the ramekins."
  - "Put the ramekins in a roasting tin and pour in hot water to halfway up their sides. Bake for 40 to 45 minutes, until set but still slightly wobbly in the centre."
  - "Cool and chill for 4 hours. Turn out onto plates so the caramel runs over."
tips:
  - "The water bath is essential: without it the custard becomes bubbly and firm."
---

When you turn it out and the runny caramel flows down the sides, there is always a little cheer at the table.
