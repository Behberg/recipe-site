---
title: Quiche Lorraine
description: A French tart with crisp pastry, bacon, cheese and a creamy egg filling.
steps:
  - Rub the flour and cold butter into crumbs, add the egg and water and knead quickly. Chill for 30 minutes.
  - Roll out and line a tart tin. Prick with a fork and bake in the oven at 190 °C for 12 minutes.
  - Chop the bacon and fry until crisp.
  - Whisk the eggs with the cream, salt, pepper and nutmeg.
  - Scatter the bacon and grated cheese over the base and pour over the egg mixture.
  - Bake in the oven at 180 °C for 25 to 30 minutes, until the filling has set.
---

It is good warm or cold, so it is great for a picnic.
