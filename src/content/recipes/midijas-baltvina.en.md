---
title: "Mussels in white wine and garlic (moules marinières)"
description: "The French classic moules marinières: mussels steamed in white wine with shallots, garlic and parsley. Ready in 25 minutes, served with baguette."
steps:
  - "Scrub the mussels in cold water and remove the beards. Discard any open ones that do not close when tapped."
  - "Soften the shallots and garlic in butter in a large pan for 3 minutes."
  - "Pour in the wine, bring to the boil, add the mussels and cover."
  - "Steam for 4 to 5 minutes, shaking the pan, until the mussels open. Discard any that stay closed."
  - "Stir in the cream and parsley. Serve in deep bowls with baguette to mop up the sauce."
tips:
  - "Fresh mussels smell of the sea: if they smell unpleasant, do not use them."
---

Mussels are one of the quickest festive dishes. The sauce at the bottom of the pan is as important as the mussels themselves.
