---
title: "Pork and potatoes baked in clay pots"
description: "Oven braised pork with potatoes, mushrooms and carrots in individual clay pots. Hearty, fragrant food for cold evenings."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C."
  - "Dice the pork and brown it in oil in a frying pan for 5 minutes. Add the onion, carrot and mushrooms and fry for another 5 minutes."
  - "Cut the potatoes into large cubes."
  - "Layer the meat, vegetables and potatoes in the pots, adding half a bay leaf, garlic, salt and pepper to each."
  - "Pour water to half the height of each pot and top with a spoonful of sour cream. Put the lids on."
  - "Braise in the oven for 60 minutes, until the potatoes are soft. Serve sprinkled with dill."
tips:
  - "One large cast iron casserole with a lid works instead of pots."
---

The pots go on the table straight from the oven and everyone lifts their own lid. That alone turns dinner into an occasion.
