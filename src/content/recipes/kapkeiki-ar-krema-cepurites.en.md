---
title: "Vanilla cupcakes with cream cheese frosting"
description: "Soft vanilla cupcakes with a fluffy cream cheese frosting and sprinkles. The star of birthdays and children’s parties."
yield: "12 cupcakes"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 175 °C and line a muffin tin with paper cases."
  - "Beat the butter with the sugar, beat in the eggs one at a time and add the vanilla."
  - "Stir in the flour with the baking powder alternately with the milk."
  - "Fill the cases two thirds full and bake for 18 to 20 minutes. Cool completely."
  - "For the frosting beat the soft butter with the icing sugar, then beat in the cold cream cheese. Pipe swirls on top and add sprinkles."
tips:
  - "Only frost completely cold cupcakes, or the frosting will melt."
---

Cupcakes are like little personal cakes. Every child gets their own, and nobody argues over the biggest piece.
