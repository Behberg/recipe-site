---
title: Korean fried chicken
description: Twice fried, extra crispy chicken wings in a sweet and spicy gochujang glaze.
steps:
  - Pat the wings dry, salt them and coat in starch.
  - Fry in oil at 160 °C for 8 minutes. Take them out and let them rest for 5 minutes.
  - Raise the oil temperature to 190 °C and fry again for 3 to 4 minutes, until very crispy.
  - For the glaze, boil all the ingredients in a small pan for 2 minutes.
  - Toss the wings in the glaze and sprinkle with sesame. Serve straight away.
---

Frying twice is why Korean chicken stays crispy even under the sauce.
