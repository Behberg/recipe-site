---
title: Cranberry mors
description: A tart, refreshing cranberry drink with honey. Warm in winter, cold in summer.
steps:
  - Crush the cranberries with a masher or in a blender. Strain off the juice and set it aside.
  - Cover the pulp with water, bring to the boil and boil for 5 minutes.
  - Strain the liquid and let it cool slightly.
  - Add the honey and the fresh juice and stir.
  - Serve warm or chilled with ice.
---

The juice is added at the end to keep the vitamins.
