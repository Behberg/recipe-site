---
title: "Cod fish cakes with dill"
description: "Soft, juicy fish cakes made with cod, dill and a crisp breadcrumb coating. Children love them and they freeze well."
steps:
  - "Soak the bread in the milk until soft."
  - "Mince the fish and onion or chop them very finely with a knife."
  - "Add the squeezed bread, the egg, chopped dill, salt and pepper. Mix well."
  - "With wet hands shape into patties and coat in breadcrumbs."
  - "Fry in oil over a medium heat for 4 to 5 minutes on each side until golden."
  - "Serve with lemon, potatoes and a fresh salad."
tips:
  - "If the mixture is too soft, add a spoonful of breadcrumbs and chill for 15 minutes."
---

Fish cakes are a way to get the family eating fish more often. With dill and lemon they taste of summer.
