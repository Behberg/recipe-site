---
title: "Marinated roasted red peppers"
description: "Sweet, smoky oven roasted peppers marinated in olive oil with garlic, balsamic and basil. A starter, side or sandwich filling."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 230 °C. Roast the whole peppers for 20 to 25 minutes, turning, until the skins are charred."
  - "Put them hot into a bowl and cover with cling film for 10 minutes. The steam loosens the skins."
  - "Peel, deseed and cut into strips."
  - "Toss with the oil, sliced garlic, balsamic, salt, pepper and basil. Leave to marinate for at least 1 hour."
tips:
  - "Kept in a jar under oil in the fridge they last a week."
---

Roasted peppers are a Mediterranean secret that turns a simple vegetable into a treat.
