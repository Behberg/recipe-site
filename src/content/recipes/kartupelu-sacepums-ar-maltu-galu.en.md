---
title: "Potato and minced meat bake with cheese"
description: "Hearty potato bake with minced meat in tomato sauce, a cream and egg custard and a cheese topping. A family dinner in one dish."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 190 °C. Peel the potatoes and slice them thinly."
  - "Fry the onion in oil, add the mince and cook for 8 minutes. Add the garlic, tomato paste, salt and pepper."
  - "Layer half the potatoes in a dish and season, top with the meat, then the rest of the potatoes."
  - "Whisk the eggs with the cream and a little salt and pour over the bake."
  - "Cover with foil and bake for 35 minutes. Uncover, scatter with cheese and bake for 15 minutes more."
tips:
  - "The bake is easier to cut if it rests for 10 minutes."
---

This is the dish that gets made in a big tin on Sunday and eaten on Monday and Tuesday too. Nobody tires of it.
