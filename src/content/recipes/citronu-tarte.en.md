---
title: "French lemon tart"
description: "The classic French lemon tart with a crisp shortcrust base and a silky, tangy lemon filling. An elegant dessert for special occasions."
steps:
  - "For the base, rub the flour, icing sugar and cold butter into crumbs, add the yolk and water and bring together. Chill for 30 minutes."
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C. Roll out the pastry, line a 24 cm tin, cover with paper and baking beans and bake for 15 minutes. Remove the beans and bake for 5 more."
  - "Lower the oven to 150 °C. Zest and juice the lemons."
  - "Whisk the eggs gently with the sugar, stir in the lemon juice, zest and cream. Strain."
  - "Pour into the case and bake for 25 to 30 minutes, until set but slightly wobbly in the centre. Chill for at least 2 hours."
tips:
  - "Whisk the eggs gently so no foam forms on top: the filling will be smooth as a mirror."
---

Lemon tart is the calling card of French pastry. Tart, sweet and shining like a sun on the plate.
