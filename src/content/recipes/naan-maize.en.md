---
title: Naan bread
description: Soft Indian flatbreads made with yogurt, cooked in a pan and brushed with garlic butter.
yield: 8 naan
steps:
  - Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, salt, yogurt, water and oil. Knead a soft dough and leave to rise for 1 hour.
  - Divide into 8 and roll each into an oval about 5 mm thick.
  - Cook in a very hot dry pan for 1 to 2 minutes, until bubbles appear on top. Flip and cook for another minute.
  - Melt the butter with the crushed garlic and chopped coriander.
  - Brush the hot breads with the garlic butter and serve with curry.
---

In India naan is baked in a tandoor oven. A very hot pan is a good home substitute.
