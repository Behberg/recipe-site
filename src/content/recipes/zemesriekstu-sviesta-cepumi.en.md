---
title: "Vegan peanut butter cookies"
description: "Soft cookies with the classic fork pattern and flakes of salt on top."
yield: "16 cookies"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C and line a baking tray with baking paper."
  - "Mix the peanut butter with the sugar, plant milk and vanilla."
  - "Stir in the flour and bicarbonate of soda until a soft dough forms."
  - "Roll 16 balls, place on the tray and flatten with a fork in a criss cross pattern. Sprinkle with a few flakes of salt."
  - "Bake for 10 to 12 minutes. The cookies are soft, so let them cool on the tray for 10 minutes."
---

Do not overbake: they should still look slightly underdone when they come out.
