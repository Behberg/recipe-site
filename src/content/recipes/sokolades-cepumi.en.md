---
title: Chocolate chip cookies
description: "Classic American cookies: crisp edges, a soft middle and lots of chocolate."
yield: about 20 cookies
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 180 °C and line a baking tray with baking paper.
  - Beat the butter with both sugars until pale and creamy.
  - Beat in the egg and vanilla.
  - Add the flour, baking soda and salt and mix. Stir in the chocolate.
  - Spoon balls of dough onto the tray, leaving space between them.
  - Bake for 10 to 12 minutes. The middle will look underdone, but it will firm up as it cools.
---

The golden rule: do not overbake. The soft middle is the whole point of these cookies.
