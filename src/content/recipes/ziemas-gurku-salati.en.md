---
title: "Preserved cucumber salad for winter"
description: "Crunchy cucumber and onion salad with dill, preserved in jars for winter. A rescue for a cucumber glut and a great side with meat and potatoes."
yield: "about 5 jars of 0.5 l"
steps:
  - "Slice the cucumbers into rounds and the onions into thin half rings."
  - "Mix in a large bowl with the sugar, salt, vinegar, oil, dill and peppercorns. Leave for 2 hours until the juices run."
  - "Transfer to a pan, bring to the boil and heat for 3 to 5 minutes, until the cucumbers turn olive green."
  - "Pack tightly into sterilised jars with the juices while hot and seal immediately."
  - "Turn upside down and cool under a blanket."
tips:
  - "Do not overcook: the cucumbers only need to change colour so they stay crunchy."
---

When cucumbers no longer fit in the fridge in August, this salad is the best way to keep summer.
