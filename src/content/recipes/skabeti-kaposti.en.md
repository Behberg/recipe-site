---
title: Homemade sauerkraut
description: Naturally fermented cabbage with carrots and caraway. Just cabbage, salt and time.
yield: about 3 litres
steps:
  - Remove the outer leaves from the cabbage and shred it finely. Grate the carrots.
  - In a large bowl, knead the cabbage with the salt firmly with your hands for 10 minutes, until it releases its juice.
  - Stir in the carrots and caraway.
  - Pack tightly into glass jars or a ceramic crock so the juice covers the cabbage.
  - Weigh it down and leave at room temperature for 5 to 7 days. Every day, poke it to the bottom with a wooden skewer to let the gases out.
  - When the cabbage is sour enough, move it to the fridge or cellar.
tips:
  - Use non iodised salt, otherwise fermentation may fail.
---

Homemade sauerkraut is a natural probiotic and a source of vitamins in winter.
