---
title: "Pico de gallo (fresh tomato salsa)"
description: "Fresh Mexican tomato salsa pico de gallo with onion, chilli, coriander and lime. For tacos, nachos, grilled meat and fish."
steps:
  - "Deseed the tomatoes and cut into small cubes."
  - "Finely chop the onion and chilli."
  - "Mix with the chopped coriander, lime juice and salt."
  - "Leave for 10 minutes to let the flavours meld, then serve."
tips:
  - "Remove the tomato seeds so the salsa is not watery."
---

In Mexico pico de gallo is on every table. It is fresh, bright and livens up any dish.
