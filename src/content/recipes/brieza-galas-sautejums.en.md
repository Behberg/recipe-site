---
title: "Venison stew with red wine and lingonberries"
description: "Rich venison stew with wild mushrooms, red wine, juniper berries and lingonberries. The taste of the forest for Christmas and autumn feasts."
steps:
  - "Cut the meat into 3 cm pieces, season with salt, pepper and flour. Brown in batches in oil in a heavy pan. Set aside."
  - "In the same pan fry the onions, carrots and mushrooms for 8 minutes."
  - "Pour in the wine, simmer for 3 minutes, then add the stock. Add the crushed juniper berries and bay leaves."
  - "Return the meat, cover and simmer gently for 2 to 2.5 hours, until tender."
  - "Stir in the lingonberry jam. Serve with mashed potatoes."
tips:
  - "Juniper is game’s best friend: it softens the strong gamey flavour."
---

Deer are common in Latvian forests, and in the autumn hunting season venison finds its way onto home tables too.
