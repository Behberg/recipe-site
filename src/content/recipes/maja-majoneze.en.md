---
title: "Homemade mayonnaise in 10 minutes"
description: "Creamy homemade mayonnaise from egg yolk, mustard, lemon and oil. No preservatives, tastier than shop bought and ready in 10 minutes."
yield: "about 250 ml"
steps:
  - "All the ingredients should be at room temperature."
  - "In a bowl whisk the yolk with the mustard, salt and half the lemon juice."
  - "Whisking constantly, add the oil drop by drop. Once it starts to thicken you can pour it in a thin stream."
  - "When all the oil is in and the mayonnaise is thick, stir in the rest of the lemon juice."
  - "Keep in the fridge for up to 4 days."
tips:
  - "If it splits, whisk another yolk in a clean bowl and add the split mixture drop by drop."
---

Once you make mayonnaise yourself you realise how simple it is. And salads taste completely different with it.
