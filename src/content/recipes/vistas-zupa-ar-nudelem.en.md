---
title: Chicken noodle soup
description: A clear chicken broth soup with noodles, carrots and dill. The best medicine for a cold.
steps:
  - Cover the chicken with cold water, bring to the boil and skim off the foam.
  - Add a whole onion, the bay leaves and salt. Simmer gently for 40 minutes.
  - Take out the chicken and remove the meat from the bones. Discard the onion.
  - Add the sliced carrots and celery to the broth and simmer for 10 minutes.
  - Add the noodles and meat and cook until the noodles are soft. Serve with dill.
---

Every grandmother has her own version. This is the simplest one, and that is exactly why it is so good.
