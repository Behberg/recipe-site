---
title: "Chicken and mushroom pie with puff pastry"
description: "A cosy chicken pie with mushrooms, carrots and peas in a creamy sauce under crisp puff pastry. A warm family dinner."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C. Cut the chicken into pieces."
  - "Fry the chicken, onion, carrot and mushrooms in butter for 8 minutes."
  - "Stir in the flour, then pour in the stock and cream. Simmer until thick. Add the peas, salt and pepper."
  - "Pour into a dish, cover with the puff pastry, press the edges down and cut a small hole in the top."
  - "Brush with egg and bake for 25 to 30 minutes until puffed and golden."
tips:
  - "You can make the filling ahead: it must be cool before baking."
---

When the puff pastry cracks and hot, creamy filling rises from underneath, everyone comes to the table on their own.
