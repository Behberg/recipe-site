---
title: Pączki with jam
description: Soft, fluffy Polish yeast doughnuts with raspberry jam and icing sugar.
yield: about 12 doughnuts
steps:
  - Dissolve the yeast in the warm milk with a teaspoon of sugar.
  - Add the flour, sugar, yolks, salt and butter. Knead for 10 minutes, until the dough is soft and smooth.
  - Leave to rise for 1 hour, then roll out 2 cm thick and cut out rounds. Leave to rise for another 20 minutes.
  - Fry in oil at 170 °C for 2 to 3 minutes on each side, until golden with a pale stripe around the middle.
  - Drain, fill with jam using a piping bag and dust with icing sugar.
---

In Poland, on Fat Thursday before Lent, everyone eats at least one pączek.
