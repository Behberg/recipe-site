---
title: "Flourless seed bread (no yeast)"
description: "Crunchy, filling seed and oat bread with no flour or yeast. Gluten free, vegan and just 10 minutes of work."
yield: "1 loaf"
steps:
  - "Line a loaf tin with baking paper."
  - "In a large bowl mix all the dry ingredients."
  - "Mix the water with the honey and oil, pour over the seeds and stir well. The mixture will get very thick."
  - "Press into the tin, smooth with a wet spoon and leave for 2 hours or overnight."
  - "Heat the oven to 175 °C and bake for 20 minutes."
  - "Lift the loaf out of the tin, peel off the paper, set it upside down on the oven rack and bake for another 40 minutes. Cool completely before slicing."
tips:
  - "Keeps in the fridge for up to a week, or freeze it ready sliced."
---

This bread is denser and more filling than any other. One slice with avocado or cheese is a proper breakfast.
