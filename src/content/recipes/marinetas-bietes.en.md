---
title: Pickled beetroot
description: Sweet and sour pickled beetroot with cloves. Ready for salads and meat all winter.
yield: about 4 jars of 0.5 l
steps:
  - Boil the beetroot in their skins until tender, about 45 minutes. Cool and peel.
  - Slice or dice and pack into sterilised jars.
  - For the brine, boil the water with the sugar, salt and spices. Take off the heat and add the vinegar.
  - Pour the hot brine into the jars.
  - Sterilise the jars in a boiling water bath for 10 minutes and seal.
---

A jar that saves you half an hour on any winter evening.
