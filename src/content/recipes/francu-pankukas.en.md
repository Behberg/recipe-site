---
title: French crêpes
description: Thin, flexible crêpes for sweet or savoury fillings.
yield: about 12 crêpes
steps:
  - In a bowl, mix the flour, sugar and salt. Make a well and crack in the eggs.
  - Gradually add the milk, whisking constantly so no lumps form.
  - Stir in the melted butter. Let the batter rest for 30 minutes.
  - Heat a pan, grease it lightly with butter, pour in a little batter and swirl the pan so it spreads thinly.
  - Cook for about 1 minute, flip and cook for 30 seconds more.
  - Serve with jam, chocolate spread or ham and cheese.
---

A Parisian street classic. Wonderful with homemade strawberry jam.
