---
title: "Homemade chicken stock (clear and rich)"
description: "Clear, golden homemade chicken stock made from chicken bones and vegetables. The base for soups, sauces and risotto, and it freezes well."
yield: "about 2.5 l"
steps:
  - "Rinse the chicken pieces, put them in a large pan and cover with the cold water."
  - "Bring slowly to the boil and skim carefully."
  - "Add the halved onion with its skin, the carrots, celery, bay leaves and peppercorns."
  - "Simmer uncovered over a very low heat for 2 to 2.5 hours. The stock should just tremble, not boil."
  - "Add the parsley and salt for the last 10 minutes. Strain through a fine sieve, cool and lift the fat off the top."
tips:
  - "The onion skin gives the stock a lovely golden colour."
---

Good stock is the foundation of good cooking. A pot of stock in the freezer means soup is always 20 minutes away.
