---
title: Japanese quince syrup
description: "Raw Japanese quince with sugar: a fragrant vitamin syrup for tea all winter long."
yield: about 2 litres
steps:
  - Wash the quinces and dry them thoroughly.
  - Cut them in half and remove the seeds. Slice thinly or cut into strips.
  - In a large bowl, mix the quince with the sugar and leave for a few hours, until the sugar starts to melt.
  - Pack into clean, dry jars and sprinkle a little more sugar on top.
  - Keep in a cool place or in the fridge. After 2 weeks a fragrant syrup will have formed.
tips:
  - Quince syrup is great in tea, cocktails and on ice cream.
---

The Latvian lemon: Japanese quince has more vitamin C than lemons, and the scent is unforgettable.
