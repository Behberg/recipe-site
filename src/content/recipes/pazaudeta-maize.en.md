---
title: "French toast with cinnamon"
description: "Bread dipped in egg and milk with cinnamon and fried in butter. A quick breakfast that rescues yesterday’s bread."
steps:
  - "Whisk the eggs with the milk, sugar, cinnamon and vanilla sugar."
  - "Soak each slice of bread in the mixture for 10 seconds on each side."
  - "Melt the butter in a frying pan and cook the bread over a medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes on each side until golden."
  - "Serve hot with maple syrup, berries or jam."
tips:
  - "Bread that is a day or two old works best: it soaks up more and does not fall apart."
---

In Latvian it is called “lost bread”, bread that would have been thrown away. Now it is Sunday breakfast.
