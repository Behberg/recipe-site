---
title: Meatball soup
description: A clear, warming soup with little meatballs, potatoes, carrots and dill.
steps:
  - Mix the mince with the egg, half an onion finely grated, salt and pepper. With wet hands, shape small balls.
  - Bring the water or stock to the boil with a bay leaf. Add the diced potatoes.
  - Grate the carrot, chop the onion and fry them lightly in a pan. Add them to the soup.
  - When the soup is boiling, drop in the meatballs one at a time and simmer for 15 minutes.
  - Season with salt and pepper. Serve with fresh dill and dark rye bread.
---

A soup children eat without being persuaded.
