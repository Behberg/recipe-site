---
title: "Green spinach pancakes"
description: "Bright green thin pancakes with spinach that children call dragon pancakes. Lovely with cream cheese, salmon or a sweet filling."
steps:
  - "Blend the spinach with the eggs and milk to a smooth green liquid."
  - "Stir in the flour, salt and oil. Rest for 10 minutes."
  - "Pour thin pancakes into a hot, lightly oiled pan and cook for 1 minute on each side."
  - "Spread with cream cheese and roll up or fold into triangles."
tips:
  - "You can hardly taste the spinach: they taste like ordinary pancakes, only green."
---

Green pancakes are like a magic trick: children eat them precisely because they are green.
