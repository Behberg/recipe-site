---
title: Danish rice pudding with cherry sauce
description: A creamy vanilla rice dessert with almonds and warm cherry sauce. A Christmas tradition.
steps:
  - Cook the rice in the milk with the vanilla over a very low heat for 40 minutes, stirring often. Let it cool.
  - Scald, skin and chop the almonds. Keep one whole.
  - Stir in the sugar and almonds. Whip the cream and fold it into the rice.
  - For the sauce, bring the cherries and their juice to the boil and thicken with starch dissolved in water.
  - Serve the rice cold with the warm cherry sauce.
---

One whole almond is hidden in the pudding. Whoever finds it gets a prize.
