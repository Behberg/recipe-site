---
title: Potato gnocchi with butter and sage
description: Soft homemade potato gnocchi in brown butter with sage and parmesan.
steps:
  - Boil the potatoes in their skins, peel them while still warm and push them through a ricer or mash them.
  - When slightly cooled, add the egg, salt and gradually the flour. Knead a soft dough, but do not knead for long.
  - Roll the dough into long ropes and cut into 2 cm pieces. Press ridges into them with a fork.
  - Cook in batches in salted water. When the gnocchi float, wait 1 minute and lift them out with a slotted spoon.
  - Melt the butter with the sage in a pan and heat until the butter turns nut brown. Toss the gnocchi in it and serve with parmesan.
---

The less flour, the softer the gnocchi. Add only enough to stop the dough sticking.
