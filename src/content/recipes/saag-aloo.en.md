---
title: "Saag aloo: spinach and potato curry"
description: "Indian home cooking with potatoes and spinach, cumin, ginger and turmeric."
steps:
  - "Peel the potatoes and cut into 2 cm cubes."
  - "In a large pan fry the onion in the oil for 5 minutes. Add the garlic, ginger and spices and fry for 1 minute."
  - "Add the potatoes, chopped tomato, water and salt. Cook covered for 15 minutes, until the potatoes are tender."
  - "Stir in the spinach a handful at a time and cook for 3 more minutes, until wilted."
  - "Add the lemon juice. Serve with rice or naan."
---

Frozen spinach works too: thaw it and squeeze out the excess water.
