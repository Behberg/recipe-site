---
title: Rhubarb and ginger jam
description: A tart and sweet rhubarb jam with fresh ginger and orange.
yield: about 3 jars of 0.5 l
steps:
  - Cut the rhubarb into 1 cm pieces, sprinkle with the sugar and leave overnight.
  - Grate the ginger finely. Grate the orange zest and squeeze out the juice.
  - Add the ginger and orange to the rhubarb and bring to the boil.
  - Boil for 20 to 25 minutes, stirring, until the jam thickens.
  - Pour the hot jam into sterilised jars and seal.
---

Ginger gives rhubarb a warm, slightly spicy flavour.
