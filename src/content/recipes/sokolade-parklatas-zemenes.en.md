---
title: "Chocolate dipped strawberries"
description: "Fresh strawberries dipped in melted dark chocolate and drizzled with white chocolate. A romantic Valentine’s dessert in 20 minutes."
steps:
  - "Wash the strawberries and dry them thoroughly: they must be completely dry. Leave the stalks on."
  - "Melt the dark chocolate over a pan of water or in the microwave in 20 second bursts, stirring."
  - "Hold each strawberry by its stalk, dip in the chocolate and lay on baking paper."
  - "Melt the white chocolate and drizzle thin lines over the strawberries with a teaspoon."
  - "Chill for 15 minutes until the chocolate sets."
tips:
  - "Even one drop of water makes chocolate thick and grainy, so the strawberries must be dry."
---

Chocolate strawberries are the simplest way to show you have been thinking of someone.
