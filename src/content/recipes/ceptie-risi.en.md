---
title: Egg fried rice with vegetables
description: A quick Chinese wok dish of yesterday’s rice with egg, peas, carrots and soy sauce.
steps:
  - Dice the carrot small and slice the spring onions.
  - Heat the oil in a wok, beat the eggs and scramble them quickly. Take them out.
  - Fry the garlic, carrot and peas for 2 minutes.
  - Add the rice and fry over a high heat for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring.
  - Add the soy sauce, eggs and spring onions. Finally drizzle with sesame oil.
tips:
  - Freshly cooked rice will clump. Use chilled rice from the day before.
---

The best way to use up leftover rice.
