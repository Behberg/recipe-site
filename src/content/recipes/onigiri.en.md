---
title: "Onigiri (Japanese rice balls with tuna)"
description: "Japanese onigiri: rice triangles filled with tuna mayonnaise and wrapped in nori. The perfect lunchbox and picnic snack."
steps:
  - "Rinse the rice until the water runs clear and cook in the water, covered, for 12 minutes. Rest for 10 minutes."
  - "Mix the tuna with the mayonnaise."
  - "Wet your hands with salted water. Take a handful of rice, put a spoonful of tuna in the middle and close it up."
  - "Press into a triangle, firmly but not too hard."
  - "Wrap the base in a strip of nori and sprinkle with sesame."
tips:
  - "Salted water on your hands stops the rice sticking and seasons it lightly."
---

In Japan onigiri are in every shop and every school lunchbox. They are like a sandwich made of rice.
