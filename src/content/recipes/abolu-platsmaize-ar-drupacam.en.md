---
title: "Latvian apple sheet cake with crumble (plātsmaize)"
description: "Soft yeasted sheet cake with a thick layer of apples, cinnamon and butter crumble. An autumn classic for a big family or the office."
steps:
  - "Mix the dough ingredients and knead for 8 minutes. Leave to rise for 1 hour."
  - "For the crumble, rub the flour and sugar with the cold butter into coarse crumbs. Chill."
  - "Roll the dough out and press it into an oiled baking tray (about 30 × 40 cm), forming small edges."
  - "Arrange the thinly sliced apples tightly on top and sprinkle with cinnamon and the crumble. Leave to rise for 20 minutes."
  - "Heat the oven to 190 °C and bake for 25 to 30 minutes, until the crumble is golden."
tips:
  - "Tart autumn apples such as Antonovka give the best flavour."
---

Plātsmaize is what gets baked when many people need feeding: at a working bee, a birthday or when the garden produces heaps of apples.
