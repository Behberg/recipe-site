---
title: "Homemade yoghurt without a yoghurt maker"
description: "Thick, mild homemade yoghurt from just milk and a spoonful of live yoghurt. No additives, cheaper than shop bought and ready overnight."
steps:
  - "Heat the milk to 85 °C, until steaming with tiny bubbles on top. Hold it at that temperature for 5 minutes."
  - "Cool to 43 °C: warm but not hot to the finger."
  - "Mix the starter with a few spoonfuls of warm milk, then stir into the rest of the milk."
  - "Pour into jars, close and wrap in a towel, or put in the turned off oven with the light on, for 8 to 10 hours."
  - "Once set, refrigerate. Each new batch can be started with a spoonful of the last one."
tips:
  - "For thicker yoghurt, strain through muslin for 2 hours: you will get Greek yoghurt."
---

Making yoghurt at home is an old and simple craft. The only ingredients are milk and patience.
