---
title: "Liver pancakes with onion"
description: "Soft liver fritters made from beef or pork liver with onion, served with sour cream. Cheap and iron rich, even liver sceptics like them."
steps:
  - "Rinse the liver, remove any membranes and cut into pieces."
  - "Blend or mince the liver together with the onion."
  - "Stir in the eggs, milk, flour, salt and pepper. The batter should be like thick sour cream."
  - "Heat oil in a frying pan and spoon in small pancakes. Fry over a medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes on each side."
  - "Serve hot with sour cream, mashed potatoes or buckwheat."
tips:
  - "Soaking the liver in milk for 30 minutes first takes away any bitterness and makes it milder."
---

Liver pancakes are how even children eat liver without noticing. Dip them in sour cream and the plate is empty.
