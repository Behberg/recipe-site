---
title: Blueberry jam
description: Wild blueberry jam with lemon. Dark, thick and rich in vitamins.
yield: about 3 jars of 0.5 l
steps:
  - Pick over the blueberries, rinse them and let them drain.
  - In a pan, mix the blueberries with the sugar and leave for 2 hours to let the juice come out.
  - Bring to the boil and simmer over a low heat for 15 minutes, stirring often.
  - Add the lemon juice and boil for 3 more minutes.
  - Pour the hot jam into sterilised jars and seal.
---

Blueberries you picked yourself in the forest taste completely different from shop bought ones.
