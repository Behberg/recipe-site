---
title: "Chocolate eclairs with vanilla custard"
description: "Crisp choux pastry eclairs with a smooth vanilla custard and glossy chocolate glaze. A patisserie classic you can make at home."
yield: "12 eclairs"
steps:
  - "For the custard, bring the milk to the boil. Whisk the yolks with the sugar, cornflour and vanilla, then slowly pour in the milk, stirring. Cook until thick. Cover with cling film and cool."
  - "For the pastry, bring the water, milk, butter and salt to the boil. Tip in the flour all at once and stir for 1 to 2 minutes until the dough comes away from the sides."
  - "Cool slightly, then beat in the eggs one at a time until glossy and the dough drops slowly from a spoon."
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C. Pipe 12 cm lines onto paper. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes without opening the oven."
  - "Fill the cooled eclairs with custard through a small hole."
  - "For the glaze, pour the hot cream over the chocolate and stir. Dip the tops of the eclairs in the glaze."
tips:
  - "Do not open the oven for the first 20 minutes: the eclairs will collapse and never puff up again."
---

Eclairs look like a pastry chef’s masterpiece, but choux pastry is simpler than it seems. The first time just needs patience.
