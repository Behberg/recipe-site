---
title: "Aronia syrup with cherry leaves"
description: "Deep ruby aronia (chokeberry) syrup with lemon and cherry leaves for winter. Dilute with water, stir into tea or pour over pancakes."
yield: "about 1.5 l"
steps:
  - "Pick over and rinse the aronia berries and put them in a pan with the rinsed cherry leaves."
  - "Pour in the water, bring to the boil and simmer gently for 15 minutes until the berries start to burst."
  - "Lightly crush the berries with a spoon and strain everything through muslin or a fine sieve, squeezing well."
  - "Add the sugar and lemon juice to the juice, bring to the boil and boil for 10 minutes, skimming off the foam."
  - "Pour the hot syrup into sterilised bottles or jars and seal straight away."
tips:
  - "Aronia berries are astringent. A night in the freezer before cooking makes them milder and sweeter."
  - "For a drink, dilute the syrup with water 1 to 5."
  - "The squeezed berries are still good for a compote or a pie filling."
---

Aronia berries ripen in September and make one of the most colourful and vitamin-rich autumn preserves. Cherry leaves give the syrup a gentle almond aroma.
