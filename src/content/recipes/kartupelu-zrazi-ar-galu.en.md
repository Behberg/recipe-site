---
title: "Potato zrazy stuffed with meat"
description: "Crisp mashed potato patties with a juicy minced meat and onion filling. A great way to use up leftover mash."
steps:
  - "Boil the potatoes in salted water, mash without milk and let cool. Mix in the egg and flour."
  - "For the filling, fry the onion in oil, add the mince and cook for 8 minutes. Season and let cool."
  - "With floured hands take some potato, flatten it in your palm, put a spoonful of meat in the middle and close it into an oval patty."
  - "Coat in breadcrumbs."
  - "Fry in oil over a medium heat for 4 to 5 minutes on each side until golden. Serve with sour cream."
tips:
  - "The mash must be completely cold: then it is firm and easy to shape."
---

Zrazy are one of the tastiest ways to turn yesterday’s mash into a completely new meal.
