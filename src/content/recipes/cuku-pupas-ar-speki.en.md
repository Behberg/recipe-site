---
title: "Broad beans with bacon and onions"
description: "A Latvian grandmother's summer dish: boiled broad beans with crispy fried bacon, onions, sour cream and dill."
steps:
  - "Rinse the beans, tip them into boiling salted water and boil for 15 minutes until tender."
  - "While the beans cook, dice the bacon and fry it in a dry pan for 8 minutes until crisp."
  - "Add the chopped onions and butter and fry for 5 more minutes until the onions are golden."
  - "Drain the beans and toss them in the pan with the bacon and onions."
  - "Serve warm with sour cream and chopped dill."
tips:
  - "With larger, older beans, slip off the grey skins after boiling: inside is a bright green, tender bean."
  - "Frozen beans need 8 minutes."
  - "With a glass of soured milk or kefir it makes a complete summer supper."
---

In July, when the broad beans are ready in the garden, this is one of the simplest and tastiest Latvian dishes. Beans, bacon and onions: nothing more is needed.
