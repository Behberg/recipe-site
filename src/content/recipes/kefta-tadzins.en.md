---
title: "Kefta tagine (Moroccan meatballs in tomato with eggs)"
description: "Moroccan kefta tagine: spiced meatballs in a thick tomato sauce with eggs baked on top. One pan, with bread to mop up the sauce."
steps:
  - "Mix the mince with half the grated onion, half the spices, chopped herbs, salt and pepper. Shape into small balls."
  - "In a wide pan fry the rest of the onion and the garlic in oil for 5 minutes, add the remaining spices and the tomatoes. Simmer for 10 minutes."
  - "Add the meatballs, cover and simmer for 15 minutes."
  - "Make 4 hollows in the sauce and crack in the eggs. Cover and cook for 5 to 7 minutes, until the whites set."
  - "Sprinkle with herbs and serve straight from the pan with bread."
tips:
  - "The yolks should stay runny: they become part of the sauce."
---

In Morocco tagine is eaten with the hands, scooping up meat and sauce with bread. It is a dish everyone shares.
