---
title: Blackcurrant jam
description: A thick, dark blackcurrant jam rich in vitamins. The quickest jam, and it always works.
yield: about 3 jars of 0.5 l
steps:
  - Remove the blackcurrants from their stalks and tails and wash them.
  - In a pan, boil the water with half the sugar until the sugar dissolves.
  - Add the blackcurrants and boil for 5 minutes.
  - Add the rest of the sugar and boil for another 5 to 7 minutes, stirring and skimming off the foam.
  - Pour the hot jam into sterilised jars and seal tightly.
tips:
  - Blackcurrants are high in pectin, so the jam thickens as it cools. Do not boil it for too long.
---

A spoonful of blackcurrant jam in hot tea is the best remedy for a winter cold.
