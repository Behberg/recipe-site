---
title: Lecsó
description: A pepper, tomato and onion stew for winter stores. A side for meat or a main dish with eggs.
yield: about 4 jars of 0.5 l
steps:
  - Scald, peel and blend the tomatoes.
  - Cut the peppers into strips and the onions into half rings.
  - Heat the oil in a large pan and fry the onions for 5 minutes.
  - Add the peppers and tomato purée, sugar, salt and paprika.
  - Simmer for 30 minutes, stirring from time to time. Add the vinegar at the end.
  - Pour the hot lecsó into sterilised jars and seal.
---

One of the most popular autumn preserves across Eastern Europe.
