---
title: "Pan fried white fish with lemon butter sauce"
description: "Tender white fish fried in butter until golden, with lemon, capers and parsley. A simple, elegant dinner in 15 minutes."
steps:
  - "Pat the fish dry, cut into portions, season with salt and pepper and dust lightly with flour."
  - "Heat the oil with a spoonful of butter in a frying pan. Fry the fish for 3 minutes on one side until golden."
  - "Turn and cook for 2 to 3 more minutes. Transfer to plates."
  - "Add the rest of the butter to the pan and heat until light brown and nutty."
  - "Add the lemon juice, capers and parsley and pour straight over the fish."
tips:
  - "Do not move the fish while it cooks: that way it does not stick and forms a golden crust."
---

Brown butter sauce with lemon is a French chef’s trick that turns a plain piece of fish into a restaurant dish.
