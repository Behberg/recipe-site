---
title: "Cranberry jam with orange"
description: "Ruby red cranberry jam with orange zest. Sweet and tart, great with tea, curd cheese and meat dishes."
yield: "about 4 jars of 0.5 l"
steps:
  - "Pick over and wash the cranberries."
  - "Bring the water to the boil with the sugar until dissolved."
  - "Add the cranberries and orange zest to the syrup. Cook over a medium heat for 15 to 20 minutes, until the berries burst and the syrup thickens."
  - "Fill sterilised jars while hot and seal."
tips:
  - "Cranberries are rich in pectin: the jam sets almost like jelly as it cools."
---

In autumn cranberries redden in Latvian bogs like little lanterns. A jar of jam is their most precious winter store.
