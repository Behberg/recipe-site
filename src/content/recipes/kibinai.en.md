---
title: Kibinai
description: Karaim pasties from Trakai with a lamb or beef and onion filling.
yield: 10 kibinai
steps:
  - Rub the flour, salt and cold butter into crumbs, add the sour cream and egg and knead quickly. Chill for 30 minutes.
  - Cut the meat into very small cubes (do not mince it) and mix with the finely chopped onions, salt and pepper.
  - Divide the dough into 10 pieces, roll each into an oval, add the filling and fold into a half moon.
  - Press the edges together and crimp them with your fingers into the typical braided edge.
  - Brush with egg and bake in the oven at 190 °C for 30 minutes.
---

The Karaim people came to Trakai in the 14th century, and kibinai are their calling card.
