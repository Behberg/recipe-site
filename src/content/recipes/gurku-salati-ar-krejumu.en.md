---
title: "Cucumber salad with sour cream and dill"
description: "The simplest summer salad: fresh cucumbers with sour cream, dill and garlic. Perfect with new potatoes and grilled meat."
steps:
  - "Slice the cucumbers thinly."
  - "Mix the sour cream with chopped dill, crushed garlic, salt and pepper."
  - "Pour the dressing over the cucumbers and toss."
  - "Serve straight away with potatoes or meat."
tips:
  - "If the cucumbers have thick skins, peel them in stripes: it looks pretty and tastes milder."
---

New potatoes with dill and cucumbers in sour cream is a Latvian summer lunch in its purest form.
