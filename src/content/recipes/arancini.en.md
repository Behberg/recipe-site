---
title: "Arancini (Italian rice balls with mozzarella)"
description: "Crisp Italian arancini rice balls with melting mozzarella inside. A brilliant way to use up leftover risotto."
steps:
  - "Mix the cold rice with the parmesan and one egg."
  - "Cut the mozzarella into 12 cubes."
  - "With damp hands take a spoonful of rice, flatten it, put a cube of cheese in the middle and shape into a ball."
  - "Coat in flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs."
  - "Fry in oil at 175 °C for 4 to 5 minutes until golden. Serve with tomato sauce."
tips:
  - "The rice must be cold and sticky: yesterday’s risotto is best."
---

In Sicily arancini are sold on every street corner. They look like little golden oranges, which is what their name means.
