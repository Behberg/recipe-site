---
title: Pad Thai
description: Thai rice noodles with prawns or chicken, egg, bean sprouts and peanuts.
steps:
  - Soak the noodles in lukewarm water for 20 minutes, until pliable. Drain.
  - Mix the sauce ingredients.
  - Fry the garlic and prawns in very hot oil in a wok for 2 minutes.
  - Push them to the side, crack in the eggs and scramble until set.
  - Add the noodles and sauce and stir quickly for 2 to 3 minutes, until the noodles absorb the sauce.
  - Stir in the sprouts and spring onions. Serve with peanuts and lime.
---

Everything happens very fast, so prepare it all before you heat the wok.
