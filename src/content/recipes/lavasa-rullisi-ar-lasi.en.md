---
title: "Smoked salmon and cream cheese lavash rolls"
description: "Party pinwheels made with lavash, smoked salmon, cream cheese, cucumber and dill. No cooking, ready in 15 minutes."
steps:
  - "Mix the cream cheese with chopped dill."
  - "Lay out the lavash and spread it evenly with the cream cheese right to the edges."
  - "Cover with lettuce leaves, slices of salmon and thin strips of cucumber."
  - "Roll up tightly, wrap in cling film and chill for 1 hour."
  - "Cut into 2 cm slices with a sharp knife."
tips:
  - "Chilling firms the roll up so it is much easier to cut into neat slices."
---

These look like they took hours. In reality they are ready before the guests have taken off their coats.
