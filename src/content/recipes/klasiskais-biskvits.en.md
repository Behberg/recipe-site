---
title: "Classic sponge cake"
description: "The basic sponge recipe with three ingredients: eggs, sugar and flour. Tall, airy and right for any layer cake."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 175 °C. Line the base of a 22 cm tin with baking paper and leave the sides ungreased."
  - "Whisk the eggs with the sugar, vanilla sugar and salt on high speed for 10 minutes until pale, tripled in volume and falling from the whisk in a thick ribbon."
  - "Sift in the flour in three batches, each time folding it in gently from the bottom up with a spatula so the foam stays airy."
  - "Pour into the tin and bake for 30 minutes. Do not open the oven for the first 20 minutes."
  - "The sponge is done when a skewer comes out dry. Cool it upside down in the tin, then run a knife round the edge."
  - "Before building a cake, let the sponge rest for at least 6 hours or overnight, then cut it into 2 or 3 layers."
tips:
  - "For a chocolate sponge, replace 30 g of the flour with cocoa."
  - "Eggs at room temperature whisk up far better."
  - "If the sponge sinks after baking, the eggs were not whisked enough or the oven was opened too early."
---

Most homemade layer cakes start with this sponge. There is no baking powder and no oil in it: all the lightness comes from well whisked eggs.
