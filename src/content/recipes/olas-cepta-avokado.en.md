---
title: "Baked eggs in avocado halves"
description: "Eggs baked in avocado halves with chilli and chives. Pretty, gluten free breakfast with just 3 main ingredients."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C."
  - "Halve the avocados, remove the stones and scoop out a little more flesh with a spoon."
  - "Set the halves in a small dish so they stay level."
  - "Crack an egg into each (with large eggs, leave out some of the white) and season with salt and pepper."
  - "Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until the whites are set. Sprinkle with chilli and chives."
tips:
  - "Sit the avocados on rings of scrunched foil so they do not tip over."
---

The avocado becomes a little bowl. This breakfast looks so pretty it gets photographed before it gets eaten.
