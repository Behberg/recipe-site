---
title: Redcurrant jelly
description: A clear, glossy redcurrant jelly with no seeds. With meat and on sandwiches.
yield: about 3 jars of 0.3 l
steps:
  - Wash the redcurrants along with their stalks. Put them in a pan with the water.
  - Heat for 5 to 7 minutes, until the berries burst.
  - Strain through muslin. You will get about 1 litre of juice.
  - Add the sugar to the juice and boil for 8 to 10 minutes, skimming off the foam.
  - Pour the hot jelly into small sterilised jars. It will set as it cools.
---

Redcurrants contain so much pectin that the jelly needs no gelatine.
