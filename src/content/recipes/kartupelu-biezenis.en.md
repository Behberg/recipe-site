---
title: Mashed potatoes
description: Smooth, creamy mashed potatoes with butter and warm milk. The simplest and best loved side dish.
steps:
  - Peel the potatoes, cut them into even pieces and boil in salted water for 20 minutes.
  - Drain and let them steam dry over a low heat for 1 minute.
  - Warm the milk.
  - Mash the potatoes with the butter, gradually add the warm milk and beat until the mash is fluffy.
  - Add salt and a pinch of nutmeg.
tips:
  - "Never mash in a blender: it turns as gluey as wallpaper paste."
---

The perfect mash is just potatoes, butter, milk and a little patience.
