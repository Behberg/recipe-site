---
title: Red lentil dal
description: A gentle Indian lentil curry with turmeric, garlic and tomatoes. Cheap, vegan and very filling.
steps:
  - Rinse the lentils and cook them in water with the turmeric for 20 minutes, until they fall apart.
  - In a pan, fry the cumin in oil for 30 seconds, add the onion and fry until golden.
  - Add the garlic, ginger and tomatoes and cook for 5 minutes. Stir in the garam masala.
  - Add the vegetables to the lentils, season with salt and simmer for another 5 minutes.
  - Serve with rice or naan bread and fresh coriander.
---

In India dal is everyday food, eaten almost every day.
