---
title: "Curd cheese and raspberry dessert in glasses"
description: "Layered curd cheese and yoghurt dessert with raspberries and biscuit crumbs in glasses. Light, fresh and ready in 15 minutes with no baking."
steps:
  - "Blend the curd cheese with the yoghurt, honey and vanilla sugar into a smooth, airy cream."
  - "Crush half the raspberries with a fork and leave the rest whole."
  - "Crush the biscuits into coarse crumbs."
  - "In glasses layer the crumbs, cream, crushed raspberries and more cream."
  - "Top with whole raspberries and a few crumbs. Serve straight away or chill."
tips:
  - "If the curd cheese is grainy, blending is essential: it turns the cream as smooth as cheesecake."
---

This is the dessert you can make while guests drink their coffee. And it looks like it came from a patisserie.
