---
title: "Vegetable rice (colourful side dish)"
description: "Fluffy rice with carrots, pepper, peas and sweetcorn, cooked in one pan. A colourful, cheap side for chicken, fish or meat."
steps:
  - "Rinse the rice until the water runs clear."
  - "In a pan fry the onion, carrot and pepper cubes in oil for 5 minutes."
  - "Add the rice and turmeric and cook for 1 minute, stirring."
  - "Pour in the hot stock, add salt, bring to the boil, cover and simmer gently for 15 minutes."
  - "Add the peas and sweetcorn, cover and leave for 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork."
tips:
  - "Turmeric gives a lovely yellow colour but hardly changes the flavour."
---

This rice turns a plain chicken patty or piece of fish into a colourful, complete meal.
