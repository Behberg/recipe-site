---
title: "Crab stick salad with rice and sweetcorn"
description: "The classic crab stick salad with rice, sweetcorn, eggs and fresh cucumber. A party table favourite, ready in 20 minutes."
steps:
  - "Cook the rice and let it cool completely. Hard boil the eggs and cool them."
  - "Dice the crab sticks, eggs and cucumber and chop the spring onions."
  - "Drain the sweetcorn."
  - "Mix everything in a bowl with the mayonnaise and season with salt and pepper."
  - "Chill for 30 minutes before serving."
tips:
  - "The fresh cucumber gives crunch: add it just before serving so it does not release water."
---

A New Year table in the nineties was unthinkable without crab stick salad. It is still the first bowl to empty.
