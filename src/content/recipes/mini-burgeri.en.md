---
title: "Party mini burgers (sliders)"
description: "Small, juicy beef burgers with cheddar, pickle and tomato in mini buns. A party and birthday hit that fits in two bites."
yield: "12 mini burgers"
steps:
  - "Divide the mince into 12 and shape into thin patties slightly larger than the buns. Press a small dent in the middle."
  - "Season with salt and pepper just before cooking."
  - "Cook in a hot pan or on the grill for 2 minutes on each side. Add half a slice of cheese in the last minute."
  - "Split and toast the buns."
  - "Assemble: bun, sauce, lettuce, cheesy patty, tomato slice, pickle. Secure with a cocktail stick."
tips:
  - "The dent in the middle stops the patty puffing up into a ball as it cooks."
---

Mini burgers are the first thing to vanish at a party. The small size means everyone can take two.
