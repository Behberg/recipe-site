---
title: Greek yogurt with honey and nuts
description: Thick Greek yogurt with honey, toasted walnuts and fresh berries. Breakfast in 3 minutes.
steps:
  - Toast the walnuts in a dry pan for 2 minutes and chop them roughly.
  - Divide the yogurt between bowls.
  - Drizzle with honey and sprinkle with the nuts, berries and a pinch of cinnamon.
---

In Greek tavernas this is served as dessert. At home it makes a great breakfast.
