---
title: "Curd cheese doughnut balls"
description: "Golden, soft curd cheese doughnut balls fried in oil and dusted with icing sugar. A childhood treat, ready in 35 minutes with no yeast."
yield: "about 30 balls"
steps:
  - "Mix the curd cheese with the eggs, sugar, vanilla sugar and salt."
  - "Stir in the flour with the baking powder to a soft, slightly sticky dough."
  - "Heat 4 cm of oil in a pan to 170 °C."
  - "With oiled hands or two spoons shape walnut sized balls."
  - "Fry in batches for 4 to 5 minutes, turning, until golden brown. Drain on kitchen paper."
  - "Dust with icing sugar and serve warm."
tips:
  - "If the oil is too hot, the balls burn outside and stay raw inside."
---

Warm curd cheese balls in icing sugar are the smell that takes you straight back to grandma’s kitchen.
