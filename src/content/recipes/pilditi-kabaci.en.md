---
title: "Stuffed courgette boats with meat and cheese"
description: "Courgette boats filled with minced meat, tomatoes and cheese, baked in the oven. A summer dinner straight from the garden."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C. Halve the courgettes lengthways and scoop out the middle, leaving a 1 cm border. Chop the flesh."
  - "Fry the onion in oil, add the meat and cook for 7 minutes."
  - "Add the courgette flesh, chopped tomatoes, garlic, oregano, salt and pepper. Simmer for 5 minutes."
  - "Fill the courgette boats with the meat and lay them in a roasting tin."
  - "Scatter with cheese and bake for 25 minutes, until the courgettes are tender and the cheese is golden."
tips:
  - "Large, overgrown courgettes are best: there is more room for the filling."
---

When a courgette has grown as big as a child’s arm in August, it is perfect for stuffing.
