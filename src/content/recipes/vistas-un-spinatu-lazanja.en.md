---
title: "Chicken and spinach lasagne with béchamel"
description: "Creamy white lasagne with chicken, spinach, béchamel sauce and melting mozzarella. A milder alternative to classic lasagne for family dinners."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 190 °C. Cut the chicken into pieces and fry in oil with the onion and garlic for 8 minutes. Add the spinach until wilted. Season."
  - "For the béchamel, melt the butter in a pan, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute. Gradually whisk in the milk until the sauce thickens. Season with salt and nutmeg."
  - "Spread a spoonful of sauce in the base of a dish, then add lasagne sheets, the chicken and spinach, sauce and mozzarella."
  - "Repeat the layers 3 times. Finish with sheets, sauce, mozzarella and parmesan."
  - "Bake for 40 to 45 minutes, until golden on top. Rest for 10 minutes before cutting."
tips:
  - "Squeeze the spinach well, otherwise the lasagne turns watery."
---

White lasagne is for those who find the classic tomato version too heavy. It is mild and very comforting.
