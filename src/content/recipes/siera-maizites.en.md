---
title: Brazilian cheese breads (pão de queijo)
description: Small, chewy cheese breads made from a starch dough. Hollow and stretchy inside, crisp outside.
yield: about 20 cheese breads
steps:
  - Bring the milk to the boil with the oil and salt in a small pan.
  - Pour the hot liquid over the starch and stir. The mixture will be sticky. Let it cool for 10 minutes.
  - Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the cheese, until the dough is smooth and sticky.
  - With oiled hands, roll into balls and place on a baking tray.
  - Bake in the oven at 200 °C for 20 to 25 minutes, until golden and puffed.
tips:
  - Tapioca starch can be found in Asian food shops. Potato starch will not work.
---

In Brazil they are eaten for breakfast with coffee. One is never enough.
