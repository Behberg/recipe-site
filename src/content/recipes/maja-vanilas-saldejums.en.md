---
title: "No churn vanilla ice cream"
description: "Creamy homemade vanilla ice cream from 3 ingredients, with no ice cream machine and no stirring in the freezer. Just whip and freeze."
steps:
  - "Whip the cold cream to stiff peaks."
  - "In another bowl mix the condensed milk with the vanilla and salt."
  - "Stir a third of the whipped cream into the condensed milk, then gently fold in the rest so the mixture stays airy."
  - "Pour into a loaf tin or box, cover with cling film and freeze for at least 6 hours."
  - "Leave at room temperature for 5 minutes before scooping."
tips:
  - "Before freezing you can fold in crushed biscuits, berry purée or chocolate chips."
---

This ice cream is so creamy that nobody believes it is not shop bought. And it needs just three things.
