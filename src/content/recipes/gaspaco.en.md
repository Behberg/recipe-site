---
title: Gazpacho
description: A cold Spanish tomato soup with cucumber, pepper and olive oil. For hot summer days.
steps:
  - Roughly chop the vegetables. Soak the bread in water and squeeze it out.
  - Blend everything together until very smooth.
  - With the blender running, pour in the olive oil in a thin stream. Add the vinegar and salt.
  - Pass through a sieve if you want a silky texture.
  - Chill for at least 2 hours. Serve with diced cucumber and drops of olive oil.
---

In Andalusia it is drunk from a glass on a hot afternoon.
