---
title: Garlic rye croutons with cheese dip
description: Crisp dark rye bread croutons with garlic and a creamy cheese dip. A Latvian pub classic.
steps:
  - Cut the rye bread into long strips.
  - Heat oil in a pan and fry the bread in batches for 2 to 3 minutes, until crisp.
  - Rub the hot croutons with a cut clove of garlic and salt them lightly.
  - For the dip, mix the processed cheese, mayonnaise, sour cream and crushed garlic until smooth.
  - Serve the croutons warm with the dip.
---

Perfect with beer or kvass when friends gather round the table.
