---
title: "White chocolate and walnut blondies"
description: "Blondies are the pale sister of brownies, with caramel notes from brown sugar, white chocolate and walnuts. Moist, chewy and very easy."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 175 °C and line a 20 × 20 cm tin with parchment."
  - "Melt the butter and mix with the brown sugar. Stir in the eggs and vanilla."
  - "Add the flour and salt and mix only until combined."
  - "Stir in the chopped white chocolate and walnuts. Spread in the tin."
  - "Bake for 22 to 25 minutes until the edges are golden but the middle is still soft. Cool and cut into 16 squares."
tips:
  - "Do not overbake: blondies firm up as they cool."
---

For those who find brownies too heavy, blondies are just right: just as chewy, with a note of caramel.
