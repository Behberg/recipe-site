---
title: "Homemade chocolate pudding"
description: "Thick, silky homemade chocolate pudding made with milk, cocoa and chocolate. Ready in 15 minutes and far better than shop bought."
steps:
  - "In a pan mix the sugar, cocoa, cornflour and salt. Stir in the milk a little at a time so no lumps form."
  - "Heat over a medium heat, stirring constantly, until it boils and thickens, about 6 minutes."
  - "Cook for 1 more minute, take off the heat and stir in the broken chocolate, butter and vanilla."
  - "Divide between bowls. To stop a skin forming, press cling film onto the surface."
  - "Serve warm or chilled with whipped cream."
tips:
  - "The cling film must touch the surface of the pudding so it cools without a skin."
---

Warm chocolate pudding is the best spoonful of comfort on a rainy evening.
