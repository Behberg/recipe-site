---
title: Rhubarb crumble cake
description: A tart and sweet rhubarb cake with a crumble topping. The simplest summer cake.
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 180 °C. Line a tin with baking paper.
  - Beat the butter with the sugar until pale, add the eggs one at a time, then the flour with the baking powder.
  - Spread the batter in the tin. Scatter the rhubarb, cut into 1 cm pieces, on top.
  - For the crumble, rub the flour, sugar and cold butter together with your fingers into a lumpy mixture and scatter it over the rhubarb.
  - Bake for 35 to 40 minutes, until the top is golden and a wooden skewer comes out dry.
  - Serve lukewarm with whipped cream or vanilla ice cream.
---

When the rhubarb starts growing in the garden, this cake gets baked every week.
