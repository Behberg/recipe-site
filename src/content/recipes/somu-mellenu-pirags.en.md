---
title: Finnish blueberry pie
description: A thin shortcrust pie with blueberries and a sour cream filling. Quick to make and very juicy.
steps:
  - Beat the butter with the sugar, add the egg, then the flour with the baking powder.
  - Press the dough into the base and sides of a pie tin.
  - Scatter over the blueberries.
  - Whisk the sour cream, egg, sugar and vanilla sugar and pour over the blueberries.
  - Bake in the oven at 200 °C for 25 to 30 minutes, until the filling has set. Serve lukewarm.
---

In Finland this pie is a symbol of summer, just like rhubarb cake is here.
