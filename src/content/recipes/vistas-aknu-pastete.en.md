---
title: "Homemade chicken liver pâté"
description: "Silky smooth homemade chicken liver pâté with butter, onion and carrot. A cheap, tasty sandwich spread and party starter."
steps:
  - "In a frying pan fry the onion and grated carrot in half the butter for 8 minutes."
  - "Add the trimmed livers and thyme and fry for 5 to 6 minutes, until the livers are just cooked."
  - "Pour in the cream and add the nutmeg, salt and pepper. Simmer for 2 minutes."
  - "Remove the thyme and blend with the rest of the soft butter until completely smooth."
  - "Spoon into small dishes, cover with cling film and chill for at least 3 hours."
tips:
  - "To keep the pâté longer, seal the surface with melted butter."
---

Homemade pâté costs a fraction of the shop price and tastes much better. On rye bread with a pickle it is unbeatable.
