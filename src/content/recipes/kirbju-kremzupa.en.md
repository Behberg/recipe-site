---
title: Cream of pumpkin soup
description: A velvety orange pumpkin soup with ginger, coconut milk and toasted pumpkin seeds.
steps:
  - Dice the pumpkin, chop the onion and garlic and grate the ginger.
  - In a pan, fry the onion, garlic and ginger in olive oil for 3 minutes.
  - Add the pumpkin and stock. Simmer for 20 minutes, until the pumpkin is soft.
  - Blend until smooth and stir in the coconut milk, salt and pepper.
  - Toast the pumpkin seeds in a dry pan and scatter them over the soup.
---
