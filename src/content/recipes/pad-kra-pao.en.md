---
title: "Pad kra pao (Thai basil chicken)"
description: "A Thai street food hit: spicy minced chicken with garlic, chilli and basil, served with rice and a crispy fried egg. Ready in 20 minutes."
steps:
  - "Chop the garlic and chilli. Mix the soy, oyster and fish sauces with the sugar."
  - "Heat a spoonful of oil in a wok and fry the garlic and chilli for 30 seconds."
  - "Add the chicken and fry over a high heat for 5 minutes, breaking it up."
  - "Add the sauce, cook for 1 minute, take off the heat and stir in the basil leaves."
  - "Fry the eggs in the rest of the oil in a very hot pan until crispy at the edges. Serve with rice."
tips:
  - "In Thailand holy basil is used, but the dish is just as good with ordinary basil."
---

In Thailand this is what people order when they do not know what they want. It never disappoints.
