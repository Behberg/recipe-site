---
title: "Cold cucumber and yoghurt soup with dill"
description: "Refreshing cold cucumber soup with yoghurt, dill, garlic and walnuts. Ready in 15 minutes with no cooking, for hot days."
steps:
  - "Coarsely grate the cucumbers or cut them into small cubes."
  - "Whisk the yoghurt with the cold water, crushed garlic and salt."
  - "Stir in the cucumbers and chopped dill."
  - "Chill in the fridge for at least 30 minutes."
  - "Serve with chopped walnuts and a drizzle of oil. Add an ice cube if you like."
tips:
  - "Kefir works instead of yoghurt for a thinner, tangier soup."
---

When the thermometer climbs over 25 degrees, nobody wants hot soup. This is the answer.
