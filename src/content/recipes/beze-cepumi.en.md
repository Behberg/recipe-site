---
title: "Crisp meringue cookies"
description: "Snow white, crisp meringues made from egg whites and sugar. A great way to use up leftover whites and a base for cake decorations."
yield: "about 30 meringues"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 100 °C and line a baking tray with paper."
  - "In a clean, dry bowl whisk the egg whites with the lemon juice to soft peaks."
  - "Still whisking, add the sugar a spoonful at a time. Whisk for 5 more minutes until stiff, glossy and the sugar has dissolved."
  - "Sift over the cornflour and fold it in gently."
  - "Spoon or pipe small mounds onto the paper."
  - "Dry in the oven for 80 to 90 minutes, then turn the oven off and leave the meringues inside until completely cool."
tips:
  - "If you can feel sugar grains when you rub the mixture between your fingers, keep whisking."
---

A recipe using yolks always leaves whites behind. Meringues are the best thing that can happen to them.
