---
title: Lingonberry jam with apples
description: A tart, slightly bitter lingonberry jam with apples. A must with meat and potato pancakes.
yield: about 4 jars of 0.5 l
steps:
  - Pick over and rinse the lingonberries. Peel the apples and cut them into small pieces.
  - Boil the water with the sugar until it becomes a syrup.
  - Add the lingonberries and apples. Simmer over a low heat for 25 to 30 minutes, until the apples turn translucent.
  - Pour the hot jam into sterilised jars and seal.
---

Lingonberries are a natural preservative, so this jam keeps very well.
