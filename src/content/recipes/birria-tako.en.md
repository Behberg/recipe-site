---
title: "Birria tacos with beef and consommé"
description: "Mexican tacos with slow-braised beef in a chilli marinade, fried until crisp and served with hot broth for dipping."
steps:
  - "Cut the beef into 4 or 5 large pieces and rub with salt."
  - "Remove the stalks and seeds from the chillies, cover with hot water and soak for 15 minutes."
  - "Blend the softened chillies with the tomatoes, half the onion, the garlic, cumin, oregano, vinegar and 250 ml of water until smooth."
  - "Put the beef in a large pot, pour over the chilli sauce, add the cinnamon, bay leaves and the rest of the water. Bring to the boil, cover and braise over low heat for 3 hours until the meat falls apart."
  - "Lift out the beef and shred it with two forks. Spoon the red fat off the top of the broth into a bowl."
  - "Dip a tortilla in the fat, lay it in a hot pan, add cheese and beef and fold in half. Fry for 2 minutes on each side until crisp."
  - "Serve with a cup of hot broth for dipping, the other half of the onion chopped, coriander and lime wedges."
tips:
  - "Dried Mexican chillies are easy to order online. Without them, use 2 tablespoons of sweet paprika and 1 teaspoon of chilli flakes."
  - "In a slow cooker the beef needs 8 hours on low."
  - "Braise the beef a day ahead: it tastes even better the next day."
---

Birria comes from the Mexican state of Jalisco, but it went global when videos of red, crispy tacos dunked in hot broth started filling social media.
