---
title: Pea soup with smoked meat
description: A thick, creamy yellow split pea soup with smoked meat and croutons.
steps:
  - Rinse the peas and soak them for 2 hours or overnight.
  - Cover the meat with water, bring to the boil and simmer for 30 minutes.
  - Add the drained peas and simmer for 40 minutes, stirring from time to time, until the peas start to break down.
  - Add the diced potatoes, grated carrot and fried onion. Simmer for another 20 minutes.
  - Take out the meat, cut it up and return it. Season with salt and pepper.
  - Serve with crispy croutons.
---

For a smoother soup, blend part of it and stir it back in.
