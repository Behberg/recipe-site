---
title: Sea buckthorn syrup
description: An orange, tangy sea buckthorn syrup made without cooking. A real vitamin boost for tea and smoothies.
yield: about 1.5 litres
steps:
  - Remove the sea buckthorn berries from the twigs and rinse.
  - Blend or crush the berries.
  - Push the pulp through a sieve to remove the seeds and skins.
  - Add the sugar to the juice and stir until it has completely dissolved.
  - Pour into clean bottles and keep in the fridge.
tips:
  - The berries come off the branches more easily if you freeze them first.
---

Sea buckthorn is one of the richest sources of vitamin C growing in Latvia.
