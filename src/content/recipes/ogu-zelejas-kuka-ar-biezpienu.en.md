---
title: "No bake curd cheese cake with berry jelly"
description: "A colourful no bake curd cheese cake with a biscuit base, soft cream and a glossy berry jelly topping. The jewel of the summer party table."
steps:
  - "Crush the biscuits, mix with the butter and press into a 24 cm springform tin. Chill."
  - "Soak the gelatine in the water for 10 minutes, then dissolve it over a gentle heat."
  - "Blend the curd cheese with the sour cream and sugar and stir in the gelatine. Pour onto the base and chill for 1 hour."
  - "Arrange the berries on top."
  - "Make the jelly according to the packet, cool to room temperature and pour carefully over the berries. Chill for 4 hours."
tips:
  - "The jelly must be cool but still liquid, or it will melt the cream."
---

This cake is an easy way to make something for a children’s party that looks like it came from a patisserie.
