---
title: "Smash burgers with crispy edges"
description: "Thin burgers smashed onto a hot pan, with a crisp crust, melted cheddar and a simple burger sauce."
steps:
  - "For the sauce, mix the mayonnaise, ketchup, mustard and chopped pickle."
  - "Divide the beef into 8 balls of 75 g. Do not knead or salt the meat beforehand so it stays loose."
  - "Split the buns, butter the cut sides and toast them in a pan for 1 minute. Set aside."
  - "Heat a cast iron pan or griddle until very hot. Add 2 balls, cover with a piece of baking paper and press down hard with a flat spatula or the base of a pan into 1 cm thick patties."
  - "Season with salt and pepper and cook for 2 minutes until there is a dark crust underneath. Flip, put a slice of cheese on each and cook for 1 more minute."
  - "Spread sauce on the bottom bun, stack two patties, add onion rings and pickles and close with the top bun."
tips:
  - "The pan must be as hot as possible: the crispy crust is the whole point of a smash burger."
  - "Smash only once, in the first 10 seconds. Pressing later squeezes out the juices."
  - "Lay a very thin slice of onion on the ball before smashing so it cooks into the meat."
---

Smash burgers are the quickest way to make a burger at home that tastes like the best burger joint. The secret is a hot pan and one hard press.
