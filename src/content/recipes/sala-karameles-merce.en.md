---
title: "Salted caramel sauce"
description: "Silky salted caramel sauce made from sugar, cream and butter. Wonderful with ice cream, pancakes, cakes and apple desserts."
yield: "about 300 ml"
steps:
  - "Warm the cream."
  - "Put the sugar and water in a heavy based pan. Heat over a medium heat without stirring, just swirling the pan, until the sugar turns amber."
  - "Take off the heat and carefully pour in the warm cream, stirring. It will bubble up strongly."
  - "Stir in the butter and salt until smooth."
  - "Let it cool. Keep in a jar in the fridge for up to 2 weeks."
tips:
  - "Be careful: hot caramel is extremely hot. The cream must be warm, or the caramel will seize."
---

A jar of salted caramel in the fridge turns any simple dessert into something special.
