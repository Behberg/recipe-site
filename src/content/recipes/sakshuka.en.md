---
title: Shakshuka
description: Eggs slowly poached in a spicy tomato and pepper sauce. The perfect weekend breakfast.
steps:
  - Heat oil in a pan and fry the sliced onion and pepper for 6 minutes.
  - Add the garlic and spices and fry for another minute, until fragrant.
  - Add the tomatoes and salt and simmer for 10 minutes, until the sauce thickens.
  - Make four hollows with a spoon and crack an egg into each.
  - Cover and cook for 5 to 7 minutes, until the whites are set but the yolks are still runny.
  - Sprinkle with feta and herbs and serve with bread.
---

One pan, few dirty dishes and plenty of flavour.
