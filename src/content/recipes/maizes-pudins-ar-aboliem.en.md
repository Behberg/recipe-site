---
title: "Bread pudding with apples and cinnamon"
description: "Warm bread pudding made from yesterday’s bread with apples, raisins and cinnamon in an egg and milk custard. Nothing wasted, dessert made."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C and butter a baking dish."
  - "Cut the bread into cubes and the apples into thin slices."
  - "Whisk the eggs with the milk, sugar and vanilla sugar."
  - "Layer the bread, apples and raisins in the dish and sprinkle with cinnamon. Pour over the egg mixture and leave to soak for 15 minutes."
  - "Dot with butter and bake for 35 to 40 minutes, until golden on top and set in the middle."
tips:
  - "The drier the bread, the more it soaks up and the creamier the pudding."
---

Grandmas never threw bread away. It became this pudding, which tastes better than many cakes.
