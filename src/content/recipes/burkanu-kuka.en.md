---
title: Carrot cake with cream cheese frosting
description: A moist, spiced carrot cake with walnuts and sweet cream cheese frosting.
steps:
  - Finely grate the carrots. Chop the nuts.
  - Whisk the eggs with the sugar and pour in the oil.
  - Fold in the flour with the baking powder and spices, then the carrots and nuts.
  - Pour into a tin and bake in the oven at 175 °C for 40 to 45 minutes. Let it cool completely.
  - For the frosting, beat the cream cheese, butter and icing sugar. Cover the cake and decorate with nuts.
---

The carrots make the cake moist and naturally sweet. You can hardly taste the carrots themselves.
