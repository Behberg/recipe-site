---
title: Hot cocoa with milk
description: A warm, creamy cocoa drink made with milk. The taste of childhood on a cold evening.
steps:
  - In a mug or small pan, mix the cocoa, sugar and salt with 3 tablespoons of milk into a smooth paste.
  - Add the rest of the milk and heat, stirring, until the drink is hot but not boiling.
  - Serve with whipped cream or marshmallows.
---

Making a paste of cocoa and a little milk is the secret to lump free cocoa.
