---
title: "Tonkatsu (Japanese breaded pork cutlet)"
description: "Japanese tonkatsu: juicy pork cutlet in an extra crispy panko coating with a sweet and tangy sauce, fresh cabbage and rice."
steps:
  - "Pound the chops lightly to 1.5 cm thick and snip the edges so they do not curl. Season with salt and pepper."
  - "Dredge in flour, then beaten egg, then panko, pressing it on well."
  - "Heat 1.5 cm of oil in a frying pan to 170 °C. Fry for 3 to 4 minutes on each side until golden brown."
  - "Rest on a rack for 3 minutes, then slice into strips."
  - "For the sauce mix the ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, soy sauce and honey. Serve with finely shredded cabbage and rice."
tips:
  - "Panko is coarser than ordinary breadcrumbs, and that is what gives the big crunch."
---

Tonkatsu is Japan’s answer to schnitzel. It has its own restaurants that serve nothing else.
