---
title: Oyakodon (chicken and egg rice bowl)
description: Chicken and onions in a sweet and savoury broth with softly set egg on steamed rice.
steps:
  - In a small pan, bring the stock to the boil with the soy sauce, mirin and sugar.
  - Add the thinly sliced onion and the chicken cut into small pieces. Simmer for 6 minutes.
  - Beat the eggs only lightly and pour them into the pan in a circle.
  - Cover and cook for 1 minute, until the eggs are half set and creamy.
  - Carefully slide onto the rice in a bowl and sprinkle with spring onions.
---

The name means “parent and child”: chicken and egg in one bowl.
