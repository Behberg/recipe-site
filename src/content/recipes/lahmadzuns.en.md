---
title: Lahmacun
description: A thin Turkish pizza with a spicy topping of minced meat, tomato and parsley. Eaten rolled up with salad.
yield: 6 lahmacun
steps:
  - Mix the dough ingredients, knead until smooth and leave to rise for 1 hour.
  - Chop or mince the onion, tomato, pepper and parsley very finely. Mix with the meat, tomato paste, spices and salt.
  - Divide the dough into 6 and roll each piece into a very thin oval.
  - Spread the meat mixture thinly right up to the edges.
  - Bake on a preheated tray in the oven at 250 °C for 6 to 8 minutes.
  - Squeeze over some lemon, add the salad and roll up.
---

In Istanbul lahmacun is eaten rolled up with ayran, and it costs less than a coffee.
