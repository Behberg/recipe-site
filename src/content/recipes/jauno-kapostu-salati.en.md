---
title: "Young cabbage salad with cucumber and dill"
description: "Crunchy summer salad of young cabbage, fresh cucumbers, dill and spring onions with a light oil dressing. The freshest salad to serve with meat."
steps:
  - "Shred the cabbage finely, add the salt and sugar and squeeze lightly with your hands."
  - "Cut the cucumbers into half moons and chop the dill and spring onions."
  - "Mix everything in a bowl."
  - "Dress with the oil and vinegar or lemon juice, toss and serve straight away."
tips:
  - "Young cabbage is tender: squeeze it much less than autumn cabbage."
---

June cabbage is so tender you can eat it almost like lettuce. This salad is the pure taste of summer.
