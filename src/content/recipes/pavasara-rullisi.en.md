---
title: Fried spring rolls
description: Crisp rolls with cabbage, carrots, mushrooms and glass noodles.
yield: 20 rolls
steps:
  - Cover the noodles with hot water for 5 minutes, drain and chop.
  - In a wok, quickly fry the thinly sliced cabbage, carrots and mushrooms with the garlic.
  - Add the noodles, soy sauce and sesame oil. Let it cool.
  - Put a spoonful of filling on each wrapper, fold in the sides and roll up tightly. Moisten the end.
  - Fry in oil at 175 °C for 3 to 4 minutes, until golden. Serve with sweet chilli sauce.
---

At Chinese New Year the golden rolls symbolise gold bars and prosperity.
