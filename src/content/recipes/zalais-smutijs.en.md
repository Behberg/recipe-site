---
title: "Green smoothie with spinach, kiwi and apple"
description: "A fresh green smoothie with spinach, banana, kiwi and apple. Naturally sweet, vegan and with no added sugar."
steps:
  - "Core the apple and peel the kiwis."
  - "Put the spinach and liquid in a blender and blend until smooth."
  - "Add the banana, apple, kiwi, ginger and ice. Blend for 1 minute."
  - "Pour into glasses and drink straight away."
tips:
  - "You can hardly taste the spinach: the banana and apple bring the sweetness."
---

A green smoothie is a way to eat a handful of spinach without even noticing.
