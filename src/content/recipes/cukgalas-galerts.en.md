---
title: "Pork aspic (Latvian meat jelly)"
description: "Clear homemade pork aspic made from trotters and shoulder with carrot and garlic. A festive starter with horseradish and mustard, no gelatine needed."
steps:
  - "Clean the trotters thoroughly and soak in cold water for 2 hours. Cover the trotters and meat with cold water and bring to the boil."
  - "Skim, lower the heat to a bare simmer and cook for 4 hours without boiling hard, so the stock stays clear."
  - "For the last hour add the whole onion, carrot, bay leaves, peppercorns and salt."
  - "Lift out the meat, take it off the bones and chop. Strain the stock through muslin and add the crushed garlic."
  - "Arrange the meat and carrot slices in dishes and pour over the stock."
  - "Cool and refrigerate for at least 6 hours, until set. Serve with horseradish or mustard."
tips:
  - "The aspic sets by itself from the collagen in the trotters: more trotters make a firmer jelly."
---

Making aspic takes all day, but it is part of the good old kitchen. On the Christmas table it is expected and respected.
