---
title: "Lasagna soup"
description: "All the flavour of lasagna in one pot and half an hour: tomato soup with minced beef, broken lasagna sheets and a spoonful of ricotta and mozzarella on top."
steps:
  - "Heat the oil in a large pot and fry the mince with the chopped onion for 8 minutes until browned and crumbly."
  - "Add the chopped garlic, tomato paste, oregano and basil and fry for 2 minutes."
  - "Add the tomatoes and stock, season with salt and pepper, bring to the boil and simmer for 10 minutes."
  - "Break the lasagna sheets into 4 to 5 cm pieces, drop them into the soup and simmer for 12 minutes until tender. Add water if the soup gets too thick."
  - "Mix the ricotta with the parmesan and a pinch of pepper in a small bowl."
  - "Ladle the soup into bowls and top each with a spoonful of ricotta, torn mozzarella and basil."
tips:
  - "Leftover soup thickens as the pasta absorbs the liquid. Loosen it with stock the next day."
  - "Curd cheese mixed with a spoonful of sour cream works instead of ricotta."
  - "Swap half the beef for Italian sausage meat for an even more aromatic soup."
