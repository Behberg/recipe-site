---
title: Strawberry jam
description: A classic jam with whole strawberries and syrup that smells of June.
yield: about 4 jars of 0.5 l
steps:
  - Wash and hull the strawberries and let them drain. Halve the large ones.
  - Layer the strawberries in a pan, sprinkling them with the sugar. Leave for 4 to 6 hours to let the berries release their juice.
  - Bring to the boil over a medium heat and boil for 5 minutes, skimming off the foam. Take off the heat and let it cool.
  - Repeat the boiling twice more. This way the berries stay whole and the syrup becomes thick.
  - At the end of the last boil, add the lemon juice.
  - Pour the hot jam into sterilised jars, seal tightly and turn upside down until cool.
tips:
  - Sterilise the jars in the oven at 120 °C for 15 minutes or in boiling water.
  - "Is the jam ready? Drop a little onto a cold saucer: the drop should hold its shape."
---

Boiling the jam in several rounds keeps the berries’ shape and colour. In winter it is the best thing to put on pancakes.
