---
title: Mapo tofu
description: Soft tofu in a spicy sauce with minced meat, garlic and chilli. A Sichuan home cooking classic.
steps:
  - Cut the tofu into 2 cm cubes and let them sit in hot salted water for 2 minutes. Drain.
  - Fry the mince in oil in a wok until crisp.
  - Add the chilli paste, garlic and ginger and fry for 1 minute.
  - Pour in the stock and soy sauce and gently fold in the tofu. Simmer for 5 minutes.
  - Stir in the starch dissolved in water to thicken the sauce. Sprinkle with spring onions and serve with rice.
tips:
  - If you have no chilli bean paste, use a teaspoon of chilli flakes and a spoonful of miso.
---

The tofu in this dish surprises even people who usually do not eat it.
