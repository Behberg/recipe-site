---
title: "Cheese stuffed meat patties"
description: "Juicy minced meat patties with a melting cheese centre. The cheese oozes out when cut and children are delighted. Dinner in 40 minutes."
steps:
  - "Soak the bread in the milk. Cut the cheese into 8 cubes."
  - "Mix the mince with grated onion, the egg, squeezed bread, salt and pepper."
  - "Divide into 8. Flatten each in your palm, put a cube of cheese in the middle and close into a patty."
  - "Coat in breadcrumbs."
  - "Fry in oil over a medium heat for 5 to 6 minutes on each side until cooked through. Serve with mash."
tips:
  - "Seal the cheese completely inside the meat or it will leak into the pan."
---

Ordinary patties with one small surprise inside become the children’s favourite dish.
