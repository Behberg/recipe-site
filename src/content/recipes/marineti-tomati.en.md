---
title: Pickled tomatoes
description: Whole cherry tomatoes in a sweet and sour marinade with garlic and dill.
yield: 4 jars of 1 l
steps:
  - Wash the tomatoes and prick each one by the stalk with a toothpick so they do not burst.
  - Put the garlic, dill and peppercorns in the bottom of the jars and the tomatoes on top.
  - Pour over boiling water, cover and leave for 10 minutes. Pour off the water.
  - For the marinade, boil the water with the sugar and salt and add the vinegar.
  - Pour the hot marinade into the jars and seal straight away. Turn upside down and wrap in a blanket.
---

Summer tomatoes in winter taste even better than fresh ones from the shop.
