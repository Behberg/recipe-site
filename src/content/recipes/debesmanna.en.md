---
title: Debesmanna (whipped berry semolina)
description: An airy pink semolina dessert with cranberries or lingonberries. Served with cold milk.
steps:
  - Crush the berries, squeeze out the juice and set it aside. Cover the pulp with water, boil for 5 minutes, then strain.
  - Add the sugar to the liquid and bring to the boil. Sprinkle in the semolina in a thin stream, stirring constantly.
  - Simmer over a low heat for 10 minutes, stirring often. Take off the heat and stir in the berry juice.
  - Stand the pan in cold water and let the mixture cool.
  - Whisk the cooled mixture with a mixer for 10 to 15 minutes, until it is airy, pale pink and doubled in volume.
  - Serve in bowls with cold milk.
---

The name says it all: manna from heaven. A taste of childhood worth revisiting as a grown up.
