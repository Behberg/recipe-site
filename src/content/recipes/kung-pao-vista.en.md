---
title: Kung pao chicken
description: Spicy Sichuan wok chicken with peanuts, chilli and a sweet and sour soy sauce.
steps:
  - Dice the chicken small and mix it with the starch and a teaspoon of soy sauce.
  - Mix the sauce ingredients.
  - Fry the chillies in very hot oil in a wok for 30 seconds, then add the chicken and fry for 4 minutes.
  - Add the garlic, ginger and spring onions and fry for 1 minute.
  - Pour in the sauce, stir in the peanuts and fry until the sauce coats the meat in a glossy glaze. Serve with rice.
---

A Sichuan classic named after a 19th century governor.
