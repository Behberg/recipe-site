---
title: Three ingredient banana pancakes
description: Simple pancakes made from banana, eggs and oats. No sugar and no flour.
steps:
  - Mash the bananas with a fork until smooth.
  - Stir in the eggs and oats. Leave the batter to swell for 5 minutes.
  - Lightly oil a pan and spoon in small pancakes.
  - Cook over a medium low heat for 2 minutes on each side.
  - Serve with yogurt and berries.
---

So simple you can make them half asleep.
