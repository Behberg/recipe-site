---
title: "Creamy mushroom soup with thyme"
description: "Thick, creamy mushroom soup with browned mushrooms, thyme and cream. Restaurant flavour from simple ingredients."
steps:
  - "Set aside a few mushrooms for garnish and slice the rest."
  - "Fry the onion in butter for 5 minutes, add the mushrooms, garlic and thyme. Cook for 10 minutes until browned."
  - "Add the diced potatoes and stock and simmer for 15 minutes."
  - "Remove the thyme stalks, blend until smooth and stir in the cream. Season with salt and pepper."
  - "Fry the reserved mushrooms in thin slices and put them on top of the soup."
tips:
  - "Brown the mushrooms deeply: the whole flavour of the soup depends on it."
---

This is the soup people order in cafés. At home it is just as good and costs far less.
