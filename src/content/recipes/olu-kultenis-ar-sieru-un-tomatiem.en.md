---
title: "Creamy scrambled eggs with cheese and tomatoes"
description: "Soft, creamy scrambled eggs with melting cheese, cherry tomatoes and chives. A proper breakfast in 10 minutes, like a good café."
steps:
  - "Whisk the eggs with the milk, salt and pepper."
  - "Melt the butter in a pan over a medium low heat."
  - "Pour in the eggs and stir slowly with a spatula to make large, soft curds."
  - "While the eggs are still slightly wet, add the cheese and halved tomatoes, stir and take off the heat."
  - "Sprinkle with chives and serve on toast."
tips:
  - "Take them off the heat while they still look a little too wet: they keep cooking on the plate."
---

Good scrambled eggs are a matter of patience. Low heat is the only secret.
