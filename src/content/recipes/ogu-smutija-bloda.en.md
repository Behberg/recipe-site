---
title: "Berry smoothie bowl with granola"
description: "A thick berry and banana smoothie in a bowl, topped with granola, fresh berries and chia seeds. A colourful, vitamin rich breakfast in 10 minutes."
steps:
  - "Put the frozen berries, half the banana, the yoghurt and honey in a blender."
  - "Blend until thick. If the blender struggles, add 2 spoonfuls of milk."
  - "Pour into bowls."
  - "Arrange rows of granola, slices of the remaining banana, fresh berries and chia seeds on top."
tips:
  - "The less liquid, the thicker the bowl: it should be like soft serve ice cream."
---

A smoothie bowl is a breakfast you eat with a spoon rather than drink. And it looks almost too pretty to eat.
