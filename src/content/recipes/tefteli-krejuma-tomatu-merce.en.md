---
title: "Meat and rice meatballs in sour cream tomato sauce"
description: "Soft meat and rice meatballs braised in a sour cream and tomato sauce, just like at kindergarten. Filling, cheap and great with potatoes or pasta."
steps:
  - "Mix the mince with the rice, one finely grated onion, the egg, salt and pepper."
  - "Shape into walnut sized balls, coat in flour and brown on all sides in oil. Transfer to a pan."
  - "In the frying pan fry the second onion, add the tomato paste and sour cream, pour in the hot water and stir."
  - "Pour the sauce over the meatballs and add the bay leaf."
  - "Cover and simmer gently for 25 minutes."
tips:
  - "The meatballs freeze well in their sauce: just reheat them."
---

Meatballs in sauce are what many remember from school and kindergarten. At home they are even better.
