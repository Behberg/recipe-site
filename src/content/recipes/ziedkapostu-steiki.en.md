---
title: "Roasted cauliflower steaks"
description: "Thick slices of cauliflower with a golden crust and tahini sauce. The vegetable takes the lead role."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 220 °C. Remove the leaves from the cauliflower and cut 2 to 3 slices 2 cm thick from the middle."
  - "Mix the olive oil with the paprika, garlic powder, turmeric, salt and pepper. Brush the slices on both sides."
  - "Place on a baking tray and roast for 15 minutes, turn and roast for another 10 to 15 minutes."
  - "Stir the tahini with the lemon juice and water into a smooth sauce."
  - "Drizzle the steaks with the sauce and scatter with parsley."
---

Use the leftover cauliflower edges for soup or a bake.
