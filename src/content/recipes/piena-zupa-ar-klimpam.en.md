---
title: "Sweet milk soup with dumplings"
description: "Sweet milk soup with soft flour dumplings, just like at kindergarten or grandma’s. Ready in 25 minutes from what is always in the house."
steps:
  - "Slowly heat the milk until almost boiling, stirring often so it does not catch."
  - "Whisk the egg with the cold milk and salt, then stir in the flour to make a smooth, thick batter."
  - "Drop the batter into the milk in small dumplings using a teaspoon."
  - "Simmer over a low heat for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring, until the dumplings are cooked through."
  - "Stir in the sugar and butter. Serve warm."
tips:
  - "Milk burns easily: use a heavy based pan and stay close."
---

For many Latvians this is the first soup they remember from childhood. Warm, sweet and always with a little pool of butter on top.
