---
title: Salade niçoise
description: A hearty salad with tuna, potatoes, green beans, eggs, olives and tomatoes.
steps:
  - Boil the potatoes in their skins, cool and halve them. Boil the beans for 4 minutes and cool them in iced water.
  - Boil the eggs for 8 minutes, cool and cut into quarters.
  - For the dressing, whisk the olive oil, vinegar, mustard, salt and pepper.
  - Arrange the lettuce, potatoes, beans, tomatoes, tuna, eggs, olives and onion rings on a plate.
  - Pour over the dressing just before serving.
---

In Nice they still argue about what really belongs in this salad. Our version is hearty and simple.
