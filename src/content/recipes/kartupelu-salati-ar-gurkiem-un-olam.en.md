---
title: "Potato salad with pickles and eggs"
description: "Classic potato salad with eggs, pickled cucumbers, onion and dill in a sour cream and mayonnaise dressing. A must at Midsummer and barbecues."
steps:
  - "Boil the potatoes in their skins for 20 to 25 minutes until tender. Cool, peel and dice."
  - "Dice the eggs, pickles and onion."
  - "Mix the mayonnaise with the sour cream, mustard, salt and pepper."
  - "Toss everything with the dressing and chopped dill."
  - "Chill for at least 1 hour before serving."
tips:
  - "Boil the potatoes in their skins so they do not fall apart and the salad stays firm."
---

At Midsummer potato salad is as essential as cheese and beer. Every family has its own recipe, and each is the best.
