---
title: "Zebra cake with sour cream"
description: "A striped vanilla and chocolate sour cream cake that surprises everyone when it is cut. A simple batter and a fun baking trick."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 175 °C and line a 22 cm round tin with paper."
  - "Whisk the eggs with the sugar until pale. Stir in the sour cream and melted butter."
  - "Sift in the flour with the baking powder and vanilla sugar and mix to a smooth batter."
  - "Divide the batter in two and stir the cocoa into one half."
  - "Pour 2 tablespoons of light and then dark batter alternately into the centre of the tin, one on top of the other. The batter spreads into stripes by itself."
  - "Bake for 40 to 45 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean."
tips:
  - "Both batters must be the same thickness, otherwise the stripes will be uneven."
---

When a zebra cake is cut, children always want to know how the stripes got inside. It is the best baking trick to show off.
