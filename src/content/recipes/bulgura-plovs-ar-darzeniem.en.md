---
title: "Bulgur pilaf with vegetables"
description: "Turkish home cooking: fluffy bulgur with tomatoes and peppers. Works as a side and as a meal on its own."
steps:
  - "Fry the chopped onion and pepper in the olive oil for 4 minutes."
  - "Add the tomato paste and paprika and fry for 1 minute. Add the grated tomatoes and cook for 3 minutes."
  - "Tip in the bulgur and stir. Pour in the hot stock and season with salt and pepper."
  - "Bring to the boil, lower the heat and cook covered for 12 to 15 minutes, until the liquid is absorbed."
  - "Take off the heat, put a clean tea towel under the lid and leave for 10 minutes. Fluff with a fork and scatter with parsley."
---

The towel under the lid soaks up the steam, so the bulgur stays fluffy, not sticky.
