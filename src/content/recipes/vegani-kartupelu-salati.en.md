---
title: "Vegan potato salad with mustard dressing"
description: "A light potato salad without mayonnaise: mustard and oil dressing, dill, radishes and gherkins."
steps:
  - "Boil the potatoes in their skins in salted water for 15 to 20 minutes, until tender. Drain and halve."
  - "Whisk the oil with the mustard, vinegar, sugar, salt and pepper."
  - "Pour the dressing over the potatoes while still warm and toss so they soak it up."
  - "Add the thinly sliced onion, gherkins and radishes."
  - "Scatter with dill and spring onions. Serve warm or chilled."
---

Dress the potatoes while warm: the flavour soaks right through.
