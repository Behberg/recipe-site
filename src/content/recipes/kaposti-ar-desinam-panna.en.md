---
title: "Braised cabbage with sausages in one pan"
description: "Hearty braised fresh cabbage with browned sausages, carrot and caraway. A cheap, simple one pan weekday dish."
steps:
  - "Slice the sausages and brown in oil in a large pan. Set aside."
  - "In the same pan fry the onion and grated carrot for 5 minutes."
  - "Add the finely shredded cabbage, caraway, salt and pepper. Fry for 5 minutes, stirring."
  - "Stir in the tomato paste and water. Cover and braise for 15 minutes."
  - "Return the sausages and braise for 5 minutes more."
tips:
  - "For a tangier flavour, add a spoonful of vinegar or some sauerkraut at the end."
---

Cabbage with sausages feeds the whole family for a few euros and tastes of home.
