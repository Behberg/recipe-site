---
title: "Crepe cake with cream cheese filling"
description: "A tall cake of 15 thin crepes layered with airy cream cheese and whipped cream filling and fresh berries. Impressive, with no oven needed."
steps:
  - "Beat the eggs with the milk, sugar and salt, sift in the flour and stir in the butter. Let the batter rest for 20 minutes."
  - "Cook about 15 thin crepes in a 22 cm pan. Let them cool completely."
  - "Beat the cream cheese with the icing sugar and fold in the stiffly whipped cream."
  - "Stack the crepes on a plate, spreading a thin layer of cream on each."
  - "Cover the top and sides with cream and decorate with berries. Chill for at least 4 hours."
tips:
  - "The crepes must be completely cold or the cream will melt."
---

A crepe cake is how ordinary Sunday pancakes turn into a birthday wonder.
