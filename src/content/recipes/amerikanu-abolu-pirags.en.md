---
title: "Classic American lattice apple pie"
description: "The classic American apple pie with buttery, flaky pastry, a cinnamon apple filling and a lattice top. An autumn dessert with vanilla ice cream."
steps:
  - "Rub the flour, salt and cold butter into crumbs, add the water and bring together quickly. Divide in 2 and chill for 1 hour."
  - "Slice the apples thinly and toss with the sugar, cinnamon, lemon juice and cornflour."
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C. Roll out the larger piece of pastry, line a 24 cm tin and fill with the apples."
  - "Roll out the rest, cut into strips and weave a lattice. Brush with egg."
  - "Bake for 20 minutes, then lower to 180 °C and bake for 35 minutes more, until bubbling. Cool for 2 hours before slicing."
tips:
  - "The pie must cool: that is when the filling thickens and slices cut neatly."
---

Apple pie is so important in the US that it is called a symbol of America. With ice cream it is even better.
