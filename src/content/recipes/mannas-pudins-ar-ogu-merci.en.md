---
title: "Semolina pudding with berry sauce"
description: "Soft vanilla semolina pudding with warm berry sauce. A childhood dessert made from milk, semolina and berries in 25 minutes."
steps:
  - "Bring the milk to the boil with half the sugar and the vanilla sugar. Stirring constantly, sprinkle in the semolina and cook for 5 minutes."
  - "Take off the heat and stir in the butter and egg yolk."
  - "Whisk the egg white and gently fold it into the pudding."
  - "Pour into moulds rinsed with water and cool."
  - "For the sauce, bring the berries and remaining sugar to the boil, stir in the starch mixed with water and cook for 1 minute. Serve the pudding with the sauce."
tips:
  - "Rinsing the moulds with water lets the pudding turn out easily."
---

Semolina pudding with red berry sauce is a dessert almost everyone who grew up in Latvia remembers.
