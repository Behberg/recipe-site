---
title: Pasta bake with sausage and cheese
description: Pasta, sausage and an egg mixture baked in the oven under a layer of cheese. A great way to use up leftovers.
steps:
  - Cook the pasta for 2 minutes less than it says on the packet and drain.
  - Dice the sausage and mix it with the pasta.
  - Whisk the eggs with the milk, salt, pepper and half the cheese.
  - Put the pasta into a buttered dish, pour over the egg mixture and sprinkle with the rest of the cheese.
  - Bake in the oven at 190 °C for 25 to 30 minutes, until the top is golden. Sprinkle with spring onions.
---
