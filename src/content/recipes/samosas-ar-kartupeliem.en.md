---
title: "Potato and pea samosas"
description: "Crispy Indian samosas with a spiced potato and pea filling. A vegan snack, great with a yoghurt or chutney dip."
yield: "12 samosas"
steps:
  - "Rub the flour and salt with the oil, add the water and knead to a firm dough. Rest for 30 minutes."
  - "Boil, peel and dice the potatoes. Fry the onion in oil, add the ginger and spices, then the potatoes and peas. Stir in the coriander and salt. Let cool."
  - "Divide the dough into 6, roll each into an oval and cut in half."
  - "Fold each half into a cone, sealing the edge with water, fill with the filling and pinch the top closed."
  - "Fry in oil at 160 °C for 8 to 10 minutes until golden and crisp."
tips:
  - "Fry over a medium heat: the pastry turns crisp with small blisters rather than soft."
---

Samosas are sold on every street corner in India. With a cup of masala chai they make the perfect afternoon snack.
