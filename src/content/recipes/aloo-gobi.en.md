---
title: "Aloo gobi (Indian potatoes with cauliflower)"
description: "The Indian home dish aloo gobi: potatoes and cauliflower with turmeric, cumin, ginger and tomatoes. Vegan, filling and fragrant."
steps:
  - "Dice the potatoes and cut the cauliflower into small florets."
  - "Fry the onion in oil for 5 minutes, add the garlic, ginger, cumin and turmeric and cook for 1 minute."
  - "Add the potatoes and cauliflower and stir to coat in the spices."
  - "Add the chopped tomatoes, salt and 3 spoonfuls of water. Cover and cook for 20 minutes, stirring now and then."
  - "Add the garam masala and coriander. Serve with rice or naan."
tips:
  - "Keep the heat medium: the vegetables should catch a little on the bottom, that is where the flavour comes from."
---

Aloo gobi is everyday food in Indian homes. Just two cheap vegetables, but the spices turn them into a feast.
