---
title: Imam bayıldı (stuffed aubergines)
description: Aubergines braised in olive oil with an onion, tomato and garlic filling. Best lukewarm.
steps:
  - Peel the aubergines in stripes, salt them and leave for 20 minutes. Pat dry.
  - Fry the aubergines in olive oil on all sides until soft. Put them in a dish.
  - In the same oil, fry the onion half rings and the garlic, then add the tomatoes, sugar, salt and parsley.
  - Cut a long slit in each aubergine and fill it with the vegetable mixture.
  - Add some water and bake in the oven at 180 °C for 30 minutes. Serve lukewarm.
---

The name means “the imam fainted”: either from the taste or from how much olive oil was used.
