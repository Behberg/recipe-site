---
title: Chicken tagine with lemons and olives
description: Slowly braised chicken with preserved lemons, olives, ginger and saffron. A Moroccan classic.
steps:
  - Rub the chicken with the spices and salt and leave for 30 minutes.
  - In a tagine or heavy based pan, brown the chicken in olive oil until golden.
  - Add the onions and garlic and cook for 5 minutes.
  - Pour in the stock and add the lemon wedges and saffron. Braise, covered, for 40 minutes.
  - Add the olives and herbs and braise for another 10 minutes. Serve with couscous.
---

A tagine is both the dish and the clay pot with a conical lid it is cooked in.
