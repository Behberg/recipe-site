---
title: Spaghetti with simple tomato sauce
description: A quick Italian tomato sauce with garlic, olive oil and basil. Lunch in 20 minutes.
steps:
  - Heat slices of garlic in olive oil in a pan over a low heat until fragrant.
  - Add the tomatoes, crush them with a spoon and simmer for 15 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
  - Cook the spaghetti al dente.
  - Toss the spaghetti with the sauce and torn basil. Serve with parmesan.
---

A sauce worth learning by heart. It goes with almost everything.
