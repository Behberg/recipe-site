---
title: "Turkish pide with minced meat and cheese"
description: "Turkish boat shaped pide bread filled with minced meat, tomato and pepper and melted cheese. Turkey’s favourite street food at home."
steps:
  - "Mix the dough ingredients, knead for 8 minutes and leave to rise for 1 hour."
  - "Fry the onion and mince, add the finely chopped tomato, pepper, paprika and salt. Cook until the liquid evaporates."
  - "Heat the oven to 240 °C. Divide the dough into 4 and roll each into an oval."
  - "Put the filling and cheese in the middle, fold the sides in and pinch the ends to form a boat. Brush the edges with egg."
  - "Bake on a hot tray for 12 to 15 minutes, until the edges are golden."
tips:
  - "The hotter the oven, the crisper the pide, like from a real stone oven."
---

In Turkey pide is what pizza is in Italy. Every town has its own shape and filling.
