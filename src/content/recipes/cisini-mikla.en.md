---
title: "Hot dogs wrapped in puff pastry with cheese"
description: "Golden sausages wrapped in crisp puff pastry with cheese and sesame. A children’s party hit, ready in 35 minutes from ready made pastry."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C and line a baking tray with paper."
  - "Roll out the pastry and cut into 12 long strips about 2 cm wide."
  - "Lay a thin strip of cheese along each sausage and wrap a pastry strip around it in a spiral."
  - "Arrange on the paper, brush with beaten egg and sprinkle with sesame."
  - "Bake for 18 to 20 minutes, until puffed and golden. Serve with ketchup."
tips:
  - "The cheese melts under the pastry, making the sausages even juicier."
---

At children’s birthday parties these always go first. The grown ups come looking for them too.
