---
title: "Homemade tomato ketchup"
description: "Thick homemade ketchup from fresh tomatoes and spices, with no preservatives or starch. So good that shop bought ketchup never tastes the same again."
yield: "about 4 bottles of 0.5 l"
steps:
  - "Chop the tomatoes and onions, put them in a pan with the garlic and simmer for 30 minutes until soft."
  - "Blend the mixture and press it through a sieve to remove the skins and seeds."
  - "Add the sugar, salt and spices. Simmer gently for 1 to 1.5 hours, stirring often, until reduced by half."
  - "Add the vinegar and simmer for 5 more minutes."
  - "Fill sterilised bottles or jars while hot and seal."
tips:
  - "The ketchup is ready when a spoon drawn across the bottom of the pan leaves a trail that closes slowly."
---

Children eat ketchup with everything. If it is homemade from garden tomatoes, there is nothing to worry about.
