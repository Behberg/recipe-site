---
title: "Homemade lemonade with mint"
description: "Refreshing homemade lemonade from fresh lemons, sugar syrup and mint topped with sparkling water. The best drink on a hot day."
steps:
  - "For the syrup, boil the sugar with the water until it dissolves. Add half the mint and let it cool."
  - "Squeeze the lemons, keeping one for slices."
  - "In a jug mix the lemon juice with the strained syrup."
  - "Top up with cold sparkling water and add ice, lemon slices and the rest of the mint."
  - "Taste and add more syrup or lemon juice if needed."
tips:
  - "The syrup can be made ahead and kept in the fridge for up to a week."
---

At Midsummer, garden parties or just on hot afternoons, this jug empties faster than anything else on the table.
