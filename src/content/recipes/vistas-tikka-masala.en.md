---
title: "Chicken tikka masala"
description: "The most popular Indian restaurant dish at home: marinated, charred chicken in a mild spiced tomato and cream sauce. With basmati rice."
steps:
  - "Cut the chicken into pieces and mix with the yoghurt, half the garam masala, the turmeric and salt. Marinate for at least 1 hour."
  - "Sear the chicken in a very hot pan or under the grill until charred at the edges. Set aside."
  - "In the same pan fry the onion in butter for 8 minutes, then add the garlic, ginger, paprika and the rest of the garam masala."
  - "Pour in the passata and simmer for 10 minutes. Stir in the cream."
  - "Return the chicken and simmer for 5 minutes. Serve with rice and coriander."
tips:
  - "The charred edges are the key to the tikka flavour: do not be afraid of a very hot pan."
---

Chicken tikka masala is one of the most popular dishes in the world. Made at home it is milder and more aromatic than in many restaurants.
