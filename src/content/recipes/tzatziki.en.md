---
title: "Tzatziki (Greek yoghurt and cucumber dip)"
description: "Classic Greek tzatziki with thick yoghurt, grated cucumber, garlic and dill. For barbecues, gyros, potatoes or as a dip with pita."
steps:
  - "Coarsely grate the cucumber, sprinkle with a pinch of salt and leave for 10 minutes."
  - "Squeeze the cucumber hard in a tea towel to get out as much liquid as possible."
  - "Mix the yoghurt with the cucumber, crushed garlic, oil, lemon juice and dill."
  - "Season with salt to taste and chill for 30 minutes."
  - "Serve with a drizzle of olive oil on top."
tips:
  - "Squeezing the cucumber is essential, otherwise the dip turns runny."
---

In Greece tzatziki is on the table at almost every meal. Fresh and cool, it goes with anything from the grill.
