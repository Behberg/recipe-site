---
title: Kimchi stew with tofu
description: A warming, filling Korean stew with ripe kimchi, pork and tofu.
steps:
  - In a pan, fry the sliced meat in sesame oil until golden.
  - Add the chopped kimchi and onion and cook for 5 minutes.
  - Add the gochujang and chilli, pour in the water and simmer for 15 minutes.
  - Add the cubes of tofu and simmer for another 5 minutes.
  - Sprinkle with spring onions and serve with rice.
---

The older the kimchi, the tastier the stew. A great way to use up sour kimchi.
