---
title: Potato gratin
description: Thin slices of potato in cream with garlic, baked in the oven until golden on top.
steps:
  - Peel the potatoes and slice them very thinly (2 to 3 mm).
  - In a pan, heat the cream, milk, crushed garlic, salt, pepper and nutmeg.
  - Add the potatoes and simmer for 10 minutes, stirring carefully.
  - Butter a dish, layer in the potatoes and pour over the cream.
  - Bake in the oven at 170 °C for 60 minutes, until the potatoes are tender and the top is golden.
---

The perfect side for a festive roast and anything off the grill.
