---
title: Crema catalana
description: A Spanish egg custard with lemon and cinnamon and a crisp caramel top.
steps:
  - Heat the milk with the lemon zest and cinnamon, take off the heat and leave to infuse for 15 minutes. Strain.
  - Whisk the yolks with the sugar and starch and gradually pour in the warm milk.
  - Heat gently, stirring constantly, until the custard thickens. Do not boil.
  - Pour into shallow dishes and chill in the fridge for at least 3 hours.
  - Before serving, sprinkle with sugar and caramelise with a blowtorch.
---

In Catalonia it is traditionally eaten on 19 March, St Joseph’s Day.
