---
title: Oven baked chicken nuggets
description: Crisp pieces of chicken in a cornflake coating, baked in the oven instead of fried.
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 210 °C.
  - Crush the cornflakes into crumbs and mix with the parmesan and paprika.
  - Cut the chicken into pieces and salt them.
  - Coat in flour, beaten eggs and the cornflake crumbs. Lay on a lined baking tray and drizzle with oil.
  - Bake for 15 to 18 minutes, until golden and cooked through.
---

Cornflakes give crunch without deep frying.
