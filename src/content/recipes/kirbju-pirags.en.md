---
title: Pumpkin pie
description: American pumpkin pie with cinnamon, ginger and nutmeg on a crisp crust.
steps:
  - Cut the pumpkin into pieces and roast in the oven at 200 °C for 40 minutes, until soft. Blend into a purée and let it cool.
  - For the crust, rub the flour, sugar and butter into crumbs, add the water and knead quickly. Chill for 30 minutes.
  - Roll out and line a pie dish. Bake in the oven at 190 °C for 12 minutes.
  - For the filling, mix the pumpkin purée, eggs, sugar, cream and spices.
  - Pour into the crust and bake in the oven at 175 °C for 45 to 50 minutes, until the middle has set.
  - Let it cool completely. Serve with whipped cream.
---

There is no need to throw away the inside of your Halloween pumpkin: it makes a great pie.
