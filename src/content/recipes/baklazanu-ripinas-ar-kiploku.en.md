---
title: "Fried aubergine rounds with garlic and tomato"
description: "Pan fried aubergine rounds topped with garlic sauce and tomato slices. A simple summer starter that disappears faster than you can make it."
steps:
  - "Slice the aubergines into 1 cm rounds, sprinkle with salt and leave for 20 minutes. Pat dry with paper."
  - "Fry the rounds in oil for 2 to 3 minutes on each side until golden and soft. Drain on paper."
  - "Mix the mayonnaise with the crushed garlic."
  - "Spread each round with garlic sauce and top with a slice of tomato."
  - "Sprinkle with parsley and serve."
tips:
  - "Salting makes the aubergines tender and they absorb less oil."
---

This starter was on many summer party tables. It is so simple people remember it without a recipe.
