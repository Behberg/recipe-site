---
title: Rasols (Latvian potato salad)
description: A festive potato salad with meat, eggs, pickles and a sour cream and mayonnaise dressing.
steps:
  - Boil the potatoes and carrots in their skins, cool and peel. Hard boil the eggs.
  - Dice the potatoes, carrots, eggs, meat, pickles and apple small.
  - Add the green peas.
  - For the dressing, mix the sour cream, mayonnaise and mustard.
  - Mix everything with the dressing and season with salt and pepper.
  - Before serving, let it sit in the fridge for at least 1 hour.
---

There is always a big bowl of rasols on a Latvian festive table.
