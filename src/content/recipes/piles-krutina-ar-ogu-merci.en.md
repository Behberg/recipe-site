---
title: "Duck breast with lingonberry sauce"
description: "Pink, juicy duck breast with crispy skin and a sweet and sour lingonberry and red wine sauce. A restaurant level romantic dinner for two."
steps:
  - "Score the duck skin in a diamond pattern without cutting into the meat. Season with salt and pepper."
  - "Place skin side down in a cold pan and heat slowly over a medium heat for 8 to 10 minutes, until the fat melts and the skin is crisp."
  - "Turn and cook for 3 to 4 minutes more. Rest for 8 minutes."
  - "Pour off excess fat, add the wine, jam, balsamic and thyme. Simmer for 3 minutes and stir in the butter."
  - "Slice the duck on the diagonal and spoon over the sauce."
tips:
  - "Keep the duck fat: potatoes roasted in it are the best in the world."
---

Duck with berries is an old European pairing. In Latvia, lingonberries from the forest suit it best.
