---
title: Semolina porridge
description: Soft, smooth semolina porridge with milk and butter. A childhood breakfast without a single lump.
steps:
  - Bring the milk to the boil with the sugar and salt.
  - Sprinkle in the semolina in a thin stream, whisking constantly. This way no lumps will form.
  - Simmer over a low heat for 3 to 4 minutes, stirring.
  - Serve with a knob of butter and a spoonful of jam.
---

The secret is a thin stream and a whisk. Then the porridge will always be smooth.
