---
title: "One pan buckwheat with pork"
description: "Hearty buckwheat with braised pork, carrots and onions, cooked in one pan. A simple, budget family dinner."
steps:
  - "Dice the pork and brown it in oil in a deep frying pan for 7 minutes."
  - "Add the onion and grated carrot and cook for 5 minutes. Add the garlic and tomato paste."
  - "Pour in half the water, add the bay leaf and braise covered for 15 minutes."
  - "Add the rinsed buckwheat and the rest of the water and season with salt and pepper. Do not stir."
  - "Cover and cook over a low heat for 20 minutes, until the water is absorbed. Stir in the butter and rest for 5 minutes."
tips:
  - "Do not lift the lid once the buckwheat is in: the steam is what makes it fluffy."
---

One pan, simple ingredients and a dinner as filling as grandma’s. That is the magic of this dish.
