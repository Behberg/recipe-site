---
title: Patatas bravas
description: Crisp fried potato cubes with a spicy tomato sauce and garlic mayonnaise.
steps:
  - Cut the potatoes into 2 cm cubes, boil in salted water for 5 minutes and drain.
  - Toss with olive oil and salt, spread on a tray and roast in the oven at 220 °C for 30 minutes, stirring once.
  - For the sauce, simmer the tomato sauce with the paprika, chilli and garlic for 10 minutes.
  - Mix the mayonnaise with the crushed garlic.
  - Pour the tomato sauce over the crisp potatoes and spoon the garlic mayonnaise on top.
---

Bravas means “brave”: the sauce should be properly hot.
