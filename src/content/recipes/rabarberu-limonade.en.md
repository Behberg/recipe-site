---
title: Rhubarb lemonade
description: A pink, refreshing rhubarb lemonade with lemon and mint.
steps:
  - Cut the rhubarb into pieces and boil with the water and sugar for 10 to 15 minutes.
  - Strain through a sieve without pressing, so the syrup stays clear.
  - Add the lemon juice and chill.
  - Serve diluted 1:1 with sparkling water, with ice and mint.
---

Use the leftover rhubarb pulp in yogurt or porridge.
