---
title: "Breaded chicken schnitzel with lemon"
description: "Thin, crispy chicken schnitzels in a breadcrumb and parmesan coating, pan fried until golden. A quick family dinner children love."
steps:
  - "Slice the breasts in half horizontally and pound between cling film to 5 mm thick. Season with salt and pepper."
  - "Mix the breadcrumbs with the parmesan."
  - "Coat the schnitzels in flour, beaten egg and breadcrumbs, pressing lightly."
  - "Heat 5 mm of oil in a frying pan and fry for 2 to 3 minutes on each side until golden."
  - "Drain on kitchen paper and serve with lemon wedges, potatoes and salad."
tips:
  - "Thin meat cooks quickly and stays juicy: that is why pounding is a must."
---

Chicken schnitzel is what children order in cafés. At home it is just as crispy and much cheaper.
