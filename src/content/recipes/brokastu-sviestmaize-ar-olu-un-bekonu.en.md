---
title: "Breakfast sandwich with egg, bacon and cheese"
description: "A hot breakfast sandwich with fried egg, crispy bacon, melted cheese and lettuce in toasted bread. 15 minutes to happiness."
steps:
  - "Fry the bacon until crisp."
  - "Toast the bread and butter it."
  - "In the same pan fry the eggs with soft yolks."
  - "Layer lettuce, bacon, egg and cheese on the toast, add ketchup and top with the second slice."
  - "Cut in half and serve straight away."
tips:
  - "The cheese melts better if you put it on the hot egg in the pan and cover for 30 seconds."
---
