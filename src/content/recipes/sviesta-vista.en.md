---
title: Butter chicken
description: Indian chicken in a mild tomato, butter and cream sauce with garam masala. Serve with rice or naan.
steps:
  - Cut the chicken into pieces and marinate in the yogurt with half the spices and salt for at least 30 minutes.
  - Fry the chicken in a pan until browned. Set aside.
  - In the same pan, melt the butter and fry the onion, garlic and ginger.
  - Add the rest of the spices and the tomato purée. Simmer for 10 minutes, then blend until smooth.
  - Return the chicken to the sauce, add the cream and simmer for 10 minutes.
  - Serve with basmati rice and coriander.
---

The world’s most popular Indian dish, mild and suitable for the whole family.
