---
title: "Baked mushroom stuffed pancakes with cheese"
description: "Thin pancakes with a creamy mushroom and onion filling, baked under a layer of cheese. A hearty vegetarian family dish."
steps:
  - "For the batter, whisk the eggs with the milk and salt, then stir in the flour and oil. Cook 10 to 12 thin pancakes."
  - "For the filling, fry the onion and mushrooms in butter for 10 minutes until browned. Stir in the sour cream, dill, salt and pepper."
  - "Heat the oven to 190 °C."
  - "Put a spoonful of filling on each pancake, roll up and arrange in a dish."
  - "Scatter with cheese and bake for 15 minutes, until the cheese is melted and golden."
tips:
  - "Filled pancakes can be frozen and baked later straight from the freezer."
---

Mushroom pancakes are an autumn dish, for when the forest gives mushrooms and the evenings grow long.
