---
title: "Buckwheat pancakes"
description: "Thin, nutty buckwheat flour pancakes, gluten free. Delicious with sour cream, smoked salmon, mushrooms or honey."
steps:
  - "Whisk the eggs with the milk, salt and sugar."
  - "Stir in the buckwheat flour to a smooth batter, then the melted butter."
  - "Let the batter rest for 20 minutes."
  - "Pour a thin layer into a hot, lightly oiled pan and cook for 1 to 2 minutes on each side."
  - "Serve with sour cream or a savoury filling."
tips:
  - "Buckwheat batter is more fragile than wheat: flip the pancakes with a wide spatula."
---

Buckwheat pancakes are an old Northern European tradition. They have a deep, nutty flavour you cannot mistake.
