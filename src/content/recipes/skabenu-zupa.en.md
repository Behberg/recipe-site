---
title: Sorrel soup
description: A slightly tangy spring soup with fresh sorrel, potatoes, egg and sour cream.
steps:
  - Bring the stock to the boil. Add the diced potatoes and grated carrot.
  - Chop the onion, fry it lightly in oil and add it to the soup.
  - Simmer for 15 to 20 minutes, until the potatoes are soft.
  - Wash the sorrel, remove the tough stalks and cut into strips. Add to the soup and cook for only 2 more minutes.
  - Season with salt and pepper.
  - Serve with a halved egg, a spoonful of sour cream and dill.
---

The first sorrel in the garden means spring has arrived. This soup is quick, cheap and very comforting.
