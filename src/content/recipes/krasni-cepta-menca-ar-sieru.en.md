---
title: "Baked cod with tomatoes and cheese"
description: "Tender cod baked under a layer of tomato, onion and cheese. One of the simplest fish dinners, loved by the whole family."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C and grease a baking dish with oil."
  - "Cut the fish into portions, lay in the dish, season with salt and pepper and sprinkle with lemon juice."
  - "Cover with thin half rings of onion and slices of tomato."
  - "Spread with mayonnaise or sour cream and scatter with grated cheese."
  - "Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until the cheese is golden. Sprinkle with dill."
tips:
  - "Any white fish works instead of cod: hake, pangasius or zander."
---

Under the cheese the fish stays juicy, and even people who do not like fish eat it to the last bite.
