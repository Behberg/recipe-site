---
title: Beef stroganoff
description: Tender strips of beef in a creamy mushroom sauce. Quick and very tasty.
steps:
  - Cut the meat into thin strips across the grain. Dust with flour.
  - In a very hot pan with half the butter, quickly fry the meat in batches for 1 to 2 minutes. Set aside.
  - In the rest of the butter, fry the onion and mushrooms for 8 minutes.
  - Add the stock, mustard and sour cream and bring to the boil.
  - Return the meat and heat for 2 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
  - Serve with mashed potatoes, buckwheat or rice.
---

The meat should be barely cooked, then it stays tender.
