---
title: "Raspberry lemonade"
description: "Bright pink homemade raspberry lemonade with lemon and mint. A refreshing summer drink from fresh or frozen raspberries."
steps:
  - "Simmer the raspberries with the sugar and water for 3 minutes until they break down."
  - "Press through a sieve and cool the syrup."
  - "Mix the syrup with the lemon juice in a jug."
  - "Top up with cold sparkling water and add ice, lemon slices and mint."
tips:
  - "A few whole raspberries in the jug make the lemonade even prettier."
---

When the raspberries ripen in the garden, this lemonade is a way to drink them, not just eat them.
