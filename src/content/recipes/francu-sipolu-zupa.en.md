---
title: French onion soup
description: Slowly caramelised onions in a rich broth, topped with a cheese crouton.
steps:
  - Slice the onions into thin half rings.
  - Melt the butter in a large pan and cook the onions over a low heat for 45 minutes, stirring often, until dark brown and sweet.
  - Stir in the flour, then pour in the wine and scrape everything off the bottom of the pan.
  - Add the stock and thyme and simmer for 20 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
  - Ladle the soup into ovenproof bowls and top with toasted baguette and grated cheese.
  - Put under the grill for 3 to 5 minutes, until the cheese has melted and browned.
---

The key is patience: onions must not be rushed.
