---
title: "Oven baked pork ribs with sauerkraut"
description: "Tender, slow baked pork ribs on a bed of sauerkraut, apple and caraway. A winter holiday classic in one tin."
steps:
  - "Rub the ribs with salt, pepper and mustard. If you have time, leave them for 2 hours."
  - "Heat the oven to 170 °C. Mix the sauerkraut with onion half rings, apple slices, caraway and sugar."
  - "Spread the sauerkraut in a deep roasting tin, pour in the water or beer and lay the ribs on top."
  - "Cover with foil and bake for 1.5 hours."
  - "Remove the foil, raise the oven to 210 °C and bake for 25 to 30 minutes more, until the ribs are brown and crisp."
tips:
  - "Cooked in the fat and juices from the ribs, the sauerkraut becomes so tasty it disappears faster than the meat."
---

Ribs with sauerkraut cook themselves in the oven while the house gets ready for a celebration.
