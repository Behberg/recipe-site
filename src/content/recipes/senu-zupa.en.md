---
title: "Wild mushroom soup with potatoes"
description: "Fragrant wild mushroom soup with porcini or other forest mushrooms, potatoes and dill. An autumn classic with sour cream."
steps:
  - "Clean the mushrooms and cut the large ones into pieces."
  - "Melt the butter in a pan and fry the chopped onion and grated carrot for 5 minutes."
  - "Add the mushrooms and fry for 10 minutes, until the juices have evaporated and they begin to brown."
  - "Pour in the water, add diced potatoes and the bay leaf. Simmer for 20 minutes, until the potatoes are soft."
  - "Season with salt, pepper and dill. Serve with a spoonful of sour cream."
tips:
  - "In winter make the same soup with 40 g dried mushrooms, soaked for 30 minutes first."
---

When the basket is full after a morning in the forest, the first mushrooms always go into the soup pot. The whole house smells of the woods.
