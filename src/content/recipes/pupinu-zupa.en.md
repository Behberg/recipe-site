---
title: "Bean soup with smoked meat"
description: "Thick, hearty bean soup with smoked ribs, potatoes and tomato. Warming on a winter evening and freezes well."
steps:
  - "Cover the smoked meat with the water, add the bay leaf and simmer for 40 minutes."
  - "Lift out the meat, take it off the bones and chop it. Add diced potatoes to the broth."
  - "In a frying pan, fry the onion and grated carrot, add the tomato paste and cook for another 2 minutes. Tip into the soup."
  - "Add the rinsed beans and the meat and simmer for 15 minutes."
  - "Crush in the garlic, add pepper and salt if needed. Sprinkle with parsley."
tips:
  - "Add salt at the end: smoked meat is already salty."
---

This soup is even better the next day. Make a big pot and freeze half for later.
