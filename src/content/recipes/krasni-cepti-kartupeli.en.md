---
title: Roast potatoes
description: Crisp golden potatoes with garlic and rosemary. The simplest side for any meal.
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 220 °C.
  - Cut the potatoes into wedges, boil them in salted water for 5 minutes and drain. Shake them gently in the pan to rough up the edges.
  - Toss with the oil, salt, pepper, garlic and rosemary.
  - Spread in a single layer on a tray and roast for 35 to 40 minutes, turning halfway.
tips:
  - The roughed up edges are what turn the crispiest.
---

A side dish nobody ever says no to.
