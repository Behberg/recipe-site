---
title: "Courgette soup with melted cheese"
description: "Pale green, smooth courgette soup with melted cheese, dill and toasted pumpkin seeds. The best thing to do with a courgette glut."
steps:
  - "Soften the onion and garlic in oil in a pan for 5 minutes."
  - "Add the diced courgettes and potato and pour in the stock. Simmer for 15 minutes."
  - "Add the processed cheese and dill and blend until smooth."
  - "Season with salt and pepper."
  - "Serve with toasted pumpkin seeds."
tips:
  - "Do not peel young courgettes: the skin gives the soup a lovely green colour."
---

When there are more courgettes in August than you can give to the neighbours, this soup solves it gently and deliciously.
