---
title: Oven baked chicken drumsticks with potatoes
description: Chicken drumsticks and potatoes in one tray with garlic and paprika. It cooks itself while you relax.
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 200 °C.
  - Cut the potatoes into wedges and the onions into large pieces.
  - Toss everything in a large roasting tray with the oil, garlic, spices, salt and pepper.
  - Arrange the drumsticks on top, skin side up.
  - Bake for 45 to 50 minutes, until the chicken is golden and the potatoes are soft.
---

One tray, few dishes and a meal for the whole family.
