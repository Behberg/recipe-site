---
title: "Flourless chocolate cookies"
description: "Chewy, shiny chocolate cookies with no flour or butter, just egg whites and cocoa. Gluten free and darker than any brownie."
yield: "about 16 cookies"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 175 °C and line two baking trays with paper."
  - "Mix the icing sugar, cocoa and salt."
  - "Add the egg whites and vanilla and stir to a thick, glossy batter. Do not whisk."
  - "Stir in the chocolate chips. Drop spoonfuls onto the paper with plenty of space, as they spread."
  - "Bake for 10 to 12 minutes, until cracked and shiny on top. Let them cool completely on the trays."
tips:
  - "Warm cookies are very soft: only lift them off the paper once completely cool."
---

These cookies look like the top of a brownie and taste of pure chocolate. And no one notices there is no flour.
