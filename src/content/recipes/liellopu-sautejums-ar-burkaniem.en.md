---
title: "Beef stew with carrots and potatoes"
description: "Slow braised, tender beef with carrots, potatoes and thyme in a rich gravy. A hearty winter dish that practically cooks itself."
steps:
  - "Cut the beef into 4 cm chunks and season with salt, pepper and the flour."
  - "Heat the oil in a heavy pan and brown the meat on all sides in batches. Set aside."
  - "In the same pan fry the onions for 5 minutes, add the garlic and tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes more."
  - "Return the meat, pour in the stock and add the bay leaves and thyme. Cover and simmer gently for 1.5 hours."
  - "Add the carrots and potatoes cut into large chunks. Simmer for another 40 minutes, until the meat and vegetables are tender."
tips:
  - "Brown the meat in batches: if the pan is crowded it releases juice and will not brown."
---

A stew takes time but not work. While it bubbles slowly, the whole house fills with the smell of Sunday.
