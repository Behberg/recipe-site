---
title: Garlic pampushky
description: Soft yeast buns with garlic and dill oil. The essential companion to borscht.
yield: 12 buns
steps:
  - Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, salt, water and oil. Knead until smooth and leave to rise for 1 hour.
  - Divide into 12 balls and pack them tightly into an oiled tin. Leave to rise for 20 minutes.
  - Bake in the oven at 200 °C for 18 to 20 minutes, until golden.
  - Crush the garlic with a pinch of salt and mix with the oil and chopped dill.
  - Brush the hot buns generously with the garlic sauce and serve with borscht.
---

In Ukraine borscht without pampushky is only half a meal.
