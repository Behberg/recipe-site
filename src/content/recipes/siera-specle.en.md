---
title: Cheese spätzle
description: Soft homemade egg noodles with melted cheese and crispy fried onions.
steps:
  - Mix the flour, eggs, water, salt and nutmeg until the batter is thick and bubbly. Let it rest for 10 minutes.
  - Slice the onions into rings and fry slowly in butter until deep golden.
  - Press the batter through a colander with large holes, or scrape it off a board with a knife, into boiling salted water.
  - When the spätzle float, lift them out with a slotted spoon.
  - Layer the spätzle and cheese in a dish, finishing with cheese. Bake in the oven at 200 °C for 10 minutes. Serve with the onions and chives.
---

A dish from Alpine villages after a long day of skiing.
