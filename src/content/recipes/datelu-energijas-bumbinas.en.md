---
title: "Date energy balls"
description: "Sweet balls of dates, almonds and cocoa with no baking and no added sugar. A snack for your bag or with coffee."
yield: "16 balls"
steps:
  - "If the dates are hard, cover them with hot water for 10 minutes and drain."
  - "Blitz the almonds and oats in a food processor to coarse crumbs."
  - "Add the dates, cocoa, peanut butter and salt. Blitz until the mixture holds together when pressed."
  - "With wet hands roll 16 balls and coat them in desiccated coconut."
  - "Chill in the fridge for 30 minutes. Keep in a sealed container for up to 2 weeks."
---

Two balls and a glass of water replace the afternoon chocolate bar.
