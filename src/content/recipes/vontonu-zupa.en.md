---
title: Wonton soup
description: A clear broth with tender pork and prawn dumplings, pak choi and spring onions.
steps:
  - Chop the prawns finely and mix with the meat, soy sauce, sesame oil, ginger and half the spring onions.
  - Put a teaspoon of filling in the middle of each square, fold into a triangle and stick the two corners together.
  - Bring the broth to the boil and season with soy sauce.
  - Cook the wontons in the broth for 4 to 5 minutes, adding the pak choi for the last minute.
  - Serve with spring onions and a drop of sesame oil.
---

In Canton wonton soup is eaten for breakfast, lunch and late at night.
