---
title: Kliņģeris (birthday pretzel)
description: A festive yeast pretzel loaf with raisins, almonds and cardamom. An essential birthday treat.
steps:
  - Dissolve the yeast in the warm milk with a teaspoon of sugar.
  - Melt the butter and let it cool. Add it to the milk with the sugar, 2 eggs, cardamom, salt and lemon zest.
  - Gradually knead in the flour and knead for 10 minutes. Finally knead in the raisins and candied peel.
  - Let the dough rise in a warm place for 1 hour.
  - Roll the dough into a long rope, shape it into a pretzel on a baking tray and leave to rise for another 30 minutes.
  - Brush with beaten egg, sprinkle with almonds and bake in the oven at 180 °C for 35 to 40 minutes.
  - Dust the cooled kliņģeris with icing sugar.
---

The kliņģeris shape is the symbol of a Latvian birthday. The scent of a big kliņģeris fills the whole house.
