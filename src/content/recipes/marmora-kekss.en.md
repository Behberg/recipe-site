---
title: Marble cake
description: A moist butter cake with a chocolate and vanilla swirl. For coffee and afternoon tea.
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 175 °C. Line a loaf tin with paper.
  - Beat the butter and sugar until pale, then add the eggs one at a time with the vanilla.
  - Fold in the flour with the baking powder, alternating with the milk.
  - Mix a third of the batter with the cocoa.
  - Spoon the light and dark batters into the tin alternately and make swirls with a fork.
  - Bake for 45 to 50 minutes, until a skewer comes out dry.
---

The pattern is different in every slice, and that is exactly the joy of this cake.
