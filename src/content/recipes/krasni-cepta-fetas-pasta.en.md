---
title: "Baked feta pasta with cherry tomatoes"
description: "The famous TikTok pasta: a whole block of feta baked with cherry tomatoes, stirred into a creamy sauce and tossed with pasta."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C."
  - "Tip the tomatoes into a baking dish, drizzle with half the oil, season with salt and pepper and toss. Place the whole block of feta in the middle, drizzle with the rest of the oil and sprinkle with oregano and chilli."
  - "Bake for 35 minutes until the tomatoes have burst and the feta is golden on top."
  - "While the tomatoes bake, cook the pasta in salted water as the packet says. Save a cup of the cooking water before draining."
  - "Crush the garlic into the hot dish and mash the feta and tomatoes with a fork into a creamy sauce."
  - "Stir in the pasta and torn basil. If the sauce is too thick, add a splash of pasta water."
tips:
  - "The recipe started in Finland as uunifetapasta, and in 2021 TikTok made shops run out of feta."
  - "Add spinach or roast chicken to make it even more filling."
  - "Goat's cheese or herb cream cheese works instead of feta."
---

One of the most famous internet recipes ever, and for good reason: 10 minutes of work, and the oven does the rest.
