---
title: "Garlic dip for barbecue and kebabs"
description: "Creamy garlic sauce with sour cream, mayonnaise and dill. The best companion for shashlik, grilled meat, potato wedges and kebabs. Ready in 5 minutes."
steps:
  - "Crush or finely grate the garlic."
  - "Mix the sour cream with the mayonnaise, garlic and lemon juice."
  - "Stir in the finely chopped dill and season with salt and pepper."
  - "Chill for at least 30 minutes so the flavours come together."
tips:
  - "With sour cream only and no mayonnaise the sauce is lighter and just as good."
---

Shashlik is not real shashlik without garlic sauce. It is the first thing to disappear from the barbecue table.
