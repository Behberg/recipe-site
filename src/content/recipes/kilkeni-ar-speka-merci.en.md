---
title: "Ķiļķeni (Latvian potato dumplings with bacon cream sauce)"
description: "Kurzeme’s ķiļķeni: soft potato and flour dumplings with a smoked bacon, onion and sour cream sauce. How boiled potatoes become a feast."
steps:
  - "Boil and drain the potatoes and mash them while warm without milk. Let cool."
  - "Mix in the egg, salt and flour and knead to a soft dough."
  - "Roll the dough into ropes and cut into 3 cm pieces, pressing each lightly with a fork."
  - "Cook in salted water for 3 to 4 minutes after they float. Lift out with a slotted spoon."
  - "For the sauce, render the bacon, fry the onion, add the sour cream and heat through."
  - "Pour the sauce over the dumplings and sprinkle with dill."
tips:
  - "Ķiļķeni are even better the next day, fried in butter until golden."
---

Ķiļķeni are Kurzeme country food made from what is always in the house. Every farm cook had her own secret.
