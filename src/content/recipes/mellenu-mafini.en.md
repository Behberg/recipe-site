---
title: "Blueberry muffins with yoghurt and lemon"
description: "Fluffy, moist blueberry muffins with yoghurt and lemon zest. Use fresh or frozen blueberries all year round."
yield: "12 muffins"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 190 °C and line a muffin tin with paper cases."
  - "Mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, salt and lemon zest. Set aside a spoonful of flour."
  - "Whisk the eggs with the yoghurt and melted butter and stir into the dry ingredients just until combined."
  - "Toss the blueberries in the reserved flour and gently fold them into the batter."
  - "Divide between the cases and bake for 22 to 25 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean."
tips:
  - "Coating the berries in flour stops them sinking. Add frozen berries straight from the freezer."
---

In July, when the forest is full of wild blueberries, these muffins get baked almost every week.
