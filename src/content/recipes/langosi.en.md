---
title: Lángos
description: Hungarian fried yeast flatbread with garlic, sour cream and grated cheese.
yield: 6 lángos
steps:
  - Mix the flour, yeast, sugar, salt and milk. Knead a soft dough and leave to rise for 1 hour.
  - Divide into 6 and, with oiled hands, stretch each piece into a thin round with a thicker rim.
  - Fry in oil at 180 °C for 1 to 2 minutes on each side, until golden.
  - Crush the garlic with a little water and salt and brush it over the hot lángos.
  - Spread with sour cream and sprinkle generously with cheese.
---

At the markets of Budapest and by Lake Balaton it is eaten while still hot.
