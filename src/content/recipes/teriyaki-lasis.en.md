---
title: "Teriyaki salmon with sesame and rice"
description: "Glossy teriyaki salmon in a sweet soy, mirin and ginger glaze with sesame and spring onions. A quick, healthy dinner in 25 minutes."
steps:
  - "For the sauce, mix the soy sauce, mirin, honey, ginger and crushed garlic."
  - "Cut the salmon into 2 or 4 pieces and pat dry."
  - "Heat the oil in a frying pan and cook the salmon for 3 minutes on each side."
  - "Pour in the sauce and let it bubble for 2 minutes, spooning it over the fish, until thick and glossy."
  - "Sprinkle with sesame and spring onions and serve with rice and cucumber."
tips:
  - "No mirin? Use a spoonful of water and a teaspoon of sugar instead."
---

Teriyaki sauce is what gets children to eat fish without complaint. Sweet, salty and sticky.
