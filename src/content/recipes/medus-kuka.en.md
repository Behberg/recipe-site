---
title: Honey cake
description: Thin honey layers with a sour cream frosting. After a night in the fridge it melts in the mouth.
steps:
  - Heat the honey, sugar and butter over a water bath until melted. Stir in the eggs and heat for another 3 minutes.
  - "Add the baking soda: the mixture will foam and turn paler. Take off the heat and stir in the flour."
  - Divide the dough into 8. Roll each piece thinly on baking paper and cut out a circle using a plate.
  - "Bake each layer in the oven at 180 °C for 4 to 5 minutes. Bake the trimmings too: they are for the crumbs."
  - For the frosting, whip the sour cream with the icing sugar and vanilla.
  - Stack the layers, spreading each one generously with frosting. Sprinkle with the crushed trimmings.
  - Let the cake soak in the fridge for at least 8 hours.
---

It takes time, but the cake is so good you will be asked for it at every celebration.
