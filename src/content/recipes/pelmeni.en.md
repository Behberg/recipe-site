---
title: Homemade pelmeni
description: Small, juicy meat dumplings in thin dough. Serve with sour cream, butter and dill.
yield: about 100 pelmeni
steps:
  - For the dough, mix the flour with the salt, add the egg and water. Knead for 10 minutes into a smooth dough. Wrap in cling film for 30 minutes.
  - Grate or mince the onion. Mix with the meat, water, salt and pepper. The water makes the filling juicy.
  - Roll the dough out thinly and cut out rounds with a glass.
  - Put half a teaspoon of filling on each round, fold into a half moon and join the two ends together.
  - Boil in a large pan of salted water with a bay leaf. When the pelmeni float, cook for another 3 to 4 minutes.
  - Serve with butter, sour cream and dill.
tips:
  - Freeze pelmeni on a tray, then tip them into a bag. Cook straight from frozen.
---

Making pelmeni is a great thing to do together. Make a big batch and freeze it.
