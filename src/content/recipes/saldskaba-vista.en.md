---
title: Sweet and sour chicken
description: Crispy pieces of chicken in a glossy sweet and sour sauce with peppers and pineapple.
steps:
  - Dice the chicken, mix it with the egg and then coat it in starch.
  - Fry in batches in hot oil until golden and crisp. Drain.
  - In a wok, fry the pepper and onion, cut into large pieces, for 2 minutes.
  - Add the sauce ingredients and the pineapple and bring to the boil until the sauce thickens.
  - Stir in the chicken just before serving so it stays crisp. Serve with rice.
---

A dish that became world famous in Chinese restaurants outside China.
