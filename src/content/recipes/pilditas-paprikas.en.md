---
title: "Stuffed peppers with minced meat and rice"
description: "Peppers filled with minced meat and rice, braised in a tomato and sour cream sauce. A late summer classic that freezes well."
steps:
  - "Parboil the rice for about 8 minutes and drain."
  - "Cut the tops off the peppers and remove the seeds."
  - "Mix the mince with the rice, half the chopped onion, salt and pepper. Stuff the peppers with the mixture."
  - "In a large pan, fry the rest of the onion and the grated carrot in oil, then add the passata and water."
  - "Stand the peppers upright in the sauce, cover and simmer gently for 45 minutes."
  - "Stir the sour cream into the sauce. Serve with dill and bread."
tips:
  - "Stuffed peppers freeze well raw; cook them straight from frozen, adding 20 minutes."
---

At the end of August, when peppers are cheapest, this is made in a huge pot with half put away for winter.
