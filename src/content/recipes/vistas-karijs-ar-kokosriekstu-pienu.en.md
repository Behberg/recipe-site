---
title: "Easy chicken curry with coconut milk"
description: "Mild, creamy chicken curry with coconut milk, ginger and spinach. A quick one pan dinner with rice in 35 minutes."
steps:
  - "Cut the chicken into pieces and brown in oil in a frying pan for 5 minutes. Set aside."
  - "In the same pan fry the onion for 5 minutes, then add the garlic, ginger, curry powder and tomato paste. Cook for 1 minute."
  - "Pour in the coconut milk, return the chicken and simmer for 12 minutes."
  - "Stir in the spinach, lime juice and salt."
  - "Serve with rice and coriander."
tips:
  - "For a hotter curry add a chopped chilli along with the garlic."
---

A curry does not have to follow any one country’s recipe. This version is simple and fits any weekday.
