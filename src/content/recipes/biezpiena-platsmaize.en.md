---
title: Cottage cheese tray bake
description: A soft yeast tray bake with a thick layer of vanilla cottage cheese. A coffee table classic.
steps:
  - Dissolve the yeast and sugar in the warm milk. Add the melted butter, egg and flour. Knead into a smooth dough and leave to rise for 45 minutes.
  - For the filling, mix the cottage cheese with the eggs, sugar, sour cream and vanilla sugar. Stir in the raisins.
  - Roll the dough out to the size of the baking tray and lay it in the lined tray, making a rim.
  - Spread the cottage cheese filling on top and leave to rise for 15 minutes.
  - Bake in the oven at 190 °C for 25 to 30 minutes, until the edges are golden.
---

One tray is enough for the whole family and the neighbours too.
