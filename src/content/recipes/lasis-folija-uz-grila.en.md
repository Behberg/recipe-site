---
title: "Foil wrapped grilled salmon with lemon and dill"
description: "Juicy salmon cooked in foil parcels on the grill with lemon, butter, garlic and dill. It never dries out or sticks to the grill."
steps:
  - "Cut the salmon into 4 pieces and lay each on a sheet of foil."
  - "Season with salt and pepper and top with lemon slices, knobs of butter, garlic slices and dill."
  - "Fold the foil into tightly sealed parcels."
  - "Place over a medium heat on the grill and cook for 12 to 15 minutes."
  - "Open the parcels carefully to avoid the steam and serve."
tips:
  - "You can also bake the parcels in the oven at 200 °C for 15 minutes."
---

Salmon in foil is the stress free way to grill fish. It steams in its own juices with butter and lemon.
