---
title: Baklava
description: Crisp layers of filo with walnuts and pistachios, soaked in honey syrup.
yield: about 24 pieces
steps:
  - Chop the nuts finely and mix them with the cinnamon.
  - Butter a roasting tin. Lay in 8 filo sheets, brushing each one with butter.
  - Scatter over a third of the nuts and top with 4 buttered sheets. Repeat, finishing with 8 sheets.
  - With a sharp knife, cut into diamonds right through to the bottom.
  - Bake in the oven at 170 °C for 45 to 50 minutes, until golden.
  - For the syrup, bring the sugar and water to the boil, add the honey and lemon juice and boil for 5 minutes.
  - Pour the cooled syrup over the hot baklava and leave to soak for at least 4 hours.
---

The golden rule: pour cold syrup over hot baklava, and it will stay crisp.
