---
title: "Sizzling chicken fajitas with peppers"
description: "Sizzling chicken fajitas with colourful peppers, onion and Mexican spices, served in tortillas. A fun family dinner in 30 minutes."
steps:
  - "Cut the chicken, peppers and onion into strips."
  - "Mix the chicken with the spices, salt, half the lime juice and a spoonful of oil."
  - "Sear the chicken in a very hot pan for 5 to 6 minutes. Set aside."
  - "In the same pan with the rest of the oil fry the peppers and onion for 4 minutes so they stay crunchy."
  - "Return the chicken and squeeze over the rest of the lime. Serve in the pan with warm tortillas, sour cream and salsa."
tips:
  - "Put the pan in the middle of the table and let everyone fill their own tortilla."
---

Fajitas arrive at the table still sizzling. The sound and smell are half the fun.
