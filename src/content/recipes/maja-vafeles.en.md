---
title: "Homemade waffles with jam and cream"
description: "Crisp outside, soft inside: simple homemade waffles in a waffle iron. A Sunday breakfast with jam and whipped cream."
yield: "8 waffles"
steps:
  - "Mix the flour, baking powder, sugar, vanilla sugar and salt."
  - "In another bowl whisk the eggs with the milk and melted butter."
  - "Stir the wet ingredients into the dry until no dry flour remains. A few lumps are fine."
  - "Rest the batter for 5 minutes. Heat the waffle iron and grease it lightly."
  - "Cook each waffle for 3 to 4 minutes until golden and crisp."
  - "Serve straight away with jam, berries or whipped cream."
tips:
  - "Put cooked waffles on a rack, not a plate, so they stay crisp."
---

The smell of waffles on a Sunday morning wakes even the people nothing else can wake.
