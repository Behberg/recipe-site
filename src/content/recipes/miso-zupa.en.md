---
title: Miso soup
description: A light Japanese soup with miso paste, tofu, seaweed and spring onions. Ready in 10 minutes.
steps:
  - Soak the seaweed in water for 5 minutes.
  - Bring the water or dashi to the boil. Lower the heat.
  - Dissolve the miso in a ladle with a little hot broth and pour it back into the pan. Do not boil it again.
  - Add the diced tofu and seaweed and heat for 1 minute.
  - Serve with spring onions.
---

Miso must not boil, or it loses its flavour and its healthy bacteria.
