---
title: "Crispy honey garlic chicken wings"
description: "Oven baked crispy chicken wings tossed in a sticky honey, garlic and soy glaze. A party and match night favourite."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 220 °C. Pat the wings dry and toss with the cornflour and salt."
  - "Arrange on a rack over a roasting tin and bake for 40 minutes, turning after 20, until the skin is crisp."
  - "For the glaze, melt the butter in a small pan, add the crushed garlic, then after 30 seconds the honey and soy sauce. Simmer for 2 minutes."
  - "Toss the hot wings in the glaze."
  - "Sprinkle with sesame and spring onions and serve straight away."
tips:
  - "The cornflour is the secret to crispness: it dries the skin without deep frying."
---

Wings are the part of the chicken eaten with your fingers, and you always want more. This glaze is why.
