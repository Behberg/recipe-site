---
title: Goulash soup
description: Hungarian beef soup with sweet paprika, potatoes and caraway.
steps:
  - Fry the chopped onions in oil in a pan until golden.
  - Take off the heat, stir in the paprika (it must not burn), then add the diced meat.
  - Return to the heat, add the caraway, garlic, salt and a little water. Simmer covered for 1 hour, adding water from time to time.
  - Add the chopped pepper, tomatoes, potatoes and the rest of the water.
  - Simmer for another 40 minutes, until the potatoes and meat are tender.
---

In Hungary goulash is a soup, not a thick stew. Herdsmen cooked it in a pot over a campfire.
