---
title: Kofta skewers
description: Middle Eastern minced meat skewers with parsley, onion and spices. Served with tahini sauce.
steps:
  - Finely grate the onion and squeeze out the liquid. Chop the parsley very finely.
  - Mix the meat with the onion, parsley, garlic, spices, salt and pepper. Knead well.
  - With wet hands, shape the meat into long sausages around the skewers.
  - Cook on a hot grill or in a griddle pan for 8 to 10 minutes, turning.
  - For the sauce, mix the tahini with the lemon juice, salt and water until creamy. Serve with pitta and salad.
---

In Lebanon and Turkey kofta is as popular as shashlik is here.
