---
title: "Radish salad with egg and sour cream"
description: "Spring radish salad with cucumber, boiled egg, spring onions and dill in a sour cream dressing. Fresh, crunchy and quick."
steps:
  - "Hard boil the eggs, cool and chop them."
  - "Slice the radishes and cucumber thinly and chop the spring onions and dill."
  - "Mix everything with the sour cream and season with salt and pepper."
  - "Serve straight away while the radishes are crisp."
tips:
  - "Salt only just before serving, otherwise the vegetables release a lot of liquid."
---

When the first radishes come up in the garden, this salad is a must. It tastes of May.
