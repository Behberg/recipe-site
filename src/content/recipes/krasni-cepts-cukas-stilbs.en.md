---
title: "Oven roasted pork knuckle with crispy crackling"
description: "Slow roasted pork knuckle with caraway and garlic: the meat falls off the bone and the skin crackles. Serve with sauerkraut, mustard and beer."
steps:
  - "Score the skin in a diamond pattern and rub with salt, pepper, caraway and crushed garlic."
  - "Heat the oven to 160 °C. Put the knuckles in a roasting tin with onion wedges and bay leaves and pour in the water or beer."
  - "Cover with foil and roast for 2.5 hours, basting now and then."
  - "Remove the foil, raise the oven to 220 °C and roast for 25 to 30 minutes more, until the skin is blistered and crisp."
  - "Serve with mustard and sauerkraut."
tips:
  - "Brush the skin with a spoonful of salty water in the last minutes for extra crunch."
---

Pork knuckle is a pub classic that is easier to make at home than it seems. The oven does it all.
