---
title: "Chia seed pudding with berries"
description: "Creamy chia pudding that sets overnight, topped with berries. A healthy, no cook breakfast with 5 minutes of work."
steps:
  - "Mix the chia seeds with the milk, honey and vanilla."
  - "Stir again after 5 minutes so the seeds do not clump."
  - "Pour into glasses or jars, cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight."
  - "Crush the berries with a fork or leave them whole. Spoon over the pudding and serve."
tips:
  - "For a vegan version use plant milk and maple syrup instead of honey."
---

A jar in the fridge at night, breakfast ready in the morning. The best way to rush out without eating a sandwich standing up.
