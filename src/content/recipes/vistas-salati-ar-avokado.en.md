---
title: "Chicken avocado salad with croutons"
description: "Hearty chicken salad with avocado, tomatoes, romaine and crunchy croutons in a honey mustard dressing. A complete lunch."
steps:
  - "Season the chicken with salt and pepper and fry in a spoonful of oil for 6 minutes on each side. Rest and slice."
  - "Cube the bread and toast in a pan with a spoonful of oil until golden."
  - "For the dressing, whisk the rest of the oil with the lemon juice, mustard, honey, salt and pepper."
  - "Tear the lettuce, slice the avocado and halve the tomatoes."
  - "Arrange everything in a bowl, top with the chicken and croutons and drizzle with dressing."
tips:
  - "Squeeze lemon over the avocado so it does not brown."
---

This salad is proof that salads can be filling. After it you will not need dinner.
