---
title: Bibimbap
description: A Korean rice bowl with vegetables, meat, a fried egg and spicy gochujang sauce.
steps:
  - Cut the meat into thin strips, marinate in the soy sauce for 15 minutes and fry quickly.
  - Cut the carrot and courgette into strips and fry each one separately for a short time. Blanch the spinach and sprouts for 1 minute.
  - For the sauce, mix the gochujang paste, sesame oil, honey and a spoonful of water.
  - Fry the eggs, keeping the yolks runny.
  - Put rice in the bowls, arrange the vegetables and meat in sections on top and an egg in the middle. Sprinkle with sesame.
  - Serve with the sauce. Mix everything well before eating.
---

Bibimbap means “mixed rice”, so be brave and stir the beautiful bowl before you eat it.
