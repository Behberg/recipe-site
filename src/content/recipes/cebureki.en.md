---
title: "Chebureki (crispy fried meat pasties)"
description: "Thin, crisp chebureki with a juicy minced meat and onion filling, fried in oil. The market stall classic made at home."
steps:
  - "Mix the flour and salt with the oil, pour in the boiling water, stir with a spoon, then knead to a smooth dough. Wrap and rest for 30 minutes."
  - "For the filling, finely grate the onions and mix with the meat, cold water, salt and pepper. It should be soft and juicy."
  - "Divide the dough into 10 and roll each thinly into a circle of about 18 cm."
  - "Put 2 spoonfuls of filling on one half, fold over and press the edges firmly, pushing out the air. Crimp with a fork."
  - "Heat 2 cm of oil in a frying pan. Fry the chebureki for 2 to 3 minutes on each side until golden and blistered."
  - "Drain on kitchen paper. Serve hot."
tips:
  - "The water in the filling is the secret: it turns into broth and makes them juicy."
---

A cheburek eaten with both hands, dodging the hot juice, is one of the tastiest childhood memories.
