---
title: "Beetroot hummus"
description: "A bright pink hummus with cooked beetroot. The colour that disappears first from the party table."
steps:
  - "Rinse and drain the chickpeas. Cut the beetroot into chunks."
  - "Put the chickpeas, beetroot, tahini, lemon juice, garlic, cumin and salt in a blender."
  - "Blend for 2 to 3 minutes, adding the ice cold water a spoonful at a time, until smooth and airy."
  - "Taste and add salt or lemon juice if needed."
  - "Spread on a plate, drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with sesame. Serve with pitta or vegetable sticks."
---

The sweetness of beetroot goes well with the nutty taste of tahini.
