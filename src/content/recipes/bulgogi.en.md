---
title: Bulgogi
description: Korean marinated beef, quickly fried with onions. Sweetish, juicy and fragrant.
steps:
  - Put the meat in the freezer for 20 minutes, then slice it very thinly.
  - Grate the pear and mix it with the soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil, garlic and ginger.
  - Marinate the meat and the thinly sliced onion for at least 30 minutes.
  - Fry in batches in a very hot pan or on a grill for 2 to 3 minutes.
  - Sprinkle with spring onions and sesame. Serve with rice and lettuce leaves to wrap the meat in.
---

The grated pear in the marinade tenderises the meat and gives it a gentle sweetness.
