---
title: Cottage cheese bake with raisins
description: A gentle oven baked cottage cheese pudding with semolina and raisins. Good warm or cold.
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 180 °C. Butter a baking dish.
  - Mix the cottage cheese with the eggs, sugar, vanilla sugar, semolina and sour cream.
  - Stir in the raisins and leave the mixture to swell for 10 minutes.
  - Pour into the dish and bake for 35 to 40 minutes, until the top is golden.
  - Serve with sour cream or jam.
---

The same bake they served at nursery school, only even tastier.
