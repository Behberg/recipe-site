---
title: "Crispy roasted chickpeas"
description: "Oven roasted chickpeas with smoked paprika: a snack instead of crisps, or a topping for salads and soups."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C."
  - "Rinse the chickpeas and dry them thoroughly with a tea towel. The drier they are, the crispier they get."
  - "Toss with the olive oil and salt and spread in a single layer on a baking tray."
  - "Roast for 25 to 30 minutes, shaking the tray halfway through."
  - "Toss with the spices while hot and leave to cool on the tray."
---

Add the spices after roasting, so they do not burn and turn bitter.
