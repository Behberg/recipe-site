---
title: "Puff pastry apple turnovers"
description: "Flaky puff pastry turnovers filled with caramelised cinnamon apples. A quick coffee time bake using ready made pastry."
yield: "8 turnovers"
steps:
  - "Peel and dice the apples. Cook in a pan with the butter, sugar and cinnamon for 5 minutes until soft. Let cool."
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C. Roll out the pastry and cut into 8 squares."
  - "Put a spoonful of apple in the middle of each, fold into a triangle and seal the edges with a fork."
  - "Brush with egg and cut 2 small slits in the top."
  - "Bake for 18 to 20 minutes, until puffed and golden. Dust with icing sugar."
tips:
  - "The apples must be completely cold, or the pastry will not puff up."
---

When guests say they will be there in an hour, these turnovers save the day and look like they came from a bakery.
