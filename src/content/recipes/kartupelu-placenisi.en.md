---
title: Mashed potato cakes
description: Golden patties made from yesterday’s mashed potatoes with cheese and spring onions.
steps:
  - Mix the mash with the egg, flour, cheese, chopped spring onions, salt and pepper.
  - With floured hands, shape into patties.
  - Fry in oil over a medium heat for 3 to 4 minutes on each side, until golden.
  - Serve with sour cream.
---

The best thing that can happen to yesterday’s mash.
