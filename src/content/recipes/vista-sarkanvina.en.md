---
title: Coq au vin
description: "A Burgundy classic: chicken slowly braised in red wine with bacon, mushrooms and onions."
steps:
  - Season the chicken with salt and pepper. In a heavy based pan, fry the chopped bacon and take it out.
  - In the bacon fat, brown the chicken pieces on all sides until golden. Take them out.
  - Add the onions and garlic and fry for 3 minutes. Sprinkle over the flour and stir.
  - Pour in the wine and stock, return the chicken and bacon and add the thyme and bay leaves.
  - Braise, covered, over a low heat for 1 hour, until the meat is very tender.
  - Fry the mushrooms in butter and stir them into the stew. Serve with potatoes and parsley.
---

In the old days this dish was made with an old rooster that needed a long time. Chicken is quicker and more tender.
