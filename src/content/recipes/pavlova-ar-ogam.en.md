---
title: "Pavlova with whipped cream and fresh berries"
description: "A meringue cake that is crisp outside and marshmallowy inside, with whipped cream and summer berries. An impressive party dessert."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 120 °C and draw a 22 cm circle on baking paper."
  - "Whisk the egg whites, adding the sugar a spoonful at a time, until stiff and glossy. Fold in the cornflour and lemon juice."
  - "Spread the meringue into the circle, making the edges higher than the centre."
  - "Lower the oven to 100 °C and bake for 80 to 90 minutes. Turn off and leave in the oven until completely cool."
  - "Just before serving whip the cream with the icing sugar, spread over the base and top with berries and mint."
tips:
  - "Assemble the pavlova only just before serving so the meringue stays crisp."
---

A pavlova looks like a cloud covered in berries. It is one of the prettiest desserts you can make from such simple things.
