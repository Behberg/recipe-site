---
title: "Chicken livers in sour cream with onions"
description: "Tender chicken livers braised in sour cream with golden onions. A cheap, iron rich dish, ready in 25 minutes."
steps:
  - "Rinse and trim the livers and cut them in half."
  - "Fry the onion half rings in butter for 8 minutes until golden."
  - "Add the livers and fry over a high heat for 3 minutes, stirring."
  - "Add the sour cream, salt and pepper. Simmer gently for 5 minutes."
  - "Sprinkle with dill and serve with mashed potatoes or buckwheat."
tips:
  - "Do not overcook the livers or they turn tough. They can be just pink inside."
---

Chicken livers are one of the cheapest sources of protein, and in sour cream they turn as soft as pâté.
