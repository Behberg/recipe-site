---
title: "Berry crumble with oats"
description: "Warm berry crumble with juicy blueberries, raspberries or currants and a crunchy oat topping. Wonderful with vanilla ice cream."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 190 °C."
  - "Mix the berries with the sugar and cornflour and spread in a baking dish."
  - "For the crumble, rub the flour, oats and brown sugar with the cold butter into coarse crumbs."
  - "Scatter the crumble evenly over the berries."
  - "Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until the topping is golden and the berry juices bubble. Serve warm with ice cream."
tips:
  - "Use frozen berries straight from the freezer and add 10 minutes to the baking time."
---

A crumble is the quickest berry dessert there is. No dough to shape, and it always works.
