---
title: Greek salad
description: Tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, olives and feta with olive oil and oregano.
steps:
  - Cut the tomatoes into large wedges and the cucumber into thick half moons.
  - Slice the onion into thin rings and the pepper into strips.
  - Put all the vegetables and olives in a bowl and salt lightly.
  - Place a whole slab of feta on top, drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with oregano.
  - Serve with fresh bread to mop up the juices at the bottom of the bowl.
---

In Greece this salad is called horiatiki, “village salad”. The feta is not crumbled but placed on top whole.
