---
title: "Kefir berry loaf cake"
description: "Moist and tender kefir loaf with blueberries, raspberries or blackcurrants. One bowl, ten minutes of work and a perfect cake for coffee."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 175 °C. Grease a loaf tin and dust with flour."
  - "Beat the eggs with the sugar and vanilla sugar, then add the kefir and oil."
  - "Sift in the flour and baking powder and mix to a smooth batter."
  - "Toss the berries in a spoonful of flour and fold into the batter. Pour into the tin."
  - "Bake for 50 to 55 minutes until a skewer comes out clean. Let cool in the tin for 10 minutes."
tips:
  - "Add frozen berries straight from the freezer so they do not colour the batter."
---

This loaf is the answer to what to do with kefir near its date and berries forgotten in the freezer.
