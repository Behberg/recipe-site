---
title: Easter paskha
description: A festive cottage cheese dessert with butter, cream, raisins and candied peel. No baking.
steps:
  - Push the cottage cheese through a sieve so it is completely smooth.
  - In a small pan, mix the yolks, sugar and cream. Heat over a low heat, stirring constantly, until thickened (do not boil!).
  - Melt the butter in the hot mixture and let it cool.
  - Mix with the cottage cheese, vanilla, raisins, candied peel and chopped almonds.
  - Put into a perforated mould or sieve lined with muslin and place a weight on top.
  - Keep in the fridge for at least 12 hours to let the liquid drain. Turn out onto a plate and decorate.
---

Traditionally paskha is shaped into a pyramid with the letters “XB” on the sides.
