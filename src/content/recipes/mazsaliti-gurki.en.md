---
title: "Lightly salted dill pickles (quick cucumbers)"
description: "Crunchy, lightly salted cucumbers with dill, garlic and blackcurrant leaves, ready after just 1 to 2 days. A summer classic with new potatoes."
yield: "1 large jar (3 l)"
steps:
  - "Wash the cucumbers and soak in ice cold water for 2 hours so they stay crunchy. Trim the ends."
  - "Put half the dill, leaves, garlic and horseradish in the bottom of a jar or pot."
  - "Pack in the cucumbers tightly and put the rest of the flavourings on top."
  - "Boil the water with the salt and sugar. Pour the hot brine over the cucumbers."
  - "Cover with a plate and leave at room temperature for 24 to 48 hours. Then refrigerate."
tips:
  - "Blackcurrant and cherry leaves contain tannins that keep the cucumbers crunchy."
---

In July, when cucumbers grow overnight, lightly salted pickles are on the table every day. There are never any left over.
