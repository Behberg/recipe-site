---
title: Cheese and bean quesadillas
description: Crisp tortillas with melting cheese, beans and sweetcorn. Ready in 15 minutes.
steps:
  - Rinse the beans and mash them lightly with a fork together with the cumin.
  - Dice the pepper small.
  - Spread beans, sweetcorn, pepper and cheese over one half of each tortilla. Fold in half.
  - Cook in a dry pan over a medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes on each side, until the cheese has melted.
  - Cut into triangles and serve with sour cream and salsa.
---

The perfect dinner when there is not much in the fridge.
