---
title: "Székely goulash (pork with sauerkraut)"
description: "Hungarian Székely goulash: pork braised with sauerkraut, paprika and caraway, finished with sour cream and dill. Warming on a cold winter day."
steps:
  - "Fry the onions in oil in a pan for 8 minutes, then add the garlic."
  - "Take off the heat, stir in the paprika and caraway, then add the diced pork and salt."
  - "Pour in the water, cover and braise for 40 minutes."
  - "Add the rinsed sauerkraut and braise for 40 minutes more, until the meat is tender."
  - "Stir in half the sour cream. Serve with the rest of the sour cream and dill."
tips:
  - "If the sauerkraut is very sour, rinse it in cold water first."
---

This dish is like a bridge between Hungary and Latvia: paprika from one side, sauerkraut from the other.
