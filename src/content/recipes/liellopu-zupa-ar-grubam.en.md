---
title: "Beef and barley soup with vegetables"
description: "Rich beef soup with pearl barley, potatoes, carrots and celery. As filling as a full meal and as warming as a stove."
steps:
  - "Cover the beef with cold water, bring to the boil and skim. Simmer gently with the bay leaves for 1.5 hours."
  - "Lift out the meat, take it off the bone and chop. Add the rinsed barley to the broth and simmer for 20 minutes."
  - "Add the diced carrots, celery, onion and potatoes. Simmer for 15 minutes."
  - "Return the meat and season with salt and pepper."
  - "Serve with parsley and rye bread."
tips:
  - "The soup thickens as it stands: add a little water the next day."
---

This is the soup made in a big pot on Sunday so there is a warm lunch all week.
