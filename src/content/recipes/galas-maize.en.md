---
title: "Meatloaf with boiled eggs"
description: "Juicy meatloaf with whole boiled eggs inside and a ketchup glaze. A budget dinner that feeds a big family and is good cold too."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C. Soak the breadcrumbs in the milk for 5 minutes."
  - "Mix the mince with grated onion, garlic, the egg, the soaked breadcrumbs, salt and pepper. Knead well."
  - "Spread half the mixture on a lined baking tray in a 10 cm wide strip and lay the peeled boiled eggs in a row along the middle."
  - "Cover with the rest of the meat and shape into a loaf, pressing the edges together well."
  - "Mix the ketchup with the mustard and brush over the top. Bake for 55 to 60 minutes."
  - "Rest for 10 minutes before slicing so the slices hold together."
tips:
  - "Cold meatloaf makes a great sandwich filling the next day."
---

When it is sliced and an egg appears in every piece, children always want the slice with the biggest yolk.
