---
title: "Baked oatmeal with apples and cinnamon"
description: "Hearty baked oats with apples, cinnamon, honey and nuts. A breakfast for the whole family in one dish, tasty cold as well."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C and butter a baking dish."
  - "Mix the oats, baking powder, cinnamon and chopped nuts."
  - "Whisk the eggs with the milk and honey."
  - "Put half the apple slices in the dish, add the oats, pour over the milk mixture and top with the rest of the apples."
  - "Bake for 30 to 35 minutes until golden. Serve warm with yoghurt."
tips:
  - "You can prepare it the night before and just put it in the oven in the morning."
---

When you want a warm breakfast for the whole family without standing at the stove, this dish is the answer.
