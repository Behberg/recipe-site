---
title: "Cheese soup with chicken and potatoes"
description: "Creamy processed cheese soup with chicken, potatoes and carrots. One of the most popular family soups in the Baltics, ready in half an hour."
steps:
  - "Put the chicken in the water, bring to the boil, skim and simmer for 15 minutes. Lift out and cut into pieces."
  - "Add diced potatoes to the broth and simmer for 10 minutes."
  - "Fry the onion and grated carrot in butter and add to the soup."
  - "Add the processed cheese in pieces and stir until it has completely melted."
  - "Return the chicken, season with salt, pepper and dill. Simmer for 2 more minutes."
tips:
  - "Put the processed cheese in the freezer for 15 minutes first: then it grates easily and melts faster."
---

Cheese soup is what children choose when you ask them. Mild, savoury and very cosy.
