---
title: "Birthday sponge cake with berries and cream"
description: "An airy sponge cake with mascarpone whipped cream and fresh berries. A pretty, light and not too sweet celebration cake."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 175 °C and line just the base of a 24 cm round tin."
  - "Whisk the eggs with the sugar and vanilla sugar for 10 minutes, until very pale and tripled in volume."
  - "Gently fold in the sifted flour so the mixture does not deflate. Pour into the tin and bake for 28 to 30 minutes. Do not open the oven for the first 20 minutes."
  - "When cool, cut the sponge into 3 layers."
  - "For the cream, whip the cold cream with the mascarpone and icing sugar until firm."
  - "Soak each layer with syrup, spread with cream and berries. Cover the top and sides too and decorate with berries. Chill for 4 hours."
tips:
  - "Berry jam thinned with water makes a good syrup."
---

This is the cake baked for summer birthdays, when berries are at their best. It looks like it came from a patisserie window.
