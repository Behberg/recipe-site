---
title: Homemade dark rye bread
description: Real sourdough rye bread with caraway. A slow process, but the result is unbeatable.
yield: 1 large loaf
steps:
  - Pour boiling water over half the flour, the malt and the caraway and mix well. Cover and leave the scalded dough in a warm place for 4 hours.
  - When the mixture has cooled to lukewarm, stir in the starter. Cover and leave to ferment for 12 hours.
  - Add the rest of the flour, the salt and honey. Knead with wet hands for 10 minutes.
  - Put into an oiled loaf tin, smooth the top with a wet hand and leave to rise for 2 hours.
  - Bake in the oven at 250 °C for 15 minutes, then lower to 180 °C and bake for another 75 minutes.
  - Brush the hot bread with water, wrap it in a tea towel and let it cool for at least 6 hours before slicing.
tips:
  - Keep a handful of dough as the starter for next time and store it in the fridge.
---

Dark rye bread is the foundation of Latvian cooking. The first bake takes patience, but the smell in the house is worth it.
