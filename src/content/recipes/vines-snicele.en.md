---
title: Wiener schnitzel
description: A thin, crisp veal or pork schnitzel with lemon and potato salad.
steps:
  - Beat the meat between sheets of cling film to 4 to 5 mm thick. Season with salt and pepper.
  - "Coat in turn in flour, beaten eggs and breadcrumbs. Do not press the crumbs on: as it fries, the coating will puff away from the meat in waves."
  - Heat plenty of fat in a pan so the schnitzel floats in it.
  - Fry for 2 to 3 minutes on each side, swirling the pan so the fat washes over the top.
  - Drain on kitchen paper and serve with a lemon wedge.
---

A real Wiener schnitzel should be so thin you can almost see through it.
