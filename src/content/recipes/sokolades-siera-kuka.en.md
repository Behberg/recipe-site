---
title: "Baked chocolate cheesecake"
description: "Thick, silky chocolate cheesecake on a cocoa biscuit base. A chocolate lover’s dream for a birthday or Valentine’s Day."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 160 °C. Crush the biscuits, mix with the cocoa and butter and press into a 24 cm tin. Bake for 10 minutes."
  - "Melt the chocolate and let it cool slightly."
  - "Beat the cream cheese with the sugar until smooth, beat in the eggs one at a time on low speed, then stir in the sour cream and chocolate."
  - "Pour onto the base and bake for 50 to 60 minutes, until the edges are firm but the centre still wobbles slightly."
  - "Turn off the oven and let the cake cool inside with the door ajar. Chill overnight."
tips:
  - "Beat on low speed: too much air makes the cake crack."
---

This cake is so rich a thin slice is enough. But usually nobody stops at one.
