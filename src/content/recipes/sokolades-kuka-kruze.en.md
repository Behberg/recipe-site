---
title: "Chocolate mug cake in the microwave"
description: "A warm chocolate cake in a mug in 5 minutes: mix it right in the mug and cook it in the microwave. Egg free and perfect for an evening treat."
steps:
  - "In a large mug mix the flour, sugar, cocoa, baking powder and salt with a fork."
  - "Add the milk and oil and stir to a smooth batter with no lumps."
  - "Press the chocolate chips into the top."
  - "Microwave on full power for 70 to 90 seconds, until the cake has risen and the top is dry."
  - "Cool for a minute and eat warm, with ice cream if you like."
tips:
  - "Do not overcook: the middle should be slightly moist, like a chocolate fondant."
---

When you want something sweet in the evening but there is no cake in the house, this recipe has saved many a night.
