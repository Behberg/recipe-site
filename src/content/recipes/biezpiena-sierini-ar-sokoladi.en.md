---
title: "Homemade chocolate coated curd cheese bars"
description: "Homemade vanilla curd cheese bars coated in dark chocolate, like Latvia’s best loved sweet snack. No preservatives."
yield: "10 bars"
steps:
  - "Press the curd cheese through a sieve or blend until completely smooth."
  - "Beat with the soft butter, icing sugar and vanilla sugar."
  - "Shape into 10 small bars, lay on paper and freeze for 30 minutes."
  - "Melt the chocolate with the oil or butter."
  - "Dip each bar in the chocolate using a fork, place on paper and chill until set."
tips:
  - "The curd cheese must be completely smooth: that makes the bars as creamy as shop ones."
---

In Latvia chocolate curd bars are more than a sweet: they are the taste of childhood. Homemade ones are even better.
