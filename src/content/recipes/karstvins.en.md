---
title: Mulled wine
description: Warm, fragrant red wine with orange, cinnamon and cloves. For winter evenings.
steps:
  - Slice the orange into rounds and stud a few of them with cloves.
  - In a pan, mix the water, honey and spices. Heat for 5 minutes to release the aroma.
  - Add the wine and orange.
  - "Heat over a low heat for 10 minutes, but do not boil: the wine should be hot, not bubbling."
  - Serve hot in mugs.
tips:
  - For an alcohol free version, use blackcurrant or grape juice.
---
