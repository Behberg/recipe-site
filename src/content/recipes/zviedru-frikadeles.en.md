---
title: Swedish meatballs
description: Small meatballs in a cream sauce with lingonberry jam and mashed potatoes.
steps:
  - Soak the breadcrumbs in the milk. Grate the onion finely.
  - Mix the meat, breadcrumbs, egg, onion, allspice, salt and pepper. Shape into small balls.
  - Fry the meatballs in butter in batches until browned and cooked through. Take them out.
  - Stir the flour into the pan, pour in the stock and cream and add the soy sauce. Cook until the sauce thickens.
  - Put the meatballs into the sauce. Serve with mashed potatoes and lingonberry jam.
---

Lingonberry jam is a must. Luckily, in Latvia almost every home has some.
