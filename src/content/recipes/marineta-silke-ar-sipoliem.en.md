---
title: "Marinated herring with onions"
description: "Mild, sweet and sour marinated herring with onion rings and spices. A classic starter with potatoes and rye bread, ready after a day."
steps:
  - "For the marinade, bring the water to the boil with the sugar, bay leaves and peppercorns. Take off the heat, add the vinegar and let cool."
  - "Cut the herring into 2 cm pieces and the onions into thin rings."
  - "Layer the herring and onions alternately in a jar."
  - "Pour over the cooled marinade and the oil."
  - "Close and refrigerate for at least 12 hours. Serve with dill, potatoes and rye bread."
tips:
  - "If the herring is very salty, soak it in milk for 1 hour before marinating."
---

Herring is the foundation of the Latvian table. A jar of homemade marinated herring in the fridge always comes in handy.
