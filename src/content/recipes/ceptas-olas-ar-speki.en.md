---
title: Fried eggs with bacon and tomatoes
description: Crispy bacon, fried eggs and tomatoes in one pan. A hearty country breakfast.
steps:
  - Cut the bacon into thin strips and fry slowly in a pan until crispy.
  - Slice the tomatoes and fry them in the bacon fat for 1 minute on each side.
  - Crack in the eggs and fry until the whites are set but the yolks are still runny.
  - Sprinkle with salt, pepper and spring onions. Serve straight from the pan with dark rye bread.
---

A breakfast that will keep you making hay until lunchtime.
