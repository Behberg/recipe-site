---
title: Palak paneer
description: Cubes of cheese in a creamy spinach sauce with garlic, ginger and garam masala.
steps:
  - Blanch the spinach in boiling water for 1 minute, cool and blend to a smooth purée.
  - Dice the cheese and fry it in a pan until golden. Take it out.
  - In butter, fry the cumin, onion, garlic and ginger, add the tomato and cook for 5 minutes.
  - Add the spinach purée, garam masala and salt and simmer for 5 minutes.
  - Stir in the cream and cheese cubes and heat through. Serve with naan or rice.
tips:
  - Instead of paneer, halloumi or even firm farmer’s cheese works well.
---

The greenest and one of the best loved Indian vegetarian dishes.
