---
title: "Crispy air fryer chicken drumsticks"
description: "Chicken drumsticks with a smoked paprika rub that turn as crispy as deep-fried in 25 minutes in the air fryer, with no oil bath."
steps:
  - "Pat the drumsticks dry with kitchen paper. The drier the skin, the crispier it gets."
  - "Mix the paprika, garlic powder, oregano, sugar, salt and pepper in a bowl."
  - "Toss the drumsticks in the oil, then in the spice mix, rubbing it in well."
  - "Heat the air fryer to 200 °C. Lay the drumsticks in the basket in one layer with space between them."
  - "Cook for 12 minutes, turn and cook for 12 more minutes until the skin is crisp and the meat by the bone is no longer pink."
  - "Rest for 5 minutes before serving."
tips:
  - "If they do not fit in one layer, cook in two batches: crowded drumsticks steam instead of crisping."
  - "Chicken wings work the same way, 20 minutes in total."
  - "No air fryer: roast on a rack in a 200 °C oven for 40 minutes."
---

The air fryer is one of the most popular kitchen gadgets, and chicken is the most searched recipe for it. The skin comes out as crisp as deep-fried, without a pan of oil.
