---
title: "One pot pasta with meat and tomato sauce"
description: "Hearty pasta with minced meat in tomato sauce, cooked in one pot with no separate boiling. One pot, 25 minutes and a fed family."
steps:
  - "In a wide pan fry the onion and mince in oil for 5 minutes, then add the garlic."
  - "Pour in the passata and water and add the oregano, salt and pepper. Bring to the boil."
  - "Add the uncooked pasta and stir."
  - "Simmer over a medium heat for 12 to 15 minutes, stirring often, until the pasta is tender and the sauce thick."
  - "Sprinkle with cheese and serve."
tips:
  - "If the sauce thickens too quickly, add a little more water."
---

One pot pasta is dinner for evenings when the washing up feels like too much.
