---
title: "Sweet and sour pickled pumpkin"
description: "A Latvian grandma classic: sweet and sour pickled pumpkin cubes with cloves and cinnamon. Great with roast meat and on the holiday table."
yield: "about 3 jars of 0.5 l"
steps:
  - "Peel the pumpkin, remove the seeds and cut into 2 cm cubes."
  - "For the brine, boil the water with the sugar and spices, then add the vinegar."
  - "Add the pumpkin cubes and simmer for 5 to 8 minutes, until translucent but not falling apart."
  - "Transfer to sterilised jars with a slotted spoon, cover with the boiling brine and seal."
  - "Turn upside down and cool. Ready after a week."
tips:
  - "Choose firm, bright orange pumpkins: soft ones fall apart in the brine."
---

Pickled pumpkin was on every grandma’s holiday table. Its sweet and sour taste is perfect with pork.
