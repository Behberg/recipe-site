---
title: Beef bourguignon
description: Beef slowly braised in red wine with carrots, onions and mushrooms until it melts in the mouth.
steps:
  - Cut the meat into 4 cm pieces, pat dry and season with salt and pepper.
  - Fry the bacon in a casserole and take it out. In the fat, brown the meat in batches until it is dark brown.
  - Fry the carrots and onions, add the garlic, tomato paste and flour and stir.
  - Pour in the wine and stock, return the meat and bacon and add the thyme.
  - Braise, covered, in the oven at 160 °C or over a very low heat for 2.5 to 3 hours.
  - Add the fried mushrooms for the last 20 minutes. Serve with mashed potatoes.
---

A stew that tastes even better the next day. Ideal for making ahead for a special occasion.
