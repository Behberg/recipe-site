---
title: "Breakfast burrito with eggs, bacon and avocado"
description: "A hearty breakfast burrito with scrambled eggs, crispy bacon, cheese, avocado and tomato in a tortilla. Breakfast you can take with you."
steps:
  - "Fry the bacon until crisp and set aside."
  - "Whisk the eggs with salt and pepper and cook gently in butter to soft scrambled eggs."
  - "Warm the tortillas in a dry pan for 20 seconds."
  - "Top each with eggs, bacon, cheese, avocado, tomato slices and a few drops of chilli sauce."
  - "Fold in the sides, roll up tightly and toast seam side down for 1 minute."
tips:
  - "Wrapped in foil, a burrito stays warm for half an hour: perfect for the road."
---

A breakfast burrito is a whole breakfast in one roll. You can walk out the door and eat it on the way.
