---
title: "Homemade caramel popcorn"
description: "Crunchy homemade caramel popcorn with butter, brown sugar and a pinch of salt. A movie night snack better than cinema popcorn."
steps:
  - "Heat the oil in a large pan, add the kernels, cover and heat, shaking the pan, until the popping stops."
  - "Heat the oven to 120 °C."
  - "Melt the butter with the sugar, honey and salt in a small pan and boil for 3 minutes. Take off the heat and stir in the bicarbonate of soda."
  - "Pour over the popcorn and stir quickly to coat everything."
  - "Spread on paper and bake for 10 minutes, stirring once. Let cool and break into pieces."
tips:
  - "The soda makes the caramel foam and turns it brittle rather than sticky."
---

A movie night with homemade caramel popcorn becomes an event children remember.
