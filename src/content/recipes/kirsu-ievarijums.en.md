---
title: Cherry jam
description: Dark red cherry jam with whole berries. Wonderful with ice cream and cottage cheese.
yield: about 3 jars of 0.5 l
steps:
  - Wash and stone the cherries. Keep the juice.
  - Sprinkle the cherries and their juice with the sugar and leave for 4 hours.
  - Add the water, bring to the boil and boil for 5 minutes. Take off the heat for 4 hours.
  - Repeat the boiling twice more. The last time, boil for 10 minutes, until the syrup thickens.
  - Pour the hot jam into sterilised jars and seal.
---

Stoning takes time, so invite the whole family to help.
