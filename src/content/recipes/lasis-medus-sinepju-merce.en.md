---
title: "Honey mustard glazed salmon"
description: "Pan fried salmon with a glossy honey, wholegrain mustard and garlic glaze, ready in 10 minutes. A quick and delicious dinner for two."
steps:
  - "Mix the honey, mustard, soy sauce, crushed garlic and lemon juice."
  - "Cut the salmon into 2 pieces and pat dry with kitchen paper."
  - "Heat the oil in a frying pan and cook the salmon for 4 minutes on one side until golden."
  - "Turn it over, lower the heat, pour over the glaze and cook for 3 to 4 more minutes, spooning the glaze over the fish."
  - "Sprinkle with dill and serve with rice or salad."
tips:
  - "The honey glaze burns quickly, so keep the heat at medium once it goes in."
---

The fastest way to turn a plain piece of salmon into a dinner that tastes like a restaurant.
