---
title: Cepelinai
description: Lithuanian potato dumplings with a meat filling and a bacon and sour cream sauce.
steps:
  - Boil a third of the potatoes and mash them. Finely grate the rest.
  - Squeeze the grated potatoes through muslin. Let the liquid settle, pour off the water and add the starch from the bottom to the mixture.
  - Mix the grated potatoes with the mash, the extra starch and salt.
  - For the filling, mix the meat with the finely chopped onion, salt and pepper.
  - With wet hands, flatten a patty, add the filling and close it into an oval shape.
  - Boil in a large pan of salted water for 25 to 30 minutes, stirring carefully.
  - For the sauce, fry the bacon with the onion and add the sour cream. Pour over the cepelinai.
---

Lithuania’s national dish, named after the shape of the zeppelin airship.
