---
title: "Potato sausages with bacon (Latgale style)"
description: "Latgale potato sausages made with grated potatoes, smoked bacon, onions and marjoram, baked until brown. Served with sour cream."
steps:
  - "Soak and rinse the casings."
  - "Dice the bacon and render it, add the onions and fry until golden."
  - "Peel and finely grate the potatoes and squeeze out some of the liquid."
  - "Mix the potatoes with the bacon and onions, eggs, marjoram, salt and pepper."
  - "Using a funnel, fill the casings loosely and tie every 30 cm. Prick the sausages with a needle."
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C and bake in an oiled tin for 60 to 70 minutes, turning, until brown. Serve with sour cream."
tips:
  - "Grate the potatoes just before filling, otherwise they darken."
---

In Latgale and Lithuania potato sausages are food for feasts and working bees. More potato than meat, yet heartier than many meat dishes.
