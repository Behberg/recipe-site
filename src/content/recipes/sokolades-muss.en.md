---
title: Chocolate mousse
description: "An airy French chocolate dessert made from just three ingredients: chocolate, eggs and sugar."
steps:
  - Melt the chocolate over a water bath and let it cool slightly.
  - Separate the yolks from the whites. Stir the yolks into the chocolate one at a time.
  - Whisk the whites with the salt, gradually add the sugar and whisk to stiff peaks.
  - Stir a third of the whites into the chocolate, then gently fold in the rest.
  - Divide between glasses and chill in the fridge for at least 4 hours.
tips:
  - Use very fresh eggs, because they are not cooked.
---

The better the chocolate, the better the mousse. There is nowhere to hide here.
