---
title: Mango lassi
description: A creamy Indian yogurt drink with mango and cardamom.
steps:
  - Peel the mango and cut it into pieces.
  - Blend the mango, yogurt, milk, honey and cardamom until smooth.
  - Pour into glasses with ice and sprinkle with a pinch of cardamom.
tips:
  - "Frozen mango works too: the drink will be even colder and thicker."
---

Perfect with a spicy curry: the yogurt calms the heat.
