---
title: Żurek
description: A Polish sour rye soup with sausage, potatoes, egg and horseradish.
steps:
  - Slice the sausage and fry it in a pan with the onion.
  - Add the stock and the diced potatoes and simmer for 15 minutes.
  - Pour in the starter and add the crushed garlic and marjoram. Simmer for another 10 minutes.
  - Stir in the sour cream and horseradish and salt to taste.
  - Serve with egg halves in each bowl.
tips:
  - Ready made żurek starter can be bought in Polish food shops or larger supermarkets.
---

In Poland żurek is the main soup of the Easter breakfast.
