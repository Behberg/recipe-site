---
title: "Hungarian pörkölt (thick beef paprika stew)"
description: "Authentic Hungarian pörkölt: beef slowly stewed with lots of onions and sweet paprika in a thick red sauce. With noodles or potatoes."
steps:
  - "Finely chop the onions and cook gently in oil in a heavy pan for 15 minutes, until soft and golden."
  - "Take the pan off the heat and stir in the paprika and caraway so the paprika does not burn."
  - "Add the beef cut into 3 cm pieces, the garlic and salt and stir. Return to the heat."
  - "Add a little water, cover and simmer for 1.5 hours, topping up with water now and then."
  - "Add the pepper and tomato and simmer for 30 minutes more, until the meat is very tender and the sauce thick."
tips:
  - "The onions are the base of the sauce: they should melt completely, so do not skimp."
---

In Hungary this is called pörkölt, abroad it is called goulash. It is a thick stew, not a soup.
