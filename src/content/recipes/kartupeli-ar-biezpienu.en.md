---
title: Potatoes with cottage cheese and dill
description: Boiled new potatoes with cottage cheese, sour cream, dill and spring onions. A summer classic.
steps:
  - Wash the potatoes and boil in salted water with a sprig of dill for 15 to 20 minutes.
  - Mix the cottage cheese with the sour cream, chopped dill, spring onions and a pinch of salt.
  - Drain the potatoes and drizzle them with butter.
  - Serve hot with the cold cottage cheese sauce.
---

A June evening, the first new potatoes and cottage cheese with dill. It does not get simpler.
