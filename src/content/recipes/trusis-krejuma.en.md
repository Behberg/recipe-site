---
title: "Rabbit in sour cream with mushrooms"
description: "Tender, slow braised rabbit in a sour cream sauce with mushrooms, carrots and white wine. A traditional country celebration dish with potatoes."
steps:
  - "Season the rabbit pieces with salt, pepper and flour. Brown in butter in a heavy pan until golden. Set aside."
  - "In the same pan fry the onions, carrot and mushrooms for 8 minutes."
  - "Add the wine and simmer for 2 minutes. Return the rabbit, add the bay leaf and thyme and a little water."
  - "Cover and simmer gently for 1 hour."
  - "Stir in the sour cream and simmer for 15 minutes more, until the meat comes easily off the bone."
tips:
  - "Soak the rabbit in milk overnight first to make it even more tender."
---

Rabbits were common on Latvian farms, and rabbit in sour cream was a festive dish. Today it is a rarity worth remembering.
