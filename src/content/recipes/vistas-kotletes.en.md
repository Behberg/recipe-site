---
title: Chicken patties
description: Soft, juicy minced chicken patties with onion and herbs.
steps:
  - Grate the onion finely.
  - Mix the minced chicken with the onion, egg, sour cream, breadcrumbs, herbs, salt and pepper.
  - With wet hands, shape into small patties.
  - Fry in oil over a medium heat for 5 to 6 minutes on each side.
  - Serve with mashed potatoes or rice and a fresh salad.
---

Sour cream in the mixture makes chicken patties especially juicy.
