---
title: "Oven baked potato wedges with paprika"
description: "Crispy potato wedges with paprika, garlic and oregano, baked in the oven with no deep frying. The best side for burgers and meat."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 220 °C."
  - "Scrub the potatoes well and cut them into wedges, skin on."
  - "Soak in cold water for 10 minutes, then dry thoroughly with a tea towel."
  - "Toss with the oil and spices and spread on baking paper in one layer, skin side down."
  - "Bake for 30 to 35 minutes, turning after 20, until golden and crisp. Serve with ketchup."
tips:
  - "Soaking removes starch and drying is essential: wet potatoes never get crisp."
---

These wedges are a healthier alternative to chips, and children eat them even more happily.
