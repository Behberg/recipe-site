---
title: "Stuffed baked sweet potatoes with black beans and feta"
description: "Sweet, soft oven baked sweet potatoes filled with black beans, sweetcorn, feta and a lime yoghurt sauce. A colourful vegetarian dinner."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C. Prick the sweet potatoes with a fork and rub with oil and salt."
  - "Bake for 45 to 50 minutes, until completely soft."
  - "Meanwhile mix the beans and sweetcorn with finely chopped onion, smoked paprika, half the lime juice, salt and pepper."
  - "Mix the yoghurt with the rest of the lime juice and salt."
  - "Split the sweet potatoes lengthways, fluff the flesh with a fork, pile in the bean mixture, scatter with feta and top with the yoghurt and coriander."
tips:
  - "Bake the sweet potatoes ahead and just reheat them before filling."
---

One sweet potato is a complete meal. Sweet, filling and the most colourful thing on the plate.
