---
title: "Buckwheat patties with mushrooms and cheese"
description: "Crisp buckwheat patties with fried mushrooms, onion and cheese. A cheap, filling vegetarian dish that uses up yesterday’s buckwheat."
steps:
  - "Fry the finely chopped onion and mushrooms in oil for 8 minutes until the juices evaporate. Add the garlic. Let cool."
  - "Lightly mash the buckwheat with a fork or potato masher."
  - "Mix the buckwheat with the mushrooms, egg, cheese, half the breadcrumbs, salt and pepper."
  - "With wet hands shape into patties and coat in the remaining breadcrumbs."
  - "Fry in oil over a medium heat for 4 minutes on each side. Serve with sour cream and salad."
tips:
  - "If the mixture is too crumbly, add a spoonful of flour or another egg."
---

Leftover buckwheat does not need to be thrown away. Turned into patties, it tastes better than the porridge itself.
