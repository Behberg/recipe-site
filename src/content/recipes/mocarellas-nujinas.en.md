---
title: "Crispy mozzarella sticks"
description: "Golden, crunchy mozzarella sticks with a stretchy cheese centre, served with tomato sauce. A party and movie night snack."
steps:
  - "Cut the mozzarella into 1.5 cm thick sticks."
  - "Mix the panko with the oregano and garlic powder."
  - "Coat the sticks in flour, beaten egg and panko. Repeat the egg and panko once more for a double coating."
  - "Freeze for 30 minutes so the cheese does not leak while frying."
  - "Fry in hot oil at 175 °C for 1 to 2 minutes until golden. Serve straight away with tomato sauce."
tips:
  - "The double coating and the freezer are key: without them the cheese oozes into the pan."
---

When you break a hot stick and the cheese stretches, children and grown ups are equally delighted.
