---
title: "Kefir berry smoothie with oats"
description: "A filling kefir smoothie with frozen berries, honey and oats. A quick breakfast in a glass, ready in 5 minutes."
steps:
  - "Put the kefir, berries, honey and oats in a blender."
  - "Blend for 1 minute until smooth and frothy."
  - "If it is too thick, add a little more kefir."
  - "Pour into glasses and drink straight away."
tips:
  - "The oats make the smoothie more filling: it keeps you going until lunch."
---

Kefir is in every Latvian fridge. With berries it becomes a breakfast children drink without being reminded.
