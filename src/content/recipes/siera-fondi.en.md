---
title: "Cheese fondue with bread and vegetables"
description: "Swiss cheese fondue of Gruyère and Emmental with white wine, for dipping bread cubes and vegetables. A fun winter dinner with friends."
steps:
  - "Grate the cheese and toss with the cornflour."
  - "Rub the inside of a fondue pot or heavy pan with a cut garlic clove."
  - "Pour in the wine and lemon juice and heat until bubbling."
  - "Add the cheese a handful at a time, stirring in a figure of eight with a wooden spoon until each handful melts. Add the nutmeg."
  - "Set on a burner in the middle of the table and dip bread cubes, boiled potatoes and vegetables on forks."
tips:
  - "Fondue must not boil: the cheese will split and release its fat."
---

Fondue is more than food: it is an evening when everyone sits around one pot and tells stories.
