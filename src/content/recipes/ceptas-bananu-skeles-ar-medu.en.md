---
title: "Caramelised bananas with honey and cinnamon"
description: "Banana halves caramelised in butter with honey and cinnamon, served with vanilla ice cream. A warm dessert ready in 10 minutes."
steps:
  - "Peel the bananas and halve them lengthways."
  - "Melt the butter in a frying pan and add the bananas cut side down."
  - "Cook for 2 minutes until golden, then turn carefully."
  - "Drizzle with honey, sprinkle with cinnamon and cook for 1 more minute, until the sauce bubbles."
  - "Serve straight away with ice cream and the pan sauce."
tips:
  - "The bananas should be ripe but firm: overripe ones fall apart when fried."
---

This dessert is so quick you can make it while the kettle boils. And it always impresses.
