---
title: Homemade rye bread kvass
description: A naturally fermented, refreshing kvass made from dark rye bread, sugar and raisins.
yield: about 3 litres
steps:
  - Cut the rye bread into pieces and dry them in the oven at 150 °C until dark and dry.
  - Bring the water to the boil, pour it over the bread and leave to infuse for 4 hours or overnight.
  - Strain the liquid through muslin and squeeze the bread well.
  - Add the sugar and the yeast dissolved in a little of the liquid. Cover with a tea towel and leave to ferment at room temperature for 12 hours.
  - Put a few raisins into each bottle, fill with kvass and seal tightly.
  - Keep in the fridge for 1 to 2 days, until the kvass becomes fizzy. Store cold.
tips:
  - "Open the bottles carefully: fermentation builds up pressure."
---

Nothing refreshes like cold homemade kvass after a day of haymaking.
