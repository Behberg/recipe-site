---
title: Maki sushi rolls
description: Homemade sushi rolls with salmon, avocado and cucumber in nori. Serve with soy sauce and pickled ginger.
yield: 6 rolls (36 pieces)
steps:
  - Wash the rice until the water runs clear. Cook it covered with the water for 12 minutes, then leave to stand for 10 minutes.
  - Warm the vinegar with the sugar and salt until they dissolve. Pour it over the hot rice, mix carefully and cool to room temperature.
  - Cut the salmon, avocado and cucumber into long strips.
  - Put a nori sheet shiny side down on a bamboo mat. With wet hands, spread the rice thinly, leaving a 2 cm border.
  - Lay the filling along the nearest edge and roll up tightly with the mat. Moisten the free edge so the roll sticks.
  - With a sharp, wet knife, cut each roll into 6 pieces. Serve with soy sauce, wasabi and ginger.
tips:
  - Wet the knife before every cut and the rice will not stick.
---

The first rolls rarely come out perfect. Never mind: they taste just as good.
