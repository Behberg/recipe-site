---
title: "Chicken salad with grapes, celery and walnuts"
description: "Creamy chicken salad with sweet grapes, crunchy celery and walnuts in a light yoghurt dressing. Great for sandwiches and parties."
steps:
  - "Dice the chicken, halve the grapes and slice the celery thinly."
  - "Lightly toast and chop the walnuts."
  - "Mix the yoghurt with the mayonnaise, mustard, salt and pepper."
  - "Toss everything with the dressing and serve on lettuce leaves or in a roll."
tips:
  - "It is even better after an hour in the fridge, once the flavours come together."
---

Grapes in chicken salad seem unusual, but they are exactly what makes it so refreshing.
