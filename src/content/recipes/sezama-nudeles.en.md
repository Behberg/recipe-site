---
title: Cold sesame noodles with cucumber
description: Quick Chinese noodles in a nutty sesame and soy sauce with crunchy cucumber and chilli.
steps:
  - Cook the noodles according to the packet and rinse in cold water.
  - Mix the sauce ingredients until smooth, adding a little warm water if needed.
  - Cut the cucumber into thin strips.
  - Toss the noodles with the sauce and top with the cucumber, spring onions and sesame seeds.
---

The perfect summer dish when you do not feel like standing at the stove.
