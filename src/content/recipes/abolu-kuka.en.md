---
title: Apple cake with cinnamon
description: A simple, moist apple cake that always turns out well. The whole house smells of autumn.
steps:
  - Heat the oven to 180 °C. Line a round tin with baking paper.
  - Whisk the eggs and sugar until pale and fluffy, about 5 minutes.
  - Gently fold in the flour with the baking powder and the melted butter.
  - Peel and core the apples and cut them into thin slices. Sprinkle with cinnamon.
  - Pour half the batter into the tin, lay the apples on top and cover with the rest of the batter.
  - Bake for 40 to 45 minutes. Dust the cooled cake with icing sugar.
---

This is the cake you bake when the garden has more apples than you can eat.
