---
title: "Mini pizza toasts"
description: "Quick mini pizzas on sandwich bread with tomato sauce, ham, pepper and cheese. Children make them themselves and they are ready in 20 minutes."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 200 °C and lay the bread slices on a baking tray."
  - "Spread each with tomato sauce and sprinkle with oregano."
  - "Top with pieces of ham and pepper."
  - "Scatter with cheese."
  - "Bake for 8 to 10 minutes, until the cheese has melted and the edges are crisp."
tips:
  - "Let children choose their own toppings: that way they even eat vegetables."
---

Mini pizzas are how children become cooks. Everyone makes their own and everyone is fed.
