---
title: "Easy kefir cabbage pie"
description: "Quick cabbage pie made with a pourable kefir batter, no yeast and no kneading. Juicy filling of cabbage, carrot and dill."
steps:
  - "Finely shred the cabbage, chop the onion and grate the carrot."
  - "Cook the vegetables in butter for 10 minutes until they wilt. Season with salt and dill and let cool."
  - "Beat the eggs with the kefir and salt, sift in the flour and baking powder and mix to a smooth batter."
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C. Pour half the batter into a greased tin, spread over the cabbage and cover with the rest of the batter."
  - "Bake for 40 to 45 minutes until golden and a skewer comes out clean."
tips:
  - "You can add a chopped boiled egg or a little ham to the filling."
---

When there is cabbage in the garden and kefir in the fridge, a pie can be on the table within an hour.
