---
title: Lasagne
description: A classic Italian lasagne with meat sauce, béchamel and melted cheese.
steps:
  - In a pan, fry the chopped onion and garlic, add the mince and fry until browned.
  - Add the tomato sauce, oregano, salt and pepper. Simmer for 20 minutes.
  - For the béchamel, melt the butter, stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute. Gradually add the milk, stirring constantly, until the sauce thickens. Add nutmeg and salt.
  - "Layer in a dish: a little meat sauce, pasta sheets, meat sauce, béchamel, mozzarella. Repeat 3 times."
  - Finish with béchamel, mozzarella and parmesan.
  - Bake in the oven at 190 °C for 40 to 45 minutes. Let it rest for 10 minutes before cutting.
---

A great Sunday lunch that tastes even better the next day.
