---
title: "Cheese, carrot and garlic salad"
description: "Quick grated cheese salad with carrot, garlic and mayonnaise. Great on rye bread, toast or as a starter in 15 minutes."
steps:
  - "Finely grate the cheese and carrot."
  - "Crush in the garlic and stir in the mayonnaise or sour cream."
  - "Add the chopped walnuts and parsley."
  - "Serve straight away or spread on slices of rye bread."
tips:
  - "If you make it ahead, use less garlic: it gets stronger as it stands."
---

You can mix this while the kettle boils. That is why it appears when guests turn up unexpectedly.
