---
title: "Skābputra (Latvian soured barley porridge)"
description: "Old Latvian skābputra: barley groats, milk and soured milk left to turn tangy for a couple of days. Wonderfully refreshing in summer heat."
steps:
  - "Rinse the groats and put them in a pan with the water and salt. Simmer over a low heat for 45 to 50 minutes, until soft and the water is almost absorbed."
  - "Let the porridge cool to room temperature."
  - "Stir in the milk and the soured milk or kefir. It should be like runny yoghurt."
  - "Pour into a glass jar or clay pot, cover with a tea towel and leave somewhere warm for 1 to 2 days, until pleasantly sour."
  - "Chill in the fridge. Serve cold with hot boiled potatoes."
tips:
  - "If it is too thick, loosen it with a little cold milk."
---

Farm families kept skābputra in the cellar and ate it all summer. Cold skābputra with hot potatoes was the classic haymaking lunch.
