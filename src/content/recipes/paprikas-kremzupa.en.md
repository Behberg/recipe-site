---
title: "Roasted red pepper and tomato soup"
description: "Sweet, smoky soup made from oven roasted red peppers and tomatoes with garlic and basil. Vegan and full of colour."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 210 °C. Halve the peppers and tomatoes and cut the onion into wedges."
  - "Spread in a roasting tin with the garlic, drizzle with oil and season with salt. Roast for 30 minutes until charred at the edges."
  - "Tip into a pan, add the stock and smoked paprika and simmer for 5 minutes."
  - "Blend until completely smooth. Season with salt and pepper."
  - "Serve with basil leaves and a drizzle of olive oil."
tips:
  - "Roasting is the key: it makes the peppers sweet and the soup smoky."
---

At the end of August, when peppers are sweetest and cheapest, this soup smells of late summer.
