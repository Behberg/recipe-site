---
title: "Garlic prawn spaghetti with lemon"
description: "Quick spaghetti with prawns fried in butter, garlic, chilli, white wine and lemon. A restaurant dinner at home in 25 minutes."
steps:
  - "Cook the spaghetti in salted water. Save 1 cup of the cooking water."
  - "Heat the sliced garlic and chilli in the oil and butter in a frying pan for 1 minute."
  - "Add the prawns and cook for 2 minutes until pink."
  - "Pour in the wine and lemon juice and simmer for 1 minute."
  - "Toss in the spaghetti, lemon zest, parsley and a splash of cooking water to make the sauce glossy."
tips:
  - "Do not overcook the prawns: they turn rubbery after 3 minutes."
---

This is what to make when you want a special dinner but time is short.
