---
title: Kugelis
description: A Lithuanian potato bake with bacon and onions. Crisp on top, soft inside.
steps:
  - Dice the bacon and onions and fry until golden.
  - Peel the potatoes and grate them finely. Squeeze out a little of the liquid.
  - Straight away add the hot milk so the potatoes do not turn dark, then the eggs, salt, pepper and the bacon with onions.
  - Pour the mixture into an oiled dish so it is 5 to 6 cm deep.
  - Bake in the oven at 180 °C for 1.5 hours, until the top is deep golden. Serve with sour cream.
---

In Lithuania kugelis is eaten hot, or cold the next day, fried in slices.
