---
title: "Rosehip tea with honey"
description: "Red, sweet and sour tea from dried rosehips with honey and lemon. A winter vitamin drink that grandmothers already made."
steps:
  - "Lightly crush the rosehips in a mortar or with the flat of a knife."
  - "Pour over the boiling water in a pan or thermos and simmer gently for 10 minutes."
  - "Leave to steep covered for another 10 minutes, or overnight in a thermos."
  - "Strain through a fine sieve or muslin to remove the tiny hairs."
  - "Stir in the honey and lemon juice. Drink warm."
tips:
  - "Straining through muslin is essential: the tiny hairs irritate the throat."
---

Rosehip tea in a thermos was a companion on winter hikes and sick days.
