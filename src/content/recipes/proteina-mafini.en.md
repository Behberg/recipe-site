---
title: "Protein muffins with curd cheese and banana"
description: "Filling breakfast muffins with curd cheese, eggs and oats. No added sugar and about 10 g of protein in each."
yield: "12 muffins"
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C. Line a muffin tin with paper cases."
  - "Blend the oats to a flour."
  - "Add the curd cheese, eggs, bananas, protein powder, baking powder and cinnamon and blend to a smooth batter."
  - "Stir in the blueberries or chocolate chips with a spoon."
  - "Divide the batter between the 12 cases and bake for 22 minutes until a skewer comes out dry."
  - "Leave to cool in the tin for 10 minutes, then lift out."
tips:
  - "Without protein powder, add another 30 g of oats and one more egg. The muffins will have a little less protein but taste just as good."
  - "They keep for 4 days in the fridge and 2 months in the freezer."
  - "The riper the bananas, the sweeter the muffins."
