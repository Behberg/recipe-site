---
title: Gooseberry jam
description: Amber gooseberry jam with whole berries. A classic from grandma’s garden.
yield: about 3 jars of 0.5 l
steps:
  - Top and tail the gooseberries. Prick each berry with a needle so they do not burst.
  - Boil the water with the sugar into a syrup.
  - Pour the hot syrup over the berries and leave for 6 hours.
  - Bring to the boil and boil for 10 minutes. Repeat after 6 hours.
  - Pour the hot jam into sterilised jars and seal.
---

Gooseberry bushes grow in almost every country garden, and this is the best way to use the harvest.
