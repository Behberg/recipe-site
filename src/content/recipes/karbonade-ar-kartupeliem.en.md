---
title: Breaded pork chop with fried potatoes
description: A thin, juicy breaded pork chop with fried potatoes and sauerkraut.
steps:
  - Peel the potatoes, cut them into wedges and boil in salted water for 8 minutes. Drain.
  - Cover the meat with cling film and beat it thin. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
  - Coat each chop in flour, then beaten egg and finally breadcrumbs.
  - Heat oil with butter in a pan and fry the chops for 3 to 4 minutes on each side, until golden.
  - In another pan, fry the potatoes until crispy. Sprinkle with dill.
  - Serve with sauerkraut or a fresh salad.
---

A Sunday lunch classic that never gets old.
