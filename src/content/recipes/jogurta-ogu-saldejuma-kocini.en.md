---
title: "Yoghurt and berry ice lollies"
description: "Healthy homemade ice lollies from yoghurt, honey and berries with pretty pink swirls. A summer treat for children with no added sugar."
yield: "8 lollies"
steps:
  - "Mix the yoghurt with half the honey and the vanilla."
  - "Blend the berries with the rest of the honey."
  - "Pour the yoghurt and berry purée alternately into lolly moulds to make stripes."
  - "Swirl lightly with a stick to make a pattern and insert the sticks."
  - "Freeze for at least 6 hours. Dip the moulds briefly in warm water to release."
tips:
  - "No lolly moulds? Paper cups and wooden spoons work too."
---

These lollies are so healthy you could eat them for breakfast, and children feel like it is a party.
