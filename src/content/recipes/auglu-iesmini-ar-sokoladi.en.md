---
title: "Chocolate drizzled fruit skewers"
description: "Colourful fruit skewers drizzled with melted dark and white chocolate. A party dessert children can make themselves in 20 minutes."
steps:
  - "Cut the fruit into large pieces and pat dry."
  - "Thread onto wooden skewers, alternating the colours."
  - "Melt the dark and white chocolate separately."
  - "Lay the skewers on paper and drizzle with stripes of chocolate using a spoon."
  - "Chill for 10 minutes until the chocolate sets."
tips:
  - "The fruit must be dry or the chocolate will not stick."
---

Fruit on skewers with chocolate is a way to win out against cakes and sweets at a party.
