---
title: "Rhubarb and strawberry jam"
description: "Sweet and tangy rhubarb and strawberry jam with lemon. A June classic where rhubarb brings freshness and strawberries colour and aroma."
yield: "about 3 jars of 0.5 l"
steps:
  - "Peel the rhubarb and cut into 2 cm pieces. Hull the strawberries and halve the large ones."
  - "Mix with the sugar and leave for 3 hours or overnight until the juices run."
  - "Bring to the boil and cook over a medium heat for 20 to 25 minutes, skimming off the foam."
  - "Add the lemon juice and cook for 3 more minutes."
  - "Fill sterilised jars while hot and seal."
tips:
  - "The jam is ready when a drop on a cold plate no longer spreads."
---

In June the garden has rhubarb and the first strawberries at the same time. This jam joins them in a jar for the whole year.
