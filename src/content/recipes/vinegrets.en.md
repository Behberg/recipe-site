---
title: Vinegret (beetroot salad)
description: A colourful beetroot salad with potatoes, carrots, pickles, sauerkraut and oil.
steps:
  - Boil the beetroot, potatoes and carrots in their skins, cool and peel.
  - Dice all the vegetables and pickles to the same size.
  - First mix the beetroot with the oil separately, so it does not colour the other vegetables too strongly.
  - Add the rest of the ingredients, the sauerkraut and the peas. Salt to taste.
---

Vinegret is the vegan, healthy sibling of rasols. Unrefined oil gives it its real flavour.
