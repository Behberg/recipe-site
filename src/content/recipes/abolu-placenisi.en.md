---
title: "Apple and cinnamon fritters"
description: "Soft kefir pancakes with grated apple and cinnamon. An autumn breakfast that smells like apple pie."
steps:
  - "Mix the kefir with the egg, sugar, salt and bicarbonate of soda."
  - "Stir in the flour to a thick batter."
  - "Peel and coarsely grate the apples. Stir into the batter with the cinnamon."
  - "Heat oil in a frying pan and spoon in the fritters. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes on each side."
  - "Serve dusted with icing sugar."
tips:
  - "Grate the apples just before frying so they do not release juice and thin the batter."
---

When autumn apples start to spoil faster than you can eat them, these fritters come to the rescue.
