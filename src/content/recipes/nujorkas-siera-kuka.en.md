---
title: New York cheesecake
description: A dense, creamy cheesecake on a biscuit base. Topped with berries or jam.
steps:
  - Crush the biscuits, mix with the butter and press into the base of the tin. Bake in the oven at 180 °C for 10 minutes.
  - Beat the cream cheese with the sugar on a low speed until smooth, so no air gets in.
  - Beat in the eggs one at a time, then the sour cream, vanilla and lemon juice.
  - Pour the filling onto the base. Bake in the oven at 160 °C for 60 minutes. The middle should still wobble slightly.
  - Turn off the oven and let the cake cool inside for 1 hour, then in the fridge overnight.
tips:
  - All the ingredients should be at room temperature, so the cheesecake is smooth.
---

Serve with strawberry jam from our jams section.
