---
title: "Homemade chips (double fried fries)"
description: "Crispy homemade chips fried twice like a proper chip shop: fluffy inside, golden and crunchy outside. For burgers, fish and patties."
steps:
  - "Peel the potatoes and cut into 1 cm thick batons. Soak in cold water for at least 30 minutes."
  - "Drain and dry very thoroughly with a tea towel."
  - "First fry: cook in batches in oil at 150 °C for 5 to 6 minutes, until soft but still pale. Drain on a rack."
  - "Second fry: heat the oil to 185 °C and fry for 2 to 3 minutes until golden and crisp."
  - "Sprinkle with salt straight away and serve with ketchup."
tips:
  - "Double frying is the whole secret: the first cooks the middle, the second makes them crisp."
---

Once you fry chips yourself, you understand why they taste so good in cafés. It is only a question of patience.
