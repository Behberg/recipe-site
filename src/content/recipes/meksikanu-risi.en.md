---
title: Mexican tomato rice
description: Fluffy rice cooked with tomatoes, onion, garlic and cumin. A side for tacos and burritos.
steps:
  - Rinse and drain the rice.
  - Fry the rice in oil in a pan for 5 minutes, until lightly golden.
  - Add the onion, garlic and cumin and fry for 2 minutes.
  - Pour in the tomato sauce and stock and season with salt. Cook covered over a low heat for 18 minutes.
  - Let it stand for 5 minutes, fluff with a fork and serve with coriander and lime.
---

Frying the rice before cooking is the key: it keeps the grains separate.
