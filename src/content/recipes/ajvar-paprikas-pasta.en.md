---
title: "Ajvar (roasted red pepper relish)"
description: "Balkan ajvar: a sweet roasted pepper and aubergine relish with garlic, preserved for winter. On bread, with meat and grilled food."
yield: "about 4 jars of 0.3 l"
steps:
  - "Roast the peppers and aubergine at 230 °C for 30 to 40 minutes, until the skins are charred."
  - "Put in a bowl, cover with cling film for 15 minutes, then peel and deseed."
  - "Mince or blend the flesh to a coarse paste."
  - "Heat the oil in a pan, add the paste and garlic. Cook gently for 30 minutes, stirring often, until thick."
  - "Add the vinegar, sugar and salt. Fill sterilised jars while hot and seal."
tips:
  - "The longer it cooks, the thicker and sweeter the ajvar becomes."
---

In the Balkans whole families roast peppers in big pans in their yards each autumn. Ajvar is their winter sunshine in a jar.
