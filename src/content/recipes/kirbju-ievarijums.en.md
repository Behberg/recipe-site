---
title: Pumpkin jam with orange
description: A sunny pumpkin jam with orange and lemon. Surprisingly delicious and very pretty.
yield: about 4 jars of 0.5 l
steps:
  - Cut the pumpkin flesh into 1 cm cubes, sprinkle with the sugar and leave overnight.
  - Wash the oranges and lemon, cut them skin and all into small pieces and remove the seeds.
  - Add them to the pumpkin and bring to the boil.
  - Simmer over a low heat for 40 to 50 minutes, until the pumpkin turns translucent.
  - Pour the hot jam into jars and seal.
---

In jam, pumpkin looks like amber and tastes of citrus.
