---
title: "Aperol spritz cocktail"
description: "The Italian summer cocktail Aperol spritz with prosecco, Aperol, soda water and a slice of orange. Light, bittersweet and refreshing in 3 minutes."
steps:
  - "Fill a large wine glass with ice."
  - "Pour in the prosecco."
  - "Add the Aperol."
  - "Top up with soda water and stir gently."
  - "Garnish with a slice of orange."
tips:
  - "The classic ratio is 3:2:1: prosecco, Aperol and soda water."
---

Aperol spritz is the Italian aperitivo hour in a glass. In Latvia it has become the symbol of summer terraces.
