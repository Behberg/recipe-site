---
title: "Crispy tofu and vegetable stir fry"
description: "Crispy fried tofu cubes with broccoli, pepper and carrot in a sweet soy and ginger sauce. A quick, healthy vegan dinner."
steps:
  - "Pat the tofu dry with a towel, cut into cubes and toss in the cornflour."
  - "Heat 2 spoonfuls of oil in a wok and fry the tofu for 6 to 8 minutes until golden on all sides. Set aside."
  - "In the rest of the oil stir fry the broccoli, pepper and carrot strips for 4 minutes."
  - "Add the garlic and ginger, then the soy sauce, honey and sesame oil."
  - "Return the tofu and toss so the sauce coats everything. Serve with rice."
tips:
  - "The drier the tofu, the crispier it gets: press it under a heavy pan for 15 minutes."
---

Tofu tastes of whatever it is cooked with. In this sauce even people who say they do not like tofu clear their plates.
