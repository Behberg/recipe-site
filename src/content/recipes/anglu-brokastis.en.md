---
title: "Full English breakfast"
description: "The full English breakfast: sausages, bacon, fried egg, baked beans, mushrooms, grilled tomatoes and toast. A hearty weekend breakfast."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 100 °C to keep things warm as they are done."
  - "Fry the sausages in oil in a large pan for 10 minutes, turning. Add the bacon and fry until crisp. Keep warm in the oven."
  - "In the same pan fry the mushrooms and halved tomatoes for 4 minutes."
  - "Heat the beans in a small pan. Toast the bread and butter it."
  - "Fry the eggs in butter. Arrange everything on large plates."
tips:
  - "Order matters: start with what takes longest and finish with the eggs."
---

A full English is more than a meal: it is a Sunday morning ceremony after which nobody needs lunch.
