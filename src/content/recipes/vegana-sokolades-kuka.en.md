---
title: "Vegan chocolate cake"
description: "A moist chocolate cake with no eggs or milk. The batter comes together in one bowl in five minutes."
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 180 °C. Oil a round 22 cm tin and line it with baking paper."
  - "In a bowl mix the flour, sugar, cocoa, bicarbonate of soda and salt."
  - "Pour in the water, oil, vinegar and vanilla sugar. Stir until the batter is smooth."
  - "Pour into the tin and bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until a skewer comes out dry. Leave to cool."
  - "Melt the chocolate with the coconut milk, stir and pour over the cooled cake."
---

A recipe from the days when eggs and butter were scarce, which is why it always works.
