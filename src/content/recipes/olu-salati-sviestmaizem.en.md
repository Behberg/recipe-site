---
title: "Egg salad with chives for sandwiches"
description: "Creamy egg salad with mayonnaise, mustard and chives. The simplest sandwich spread for breakfast and the Easter table."
steps:
  - "Boil the eggs for 10 minutes, cool in cold water and peel."
  - "Finely chop the eggs or mash them with a fork."
  - "Stir in the mayonnaise or yoghurt, mustard and chopped chives."
  - "Season with salt, pepper and a pinch of paprika."
  - "Spread on bread or spoon into lettuce leaves."
tips:
  - "After Easter this salad is the best way to use up the dyed eggs."
---

Egg salad is so simple you can make it half asleep, and it always tastes good.
