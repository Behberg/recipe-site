---
title: "Rassolnik (pickle and barley soup)"
description: "Hearty rassolnik soup with beef, pearl barley and pickled cucumbers. Gently tangy, warming and perfect on a cold day."
steps:
  - "Cover the beef with the water, bring to the boil, skim and simmer with the bay leaf for 1 hour. Soak the barley in the meantime."
  - "Lift out the meat and chop it. Add the barley to the broth and simmer for 20 minutes."
  - "Add the diced potatoes and simmer for 10 minutes."
  - "Fry the onion and carrot in oil, add the grated pickles and cook for 5 minutes. Tip into the soup."
  - "Add pickle brine to taste, return the meat, season with pepper and simmer for 5 minutes. Serve with sour cream and dill."
tips:
  - "Salt only at the end: the pickles and brine are already salty."
---

Rassolnik is how the last pickles and brine in the jar get used up. The flavour is one people crave.
